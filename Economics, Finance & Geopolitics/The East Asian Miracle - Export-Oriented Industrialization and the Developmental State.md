# The East Asian Miracle - Export-Oriented Industrialization and the Developmental State

## Overview
The 'East Asian Miracle' refers to the unprecedented period of rapid, sustained economic growth and poverty eradication achieved by East and Southeast Asian economies between the 1960s and 1990s.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
The Developmental State & Governed Markets: Championed by the **Four Asian Tigers** (South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Hong Kong) and later adopted by China, development defied both Soviet central planning and Anglo-American pure laissez-faire orthodoxy. Alice Amsden and Robert Wade demonstrated that these economies succeeded through the **Developmental State** model: authoritarian or technocratic governments actively 'governed the market' by: (1) Targeted subsidized credit and infant-industry protection contingent strictly on **export performance standards** (disciplined capitalism); (2) Land reform that eliminated feudal landlord rentierism and raised rural productivity; (3) Massive public investment in universal education and STEM technical training; (4) Strategic state-directed industrial conglomerates (e.g., South Korean *Chaebols* like Samsung, Hyundai).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Export-Oriented Industrialization (EOI): Rather than relying on closed-market Import Substitution Industrialization (ISI), East Asian economies adopted EOI, forcing domestic firms to compete in cutthroat global export markets, rapidly climbing the technological value curve from textiles to electronics, semiconductors, and heavy shipbuilding.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
World Bank 1993 flagship report acknowledged the unique synthesis of state guidance and outward orientation, serving as the primary blueprint for modern industrial policy worldwide.

## Key Terminology
- **Developmental State**: State model where the government guides national economic development through strategic industrial planning and disciplined subsidies.
- **Export-Oriented Industrialization (EOI)**: Economic strategy accelerating development through manufacturing products intended primarily for export to global markets.
- **Chaebol**: Large, family-controlled South Korean corporate conglomerates that drove national industrial modernization under government coordination.
