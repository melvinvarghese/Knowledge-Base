# Shock Therapy and Post-Soviet Transition - Privatization, Oligarchy, and Hyper-Inflation

## Overview
Shock Therapy was the radical economic doctrine applied across Russia and Eastern Europe following the 1991 dissolution of the Soviet Union, attempting an instantaneous transition from central planning to free-market capitalism.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
The Neoliberal Formula: Advised by Western economists (Jeffrey Sachs) and implemented in Russia under President Boris Yeltsin and Yegor Gaidar. Grounded in Washington Consensus ideology, it mandated simultaneous: (1) Immediate price liberalization (abolishing state price controls overnight); (2) Rapid mass voucher privatization of state-owned enterprises; (3) Drastic fiscal austerity and monetary contraction to suppress inflation; (4) Trade and currency liberalization.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Economic Catastrophe & The Rise of the Oligarchs: The shock was catastrophic for Russia: overnight price decontrol unleashed hyperinflation exceeding 2,500% in 1992, completely wiping out citizens' lifetime savings. Real Russian GDP collapsed by over 40% between 1991 and 1998 (worse than the Great Depression). Through the corrupt **Loans-for-Shares** scheme in 1995, the Kremlin auctioned off Russia's crown jewel oil, gas, and metallurgical monopolies to a handful of well-connected insiders at bargain-basement prices, creating Russia's notorious billionaire **Oligarchs** while life expectancy and living standards plummeted.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Stands in stark contrast to China's gradualist, dual-track reform approach, explaining modern Russian geopolitical revanchism and distrust of Western institutions.

## Key Terminology
- **Shock Therapy**: Sudden and dramatic release of price and currency controls, withdrawal of state subsidies, and immediate privatization of state assets.
- **Loans-for-Shares**: Controversial 1995 Russian privatization scheme where private commercial banks lent money to the bankrupt state in exchange for controlling stakes in major enterprises.
- **Voucher Privatization**: Distribution of state privatization certificates to all citizens, which were quickly bought up by speculators and oligarchs for cash.
