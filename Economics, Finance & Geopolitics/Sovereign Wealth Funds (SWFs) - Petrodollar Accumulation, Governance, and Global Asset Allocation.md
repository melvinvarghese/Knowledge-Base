# Sovereign Wealth Funds (SWFs) - Petrodollar Accumulation, Governance, and Global Asset Allocation

## Overview
A Sovereign Wealth Fund (SWF) is a state-owned investment vehicle funded by foreign exchange reserves, commodity export surpluses, or fiscal savings, managed independently to achieve long-term financial returns.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Funding Sources & Economic Functions: SWFs serve three primary macroeconomic objectives: (1) **Fiscal Stabilization**: Insulating national budgets from extreme commodity price volatility (saving windfalls during booms, drawing down during busts to counter Dutch Disease); (2) **Intergenerational Savings**: Transforming finite, depletable underground natural resources (crude oil, natural gas, minerals) into perpetual, diversified international financial capital for future generations; (3) **Strategic Industrial Development**: Directing domestic and foreign capital into cutting-edge technologies, infrastructure, and national strategic champions.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Major Global Funds & The Santiago Principles: Leading funds include Norway's Government Pension Fund Global (over $1.5 trillion, funded by North Sea oil, holding ~1.5% of all global public equities), China Investment Corporation (CIC), Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA), and Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF). Due to historical concerns that foreign SWFs might pursue political or national security objectives through corporate acquisitions, the **Santiago Principles** (2008) established voluntary international standards for SWF governance, transparency, and commercial-only investment mandates.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
SWFs manage over $12 trillion in assets globally, serving as anchor institutional investors in global real estate, private equity, tech unicorns, and sovereign debt markets.

## Key Terminology
- **Sovereign Wealth Fund (SWF)**: State-owned investment fund composed of financial assets derived from commodity windfalls or national balance of payments surpluses.
- **Intergenerational Equity**: Economic fairness principle ensuring that the exploitation of non-renewable natural resources benefits future generations equally.
- **Santiago Principles**: Set of 24 international guidelines promoting good governance, accountability, and commercial investment orientation for sovereign wealth funds.
