# Free Trade Agreements - NAFTA-USMCA, CPTPP, and Regional Trade Blocs

## Overview
Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) are bilateral or plurilateral treaties between sovereign states that eliminate reciprocal tariffs, reduce regulatory barriers, and liberalize cross-border investment.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Viner's Analysis: Trade Creation vs Trade Diversion: Economists analyze FTAs using Jacob Viner's customs union theory: **Trade Creation** occurs when an FTA allows lower-cost partner production to replace high-cost domestic production, boosting global economic efficiency. **Trade Diversion** occurs when preferential zero tariffs cause imports to switch from an efficient non-member producer to an inefficient member producer simply due to tariff avoidance, creating net welfare losses.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Major Modern Trade Blocs: (1) **USMCA** (United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement, replacing NAFTA 1994): Imposes stringent rules of origin for automotive manufacturing (75% regional value content) and labor value requirements. (2) **CPTPP** (Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership): High-standard Asia-Pacific pact setting rules for digital economy, intellectual property, state-owned enterprise transparency, and environmental standards. (3) **RCEP** (Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership): World's largest FTA by population and GDP, uniting ASEAN, China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, and New Zealand.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Illustrates the fragmentation of global trade from universal multilateral WTO rules into competing regional geoeconomic coalitions.

## Key Terminology
- **Trade Creation**: Economic gain achieved when free trade allows lower-cost foreign goods to replace more expensive domestic production.
- **Trade Diversion**: Economic inefficiency caused when preferential trade pacts redirect trade from a low-cost global producer to a higher-cost member state.
- **Rules of Origin**: Customs criteria used to determine the national source of a product to qualify for preferential zero-tariff FTA treatment.
