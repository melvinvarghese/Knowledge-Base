# Inflation and Hyperinflation - Demand-Pull, Cost-Push, and Monetary Collapse

## Overview
Inflation represents the sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services over time, eroding the purchasing power of money. When price increases accelerate out of control, it degrades into hyperinflation.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Theoretical Foundations & Causation: Inflation is classified into **Demand-Pull Inflation** (aggregate demand outpaces aggregate supply, often characterized as 'too much money chasing too few goods') and **Cost-Push Inflation** (aggregate supply drops due to wage increases or raw material supply shocks). In monetarist theory governed by the equation of exchange $MV = PY$, sustained inflation is fundamentally a monetary phenomenon driven by the growth of money supply $M$ exceeding real output growth $Y$. **Hyperinflation** (defined by Phillip Cagan as inflation exceeding 50% per month) occurs when governments finance catastrophic fiscal deficits entirely through unbacked fiat currency creation, causing a collapse in real money demand and currency velocity $V \to \infty$.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Historical & Modern Case Studies: Historical hyperinflation episodes include the Weimar Republic (1923), Hungary (1946, highest recorded peak doubling prices every 15 hours), and Zimbabwe (2008). Modern episodes in Venezuela (2016-present) demonstrate the devastating interplay of oil-revenue collapse, price controls, and monetization of public deficits.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Central banks target a credible 2% inflation anchor to prevent deflationary stagnation while avoiding compounding inflation risks. Indexation of sovereign bonds (TIPS) and cost-of-living adjustments (COLA) safeguard asset values.

## Key Terminology
- **Demand-Pull Inflation**: Price increases driven by aggregate economic demand exceeding productive capacity.
- **Cost-Push Inflation**: Price surges caused by rising production costs such as wages or critical imported inputs.
- **Hyperinflation**: Rapid, out-of-control inflation exceeding 50% per month that completely destroys currency trust.
