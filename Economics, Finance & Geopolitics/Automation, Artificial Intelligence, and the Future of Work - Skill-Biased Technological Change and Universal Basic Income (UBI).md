# Automation, Artificial Intelligence, and the Future of Work - Skill-Biased Technological Change and Universal Basic Income (UBI)

## Overview
The acceleration of robotics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence is reshaping the global labor market, raising profound questions regarding structural technological unemployment, wage polarization, and social safety nets.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Skill-Biased vs Cognitive Automation: Historically, the Industrial Revolution automated routine physical labor while creating millions of factory and clerical jobs. In the late 20th century, **Skill-Biased Technological Change (SBTC)** automated routine cognitive and administrative tasks, polarizing the labor market into high-wage analytical professionals and low-wage service workers while hollowing out the middle class. Modern generative AI and autonomous systems represent a paradigm shift: automating non-routine cognitive, creative, and software tasks, directly threatening white-collar professions.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Economic Policy Responses: Economists debate whether AI will create new employment categories faster than it destroys old ones (the 'Luddite Fallacy' debate). If capital owners capture all productivity gains while labor's share of national income collapses, radical redistributive mechanisms are proposed: (1) **Universal Basic Income (UBI)**: Guaranteed unconditional cash payments to all citizens to ensure baseline survival; (2) **Robot Taxes**: Levies on automated capital to fund worker retraining; (3) **Sovereign Wealth Funds for AI Dividends**.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Shapes international labor regulations, corporate workforce restructuring, and political debates over inequality and technological transition.

## Key Terminology
- **Skill-Biased Technological Change (SBTC)**: A shift in technology that increases the relative demand for and wages of skilled, highly educated workers over unskilled workers.
- **Universal Basic Income (UBI)**: A sociopolitical model where all permanent residents of a country receive a regular, unconditional sum of money from the government.
- **Labor Share of Income**: The proportion of national gross domestic product paid out as compensation, wages, and benefits to workers rather than capital owners.
