# The World Trade Organization (WTO) - GATT Principles, Dispute Settlement, and Most-Favored-Nation

## Overview
The World Trade Organization (WTO) is the multilateral intergovernmental organization responsible for regulating, negotiating, and enforcing international trade agreements across 164 member nations.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Core Principles of Multilateral Trade: Established on January 1, 1995, succeeding the 1947 General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT) via the Marrakesh Agreement. The WTO framework rests on three pillars: (1) **Most-Favored-Nation (MFN) Rule** (GATT Article I): Any trade advantage, tariff reduction, or privilege granted to one nation must be extended immediately and unconditionally to all WTO members. (2) **National Treatment Rule** (GATT Article III): Once imported goods have entered a domestic market, they must be treated no less favorably than domestic goods in terms of taxes, regulations, and internal sales rules. (3) **Bound Tariffs**: Members commit to maximum ceiling rates that cannot be raised without compensatory negotiations.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Dispute Settlement & Modern Impasse: The WTO Dispute Settlement Body (DSB) historically provided binding adjudication through panels and an Appellate Body. However, the system has been paralyzed since 2019 due to the US veto of new Appellate Body appointments, reflecting dissatisfaction with multilateral dispute overreach and the inability to discipline non-market state subsidies (e.g., China's state-owned enterprises).

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Lowered global average industrial tariffs from over 40% in 1947 to below 5% today, but struggles with the rise of plurilateralism, digital trade, and industrial subsidies.

## Key Terminology
- **Most-Favored-Nation (MFN)**: Foundational treaty principle requiring equal, non-discriminatory trade treatment for all members of an agreement.
- **National Treatment**: International trade obligation requiring imported goods to receive treatment equal to domestically produced goods after crossing the border.
- **Appellate Body**: The seven-member judicial organ of the WTO authorized to uphold, modify, or reverse legal findings in multilateral trade disputes.
