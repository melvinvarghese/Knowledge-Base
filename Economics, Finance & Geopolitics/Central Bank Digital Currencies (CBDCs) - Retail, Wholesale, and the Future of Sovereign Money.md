# Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) - Retail, Wholesale, and the Future of Sovereign Money

## Overview
A Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) is a digital form of sovereign fiat currency issued directly by a nation's central bank, representing a direct legal claim against the monetary authority rather than a commercial bank.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Architecture & Typologies: CBDCs are divided into two fundamental models: (1) **Wholesale CBDCs**: Accessible exclusively by regulated commercial banks and clearing institutions to settle high-value interbank payments, cross-border foreign exchange, and securities tokenization, radically improving settlement finality and counterparty liquidity; (2) **Retail (General-Purpose) CBDCs**: Available directly to the general public as a digital equivalent of physical cash, designed for daily consumer and business transactions.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Structural Trade-Offs & Financial Disintermediation: Retail CBDCs threaten **bank disintermediation**: during a banking panic, citizens could instantly transfer deposits from commercial banks directly into risk-free central bank accounts with a smartphone click, destroying commercial bank deposit bases and their ability to extend private sector credit. To prevent this, central banks propose holding caps and tiered interest rates. Furthermore, CBDCs raise profound civil liberty concerns regarding state surveillance of transactions and programmatic money expiration.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Piloted globally by China (e-CNY), the Bahamas (Sand Dollar), the European Central Bank (Digital Euro project), and the Bank for International Settlements (Project Agora).

## Key Terminology
- **Retail CBDC**: Digital sovereign currency issued directly to consumers as a risk-free digital alternative to physical bank notes.
- **Bank Disintermediation**: The withdrawal of funds from commercial banks into direct central bank deposits or non-bank assets, impairing commercial lending capacity.
- **Settlement Finality**: The legally irrevocable and unconditional transfer of funds or assets extinguishing the underlying obligation.
