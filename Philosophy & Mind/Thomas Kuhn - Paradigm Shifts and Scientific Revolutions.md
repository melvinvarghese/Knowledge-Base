# Thomas Kuhn - Paradigm Shifts and Scientific Revolutions

## Overview
In *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions* (1962), Thomas Kuhn overturned cumulative models of scientific progress, demonstrating that science advances through cyclical **Paradigm Shifts**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Cycle of Scientific Revolutions: 1. Normal Science (puzzle-solving within an accepted shared paradigm); 2. Model Crisis (accumulation of unexplained anomalies); 3. Scientific Revolution (young researchers forge a new, competing conceptual paradigm).

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Incommensurability: Competing paradigms cannot be directly compared using neutral objective criteria because they redefine observational vocabulary, standards of evidence, and worldview frameworks (e.g., Newtonian vs Einsteinian physics).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Sociology of science, corporate organizational innovation models, critiques of absolute scientism.

---

## Key Terms
- **Paradigm Shift**: Fundamental change in the basic concepts and experimental practices of a scientific discipline.
- **Normal Science**: Scientific research carried out within an established, unquestioned conceptual paradigm.
- **Incommensurability**: Inability to directly evaluate or translate two conceptual paradigms using a shared objective metric.
