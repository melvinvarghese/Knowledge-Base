# Thomas Aquinas - Quinque Viae (The Five Ways)

## Overview
In the *Summa Theologiae*, Thomas Aquinas formulated the **Quinque Viae (The Five Ways)**—five cosmological arguments for the existence of God grounded in Aristotelian physics and metaphysics.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Five Proofs Breakdown: 1. Argument from Motion (Unmoved Mover); 2. Argument from Efficient Cause (First Cause); 3. Argument from Contingency (Necessary Being); 4. Argument from Degree (Absolute Perfection); 5. Teleological Argument (Intelligent Designer directing natural bodies to their ends).

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Denial of Infinite Regress: An actual infinite series of dependent instrumental causes cannot explain contemporary existence; there must exist a primary unconditioned entity possessing existence intrinsically (*Ipsum Esse Subsistens*).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Classical theistic philosophy, Catholic scholasticism, development of formal metaphysical cosmological arguments.

---

## Key Terms
- **Unmoved Mover**: First primary cause of all motion in the universe, which is not itself moved by anything.
- **Contingency**: Status of an entity whose existence is not logically necessary and depends on prior causes.
- **Ipsum Esse Subsistens**: Being subsisting through itself alone, Thomas Aquinas's ontological definition of God.
