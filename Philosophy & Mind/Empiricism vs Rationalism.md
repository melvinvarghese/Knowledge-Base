# Empiricism vs Rationalism

> **The Big Idea:** The great philosophical clash of the Enlightenment over where human knowledge comes from. **Rationalists** claim knowledge comes from pure reason, logic, and innate ideas; **Empiricists** claim the mind starts as a blank slate and all knowledge comes through sensory experience.

---

## ⚖️ Side-by-Side Comparison

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│             RATIONALISM              │       │              EMPIRICISM              │
│       (Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz)  │       │       (Locke, Berkeley, Hume)        │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤       ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Core Source: Pure Reason & Logic     │  vs.  │ Core Source: Sensory Observation     │
│ Truth is discovered from the "inside │       │ Truth is absorbed from the "outside  │
│ out" using deduction.                │       │ in" using experimentation.           │
│                                      │       │                                      │
│ Mind has innate ideas at birth       │       │ Mind is a "Tabula Rasa" (Blank Slate)│
│ Favorite field: Mathematics/Geometry │       │ Favorite field: Natural Sciences     │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🧠 1. Rationalism in Action

* **Argument:** You don't need to measure 1,000 triangles in the dirt with a ruler to know that the angles of a triangle add up to 180 degrees. Pure mathematical deduction proves it universally for all triangles that will ever exist.
* Mathematics, geometry, and fundamental logic exist prior to and independent of our eyesight or touch (**A Priori** knowledge).

---

## 👁️ 2. Empiricism in Action

* **John Locke's "Tabula Rasa":** A newborn baby’s mind is a blank sheet of white paper. There are no pre-installed thoughts or moral concepts. Everything you know—colors, heat, gravity, love—was written onto the paper by your five senses.
* **David Hume’s Skepticism:** How do you know the sun will rise tomorrow? You cannot prove it with pure logic. You only expect it because you have observed it every single day (**A Posteriori** knowledge).

---

## 🤝 The Great Synthesis: Immanuel Kant

In 1781, Immanuel Kant ended the feud:
> *"Thoughts without content are empty; intuitions without concepts are blind."*

* The Empiricists were right: all knowledge begins with **sensory experience**.
* But the Rationalists were also right: our minds possess **built-in hardware** (concepts of space, time, and cause-and-effect) that processes and organizes that sensory data.
