# Philippa Foot - Virtue Ethics and Natural Goodness

## Overview
Philippa Foot revitalized contemporary Aristotelian **Virtue Ethics**, introduced the iconic **Trolley Problem**, and grounded moral goodness in biological teleology in *Natural Goodness* (2001).

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Original Trolley Problem: Introduced the moral dilemma of diverting a runaway trolley to kill one person rather than five, analyzing the Doctrine of Double Effect (foreseeing harm vs intending harm).

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Natural Goodness & Moral Realism: Moral evaluation is not an arbitrary subjective emotivist preference, but a form of objective species-relative natural normativity, identical to evaluating whether an oak tree has healthy roots or a wolf pack functions cooperatively.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Deontological vs consequentialist ethical debates, moral psychology, bioethics decision frameworks.

---

## Key Terms
- **Virtue Ethics**: Ethical framework centering moral evaluation on character virtues rather than rules or outcomes.
- **Doctrine of Double Effect**: Ethical rule permitting an action with foreseen bad side-effects if the bad effect is not intended as a means.
- **Natural Normativity**: Evaluation of goodness based on whether an organism fulfills the characteristic life-form needs of its species.
