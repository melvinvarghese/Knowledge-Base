# Classical vs Operant Conditioning

> **The Big Idea:** The two foundational pillars of behavioral psychology. **Classical Conditioning** trains involuntary, automatic reflexes to new triggers; **Operant Conditioning** shapes voluntary choices using rewards and punishments.

---

## 🔔 Classical Conditioning in Real Life

* **Pavlov's Experiment:** Ringing a bell right before feeding a dog eventually caused the dog to salivate just hearing the bell alone.
* **Human Example:** If you get terrible food poisoning after eating oysters, the mere smell or photo of an oyster makes you instantly nauseous for months.
* **Smartphone ding:** The notification chime triggers a spike in dopamine before you even see what message arrived.

---

## 📦 Operant Conditioning (The 4 Quadrants)

Skinner organized consequences by whether you **Add (+)** or **Remove (-)** a stimulus, and whether you want to **Increase (Reinforce)** or **Decrease (Punish)** behavior:

| | Reinforcement (Increase Behavior) | Punishment (Decrease Behavior) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)**<br>*(Add something)* | **Positive Reinforcement:** Give a dog a treat for sitting. | **Positive Punishment:** Give a speeding ticket for driving 90 mph. |
| **Negative (-)**<br>*(Remove something)* | **Negative Reinforcement:** The annoying car seatbelt buzzer turns off when you buckle up. | **Negative Punishment:** Take away a teenager’s phone for missing curfew. |

---

## 🎰 The Addictive Secret: Variable Ratio Schedules

Why are slot machines and social media notification feeds so addictive?
* If a lever gives a treat every single time, you use it when hungry and stop.
* If a lever gives a treat at **random, unpredictable intervals** (Variable Ratio), the brain stays hyper-alert and pulls the lever endlessly.

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway

* **Design your habits with Operant Conditioning:** Immediately reward yourself with something pleasant (a hot espresso, checking off a box) right after doing hard work.
* **Audit your Classical triggers:** If sitting at your couch automatically triggers opening Instagram, move your phone or work at a dedicated desk.
