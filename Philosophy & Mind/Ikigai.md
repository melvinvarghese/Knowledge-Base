# Ikigai (生きがい)

> **The Big Idea:** Your *Ikigai* is your reason for being—the sweet spot where what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can get paid for meet.

---

## 🎯 What Does It Mean?

*Iki* means "life" and *gai* means "worth" or "value." In simple terms, it is **the reason you get out of bed in the morning**.

In Okinawa (one of the world's longest-living communities), elders don't have a word for "retirement." Instead, they stay active, creative, and connected throughout their entire lives because they have an *Ikigai*.

---

## 🧭 The 4 Pillars of Ikigai

To discover your sweet spot, look at the intersection of four questions:

1. **What do you love?** (Your Passion)
2. **What are you good at?** (Your Vocation)
3. **What does the world need?** (Your Mission)
4. **What can you get paid for?** (Your Profession)

```
        [ What You Love ]
                ▲
    Passion    │    Mission
  ◄────────────┼────────────►
    Profession │    Vocation
                ▼
      [ What Pays You ]
```

- If you do what you love and are good at, but don't get paid: you have passion, but no financial security.
- If you get paid well and are good at it, but don't love it: you feel comfortable, but empty inside.
- **Ikigai is when all four overlap.**

---

## 🛠️ How to Find and Nurture Your Ikigai

- **Start small:** Ikigai doesn't have to be a multi-billion dollar tech startup. It can be baking bread, teaching children, organizing books, or writing code.
- **Notice what gives you energy:** What tasks make you lose track of time? What topics do you read about without anyone asking you to?
- **Connect to others:** Having a role that helps even one person in your community gives deep purpose to your daily routine.
