# Ludwig Wittgenstein - Tractatus and Language-Games

## Overview
Ludwig Wittgenstein revolutionized 20th-century philosophy twice: first with the logical atomism of the *Tractatus*, and later with the linguistic pragmatic **Language-Games** of *Philosophical Investigations*.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Early Wittgenstein (Picture Theory of Meaning): 'The world is the totality of facts, not of things.' Meaningful propositions picture logical states of affairs; what cannot be spoken of logically must be passed over in silence ('Wovon man nicht sprechen kann, darüber muss man schweigen').

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Late Wittgenstein (Language-Games & Meaning as Use): Rejects universal ideal logic; words are tools whose meanings are determined by their use within dynamic social activities ('language-games'). Philosophical problems are linguistic confusions ('cramps') cured by showing the fly the way out of the fly-bottle.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Analytic philosophy, ordinary language philosophy, artificial intelligence natural language understanding.

---

## Key Terms
- **Language-Game**: Rule-governed social practice or activity embedded within a human form of life involving language.
- **Picture Theory**: Early doctrine holding that meaningful propositions logically mirror states of affairs in reality.
- **Family Resemblance**: Concept holding that categories are linked by overlapping shared features rather than a single essential trait.
