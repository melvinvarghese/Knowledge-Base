# Daniel Dennett - The Intentional Stance and Multiple Drafts Model

## Overview
Daniel Dennett proposed that consciousness is an evolved user-illusion produced by parallel cognitive processes (**Multiple Drafts Model**), analyzed through the **Intentional Stance**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Three Predictive Stances: 1. Physical Stance (predicting behavior via physics/chemistry); 2. Design Stance (predicting behavior via functional purpose/programming); 3. **Intentional Stance** (treating an entity as a rational agent possessing beliefs, desires, and goals).

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
The Multiple Drafts Model of Consciousness: Rejects the 'Cartesian Theater' where a central mental observer views a unified stream of experience; instead, the brain constantly processes competing, overlapping streams of editorial interpretation without a single final master draft.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Artificial intelligence theory of mind, behavioral robotics, evolutionary cognitive science.

---

## Key Terms
- **Intentional Stance**: Predictive strategy interpreting the behavior of an entity by treating it as a rational agent with beliefs.
- **Cartesian Theater**: Flawed mental model imagining a central internal conscious observer watching brain experiences.
- **User-Illusion**: Dennett's thesis that conscious self-awareness is an evolved interface simplification of complex neural processes.
