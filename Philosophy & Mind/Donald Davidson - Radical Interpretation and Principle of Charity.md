# Donald Davidson - Radical Interpretation and Principle of Charity

## Overview
Donald Davidson developed theories of meaning and mind centered on **Radical Interpretation**, **Anomalous Monism**, and the indispensable **Principle of Charity**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Principle of Charity: When interpreting the speech and beliefs of an unknown speaker, we must rationally assume that the majority of their beliefs are true, coherent, and consistent with the world; without this assumption, mutual interpretation is impossible.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Anomalous Monism: Mental events are identical to physical brain events (token physicalism), yet mental descriptions cannot be reduced to strict deterministic physical laws, preserving mental autonomy without Cartesian dualism.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Theories of action and intentionality, natural language semantics (Tarskian truth conditions), hermeneutics.

---

## Key Terms
- **Principle of Charity**: Methodological presumption that a speaker's beliefs are largely true and rational.
- **Radical Interpretation**: Interpreting the language of a speaker from scratch without preexisting translation manuals.
- **Anomalous Monism**: Position that mental events are physical events, but are not governed by strict physical laws.
