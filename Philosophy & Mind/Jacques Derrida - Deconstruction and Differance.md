# Jacques Derrida - Deconstruction and Differance

## Overview
Jacques Derrida pioneered **Deconstruction**, demonstrating that all Western philosophical texts contain internal structural contradictions and destabilizing hierarchical binary oppositions.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Logocentrism and Binary Inversions: Western thought privileges speech over writing, presence over absence, and reason over emotion (Logocentrism); deconstruction inverts and displaces these hierarchies, showing the favored term depends upon its excluded opposite.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
The Concept of Différance: A neologism combining *differing* (spatial distinction) and *deferring* (temporal postponement); meaning in language is never immediately present in a sign, but endlessly deferred across an infinite chain of linguistic signifiers.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Literary criticism, legal critical theory (Critical Legal Studies), post-modern architecture and humanities.

---

## Key Terms
- **Deconstruction**: Philosophical method interrogating texts to reveal internal contradictions and unstable assumptions.
- **Différance**: Derrida's term denoting that meaning is simultaneously differentiated and endlessly postponed.
- **Logocentrism**: Philosophical bias privileging speech, direct presence, and absolute foundational truth.
