# Emotional Intelligence (EQ)

> **The Big Idea:** Daniel Goleman demonstrated that raw cognitive intelligence (IQ) gets you hired, but **Emotional Intelligence (EQ)**—the ability to identify, understand, and manage emotions in yourself and others—is what determines leadership, resilience, and life satisfaction.

## 🔍 The 5 Core Pillars in Plain English

1. **Self-Awareness:** Knowing what you are feeling in real time. Can you tell the difference between feeling *angry*, *hungry*, *tired*, or *embarrassed*?
2. **Self-Regulation:** Pausing before reacting. Not sending the heated email at 11 PM or screaming during traffic.
3. **Internal Motivation:** Being driven by internal passion, craft, and purpose rather than superficial status symbols or praise.
4. **Empathy:** Truly understanding another person's emotional reality and meeting them where they are without judgment.
5. **Social Skills:** Navigating social dynamics, de-escalating tense disagreements, and building genuine trust.

---

## 🛠️ How to Practice EQ Daily

* **Name it to tame it:** When overwhelmed, assign a precise word to your emotion. Neuroscience shows that labeling a feeling instantly reduces activity in the amygdala (fear/anger center).
* **Take the 6-second pause:** Emotional neurochemicals flood the bloodstream for about 6 seconds. When triggered, take 3 slow breaths before answering.
* **Listen to understand, not to rebut:** In an argument, don't formulate your comeback while the other person is talking. Listen until you can summarize their viewpoint accurately.
