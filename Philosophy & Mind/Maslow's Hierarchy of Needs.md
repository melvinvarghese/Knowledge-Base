# Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

> **The Big Idea:** Abraham Maslow’s pyramid of human motivation. Human beings cannot focus on higher goals like art, morality, and purpose until their basic physical and emotional survival needs are safely met.

---

## 🔺 The 5 Levels of the Pyramid

```
                     ▲
                    / \
                   /   \
                  /     \
                 /  (5)  \      Self-Actualization (Fulfilling potential)
                /─────────\
               /    (4)    \    Esteem (Confidence, respect, status)
              /─────────────\
             /      (3)      \  Love & Belonging (Friendship, family, intimacy)
            /─────────────────\
           /        (2)        \ Safety & Security (Health, employment, shelter)
          /─────────────────────\
         /          (1)          \ Physiological Needs (Food, water, sleep, warmth)
        └─────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🔍 The Levels in Plain English

1. **Physiological Needs:** If you are starving, freezing, or haven't slept in 48 hours, your entire consciousness cares only about survival.
2. **Safety Needs:** Predictability, financial security, stable housing, physical protection from violence and sickness.
3. **Love & Belonging:** Humans are tribal mammals. Isolation, loneliness, and rejection trigger physical pain pathways in the brain.
4. **Esteem Needs:** Feeling capable, achieving competence, earning self-respect, and receiving recognition from peers.
5. **Self-Actualization:** Becoming everything you are capable of becoming. Writing the symphony, mastering your craft, serving others, and realizing your highest creative potential.

---

## 💡 Deficiency Needs (D-Needs) vs. Growth Needs (B-Needs)

* **D-Needs (Levels 1–4):** You only notice them when you *lack* them. (You don't think about oxygen until you are underwater; you don't think about belonging until you feel excluded). Once satisfied, the craving goes away.
* **B-Needs (Being / Growth - Level 5):** Self-actualization is never "satisfied." The more you learn and create, the more energized you become to explore further.

---

## 🛠️ Practical Application

* **Troubleshoot low motivation:** If you feel depressed or unable to focus on big life dreams, check the base of your pyramid first: Are you sleeping 8 hours? Are you eating real food? Are you isolated? Fix the base before judging your willpower.
* **Leadership & Teamwork:** Employees worried about job security or paying rent cannot produce innovative, creative work. Safety unlocks creativity.
