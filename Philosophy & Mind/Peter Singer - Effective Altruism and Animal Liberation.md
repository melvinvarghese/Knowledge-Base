# Peter Singer - Effective Altruism and Animal Liberation

## Overview
Peter Singer pioneered modern utilitarian applied ethics, arguing in *Animal Liberation* (1975) against speciesism and formulating the global movement of **Effective Altruism**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Equal Consideration of Interests & Speciesism: Sentience (the capacity to suffer and feel pleasure) is the only defensible boundary for moral consideration; prioritizing human interests over animal suffering solely based on species is arbitrary **speciesism**.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
The Drowning Child Analogy & Effective Altruism: If walking past a shallow pond and seeing a drowning child, you are morally obligated to save them despite ruining your clothes; by analogy, failing to donate dispensable wealth to prevent preventable child starvation in developing nations is morally equivalent.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Global philanthropic charity evaluator organizations (GiveWell), animal welfare legal reform, utilitarian global ethics.

---

## Key Terms
- **Speciesism**: Prejudice or discrimination against individuals based solely on their biological species membership.
- **Effective Altruism**: Philosophical movement using evidence and rigorous reasoning to maximize global positive impact.
- **Sentience**: The biological capacity to experience positive and negative conscious states, particularly suffering and pleasure.
