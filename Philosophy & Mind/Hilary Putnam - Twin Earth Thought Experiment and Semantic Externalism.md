# Hilary Putnam - Twin Earth Thought Experiment and Semantic Externalism

## Overview
Hilary Putnam established **Semantic Externalism** with his famous 'Twin Earth' thought experiment (1975), summarizing his radical linguistic thesis: **'Cut the pie any way you like, meanings just ain't in the head!'**

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Twin Earth Thought Experiment: Imagine an identical planet 'Twin Earth' where everything is duplicate, except the liquid called 'water' is chemical compound $XYZ$ rather than $H_2O$. When Earthling Oscar and Twin-Oscar both say 'water', their internal brain states are identical, yet they mean different physical substances.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Semantic Externalism: The meaning and truth-conditions of linguistic terms are determined partially by the external environment and historical social division of linguistic labor, not exclusively by internal mental psychology.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Externalist philosophy of mind, computational functionalism, epistemology of content.

---

## Key Terms
- **Semantic Externalism**: View that the meaning of words and mental content depend upon factors external to the individual.
- **Twin Earth**: Thought experiment demonstrating that identical internal mental states can have different reference meanings.
- **Division of Linguistic Labor**: Societal cooperation where specialists (chemists, jewelers) determine precise technical terms for the public.
