# Pyrrhonian Skepticism and Epoche - Suspension of Judgment

## Overview
Pyrrhonian Skepticism, formulated by Pyrrho of Elis and recorded by Sextus Empiricus, seeks mental tranquility through the systematic suspension of all theoretical dogmatic belief (**Epoché**).

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Ten Modes of Aenesidemus & Agrippa's Trilemma: Systematically demonstrating that for every rational claim, an equally valid contrary claim exists (**Isostheneia**). Any attempt to justify belief collapses into Agrippa's Trilemma: infinite regress, circular reasoning, or arbitrary dogmatic assertion.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Epoché Leading to Ataraxia: When the philosopher gives up striving to establish definitive dogmatic certainty and practices **Epoché** (suspension of judgment), mental anxiety vanishes and psychological tranquility (**Ataraxia**) follows like a shadow.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Precursor to modern radical fallibilism, epistemological foundationalism critiques, philosophy of science.

---

## Key Terms
- **Epoché**: The active suspension of assent or judgment regarding the objective truth of theoretical propositions.
- **Agrippa's Trilemma**: Epistemological paradox showing dogmatic justification must end in circularity, regress, or assumption.
- **Isostheneia**: Equivalence of opposing rational arguments producing balanced suspension of belief.
