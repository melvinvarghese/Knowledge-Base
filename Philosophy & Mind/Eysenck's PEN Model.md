# Eysenck's PEN Model

> **The Big Idea:** Hans Eysenck argued that personality is deeply rooted in our genetics and nervous system biology. He boiled personality down to three major biological dimensions: **Psychoticism**, **Extraversion**, and **Neuroticism** (PEN).

---

## 🧬 The 3 PEN Dimensions

```
  P ── Psychoticism   (Aggressive & Non-Conforming vs. Empathetic & Conventional)
  E ── Extraversion   (Under-aroused brain vs. Over-aroused brain)
  N ── Neuroticism    (Hyper-reactive nervous system vs. Stable nervous system)
```

### 1. Extraversion (E) — Brain Arousal Theory
* **Eysenck's Discovery:** Extroverts actually have **lower baseline brain arousal** in the cerebral cortex. Because their brains are under-stimulated, they seek loud music, crowds, parties, and risky thrills to feel awake.
* **Introverts** have **high baseline brain arousal**. Their brains are naturally stimulated, so crowded rooms and loud noise quickly overwhelm them. They prefer quiet reading and calm environments.

### 2. Neuroticism (N) — Sympathetic Nervous System
* Measures how quickly your "fight-or-flight" autonomic nervous system triggers.
* **High Neuroticism:** Rapid heart rate spike, quick panic, slow return to baseline after a scare.
* **Low Neuroticism:** Calm, slow to stress, quick recovery from shock.

### 3. Psychoticism (P) — Tough-Mindedness
* Added later by Eysenck to account for mental toughness, non-conformity, and impulsivity.
* **High P:** Cold, rebellious, hostile, unconventional, highly creative, prone to rule-breaking.
* **Low P:** Warm, empathetic, law-abiding, agreeable, socially compliant.

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway

* Understand your physiological wiring: If you are an introvert, you aren't "antisocial"—your nervous system is simply easily saturated. Protect your quiet recharge time without shame.
