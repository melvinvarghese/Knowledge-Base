# Carl Rogers - Self-Concept and Congruence

> **The Big Idea:** Humanistic psychologist Carl Rogers proposed that emotional suffering happens when the person you *actually are* (**Real Self**) is miles apart from the person you *think you should be* (**Ideal Self**). Health is closing that gap (**Congruence**).

---

## 🪞 The 3 Components of Self-Concept

```
1. Self-Image:  How you actually see yourself right now.
2. Ideal Self:  The fantasy version of who you wish you were.
3. Self-Worth:  How much value and love you believe you deserve.
```

---

## ⚖️ Congruence vs. Incongruence

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│          INCONGRUENCE           │       │           CONGRUENCE            │
│  (High Anxiety & Neurosis)      │       │     (Peace & Authentic Power)   │
├─────────────────────────────────┤       ├─────────────────────────────────┤
│                                 │       │                                 │
│   ┌────────┐       ┌────────┐   │       │          ┌───────┬───────┐      │
│   │  REAL  │       │ IDEAL  │   │       │          │ REAL  │ IDEAL │      │
│   │  SELF  │       │  SELF  │   │       │          │     SELF      │      │
│   └────────┘       └────────┘   │       │          └───────┴───────┘      │
│     (Large gap between them)    │       │         (High overlap/peace)    │
└─────────────────────────────────┘       └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Incongruence:** You work as an accountant, but constantly feel shame because you aren't a rockstar billionaire. You hide your flaws and live in constant fear of being exposed as a fraud.
* **Congruence:** You accept your real strengths, limitations, and passions. Your daily actions align with your genuine internal values.

---

## 🛑 Conditions of Worth (The Source of Incongruence)

Why do we develop an unrealistic Ideal Self?
* In childhood, parents and society often give **conditional love**: *"We only love and praise you when you get straight A's, stay quiet, and don't cry."*
* To keep that love, children repress their true feelings and fabricate an artificial "Ideal Self" to win approval.

---

## 🛠️ How to Achieve Congruence

1. **Audit your "Shoulds":** Write down things you feel guilty about. Ask: *"Do I actually care about this, or was this a standard imposed on me by someone else?"*
2. **Practice self-acceptance:** Acknowledge your current flaws without self-loathing. You cannot change what you refuse to look at honestly.
3. **Align actions with reality:** Live authentically today instead of waiting for a magical day when you become "perfect."
