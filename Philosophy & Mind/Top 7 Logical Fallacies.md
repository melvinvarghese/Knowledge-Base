# Top 7 Logical Fallacies

> **The Big Idea:** A logical fallacy is a flaw in reasoning that ruins an argument. People use them constantly in debates, politics, and social media—sometimes accidentally, often deliberately to manipulate. Spotting them gives you an intellectual shield.

---

## 🛡️ The 7 Most Common Fallacies

### 1. Ad Hominem (Attacking the Person)
* **What it is:** Attacking your opponent's character, appearance, or background instead of addressing their actual argument.
* *Example:* *"You claim lowering taxes will help small businesses, but you dropped out of college, so what would you know?"*

### 2. The Straw Man
* **What it is:** Misrepresenting or exaggerating an opponent's argument to make it look ridiculous and easy to defeat.
* *Example:*
  * Person A: *"We should allocate more budget to public schools."*
  * Person B: *"Why do you hate our police and military so much that you want to leave our city defenseless?"*

### 3. Begging the Question (Circular Reasoning)
* **What it is:** When the conclusion of an argument is quietly smuggled into the premise itself.
* *Example:* *"This book is completely infallible because it was written by the supreme authority, and we know that authority is supreme because the book says so."*

### 4. False Dilemma (Black-and-White Thinking)
* **What it is:** Forcing a complex issue into only two extreme choices while ignoring all middle ground.
* *Example:* *"Either you support our bill 100%, or you hate our country."*

### 5. Appeal to Ignorance (*Argumentum Ad Ignorantiam*)
* **What it is:** Arguing that a claim must be true simply because it hasn't been proven false yet (or vice-versa).
* *Example:* *"Nobody has definitively proven that ghosts don't exist, therefore haunted houses are real."*

### 6. Slippery Slope
* **What it is:** Claiming without evidence that one small step will inevitably trigger a catastrophic chain reaction of disaster.
* *Example:* *"If we let employees work from home on Fridays, nobody will ever come to the office, productivity will plummet, and our company will go bankrupt in six months."*

### 7. Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc (False Cause)
* **What it is:** Assuming that because event B happened *after* event A, event A must have *caused* event B.
* *Example:* *"I wore my green socks today and passed the exam. Those green socks are lucky!"*

---

## 🛠️ How to Defend Your Arguments

1. **Separate the person from the claim:** Even a convicted liar can state a mathematically correct truth. Attack the premise, not the messenger.
2. **Steel-man before you rebut:** Repeat your opponent's position in its strongest, most generous form before critiquing it. It destroys straw men instantly.
3. **Ask for the connecting link:** When someone asserts a slippery slope or false cause, ask: *"What evidence proves that step 1 inevitably causes step 4?"*
