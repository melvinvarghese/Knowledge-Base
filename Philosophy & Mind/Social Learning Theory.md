# Social Learning Theory

> **The Big Idea:** Albert Bandura showed that humans do not need to experience direct reward or punishment to learn. We learn the vast majority of our behaviors, attitudes, and emotional reactions simply by **observing and imitating others** (Modeling).

---

## 🧸 The Famous Bobo Doll Experiment (1961)

Bandura divided children into groups:
* Group 1 watched an adult beat up, punch, kick, and scream insults at an inflatable "Bobo doll."
* Group 2 watched an adult play quietly with toys, ignoring the doll.

Later, the children were left alone in a room with toys, including the Bobo doll:
* The children who watched the aggressive adult imitated the exact same physical violence and verbal insults.
* They even invented new aggressive tools (like toy guns) that the adult hadn't used.

**Conclusion:** Behavior is not merely genetic or internally driven—it is absorbed from models in our environment.

---

## 🧠 The 4 Stages of Observational Learning

```
1. ATTENTION:   You must notice and pay attention to the model.
2. RETENTION:   You must encode and remember what they did.
3. REPRODUCTION:You must possess the physical/mental skill to execute it.
4. MOTIVATION:  You must have a reason (reward, admiration) to reproduce it.
```

---

## 💡 Vicarious Reinforcement

You don't need to get burned by a hot stove to learn it's hot—you watch someone else flinch.
* If a coworker gets publicly praised for asking good questions in meetings, you start doing the same (**Vicarious Reward**).
* If someone gets reprimanded for being late, you make sure to arrive early (**Vicarious Punishment**).

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway

* **You become your models:** You absorb the vocabulary, financial habits, and emotional reactions of the people and media you consume daily.
* **Lead by demonstration:** In parenting, management, and teaching, what you *do* speaks a thousand times louder than what you *say*.
