# Ichigo Ichie (一期一会)

> **The Big Idea:** *"One time, one meeting."* Every single encounter, conversation, and moment is completely unique and will never happen again in the exact same way. Treat it like a once-in-a-lifetime treasure.

---

## ⏳ What Does It Mean?

Originated in traditional Japanese tea ceremonies by tea master Sen no Rikyu. Even if the host and guests gather in the exact same tea room every single week, today's gathering is distinct:
- The weather outside is different.
- The minds of the people in the room have changed.
- The water boils at a slightly different temperature.

Therefore, the tea master treats every cup of tea with supreme care and undivided attention, as if it were the only time they will ever meet.

---

## 💡 Why It Matters Today

In our modern world, we are physically present but mentally somewhere else:
- Looking at Twitter while eating dinner with family.
- Thinking about tomorrow's to-do list while a friend is telling a story.
- Rushing through conversations just to get back to a screen.

*Ichigo Ichie* calls you back to reality. It whispers: **Be here now, because you cannot replay this moment.**

---

## 🛠️ How to Live Ichigo Ichie

1. **Give undivided presence:** When someone speaks to you, close your laptop screen or set your smartphone face down. Look them in the eyes.
2. **Never leave bad endings lingering:** Because life is unpredictable, treat every goodbye with warmth and kindness. Never walk out in petty anger.
3. **Celebrate ordinary rituals:** A quiet morning cup of coffee isn't just fuel—it is a unique sensory experience happening right now. Savor the warmth of the mug.
