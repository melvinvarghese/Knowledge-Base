# Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz - Monadology and Pre-Established Harmony

## Overview
Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz's *Monadology* (1714) posits that the universe is composed of infinite, simple, immaterial, soul-like dynamic units called **Monads**, coordinated by God via **Pre-Established Harmony**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Windowless Monads: Monads have no physical parts, cannot be divided, and 'have no windows through which anything could enter or depart'; each monad internally reflects the entire universe from its unique perspective.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Pre-Established Harmony & Mind-Body Dualism: Mind and body do not interact physically; rather, God synchronized mental states and physical motions at the moment of Creation like two perfectly synchronized clocks ticking in unison.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Early precursor to informational computational ontology, theodicy ('best of all possible worlds'), dynamic calculus physics.

---

## Key Terms
- **Monad**: Indivisible, immaterial metaphysical entity possessing internal perception and appetition.
- **Pre-Established Harmony**: Doctrine that apparent causal interactions between mind and matter are pre-synchronized by God.
- **Principle of Sufficient Reason**: Philosophical axiom that nothing happens without a reason why it is so and not otherwise.
