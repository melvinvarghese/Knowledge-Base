# Erik Erikson - 8 Stages of Psychosocial Development

> **The Big Idea:** Human personality evolves over your entire lifetime across 8 distinct stages. At each stage, you face a central psychological crisis. Resolving it gives you a lasting mental strength; failing leaves an emotional wound.

---

## 🗺️ The 8 Lifespan Stages

```
1. Infancy (0-1.5):       Trust vs. Mistrust             ──► Hope
2. Toddlerhood (1.5-3):   Autonomy vs. Shame & Doubt     ──► Will
3. Preschool (3-5):       Initiative vs. Guilt           ──► Purpose
4. School Age (5-12):     Industry vs. Inferiority       ──► Competence
5. Adolescence (12-18):   Identity vs. Role Confusion    ──► Fidelity
6. Young Adult (18-40):   Intimacy vs. Isolation         ──► Love
7. Middle Age (40-65):    Generativity vs. Stagnation    ──► Care
8. Older Age (65+):       Integrity vs. Despair          ──► Wisdom
```

---

## 🔍 The 4 Key Adult Stages in Detail

### Stage 5: Identity vs. Role Confusion (Teens)
* **Question:** *"Who am I, and where do I fit?"*
* **Success:** Discovering your personal values, beliefs, and career interests.
* **Failure:** Peer pressure conformity, identity crises, drifting without direction.

### Stage 6: Intimacy vs. Isolation (Young Adulthood)
* **Question:** *"Can I love and be loved deeply?"*
* **Success:** Building honest, committed friendships and romantic partnerships without losing your sense of self.
* **Failure:** Fear of commitment, emotional walls, chronic loneliness.

### Stage 7: Generativity vs. Stagnation (Middle Adulthood)
* **Question:** *"Can I make my life count for future generations?"*
* **Success:** Mentoring younger people, raising healthy children, contributing work that outlasts you.
* **Failure:** Midlife crisis, selfish hoarding of comfort, feeling bored and stagnant.

### Stage 8: Ego Integrity vs. Despair (Late Adulthood)
* **Question:** *"Did I live a good, honest life?"*
* **Success:** Looking back with gratitude, peace, and acceptance of life’s imperfections.
* **Failure:** Regret, bitterness, fearing death because you never truly lived.

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway

* **You never stop developing:** Your personality is not locked at age 18. Growth continues in your 30s, 50s, and 70s.
* **Unresolved crises can be healed:** If you struggle with trust today because of infancy or childhood wounds, recognizing the stage allows you to consciously rebuild security and competence now.
