# Bandura's Self-Efficacy Theory

> **The Big Idea:** Self-efficacy is not general self-esteem; it is your specific belief in your capability to execute the actions required to succeed at a specific task. If you believe you can, you persist; if you believe you can't, you quit at the first obstacle.

---

## ⚡ Self-Esteem vs. Self-Efficacy

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│          SELF-ESTEEM            │       │          SELF-EFFICACY          │
├─────────────────────────────────┤       ├─────────────────────────────────┤
│ "I am a good, worthy person."   │  vs.  │ "I have what it takes to learn  │
│  (General feeling of self-worth)│       │  Python, run 5 miles, or lead   │
│                                 │       │  this client presentation."     │
└─────────────────────────────────┘       └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

You can have high self-esteem (you love who you are) but low self-efficacy in statistics (you believe you will inevitably fail math tests).

---

## 🏗️ The 4 Sources of Self-Efficacy

Albert Bandura identified four ways to build unshakable self-efficacy:

```
            ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
            │ 1. MASTERY EXPERIENCES (Most Powerful) │
            │ Small, tangible past wins              │
            └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                │
            ┌───────────────────┴────────────────────┐
            │ 2. VICARIOUS MODELING                  │
            │ Seeing someone like you succeed        │
            └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                │
            ┌───────────────────┴────────────────────┐
            │ 3. SOCIAL PERSUASION                   │
            │ Encouragement from a respected mentor  │
            └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                │
            ┌───────────────────┴────────────────────┐
            │ 4. PHYSIOLOGICAL STATE                 │
            │ Interpreting arousal as excitement     │
            └────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Mastery Experiences:** Nothing builds confidence like actual proof. When you successfully finish a hard task, your brain registers: *"I can handle this."*
2. **Vicarious Modeling:** Watching someone with similar background or skills succeed makes you realize: *"If they can do it, so can I."*
3. **Social Persuasion:** Realistic encouragement from someone you respect can push you through self-doubt.
4. **Physiological State:** When your heart races before a big event, interpret it as your body preparing to perform (excitement), not as proof of impending doom (panic).

---

## 🛠️ How to Boost Your Self-Efficacy

* **Stack micro-wins:** Break an overwhelming goal into tiny milestones so small you cannot fail. Each completed step deposits proof into your confidence account.
* **Curate your circle:** Distance yourself from chronic cynics who predict failure; spend time around people who are actively striving and learning.
