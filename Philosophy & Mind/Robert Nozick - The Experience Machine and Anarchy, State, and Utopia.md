# Robert Nozick - The Experience Machine and Anarchy, State, and Utopia

## Overview
In *Anarchy, State, and Utopia* (1974), Robert Nozick refuted psychological and ethical hedonism with his **Experience Machine** thought experiment and articulated a libertarian minimal-state framework.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Experience Machine Thought Experiment: Imagine a machine that could stimulate your brain to give you any pleasurable, rewarding experience you desire while floating in a tank. Nozick asks: *Would you plug in for life?*

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Why We Reject the Machine: Most people refuse because we value actually *doing* things rather than merely having the illusion of doing them; we want to *be* a certain type of person and maintain contact with real reality, proving that pleasure is not our sole intrinsic good.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Critique of utilitarianism, foundations of libertarian property rights entitlement theory, philosophical ethics of virtual reality.

---

## Key Terms
- **Experience Machine**: Thought experiment demonstrating humans value authentic reality over simulated subjective pleasure.
- **Entitlement Theory**: Nozick's theory of justice holding that any distribution of property is just if acquired justly.
- **Libertarianism**: Political philosophy advocating minimal state intervention, individual liberties, and property rights.
