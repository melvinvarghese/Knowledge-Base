# Reciprocal Determinism

> **The Big Idea:** You are neither a helpless puppet of your environment nor a god of pure free will. Personality and behavior are created by a continuous, two-way feedback loop between **The Person**, **Their Behavior**, and **The Environment**.

---

## 🔄 The Triangular Feedback Loop

Albert Bandura proposed that three elements constantly influence and recreate each other:

```
                     ┌───────────────────┐
                     │    THE PERSON     │
                     │ (Beliefs, traits, │
                     │  expectations)    │
                     └─────────┬─────────┘
                              ▲ ▲
                             /   \
                            /     \
                           ▼       ▼
       ┌───────────────────┐       ┌───────────────────┐
       │     BEHAVIOR      │◄─────►│    ENVIRONMENT    │
       │ (Actions, words,  │       │ (Social context,  │
       │  work, choices)   │       │  rewards, room)   │
       └───────────────────┘       └───────────────────┘
```

---

## 🌍 Real-World Example: The Hostile Meeting

Look at how the triangle feeds on itself:

1. **The Person:** A manager has a cynical internal belief (*"My team is lazy and plotting against me"*).
2. **The Behavior:** Because of that belief, the manager speaks bluntly and micromanages every email.
3. **The Environment:** In reaction to the micromanagement, team members pull back, stop speaking up, and act resentful.
4. **The Loop Closes:** The team's cold reaction confirms the manager's original suspicion (*"See? I knew they were uncooperative!"*).

---

## 🛠️ How to Break Negative Loops

Because the system is reciprocal, changing **any single corner** changes the whole system:

* **Change the Environment:** Move to a quiet workspace, delete distracting social apps, or hang out with energetic friends.
* **Change the Behavior:** Even if you feel anxious inside, walk into the room with good posture, smile, and ask a warm question. The room's positive reaction will immediately alter your internal feelings.
* **Change the Internal Belief:** Reframe a situation before entering it (*"These people want this project to succeed just like I do"*).
