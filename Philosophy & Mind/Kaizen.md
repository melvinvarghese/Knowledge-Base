# Kaizen (改善)

> **The Big Idea:** Continuous, tiny improvements every day. Instead of trying to radically overhaul your life in a week, you focus on getting **1% better** day after day.

---

## 📈 What Does It Mean?

*Kai* means "change" and *Zen* means "good." 

Originally made famous by Japanese manufacturers like Toyota, *Kaizen* revolutionized how businesses operate. Rather than waiting for a massive crisis or trying giant, risky makeovers, workers looked for small, 10-second improvements to their station every single morning.

Over weeks and months, tiny incremental changes compound into unstoppable results.

---

## 💡 The Power of 1% Compounding

- If you get **1% better** every day for a year:  
  $1.01^{365} \approx 37.78$ (You end up nearly **38 times better**).
- If you get **1% worse** every day:  
  $0.99^{365} \approx 0.03$ (You decline to almost zero).

Big goals fail because they trigger fear and burnout. Small steps succeed because they are too small to fail.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use Kaizen in Daily Life

1. **Make the threshold ridiculously easy:**
   - Instead of reading 1 hour a day: read **2 pages**.
   - Instead of going to the gym for 90 minutes: do **5 push-ups**.
   - Instead of deep-cleaning your entire house: clean **one drawer**.
2. **Never break the chain:** Consistency beats intensity every single time.
3. **Ask the daily Kaizen question:**
   > *"What is one small thing I can make 1% easier or better today?"*
