# Oubaitori (桜梅桃李)

> **The Big Idea:** Never compare your journey to anyone else's. Just as cherry, plum, peach, and apricot trees bloom at different times with different petals, you grow and bloom at your own unique pace.

---

## 🌲 What Does It Mean?

The word *Oubaitori* is formed from the kanji for four iconic spring-blossoming trees:
1. **Ou (桜):** Cherry blossom
2. **Bai (梅):** Plum blossom
3. **To (桃):** Peach blossom
4. **Ri (李):** Apricot / Damson blossom

All four trees grow near each other. They all bloom in the springtime. Yet, none of them competes with the others:
- The plum blossoms early in the biting cold of late winter.
- The cherry burst into dazzling clouds in mid-spring.
- The peach blossoms with deep pink petals shortly after.
- The apricot bears sweet fruit in its own season.

The cherry tree never looks over at the plum tree and thinks: *"Why was she faster than me?"* Each tree simply trusts its own internal clock.

---

## 💡 The Trap of Social Comparison

In modern life, social media constantly rubs other people's highlights in our faces:
- Someone bought a house at 24.
- Someone became a CEO at 30.
- Someone got in top shape in 6 months.

Comparing your messy behind-the-scenes to someone else's highlight reel leads only to jealousy, self-doubt, and discouragement.

---

## 🛠️ How to Practice Oubaitori

1. **Focus on your own roots:** Spend your energy watering your own soil—your skills, your character, your health—rather than staring over the fence.
2. **Celebrate others without feeling small:** When a friend succeeds, be happy for their blooming. Their win does not take away your capacity to bloom.
3. **Trust your timing:** Some people find their calling in their twenties; others hit their stride in their forties or fifties. Run your own race.
