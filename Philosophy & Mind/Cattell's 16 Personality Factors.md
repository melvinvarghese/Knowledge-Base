# Cattell's 16 Personality Factors

> **The Big Idea:** Raymond Cattell used mathematical factor analysis to reduce thousands of English dictionary adjectives down to **16 fundamental source traits** that account for human personality.

---

## 📊 Surface Traits vs. Source Traits

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SURFACE TRAITS: What you see on the outside           │
│  (e.g., talkative, smiling, buying drinks)             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                           ▼                            │
│  SOURCE TRAITS: The underlying mathematical engines    │
│  (e.g., Warmth, Social Boldness, Low Tension)          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Surface Traits:** Visible, day-to-day behaviors that cluster together.
* **Source Traits:** The deep psychological structures that cause those behaviors.

---

## 🧪 The 16 Primary Factors (16PF)

Cattell measured each person on a 1-to-10 continuum across 16 spectrums:

1. **Warmth:** Reserved vs. Outgoing
2. **Reasoning:** Concrete thinker vs. Abstract problem solver
3. **Emotional Stability:** Reactive vs. Calm and mature
4. **Dominance:** Submissive vs. Forceful and assertive
5. **Liveliness:** Serious and quiet vs. Spontaneous and enthusiastic
6. **Rule-Consciousness:** Expedient (bends rules) vs. Dutiful (by the book)
7. **Social Boldness:** Shy and hesitant vs. Bold and venturesome
8. **Sensitivity:** Tough-minded and utilitarian vs. Sensitive and aesthetic
9. **Vigilance:** Trusting vs. Suspicious and skeptical
10. **Abstractedness:** Practical and grounded vs. Imaginative and daydreaming
11. **Privateness:** Open and genuine vs. Discreet and guarded
12. **Apprehension:** Self-assured vs. Insecure and self-blaming
13. **Openness to Change:** Traditional vs. Experimenting and progressive
14. **Self-Reliance:** Group-oriented vs. Independent and self-sufficient
15. **Perfectionism:** Tolerates disorder vs. Precise and disciplined
16. **Tension:** Relaxed and patient vs. Driven, restless, and impatient

---

## 🛠️ Legacy and Modern Value

While the Big Five (OCEAN) is simpler for daily conversation, Cattell's **16PF Questionnaire** remains one of the most widely used psychometric tools for executive hiring, military selection, and career counseling because of its nuanced profile breakdown.
