# Global Health Governance and Pandemics

## Overview
Pathogens ignore sovereign borders and customs checkpoints, turning local outbreaks into planetary economic and security crises within days. Global health governance coordinates disease surveillance, vaccine equity, and emergency rules through the World Health Organization (WHO) and public-private alliances.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Anchored in the WHO's legally binding International Health Regulations (IHR, 2005), 196 nations pledge to build early-warning lab capacity and promptly report Public Health Emergencies of International Concern (PHEIC) — while avoiding panicked, counterproductive travel/trade bans that punish transparent reporting. Historically, coordinated global vaccination led by WHO achieved humanity's greatest medical triumph: eradicating smallpox completely in 1980.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Yet COVID-19 exposed severe structural weaknesses: WHO lacks independent inspection powers inside uncooperative states, while wealthy countries hoarded early mRNA vaccine doses ('vaccine nationalism'), leaving the COVAX equity facility chronically under-supplied and fueling fierce TRIPS intellectual-property waiver battles.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Post-pandemic reforms advance a binding Pandemic Accord, regional African/Asian mRNA manufacturing hubs, and 'One Health' surveillance linking human clinics, livestock, and wildlife spillovers.

## Key Terminology
- **International Health Regulations**: Binding 2005 WHO legal framework obligating countries to detect and report cross-border disease threats.
- **PHEIC**: Public Health Emergency of International Concern — WHO's highest formal global outbreak alert.
- **Vaccine nationalism**: Wealthy nations pre-purchasing scarce pandemic vaccine supplies ahead of global equitable distribution.
- **COVAX**: Global multilateral initiative aiming to pool procurement and deliver vaccines to lower-income countries.
- **One Health**: Integrated surveillance linking human medicine, veterinary livestock health, and wildlife ecology.
- **TRIPS waiver**: Proposal to temporarily suspend patent monopolies on pandemic vaccines and diagnostics during global emergencies.
