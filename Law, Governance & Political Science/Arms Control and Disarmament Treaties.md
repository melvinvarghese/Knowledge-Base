# Arms Control and Disarmament Treaties

## Overview
Arms control and disarmament treaties seek to cap arsenals, ban inhumane weapons, and prevent accidental war through mutual verification. From Cold War bilateral nuclear limits (SALT, START, INF) to near-universal bans on chemical, biological, and cluster munitions, arms control stabilizes strategic rivalry.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Unlike sweeping pacifist disarmament, practical arms control manages rivalry: the 1972 ABM Treaty and SALT I, the 1987 Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces (INF) Treaty eliminating an entire class of missiles, and New START capped deployed US and Russian strategic warheads with intrusive on-site inspections and satellite telemetry verification ('trust, but verify'). Simultaneously, multilateral treaties outlawed entire inhumane categories: the 1972 Biological Weapons Convention (BWC), 1993 Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC, verified by OPCW), and 1997 Ottawa Landmine Ban.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
In the 2010s–2020s, renewed great-power rivalry unraveled the INF Treaty, Open Skies, and New START dialogue, while the 2017 Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW) reflected non-nuclear states' frustration over slow disarmament.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Modern arms control confronts autonomous AI 'killer robots,' hypersonic glide vehicles, and dual-use biotechnology requiring fresh verification norms.

## Key Terminology
- **Strategic Arms Reduction (START)**: US-Russian treaties reducing deployed strategic nuclear warheads and bombers with on-site verification.
- **Chemical Weapons Convention**: 1993 binding global treaty banning chemical weapons production and stockpiles, monitored by OPCW.
- **Verification**: On-site inspections, satellite monitoring, and data exchanges confirming treaty compliance.
- **Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons**: 2017 UN treaty banning nuclear weapons comprehensively, supported largely by non-nuclear states.
- **Lethal Autonomous Weapons**: Emerging military AI systems capable of selecting and engaging targets without human intervention.
- **Confidence-Building Measures**: Notification of military exercises, crisis hotlines, and observer exchanges reducing accidental escalation.
