# Corporate Governance and Business Law

## Overview
Corporate law enables strangers to pool vast capital into enduring business enterprises through legal personhood and limited liability. Corporate governance regulates the perpetual 'principal-agent' tension between dispersed shareholders, executives, boards of directors, and broader stakeholders.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Two legal inventions powered modern capitalism: separate 'corporate personhood' (allowing companies to own assets, sign contracts, and survive founders' deaths) and 'limited liability' (capping investors' risk at their paid-in share value, encouraging broad equity markets). Because day-to-day managers control resources they don't wholly own, corporate law imposes strict 'fiduciary duties' of Care (informed business judgment) and Loyalty (no self-dealing or secret conflicts).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Governance debates pit Milton Friedman's 'shareholder primacy' (maximizing long-term equity value) against 'stakeholder capitalism' (balancing workers, suppliers, communities, and climate — echoed in German two-tier boards with mandatory labor co-determination and US Benefit Corporations).

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Securities laws (US SEC disclosure rules, Sarbanes-Oxley accounting audits) and insider-trading bans protect market trust, while insolvency/bankruptcy laws reorganize distressed firms rather than destroying viable businesses.

## Key Terminology
- **Limited liability**: Legal shield limiting investors' personal financial exposure strictly to their invested capital.
- **Fiduciary duty (Care & Loyalty)**: Strict legal obligations requiring directors to act informedly and solely in the corporation's best interest.
- **Principal-agent problem**: Conflict arising when corporate executives prioritize personal perks or short-term pay over owners' interests.
- **Business Judgment Rule**: Judicial deference shielding good-faith, informed board decisions from hindsight second-guessing.
- **Co-determination**: German corporate governance model granting workers mandatory seats on supervisory boards.
- **Insolvency / Bankruptcy**: Legal process liquidating or restructuring insolvent firms while protecting creditor order.
