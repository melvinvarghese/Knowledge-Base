# Electoral Integrity and Democratic Transitions

## Overview
Free and fair elections require far more than ballot boxes on election day: Pippa Norris defines electoral integrity across the entire cycle — independent boundary drawing, fair media access, transparent voter rolls, secret ballots, and peaceful loser consent.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Samuel Huntington's 'Third Wave' of democratization (1974–1990s) toppled dictatorships across Southern Europe, Latin America, East Asia, and Eastern Europe. Comparative scholars (Guillermo O'Donnell, Philippe Schmitter) showed peaceful transitions succeed best through 'pacted' negotiations assuring outgoing elites physical safety alongside broad civil-society mobilization.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Once elections begin, Adam Przeworski's minimalist criterion holds democracy survives only when incumbents actually *lose* and step down peacefully. Modern electoral integrity faces twin threats: overt fraud (ballot stuffing, banned opponents, biased election commissions) and subtle structural tilting (voter-ID hurdles, disinformation, dark-money distortion). Independent domestic citizen observers ('parallel vote tabulation') and international OSCE/Carter Center monitors deter rigging.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Protecting non-partisan election administrators, paper-trail verifiable ballots, and civic norms of conceding defeat anchors democratic stability.

## Key Terminology
- **Electoral integrity**: Adherence to international standards of fairness, transparency, and impartiality across the entire electoral cycle.
- **Third Wave of democratization**: Huntington's term for the global surge of democratic transitions from Portugal (1974) through post-Cold War Europe.
- **Loser's consent**: Willingness of defeated candidates and voters to accept election results peacefully because rules seemed fair.
- **Parallel Vote Tabulation**: Independent citizen-observer statistical audit verifying official election tallies from sampled polling stations.
- **Independent Election Commission**: Politically insulated non-partisan body administering voter rolls, ballots, and dispute adjudication.
- **Paper-trail ballot**: Voter-verified physical paper record enabling post-election risk-limiting audits against electronic hacking.
