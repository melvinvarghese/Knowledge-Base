# The Nibelungenlied - Siegfried, Brunhild, Hagen, and Germanic Heroic Tragedy

## Overview
The Nibelungenlied ('Song of the Nibelungs', c. 1200) is a Middle High German epic poem synthesizing ancient Germanic heroic legend, dragon lore, and the historical 5th-century destruction of the Burgundian kingdom by the Huns.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Part One: Siegfried's Triumph & Murder: The hero **Siegfried** of Xanten acquires the cursed treasure of the Nibelungs, the cloak of invisibility (*Tarnkappe*), and an invulnerable skin by bathing in the blood of a slain dragon (save for a single spot between his shoulder blades where a linden leaf fell). Siegfried aids King Gunther of Burgundy in winning the formidable warrior-queen **Brunhild** of Iceland by taking Gunther's place invisibly during feats of strength. In return, Siegfried marries Gunther's sister, **Kriemhild**. Years later, a bitter public quarrel over royal status between Brunhild and Kriemhild reveals the deception. Gunther's sinister, loyal vassal **Hagen of Tronje** tricks Kriemhild into marking Siegfried's vulnerable spot, stabbing him in the back at a forest spring and dumping the Nibelung gold hoard into the Rhine.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Part Two: Kriemhild's Revenge: Thirteen years later, Kriemhild marries **Etzel** (Attila the Hun). She invites her Burgundian brothers and Hagen to a grand feast in Hungary, orchestrating a bloodbath that consumes both armies. In the fiery hall, Kriemhild decapitates Hagen with Siegfried's sword Balmung, before being struck down herself by the veteran hero Hildebrand.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
The foundation of German national epic consciousness, inspiring Richard Wagner's monumental four-opera cycle *Der Ring des Nibelungen*.

## Key Terminology
- **Nibelung Hoard**: The vast, cursed treasure of gold guarded by dwarves, which brings destruction upon anyone who possesses it.
- **Hagen of Tronje**: The dark, ruthlessly loyal Burgundian warrior who assassinated Siegfried and concealed the Nibelung treasure in the Rhine.
- **Tarnkappe**: A magical cloak of invisibility that also multiplies the wearer's physical strength twelvefold.
