# Epic of Sundiata - The Lion King of Mali, Griot Oral Epics, and Mandinka Empire

## Overview
The Epic of Sundiata is the definitive oral epic of the Mandinka people of West Africa, recounting the rise of Sundiata Keita, the crippled prince who overcame exile and sorcery to found the Mali Empire in the 13th century.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Oral Transmission & The Role of the Griot: Preserved through centuries of oral performance by **Jelis / Griots**—the hereditary caste of West African oral historians, poets, genealogists, and diplomatic advisors (symbolized by the master griot Djeli Mamadou Kouyaté). The griot proclaims: 'We are vessels of speech, we are the repositories which harbor secrets many centuries old.'

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Narrative Arc: Prophecy & The Lion King: Maghan Kon Fatta, King of Niani, is prophesied to father a great emperor with an ugly 'buffalo woman', Sogolon Kedjou. Sundiata is born unable to walk, crawling on all fours until age seven. Mocked by his father's first wife (Sassouma Bérété), Sundiata rises miraculously to defend his mother's honor: pulling up a mighty baobab tree using an iron rod bent into an arch. Following his father's death, Sundiata is driven into exile across neighboring Sahelian kingdoms, where he matures into a brilliant commander and diplomat.

The Battle of Kirina & The Kouroukan Fouga: Sundiata returns to liberate his homeland from the evil sorcerer-king **Soumaoro Kanté** of Sosso. In the climatic Battle of Kirina (c. 1235), Sundiata pierces Soumaoro with an arrow tipped with a white rooster's spur (the secret weakness of his magic), dismantling the Sosso empire. Sundiata proclaims the **Kouroukan Fouga**, an oral constitution establishing the Mali Empire, abolishing slave mistreatment, establishing women's rights, and organizing societal guilds.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Serves as the foundation for modern West African identity and inspired the archetype of the exiled royal reclaiming the throne (including Disney's *The Lion King*).

## Key Terminology
- **Griot (Jeli)**: A West African hereditary praise singer, poet, and oral historian responsible for preserving cultural genealogies and epics.
- **Sundiata Keita**: The historic founder of the Mali Empire (reigned c. 1235–1255) celebrated as the 'Lion King' in oral literature.
- **Kouroukan Fouga**: The constitutional assembly of the Mali Empire that established legal structures, peace treaties, and social divisions.
