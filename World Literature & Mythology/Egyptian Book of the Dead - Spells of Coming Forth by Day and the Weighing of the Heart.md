# Egyptian Book of the Dead - Spells of Coming Forth by Day and the Weighing of the Heart

## Overview
The Egyptian *Book of the Dead* (known to ancient Egyptians as the *Spells of Coming Forth by Day*, *rw nw prt m hrw*) is a collection of magic funerary spells designed to guide the deceased through the underworld (*Duat*) to eternal paradise (*Aaru*).

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Evolution of Egyptian Funerary Literature: Evolved from Old Kingdom royal **Pyramid Texts** (carved on tomb walls) and Middle Kingdom noble **Coffin Texts** into New Kingdom papyrus scrolls custom-commissioned for private individuals and placed inside burial coffins.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Spell 125 & The Weighing of the Heart (Psychostasia): The moral and dramatic climax of the underworld journey occurs in the Hall of the Two Truths. The deceased recites the **Negative Confession** before the god Osiris and 42 assessor deities, declaring innocence of sins ('I have not stolen', 'I have not polluted the water', 'I have not spoken lies'). The jackal-headed god **Anubis** places the deceased's physical heart (*Ib*) on a scale balanced against the feather of **Ma'at** (truth, cosmic order, and justice). The ibis-headed scribe **Thoth** records the verdict. If the heart is heavy with sin, it is devoured by **Ammit** (the crocodile-lion-hippopotamus monster), annihilating the soul; if balanced, Horus leads the soul before Osiris into the reed fields of paradise.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Reflects humanity's earliest codified integration of personal ethics, afterlife judgment, and magical ritual literature.

## Key Terminology
- **Duat**: The ancient Egyptian underworld realm traversed by the sun god Ra at night and deceased souls seeking eternal life.
- **Psychostasia**: The sacred weighing of the deceased's heart against the feather of truth (Ma'at) to judge moral worthiness.
- **Ma'at**: The ancient Egyptian concept of truth, balance, cosmic order, harmony, law, morality, and justice.
