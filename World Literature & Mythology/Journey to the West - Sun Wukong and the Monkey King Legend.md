# Journey to the West - Sun Wukong and the Monkey King Legend

## Overview
Wu Cheng'en's *Journey to the West* (16th century, Ming Dynasty) is one of the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature, chronicling the pilgrimage of monk Tang Sanzang.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Sun Wukong - The Monkey King: Born from a mystical stone, Sun Wukong masters 72 Earthly Transformations, cloud-somersaulting, and the size-shifting Ruyi Jingu Bang iron staff. Defying Heaven, he erases his name from the Book of Life and Death and defeats the celestial armies before the Buddha pins him under Five Elements Mountain for 500 years.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Pilgrimage & Buddhist Allegory: The Bodhisattva Guanyin recruits Wukong, Zhu Bajie (Pigsy), and Sha Wujing (Sandy) to protect Buddhist monk Xuanzang on his journey along the Silk Road to India to retrieve Buddhist scriptures. Each trial allegorizes Buddhist mental purification and self-cultivation.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Enduring hero of East Asian popular culture, celebrated in Peking opera, contemporary animations, and video games.

## Key Terminology
- **Sun Wukong**: The Monkey King; supernatural hero of Journey to the West possessing immortality and shape-shifting powers.
- **Xuanzang**: Historical 7th-century Chinese Buddhist monk whose 16-year journey to India inspired the mythological epic novel.
- **Ruyi Jingu Bang**: Magical iron staff of Sun Wukong that can shrink to a needle or expand to touch the heavens.
