# Egyptian Cosmogony - The Heliopolitan Ogdoad, Osiris, and Isis

## Overview
Ancient Egyptian mythology explained cosmic order through primeval mound cosmogonies and the sacred funerary myth of Osiris and Isis.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Primeval Emergence & The Ennead: Out of the dark, boundless waters of Nun, the primordial mound (*Benben*) emerged. The creator sun god Atum-Ra generated Shu (air) and Tefnut (moisture), who begat Geb (earth) and Nut (sky), forming the ninefold pantheon (*Ennead*) of Heliopolis.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Osiris Myth - Death, Resurrection, and Pharaonic Rule: Osiris, righteous king of Egypt, was murdered and dismembered into fourteen pieces by his jealous brother Set. His devoted sister-wife Isis collected his parts, wrapped him in linen as the first mummy, and breathed life into him, conceiving Horus. Osiris became Lord of the Duat (underworld); Horus avenged his father, defeating Set to become divine pharaoh.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Theology of mummification, judgment of the dead (*Weighing of the Heart* against Ma'at's feather), royal legitimacy of pharaohs as living Horus.

## Key Terminology
- **Osiris**: Egyptian god of the underworld, afterlife, resurrection, and agriculture.
- **Duat**: The realm of the dead in ancient Egyptian mythology through which souls traveled to reach judgment.
- **Weighing of the Heart**: Funerary judgment where the heart of the deceased was weighed against the feather of truth (*Ma'at*).
