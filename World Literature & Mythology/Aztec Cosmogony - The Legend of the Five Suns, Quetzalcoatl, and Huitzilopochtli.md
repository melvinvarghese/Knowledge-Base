# Aztec Cosmogony - The Legend of the Five Suns, Quetzalcoatl, and Huitzilopochtli

## Overview
Aztec (Nahua) cosmogony articulates a dramatic, cyclic universe sustained through precarious cosmic balance, divine sacrifice, and ritual blood offering, recorded in codices such as the *Codex Chimalpopoca* and the Sun Stone.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
The Legend of the Five Suns: The universe is not eternal; it has been created and destroyed through four previous cosmic eras ('Suns'), each ruled by a different deity and destroyed by a catastrophic elemental collapse: (1) **Nahui-Ocelotl (4-Jaguar)**: Giants devoured by jaguars; (2) **Nahui-Ehecatl (4-Wind)**: Destroyed by hurricanes, humans turned to monkeys; (3) **Nahui-Quiahuitl (4-Rain)**: Destroyed by a rain of fire; (4) **Nahui-Atl (4-Water)**: Destroyed by a massive flood, humans turned to fish. The current era is the **Fifth Sun: Nahui-Ollin (4-Movement)**, doomed to be destroyed by apocalyptic earthquakes.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Divine Self-Sacrifice & Teotihuacan: At Teotihuacan, the humble, pustule-covered god **Nanahuatzin** threw himself into a sacrificial fire pit to emerge as the Fifth Sun (Tonatiuh), followed by Tecuciztecatl, who became the Moon. However, the Sun was motionless in the sky. To set it in motion, the gods collectively sacrificed their own divine blood.

Quetzalcoatl & Huitzilopochtli: **Quetzalcoatl** (the Feathered Serpent) descended to the underworld of Mictlan to gather the bones of ancestral humans, sprinkling them with his own blood to birth current humanity. **Huitzilopochtli**, the patron solar and war deity of the Mexica, required constant human heart and blood nourishment to wage nightly battle against the moon (Coyolxauhqui) and stars.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Governed the religious imperative behind imperial expansion, the 'Flowery Wars' (*Xochiyaoyotl*), and Tenochtitlan temple rituals.

## Key Terminology
- **Five Suns**: The Aztec cosmological doctrine asserting that the universe undergoes cyclic destruction and recreation through five cosmic solar eras.
- **Quetzalcoatl**: The Feathered Serpent deity of wisdom, priesthood, wind, and dawn who created Fifth-Sun humanity.
- **Huitzilopochtli**: The Mexica tribal patron deity of war and the sun, demanding vital blood nourishment to sustain cosmic dawn.
