# Zoroastrian Cosmogony - Ahura Mazda, Angra Mainyu, and Dualistic Eschatology

## Overview
Zoroastrian cosmogony, codified in the sacred Avesta and Pahlavi texts (such as the *Bundahishn*), represents one of history's earliest and purest systems of theological dualism and moral eschatology.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Cosmic Dualism: Light vs Darkness: The cosmos is an epic battleground between two primordial, uncreated forces: **Ahura Mazda** (the Wise Lord, representing uncorrupted truth, light, wisdom, and life) and **Angra Mainyu / Ahriman** (the Destructive Spirit, representing deceit, darkness, chaos, and death). Ahura Mazda creates the universe through his Holy Spirit (*Spenta Mainyu*) and the six **Amesha Spentas** (Holy Immortals). Ahriman responds by creating a counter-legion of demons (*daevas*) and corrupting the pure material creation with disease, winter, mortality, and lies (*Druj*).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Three Ages & The Chinvat Bridge: Cosmic time spans 12,000 years divided into four 3,000-year eras: Creation (*Bundahishn*), Mixture (*Gumezishn*, where good and evil battle in mortal human hearts), Separation, and the final Renovation. Upon physical death, souls cross the **Chinvat Bridge**: for the righteous, the bridge widens into a broad, fragrant path led by the beautiful maiden Daena into the House of Song; for the wicked, it narrows to a razor's edge, plunging the soul into the abyss of punishment.

The Saoshyant & Frashokereti: History concludes with the arrival of the savior (**Saoshyant**), who will resurrect all dead souls. In the final purification (**Frashokereti**), a river of molten bronze purges all cosmic evil, melting Ahriman into oblivion and establishing an immortal, perfected paradise on Earth.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Foundational ancestor of Judeo-Christian and Islamic concepts of cosmic dualism, angels, the devil, final judgment, heaven, hell, and the messianic apocalypse.

## Key Terminology
- **Ahura Mazda**: The supreme, uncreated creator deity of Zoroastrianism embodying light, truth, and cosmic order.
- **Frashokereti**: The Zoroastrian eschatological restoration of the cosmos, where all evil is eradicated and creation is permanently perfected.
- **Chinvat Bridge**: The judgment bridge in Zoroastrian afterlife beliefs that sifts righteous souls from the wicked.
