# Mesopotamian Cosmogony - Enuma Elish and the Triumph of Marduk

## Overview
The *Enuma Elish* ('When on high...') is the Babylonian creation epic, inscribed on seven clay tablets, celebrating the ascent of Marduk to supreme kingship over the gods.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
The Primordial Chaos & Generational War: In the beginning exist only fresh primordial waters (Apsu) and salt chaotic ocean waters (Tiamat). When their divine offspring disturb them, Apsu plots to destroy them, but is slain by Ea. In retribution, Tiamat spawns an army of venomous horned serpents, dragons, and demons led by Qingu.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Marduk's Victory & Creation of Humanity: The young champion Marduk agrees to fight Tiamat on the condition that he receives supreme sovereignty. Marduk slays Tiamat with a cosmic net and hurricane arrow, cleaving her colossal body in two to construct the heavens and the earth. From the blood of slain rebel commander Qingu, Marduk creates humankind (*lullu*) to bear the toil of the gods.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Recited annually during the Babylonian Akitu New Year festival, legitimizing Babylon's religious and political primacy.

## Key Terminology
- **Marduk**: Patron storm deity of the city of Babylon elevated to king of the gods in the Enuma Elish.
- **Tiamat**: Primordial goddess of the salt sea and embodiment of primordial cosmic chaos in Mesopotamian myth.
- **Akitu Festival**: Ancient Babylonian New Year festival celebrating the renewal of kingship and recitation of the creation epic.
