# Shinto Mythology - Izanami, Izanagi, Amaterasu, and the Kojiki

## Overview
Shinto mythology represents the indigenous animistic, polytheistic cosmogony and divine ancestral lore of Japan, compiled in the 8th-century imperial chronicles *Kojiki* (712 CE) and *Nihon Shoki* (720 CE).

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Creation & The Primordial Kami: Primordial deities stir the chaotic ocean with the jeweled spear *Ame-no-nuboko*, forming the islands of Japan. The divine couple **Izanagi** (He-Who-Invites) and **Izanami** (She-Who-Invites) descend to Earth. When Izanami dies giving birth to the fire god Kagutsuchi, she descends to **Yomi** (the underworld). Izanagi journeys to Yomi to retrieve her, but horrified by her decomposing, maggot-ridden body, he flees and seals the entrance with a boulder. Performing the sacred ritual purification (*misogi*) in a river, Izanagi washes his left eye to birth **Amaterasu-ōmikami** (the Sun Goddess), his right eye to birth **Tsukuyomi** (the Moon God), and his nose to birth **Susanoo-no-Mikoto** (the tempestuous Storm God).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Heavenly Rock Cave & Imperial Descent: Angered by Susanoo's destructive insolence, Amaterasu conceals herself in the **Ame-no-Iwato** (Heavenly Rock Cave), plunging the cosmos into pitch darkness. The gods lure her out with joyous dancing and sacred laughter. Amaterasu later dispatches her grandson, Ninigi-no-Mikoto, to rule the terrestrial realm, bestowing upon him the **Three Sacred Imperial Regalia**: the mirror (Yata no Kagami), the jewel (Yasakani no Magatama), and the sword (Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi).

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Established the divine legitimacy of the Japanese Imperial House, who trace direct lineage to Amaterasu.

## Key Terminology
- **Kami**: Spirits, divine powers, or natural phenomena revered in the Shinto religion encompassing ancestors, deities, and natural forces.
- **Amaterasu**: The supreme Sun Goddess in Shinto mythology and divine ancestor of the Japanese Imperial Family.
- **Imperial Regalia**: The sword, mirror, and jewel representing divine authority, wisdom, and benevolence in Japanese monarchy.
