# Yoruba Mythology and the Orishas - Olodumare, Obatala, and Eshu the Trickster

## Overview
Yoruba mythology is the foundational cosmological and spiritual tradition of the Yoruba people of southwestern Nigeria and Benin, which expanded via the transatlantic diaspora into Santería, Candomblé, and Vodou.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Cosmogony & The Orishas: The supreme, transcendent creator god is **Olodumare** (or Olorun), who reigns over the spiritual realm (*Orun*), leaving earthly affairs (*Aye*) to divine intermediaries known as **Orishas**. Olodumare tasked **Obatala**, the King of the White Cloth, with creating dry earth upon the primordial waters using a snail shell of soil, a sacred five-toed chicken, and a palm nut. When Obatala drank palm wine and molded humans with deformities, he swore eternal sobriety, becoming the special protector of people with disabilities.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Prominent Deities & Eshu the Crossroads Trickster: Key Orishas include: (1) **Eshu / Elegba**: The divine trickster, messenger of the gods, and guardian of the **crossroads**; without honoring Eshu, no prayer or sacrifice can cross between realms; (2) **Shango**: The fierce orisha of thunder, lightning, justice, and drumming; (3) **Ogun**: The orisha of iron, metallurgy, blacksmithing, and warfare; (4) **Yemoja**: Mother of the oceans and maternal protectress; (5) **Oshun**: Goddess of sweet fresh waters, beauty, fertility, and wealth.

The Ifá Divination System: Governed by Orunmila (spirit of wisdom), Ifá utilizes the sacred palm nuts and the 256 Odu (corpus of oral poetic verses) to diagnose spiritual balance and destiny.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Recognized by UNESCO as a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity, central to Afro-Atlantic identity.

## Key Terminology
- **Orisha**: Deities or spirits that reflect one of the manifestations of God in the Yoruba religion.
- **Eshu**: The Yoruba trickster orisha of the crossroads, communication, balance, and the transmission of prayers.
- **Ifá**: The complex Yoruba divination system relying on the Odu corpus to discern personal destiny and divine will.
