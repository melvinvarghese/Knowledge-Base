# Astrobiology - The Search for Extraterrestrial Life

> **The Big Idea:** The interdisciplinary study of the origin, evolution, and distribution of life in the universe, focusing on extremophiles on Earth and ocean worlds like Europa and Enceladus.

---

## 👽 What Life Needs
* **The 3 Universal Requirements:** Liquid water (universal solvent), organic carbon compounds (chemical building blocks), and an energy source (sunlight or hydrothermal chemical vents).
* **Prime Candidates in the Solar System:**
  * **Europa (Jupiter's Moon):** Houses a subsurface liquid ocean beneath an ice crust containing more water than all of Earth's oceans combined.
  * **Enceladus (Saturn's Moon):** Spews organic-rich saltwater geysers into space from hydrothermal vents.
  * **Titan:** Liquid methane lakes; potential home for exotic non-water-based biochemistry.
