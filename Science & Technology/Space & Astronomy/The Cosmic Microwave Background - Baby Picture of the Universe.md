# The Cosmic Microwave Background - Baby Picture of the Universe

> **The Big Idea:** The faint radiation leftover from 380,000 years after the Big Bang, offering the oldest thermal snapshot of the cosmos.

## 1. What It Is
The faint radiation leftover from 380,000 years after the Big Bang, offering the oldest thermal snapshot of the cosmos.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Cosmic Background Explorer (COBE) and Planck mapped subtle temperature fluctuations ($10^{-5}$ kelvin) that seeded all modern galaxies.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Cosmic Inflation -> Plasma Decoupling -> 2.725K Ambient Glow
```
