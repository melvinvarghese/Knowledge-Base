# International Space Station - Orbital Laboratory

> **The Big Idea:** A microgravity research laboratory orbiting Earth at 28,000 km/h. Continuously occupied by international astronauts since November 2000, representing humanity's greatest cooperative engineering feat.

---

## 🌍 Orbiting Laboratory
* **Speed & Altitude:** Orbits at roughly 400 km altitude, circling the globe every 90 minutes (witnessing 16 sunrises and sunsets every 24 hours).
* **Solar Power:** Solar array wings span the length of an American football field, generating 120 kilowatts of power.
* **Microgravity Research:** Testing human bone and muscle loss, fluid dynamics, protein crystallization, and closed-loop life support systems for future Mars missions.
