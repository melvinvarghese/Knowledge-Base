# Arecibo Message and Breakthrough Listen Optical SETI

## Overview
Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence (SETI) employs high-sensitivity radio telescopes and optical pulsed laser arrays to detect artificial, non-random electromagnetic signals transmitted by alien civilizations.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The 1974 Arecibo Interstellar Message: Frank Drake and Carl Sagan transmitted a 1,679-bit frequency-modulated binary message toward globular cluster M13; factoring into semi-prime dimensions ($23 	imes 73$) decodes a pixel bitmap displaying the numbers 1–10, DNA nucleotides, human anatomy, and the Arecibo dish.

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Breakthrough Listen Multi-Wavelength Strategy: Comprehensive astronomical survey scanning thousands of stars across 1–10 GHz radio frequencies searching for narrowband carrier signals ($<5	ext{ Hz}$, narrower than natural astrophysical emission) and nanosecond optical laser pulses.

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact

## Key Terms
- **Arecibo Message**: Interstellar radio message broadcast in 1974 encoding fundamental human and terrestrial information.
- **Narrowband Signal**: Electromagnetic transmission confined to an extremely narrow frequency slice indicative of artificial generation.
- **Technosignature**: Measurable property or physical artifact providing scientific evidence of past or present technological intelligence.
