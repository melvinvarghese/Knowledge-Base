# Pluto and Dwarf Planets

> **The Big Idea:** Reclassified in 2006 as a dwarf planet, Pluto is an icy world in the Kuiper Belt featuring nitrogen glaciers, ice mountains, and a heart-shaped plain (Tombaugh Regio).

---

## 🧊 What Makes a Dwarf Planet?

To be a full planet under IAU rules, a body must:
1. Orbit the Sun.
2. Have enough mass for round self-gravity.
3. **Have cleared the neighborhood around its orbit.** (Pluto shares its orbital zone with thousands of Kuiper Belt objects).

* **Other Dwarf Planets:** Eris (nearly Pluto's size), Ceres (in the asteroid belt), Haumea, and Makemake.
