# Gamma-Ray Bursts - The Most Energetic Explosions

> **The Big Idea:** Short, intense flashes of gamma rays lasting from milliseconds to minutes, releasing more energy in seconds than our Sun in its lifetime.

---

## 1. What It Is
Short, intense flashes of gamma rays lasting from milliseconds to minutes, releasing more energy in seconds than our Sun in its lifetime.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Long bursts originate from hypernovae (collapsing massive stars), while short bursts arise from colliding neutron stars.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Massive Star Collapse / Merger -> Ultra-Relativistic Jet -> Collimated Gamma Flash
```
