# Stellar Nucleosynthesis - How Elements are Forged in Stars

> **The Big Idea:** The fusion process inside stellar cores that converts hydrogen and helium into carbon, oxygen, neon, silicon, and iron.

## 1. What It Is
The fusion process inside stellar cores that converts hydrogen and helium into carbon, oxygen, neon, silicon, and iron.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Fusion stops at iron because iron-56 requires energy rather than releasing it, leading inevitably to core collapse.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
H -> He -> C & O -> Ne & Mg -> Si & S -> Iron Core (End of Fusion)
```
