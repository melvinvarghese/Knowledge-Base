# Voyager Missions - Into Interstellar Space

> **The Big Idea:** Launched in 1977, Voyager 1 and 2 completed the grand tour of the outer planets and became the first human-made objects to enter interstellar space, carrying the Golden Record.

---

## 🛰️ Beyond the Solar System
* **Voyager 1:** Entered interstellar space in 2012; currently over 24 billion kilometers from Earth (the most distant human artifact).
* **The Pale Blue Dot:** In 1990, Voyager turned its camera around to take a final photo of Earth from 6 billion km away—a tiny speck of dust suspended in a sunbeam.
* **The Golden Record:** A gold-plated phonograph record carrying greetings in 55 languages, animal sounds, music by Bach and Chuck Berry, and mathematical diagrams for extraterrestrial intelligence.
