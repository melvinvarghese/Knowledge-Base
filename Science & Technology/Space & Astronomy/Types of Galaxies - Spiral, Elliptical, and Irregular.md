# Types of Galaxies - Spiral, Elliptical, and Irregular

> **The Big Idea:** Edwin Hubble’s classification of the universe's island universes: rotating Spirals, aged smooth Ellipticals, and chaotic colliding Irregulars.

---

## 🔭 The Hubble Tuning Fork

* **Spiral Galaxies:** Flat rotating disks with spiral arms, rich in dust and gas, actively birthing stars (e.g., Milky Way, Andromeda).
* **Elliptical Galaxies:** Football-shaped swarms of older, reddish stars with little gas; range from dwarfs to cosmic giants containing trillions of stars.
* **Irregular Galaxies:** Chaotic shapes distorted by gravitational collisions (e.g., Magellanic Clouds).
