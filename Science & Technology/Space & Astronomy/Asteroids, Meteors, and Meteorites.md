# Asteroids, Meteors, and Meteorites

> **The Big Idea:** The debris left over from the formation of the Solar System. Understanding the difference between rocks in space, streaks of light in the sky, and stones that land on Earth.

---

## ☄️ Vocabulary Demystified

* **Asteroid:** A large rocky body orbiting the Sun (mostly in the Asteroid Belt between Mars and Jupiter).
* **Meteoroid:** A small chunk of rock or dust floating in space.
* **Meteor:** The streak of glowing light ('shooting star') produced when a meteoroid burns up from atmospheric friction.
* **Meteorite:** Any fragment that survives atmospheric entry and physically strikes Earth's ground.
