# Jupiter - The Gas Giant and Storm King

> **The Big Idea:** The undisputed heavyweight of our Solar System. A gas giant holding more mass than all other planets combined, with a swirling atmosphere of hydrogen and helium and dozens of moons.

---

## 🌀 The King of Planets

* **The Great Red Spot:** A colossal anticyclonic storm wider than Earth that has raged for at least 350 years.
* **Ocean of Metallic Hydrogen:** Deep inside, crushing pressure turns hydrogen gas into a liquid metallic electrical conductor, generating a magnetic field 20,000 times stronger than Earth's.
* **Cosmic Shield:** Jupiter’s enormous gravity captures or deflects dangerous comets, shielding the inner planets from frequent cataclysms.
