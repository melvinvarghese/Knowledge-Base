# Lagrange Points - Gravitational Parking Spots in Space

> **The Big Idea:** Five equilibrium positions where the gravitational pull of two large bodies equals the centripetal force required to orbit with them.

---

## 1. What It Is
Five equilibrium positions where the gravitational pull of two large bodies equals the centripetal force required to orbit with them.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** L1 and L2 (1.5 million km from Earth) are prime scientific orbits for solar observatories (SOHO) and space telescopes (JWST).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Sun --- L1 --- Earth --- L2 (JWST Station)
```
