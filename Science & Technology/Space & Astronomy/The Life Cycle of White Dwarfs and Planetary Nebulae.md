## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Degeneracy pressure holds white dwarfs up against gravity until they slowly cool over trillions of years into black dwarfs.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Main Sequence Star -> Red Giant Expansion -> Planetary Nebula Ejection -> White Dwarf
```
