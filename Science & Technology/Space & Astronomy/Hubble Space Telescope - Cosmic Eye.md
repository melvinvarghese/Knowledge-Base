# Hubble Space Telescope - Cosmic Eye

> **The Big Idea:** Launched in 1990 into low Earth orbit, Hubble revolutionized astronomy by observing the universe free from atmospheric distortion, determining the age of the cosmos and discovering dark energy.

---

## 🔭 The Orbital Eye
* **Key Discovery:** Allowed astronomers to calculate the expansion rate of the universe (Hubble Constant) and establish the universe's age at 13.8 billion years.
* **The Deep Field Image:** Pointed at a tiny, seemingly empty patch of dark sky for 10 consecutive days, revealing over 3,000 previously unknown distant galaxies.
* **Servicing Missions:** Designed to be serviced by space shuttle astronauts, repairing its initial blurred mirror flaw with corrective optics in 1993.
