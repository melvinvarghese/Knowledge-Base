# Solar Eclipses and Lunar Eclipses

> **The Big Idea:** Celestial alignments of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. A Solar Eclipse occurs when the Moon blocks sunlight from Earth; a Lunar Eclipse occurs when Earth's shadow falls on the Moon.

---

## 🌑 The Geometry of Shadows
* **Total Solar Eclipse:** Occurs because of an extraordinary cosmic coincidence: the Sun is 400x larger than the Moon, but also 400x farther away, making them appear identical in size in Earth's sky.
* **Blood Moon (Lunar Eclipse):** During totality, sunlight filters through all the sunrises and sunsets around Earth's atmospheric rim, bending red light into Earth's shadow and coloring the Moon crimson.
* **Umbra vs. Penumbra:** The dark inner shadow (Umbra) where light is completely blocked, and the lighter outer shadow (Penumbra).
