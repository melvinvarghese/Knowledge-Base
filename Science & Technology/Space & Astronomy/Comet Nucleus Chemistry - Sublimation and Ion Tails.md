# Comet Nucleus Chemistry - Sublimation and Ion Tails

## Overview
Comets are pristine, porous 'dirty snowballs' composed of water ice, frozen volatile gases, and primitive organic refractory dust preserved in the outer Solar System since the protoplanetary disk epoch.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Sublimation & Coma Formation: Approaching perihelion ($<3	ext{ AU}$), solar heating sublimates surface volatile ices directly into gas, forming an expanding diffuse spherical atmosphere (**coma**) surrounding the nucleus.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Dual Tail Mechanics: Solar radiation pressure pushes micron-sized dust grains into a curved, yellow-white **dust tail**; the solar wind ionizes gases ($CO^+, N_2^+$) and sweeps them along interplanetary magnetic field lines into a straight, glowing blue **ion tail** pointed directly away from the Sun.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
ESA Rosetta spacecraft exploration of comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko, delivery of water and prebiotic amino acids (glycine) to early Earth.

---

## Key Terms
- **Sublimation**: Direct thermodynamic phase transition of a substance from solid to gas without entering a liquid state.
- **Ion Tail**: Straight, bluish comet tail composed of ionized gases swept directly anti-solar by the solar wind.
- **Coma**: Nebulous envelope of gas and dust surrounding the nucleus of a comet.
