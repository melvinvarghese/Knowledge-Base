# Neptune - The Windy Outpost

> **The Big Idea:** The most distant major planet in our Solar System. A deep blue ice giant famous for supersonic supersonic winds howling at over 2,000 km/h and its captured moon Triton.

---

## 💨 Supersonic Storms

* **Wildest Winds:** Features the fastest wind speeds recorded in the solar system, topping 2,100 km/h.
* **Mathematical Discovery:** Unlike earlier planets, Neptune was predicted by mathematical calculations based on irregularities in Uranus's orbit before being visually spotted.
* **Triton:** A frozen moon orbiting backwards (retrograde), featuring nitrogen ice geysers; likely a captured Kuiper Belt object.
