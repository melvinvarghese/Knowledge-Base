# Space Exploration and Human Spaceflight

> **The Big Idea:** From Sputnik and Apollo 11 to the International Space Station and the Artemis lunar program—humanity's quest to leave Earth's gravity well and colonize the cosmos.

---

## 🚀 Key Milestones

* **1957:** Sputnik 1 (first artificial satellite).
* **1961:** Yuri Gagarin (first human in space).
* **1969:** Apollo 11 (Neil Armstrong & Buzz Aldrin walk on the Moon).
* **1998–Present:** International Space Station (continuous human presence in orbit).
* **Modern Era:** Reusable orbital rockets (SpaceX) and the Artemis mission returning humans to the Moon.
