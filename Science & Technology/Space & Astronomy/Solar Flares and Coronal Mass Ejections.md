> **The Big Idea:** Explosive releases of magnetic energy in the Sun's corona that eject billions of tons of magnetized plasma across space.

## 1. What It Is

Magnetic Reconnection -> High-Energy Solar Flare -> CME Plasma Cloud -> Earth Magnetosphere
