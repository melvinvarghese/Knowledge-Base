# Exoplanets - Worlds Beyond Our Solar System

> **The Big Idea:** Planets orbiting stars outside our Solar System. Over 5,500 exoplanets have been confirmed, ranging from boiling 'Hot Jupiters' to rocky worlds in the habitable Goldilocks zone.

---

## 🔭 How We Find Exoplanets

* **The Transit Method (Kepler, TESS):** Measuring the tiny dip in brightness when a planet crosses in front of its host star.
* **Radial Velocity (Wobble):** Detecting the gravitational tug of a planet causing its star to wobble, shifting starlight via the Doppler effect.
* **The Habitable Zone:** The orbital region where liquid water can pool on a rocky planet's surface.
