## 1. What It Is

- **Core Mechanism:** Discovered in 1998 through observations of Type Ia distant supernovae; accounts for roughly 68% of the total cosmic energy density.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
