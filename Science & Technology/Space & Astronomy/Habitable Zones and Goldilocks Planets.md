# Habitable Zones and Goldilocks Planets

> **The Big Idea:** The orbital region around a star where liquid water can stably exist on a rocky planet's surface.

---

## 1. What It Is
The orbital region around a star where liquid water can stably exist on a rocky planet's surface.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Depends strictly on stellar luminosity: red dwarfs have tight habitable zones, while hot F-type stars have distant ones.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Too Hot (Water Boils) | Goldilocks (Liquid Water) | Too Cold (Glaciated)
```
