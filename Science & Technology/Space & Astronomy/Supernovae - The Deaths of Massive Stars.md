# Supernovae - The Deaths of Massive Stars

> **The Big Idea:** The most cataclysmic explosions in the cosmos. In a fraction of a second, a dying massive star unleashes as much energy as our Sun will emit over its entire 10-billion-year life.

---

## 💥 Type II vs Type Ia

* **Type II (Core Collapse):** A massive star fuses elements up to iron; the core collapses under gravity in milliseconds, rebounding as a blinding blast.
* **Type Ia (White Dwarf Detonation):** A white dwarf in a binary system steals matter from a companion star until hitting the Chandrasekhar limit (1.44 solar masses), detonating like a nuclear bomb. Used as 'standard candles' to measure cosmic expansion.
