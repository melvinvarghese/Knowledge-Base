# Mars Rovers - Curiosity and Perseverance

> **The Big Idea:** Car-sized nuclear-powered robotic laboratories exploring the Martian surface, drilling rocks, analyzing soil chemistry, and searching for ancient microbial biosignatures.

---

## 🤖 Robotic Geologists
* **Curiosity (2012):** Landed in Gale Crater using a revolutionary rocket-powered sky crane; proved Mars once had freshwater lakes capable of supporting microbial life.
* **Perseverance (2021):** Exploring Jezero Crater delta, caching rock core samples for future return to Earth, and carrying the **Ingenuity** helicopter (the first powered flight on another planet).
* **Power Source:** Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs) converting plutonium radioactive decay heat into continuous electricity.
