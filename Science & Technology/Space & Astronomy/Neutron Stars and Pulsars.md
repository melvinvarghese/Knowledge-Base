# Neutron Stars and Pulsars

> **The Big Idea:** The collapsed core of an exploded massive star. So dense that protons and electrons are crushed into pure neutrons—packing the mass of two Suns into a sphere just 20 km across.

---

## ⚡ Extreme Physics

* **Staggering Density:** A single sugar-cube amount of neutron star material weighs roughly 1 billion tons on Earth.
* **Pulsars:** Rapidly spinning neutron stars with intense magnetic fields that sweep lighthouse-like beams of radiation across space hundreds of times per second.
* **Magnetars:** Neutron stars with magnetic fields a quadrillion times stronger than Earth's.
