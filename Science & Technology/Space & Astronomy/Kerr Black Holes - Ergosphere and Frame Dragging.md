# Kerr Black Holes - Ergosphere and Frame Dragging

## Overview
The Kerr Metric describes spacetime geometry surrounding an uncharged, rotating stationary black hole, introducing **frame dragging** and an exterior energy-extractable **ergosphere**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Lense-Thirring Frame Dragging: A rotating mass drags spacetime around itself; inside the stationary limit surface (ergosphere), spacetime rotates faster than light relative to distant observers, making it impossible for physical objects to remain stationary.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
The Penrose Energy Extraction Process: Particles entering the ergosphere can split into two; one fragment falls through the event horizon with negative energy, while the second escapes with more energy than the original parent particle ($E_{\text{out}} > E_{\text{in}}$).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Astrophysical power source for relativistic AGN blazar jets (Blandford-Znajek process), gravitational wave inspiral ringdown signals.

---

## Key Terms
- **Kerr Metric**: Exact vacuum solution to Einstein's field equations describing a rotating black hole.
- **Ergosphere**: Region outside a rotating black hole's event horizon where spacetime itself is dragged in rotation.
- **Penrose Process**: Theoretical mechanism extracting rotational kinetic energy from a rotating Kerr black hole.
