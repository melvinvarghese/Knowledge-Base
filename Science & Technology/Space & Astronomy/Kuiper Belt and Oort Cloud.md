# Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud

> **The Big Idea:** The icy frontiers of our Solar System. The Kuiper Belt is a donut-shaped ring beyond Neptune, while the Oort Cloud is a colossal spherical shell of comets extending a light-year into space.

---

## ❄️ The Frozen Outer Realms
* **Kuiper Belt (30–50 AU):** Home to dwarf planets Pluto, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake, plus short-period comets (like Halley's Comet).
* **The Oort Cloud (2,000–100,000 AU):** A theoretical spherical shell containing trillions of icy planetesimals, the source of long-period comets.
* **Planetesimal Fossils:** These bodies preserve the pristine, unaltered chemical composition of the solar nebula from 4.6 billion years ago.
