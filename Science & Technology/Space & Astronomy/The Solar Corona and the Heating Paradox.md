# The Solar Corona and the Heating Paradox

> **The Big Idea:** The enigma of why the Sun's outermost atmosphere reaches millions of kelvins while its visible surface is only 5,800 K.

## 1. What It Is

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Magnetic reconnection events (nanoflares) and Alfven wave turbulence are prime mechanisms pumping thermal energy upward.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Core (15M K) -> Photosphere (5,800 K) -> Corona (1,000,000+ K)
```
