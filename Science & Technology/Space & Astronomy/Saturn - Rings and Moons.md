# Saturn - Rings and Moons

> **The Big Idea:** The sixth planet from the Sun, world-famous for its majestic ring system made of billions of icy particles, and fascinating moons like Titan and Enceladus.

---

## 🪐 The Jewels of Saturn

* **The Ring System:** Stretches 282,000 km across, but averages only **10 meters thick**. The rings are 99% pure water ice fragments ranging from dust grains to boulder sizes.
* **Titan:** Saturn's largest moon; the only moon with a dense nitrogen atmosphere and liquid hydrocarbon lakes (methane and ethane).
* **Enceladus:** A small ice moon shooting geysers of liquid water from an underground ocean through south-pole 'tiger stripe' cracks.
