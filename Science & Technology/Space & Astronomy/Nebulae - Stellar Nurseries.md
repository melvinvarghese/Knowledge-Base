# Nebulae - Stellar Nurseries

> **The Big Idea:** Vast interstellar clouds of dust, hydrogen, and helium gas. Nebulae are both the maternity wards where young stars are born and the glowing remnants of dead stars.

---

## ✨ The Main Types of Nebulae

* **Emission Nebulae:** Heated by nearby newborn stars, glowing vibrant pink and red (e.g., Orion Nebula).
* **Reflection Nebulae:** Dust clouds that scatter blue starlight (e.g., around the Pleiades).
* **Dark Nebulae:** Clouds so dense they block background stars (e.g., Horsehead Nebula).
* **Planetary Nebulae:** Glowing shells of gas puffed out by dying average stars.
