# Uranus - The Tilted Ice Giant

> **The Big Idea:** An ice giant composed of water, methane, and ammonia ices. Unique for spinning completely on its side, likely caused by a colossal planetary collision in early solar system history.

---

## ❄️ The Sideways World

* **98-Degree Tilt:** Rotates almost horizontally, meaning its poles spend 42 years in direct sunlight followed by 42 years of continuous darkness.
* **Cold Atmosphere:** Features the coldest planetary atmosphere in the Solar System, plunging down to -224°C.
* **Methane Blue:** Atmospheric methane absorbs red light and reflects cyan-blue.
