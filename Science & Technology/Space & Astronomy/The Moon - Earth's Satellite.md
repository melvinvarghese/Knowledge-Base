# The Moon - Earth's Satellite

> **The Big Idea:** Earth’s only natural satellite, formed 4.5 billion years ago when a Mars-sized planet (Theia) collided with young Earth. The Moon stabilizes Earth's axial tilt and drives ocean tides.

---

## 🌕 Key Features of the Moon

* **Tidal Locking:** The Moon takes the exact same time to rotate on its axis as it does to orbit Earth (27.3 days). Because of this, the same side always faces Earth.
* **The Giant Impact Hypothesis:** Debris from the collision between proto-Earth and Theia clumped together in orbit within thousands of years.
* **Maria (Dark Seas):** Smooth, dark plains of ancient basaltic lava that flooded huge impact basins billions of years ago.
* **No Atmosphere:** With no air to trap heat or weather the surface, footprints left by Apollo astronauts will remain intact for millions of years.

## 🛠️ Practical Impact on Earth
Without the Moon, Earth’s tilt would wobble chaotically between 0° and 85°, creating catastrophic climate shifts that would make complex life nearly impossible.
