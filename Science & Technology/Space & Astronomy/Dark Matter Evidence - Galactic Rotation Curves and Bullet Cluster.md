# Dark Matter Evidence - Galactic Rotation Curves and Bullet Cluster

## Overview
Dark Matter is an invisible, non-baryonic form of matter that interacts solely via gravity (and possibly weak interactions), constituting approximately **85% of total cosmic matter**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Vera Rubin's Flat Galactic Rotation Curves: Newtonian mechanics predicts stellar orbital velocities drop as $v \propto 1/\sqrt{r}$ outside the visible galactic disk; observations revealed flat velocity profiles ($v(r) \approx \text{constant}$), proving galaxies are embedded in massive spherical dark matter halos.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
The Bullet Cluster (1E 0657-56) Smoking Gun: Collision of two galaxy clusters separated hot X-ray emitting baryonic gas (slowed by electromagnetic drag) from the total gravitational mass (mapped by weak lensing), proving dark matter is physical collisionless matter rather than modified gravity (MOND).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Direct detection experiments (XENONnT searching for WIMPs), axion search haloscopes (ADMX), large-scale cosmic structure formation simulations.

---

## Key Terms
- **Dark Matter**: Hypothetical form of non-luminous matter accounting for the majority of mass in galaxies.
- **Flat Rotation Curve**: Observation that stellar orbital speeds remain constant at large distances from galactic centers.
- **Bullet Cluster**: Colliding galaxy cluster providing direct empirical spatial separation of dark matter from baryonic gas.
