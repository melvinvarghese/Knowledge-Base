# Cosmic Rays - High-Energy Particles

> **The Big Idea:** Ultra-relativistic atomic nuclei (mostly protons) originating from outside our Solar System, accelerated across interstellar space by supernovae shockwaves and black hole accretion jets.

---

## 🌠 Subatomic Messengers
* **Atmospheric Showers:** When an ultra-high-energy cosmic ray strikes nitrogen or oxygen atoms in the upper atmosphere, it shatters into a cascade of secondary particles (muons, pions, electrons).
* **The Oh-My-God Particle (1991):** A single cosmic ray detected with 320 exa-electronvolts of kinetic energy—a subatomic particle carrying the kinetic energy of a fast-pitch baseball!
* **Astronaut Radiation:** Cosmic rays pose the greatest long-term health risk for interplanetary travel, penetrating spacecraft hulls and damaging cellular DNA.
