# The Solar System - Rocky Planets vs Gas Giants

> **The Big Idea:** Born 4.6 billion years ago from a spinning cloud of cosmic dust, our Solar System divided into two distinct neighborhoods: four small, dense **Rocky Planets** near the Sun, and four gigantic **Gas and Ice Giants** in the cold outer depths.

## ⚖️ The Two Planetary Families

### 1. The Inner Rocky Planets (Terrestrial)
* **Mercury:** Smallest planet; no atmosphere; scorching days (430°C) and freezing nights (-180°C).
* **Venus:** Earth’s "evil twin." Runaway greenhouse effect with thick carbon dioxide clouds and sulfuric acid rain; surface temperature is 465°C (hot enough to melt lead).
* **Earth:** The Goldilocks planet. Liquid water, dynamic plate tectonics, protective magnetic field, and teeming life.
* **Mars:** The Red Planet. Oxidized iron soil; home to Olympus Mons (the solar system's largest volcano, 3x taller than Mt. Everest).

### 2. The Outer Gas & Ice Giants (Jovian)
* **Jupiter:** The King of Planets. 2.5 times heavier than all other planets combined; features the Great Red Spot (a storm wider than Earth raging for 300+ years).
* **Saturn:** Famous for spectacular rings made of billions of chunks of ice and rock orbiting in perfect sheets just 10 meters thick.
* **Uranus (Ice Giant):** Tilted sideways at 98 degrees (spins on its side); atmosphere of hydrogen, helium, and blue methane ice.
* **Neptune (Ice Giant):** The windiest planet; supersonic winds howl at over 2,000 km/h; deep blue methane atmosphere.

---

## ☄️ The Debris Zones: Asteroid Belt & Kuiper Belt

* **The Asteroid Belt:** Millions of rocky leftover fragments between Mars and Jupiter that Jupiter’s immense gravity prevented from clumping into a planet.
* **The Kuiper Belt:** A vast frozen ring beyond Neptune filled with icy comets and dwarf planets like **Pluto**.
* **The Oort Cloud:** A giant spherical shell of icy comets enveloping the outer boundary of the solar system, nearly a light-year away.

---

## 🛠️ Key Takeaway

* Why are inner planets rocky and outer planets gaseous? **The Frost Line.** Near the hot young Sun, only heavy rock and iron could condense into solids. Farther out past the frost line, light volatile gases (hydrogen, helium, water, methane) could freeze into giant gas spheres.
