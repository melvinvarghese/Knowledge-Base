# Mercury - The Iron Planet

> **The Big Idea:** The smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun. Mercury is essentially a giant iron core covered in a thin, cratered rocky crust with wild temperature swings.

---

## 🪐 Extreme Conditions

* **Temperature Extremes:** Daytime temperatures reach a blistering 430°C, but without an atmosphere to retain heat, nighttime plunges to -180°C.
* **Giant Iron Core:** Mercury's iron core takes up roughly 85% of its entire planetary radius, making it extraordinarily dense.
* **Orbital Speed:** Zips around the Sun at 47 km/s, completing an entire year in just 88 Earth days.
* **Ice in Permanent Shadows:** Deep craters at Mercury's poles never see sunlight and contain frozen water ice.
