# The Oort Cloud - The Solar System's Deep Freeze Boundary

> **The Big Idea:** A colossal spherical shell of icy planetesimals surrounding our solar system out to nearly a light-year away.

---

## 1. What It Is
A colossal spherical shell of icy planetesimals surrounding our solar system out to nearly a light-year away.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** The origin source of long-period comets that take thousands to millions of years to orbit the Sun.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Sun -> Kuiper Belt (50 AU) -> Heliopause (120 AU) -> Oort Cloud (100,000 AU)
```
