# Gravitational Waves - Ripples Across Spacetime

> **The Big Idea:** Distortions in spacetime caused by cataclysmic events like colliding black holes and neutron stars, detected by LIGO and Virgo.

---

## 1. What It Is
Distortions in spacetime caused by cataclysmic events like colliding black holes and neutron stars, detected by LIGO and Virgo.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Einstein predicted them in 1916; directly observed in 2015 when two black holes merged 1.3 billion light-years away.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Binary Orbit -> Orbital Decay -> Inspiral & Merger -> Ringdown Wave
```
