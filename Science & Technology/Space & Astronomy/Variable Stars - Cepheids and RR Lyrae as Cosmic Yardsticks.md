# Variable Stars - Cepheids and RR Lyrae as Cosmic Yardsticks

> **The Big Idea:** Stars whose brightness fluctuates periodically due to internal pulsations in their opacity and temperature.

---

## 1. What It Is
Stars whose brightness fluctuates periodically due to internal pulsations in their opacity and temperature.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Henrietta Leavitt discovered the period-luminosity relation of Cepheid variables, allowing Edwin Hubble to measure distance to Andromeda.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Luminosity Pulsation Period -> Absolute Brightness Derived -> Distance Calculated
```
