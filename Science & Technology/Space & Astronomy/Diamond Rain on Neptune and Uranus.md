# Diamond Rain on Neptune and Uranus

> **The Big Idea:** The deep hydrocarbon-rich atmospheres of ice giant planets reach temperatures and pressures so extreme that methane splits, crystallizing carbon into diamond hailstones.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The deep hydrocarbon-rich atmospheres of ice giant planets reach temperatures and pressures so extreme that methane splits, crystallizing carbon into diamond hailstones.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Thousands of kilometers beneath the cloud tops of Neptune and Uranus, temperatures reach $4,000\text{ K}$ and pressures exceed $100\text{ GPa}$. In these conditions, pyrolysis breaks $CH_4$ molecules; carbon atoms bond into dense $sp^3$ tetrahedral diamond lattices that precipitate downward through a super-hot liquid mantle sea.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Space & Astronomy**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Atmospheric Methane (CH4) -> High Pressure Pyrolysis -> Carbon sp3 Bonding -> Diamond Hail Sinks to Core
```
