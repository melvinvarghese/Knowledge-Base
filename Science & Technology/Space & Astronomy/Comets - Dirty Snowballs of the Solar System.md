# Comets - Dirty Snowballs of the Solar System

> **The Big Idea:** Cosmic snowballs of frozen gases, rock, and dust orbiting the Sun in elongated ellipses. When nearing the Sun, solar radiation vaporizes ice into luminous glowing tails.

---

## ❄️ Anatomy of a Comet

* **Nucleus:** The solid frozen core of ice, rock, and organic carbon compounds.
* **Coma:** The glowing halo of evaporated gas and dust surrounding the nucleus.
* **The Two Tails:**
  * **Dust Tail:** White/yellow tail curved by solar radiation pressure.
  * **Ion (Gas) Tail:** Glowing blue tail pushed directly away from the Sun by solar wind.
