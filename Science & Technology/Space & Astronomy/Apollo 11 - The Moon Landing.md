# Apollo 11 - The Moon Landing

> **The Big Idea:** On July 20, 1969, American astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first humans to land on the Moon, fulfilling John F. Kennedy's challenge and winning the Space Race.

---

## 🌕 One Giant Leap
* **Saturn V Rocket:** The most powerful rocket successfully flown, standing 111 meters tall and generating 34.5 million newtons of thrust.
* **The Eagle Lander:** Touched down in the Sea of Tranquility with only 25 seconds of fuel remaining.
* **Scientific Yield:** Returned 21.5 kg of lunar soil and rock samples that proved the Moon is dry, lifeless, and formed from ancient impact debris.
