# The Sun's Solar Wind and Geomagnetic Storms

> **The Big Idea:** A continuous stream of charged particles (plasma) blowing outward from the Sun at 400 km/s. When Coronal Mass Ejections strike Earth's magnetosphere, they cause geomagnetic storms and auroras.

---

## ⚡ Space Weather
* **The Carrington Event (1859):** The most intense geomagnetic storm on record; caused telegraph wires to spark, shocking operators, and created auroras so bright people read newspapers at midnight.
* **Modern Vulnerability:** A storm of Carrington's magnitude today could fry high-voltage transformers, knock out satellite GPS, and cause continent-wide electrical grid blackouts for months.
* **The Bow Shock:** Earth's magnetic field deflects the solar wind around the planet like water parting around the bow of a ship.
