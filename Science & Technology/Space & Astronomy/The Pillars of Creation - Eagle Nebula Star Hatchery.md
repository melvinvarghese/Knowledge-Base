# The Pillars of Creation - Eagle Nebula Star Hatchery

> **The Big Idea:** Vast spires of cold molecular hydrogen gas and interstellar dust in the Eagle Nebula (M16) sculpting infant stellar systems.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
Vast spires of cold molecular hydrogen gas and interstellar dust in the Eagle Nebula (M16) sculpting infant stellar systems.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Stretching roughly 4 to 5 light-years in height, these cosmic pillars are eroded by the intense ionizing ultraviolet radiation and stellar winds emitted by nearby massive newborn stars (photoevaporation), leaving dense evaporating gaseous globules (EGGs) that collapse to form stars.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Space & Astronomy**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Cold Molecular Gas Spire -> Ultraviolet Photoevaporation -> Dense EGGs Emerge -> Protostar Ignition
```
