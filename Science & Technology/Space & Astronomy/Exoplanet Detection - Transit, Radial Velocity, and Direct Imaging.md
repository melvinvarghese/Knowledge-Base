## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Kepler and TESS primarily use transit photometry (measuring minute dips in star brightness as planets cross in front).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Star Light Emission -> Planet Crosses Star -> Dip in Light Curve Recorded
```
