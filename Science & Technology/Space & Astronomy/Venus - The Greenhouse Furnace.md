# Venus - The Greenhouse Furnace

> **The Big Idea:** Earth's nearest neighbor and 'evil twin.' Venus suffered runaway greenhouse heating, creating a hellish surface hot enough to melt lead under crushing atmospheric pressure.

---

## 🌋 A Runaway Greenhouse Planet

* **Crushing Atmosphere:** 96% Carbon Dioxide with sulfuric acid clouds. Surface atmospheric pressure is 92 times greater than Earth's (equivalent to being 1 km deep underwater).
* **Surface Heat:** A uniform 465°C across the entire planet, day and night—hotter than Mercury, despite being twice as far from the Sun.
* **Retrograde Rotation:** Spins backwards compared to most planets; the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east. One day on Venus (243 Earth days) is longer than its year (225 Earth days)!
