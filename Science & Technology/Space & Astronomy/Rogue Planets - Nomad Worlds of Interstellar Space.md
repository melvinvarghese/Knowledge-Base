## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
Colossal planetary-mass bodies that wander through the dark expanse of interstellar space unbound to any host star.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Ejected from nascent planetary systems by chaotic gravitational slingshot interactions during solar system formation, these nomad worlds have no sunrise. Many retain thick insulating atmospheres and geothermal heating from internal radioactive decay, allowing liquid subsurface oceans beneath ice shells.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Space & Astronomy**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Planetesimal Gravitational Scattering -> Ejection Velocity Exceeded -> Interstellar Nomad World
```
