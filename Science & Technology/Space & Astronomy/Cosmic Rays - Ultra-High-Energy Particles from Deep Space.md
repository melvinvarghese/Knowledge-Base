# Cosmic Rays - Ultra-High-Energy Particles from Deep Space

> **The Big Idea:** Atomic nuclei and protons traveling near the speed of light that bombard Earth's upper atmosphere continuously.

---

## 1. What It Is
Atomic nuclei and protons traveling near the speed of light that bombard Earth's upper atmosphere continuously.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** They produce secondary particle cascades (muons, pions, electrons) known as air showers upon collision with atmospheric molecules.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of space & astronomy, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Primary Cosmic Ray (Proton) -> Atmospheric Nucleus Collision -> Particle Shower Cascade
```
