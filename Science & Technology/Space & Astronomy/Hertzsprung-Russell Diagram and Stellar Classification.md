# Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram and Stellar Classification

## Overview
The Hertzsprung-Russell (H-R) Diagram plots stellar absolute luminosity against effective surface temperature (or spectral type), revealing the fundamental evolutionary tracks of stars across the cosmos.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Stefan-Boltzmann Luminosity Relation: Stellar luminosity scales with surface area and temperature: $L = 4\pi R^2 \sigma T_{\text{eff}}^4$. Diagonal lines across the H-R diagram map lines of constant stellar radius $R$.

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Main Sequence to Giant Branches: 90% of stars lie along the Main Sequence burning core hydrogen via the $p-p$ chain or CNO cycle; hydrogen core exhaustion initiates core contraction, hydrogen shell burning, and expansion into Red Giants and Supergiants.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Stellar cluster age determination (Main Sequence turnoff point), distance modulus calibration, stellar population modeling.

---

## Key Terms
- **H-R Diagram**: Scatter plot of stars showing the relationship between absolute magnitudes and spectral classifications.
- **Main Sequence**: Continuous band of stable stars fusing hydrogen into helium in their cores.
- **Spectral Classification**: System categorizing stars based on spectral absorption lines (O, B, A, F, G, K, M).
