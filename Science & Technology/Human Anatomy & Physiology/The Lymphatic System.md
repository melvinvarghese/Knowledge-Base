# The Lymphatic System

> **The Big Idea:** The drainage and filtration network of the body. Collects excess fluid leaking from tissues, filters it through lymph nodes for bacteria, and returns it to blood.

---

## 💧 Lymph Mechanics

* **Lymph Fluid:** Clear watery fluid containing white blood cells and leaked plasma proteins.
* **Lymph Nodes:** Tiny bean-shaped filter stations (neck, armpits, groin) packed with lymphocytes that swell when fighting off an infection.
* **The Spleen:** The largest lymphatic organ, filtering out old, damaged red blood cells.
