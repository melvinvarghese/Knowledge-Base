# Thermoregulation - Sweating, Shivering, and Vasodilation

> **The Big Idea:** The homeostatic physiological mechanisms coordinated by the hypothalamus to maintain core body temperature near $37^\circ\text{C}$.

---

## 1. What It Is
The homeostatic physiological mechanisms coordinated by the hypothalamus to maintain core body temperature near $37^\circ\text{C}$.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** When overheated, cutaneous blood vessels dilate and eccrine sweat glands evaporate heat; when cold, skeletal muscles shiver and vessels constrict.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Hypothalamus Sensor -> Hot: Vasodilation & Evaporative Sweat | Cold: Vasoconstriction & Shivering
```
