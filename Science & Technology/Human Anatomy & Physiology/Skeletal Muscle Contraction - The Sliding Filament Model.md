# Skeletal Muscle Contraction - The Sliding Filament Model

> **The Big Idea:** How electrical nerve impulses trigger actin and myosin protein filaments to slide past each other to generate contractile pulling force.

## 1. What It Is
How electrical nerve impulses trigger actin and myosin protein filaments to slide past each other to generate contractile pulling force.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Calcium release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum exposes actin binding sites, allowing myosin heads to bind, power-stroke, and consume ATP.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Action Potential -> Sarcoplasmic Calcium Release -> Myosin Power Stroke -> Sarcomere Shortens
```
