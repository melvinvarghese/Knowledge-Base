# Myelin Sheaths and Saltatory Conduction

> **The Big Idea:** The fatty, multi-layered insulating membranes wrapped around neuronal axons that increase nerve impulse velocity up to a hundredfold.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The fatty, multi-layered insulating membranes wrapped around neuronal axons that increase nerve impulse velocity up to a hundredfold.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Formed by Schwann cells in the PNS and oligodendrocytes in the CNS, myelin acts as an electrical insulator. Action potentials cannot pass through the sheath and instead jump between uninsulated gaps called Nodes of Ranvier, accelerating conduction speeds up to $120\text{ m/s}$.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Human Anatomy & Physiology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Axon Membrane -> Insulating Myelin Wraps -> Node of Ranvier Ion Jump -> 120 m/s Transmission
```
