# The Spinal Cord and Reflex Arcs

> **The Big Idea:** The high-speed neural data highway connecting brain to body. Reflex arcs bypass the brain entirely, allowing spinal nerves to yank your hand off a hot stove before you even feel pain.

---

## ⚡ The Reflex Arc
* **The Emergency Circuit:** Sensory receptor in finger $\rightarrow$ Sensory neuron $\rightarrow$ Interneuron in Spinal Cord $\rightarrow$ Motor neuron $\rightarrow$ Bicep contracts.
* **Time Saved:** Takes less than 20 milliseconds, saving skin from deep third-degree burns before the sensory signal has even finished traveling up the spinal cord to the brain's conscious pain cortex.
