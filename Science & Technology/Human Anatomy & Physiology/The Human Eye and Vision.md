# The Human Eye and Vision

> **The Big Idea:** The eye is a living biological camera that focuses light onto a light-sensitive screen (the retina), translating photons into electrical nerve impulses for the brain.

---

## 👁️ Optical Anatomy

* **Cornea & Lens:** Transparent curved windows that refract light onto the retina.
* **Retina:** The sensor screen packed with 120 million **Rods** (night vision, black & white) and 6 million **Cones** (daytime sharp color vision: Red, Green, Blue).
* **The Blind Spot:** Where the optic nerve exits the back of the eye, leaving a spot with zero photoreceptors (your brain automatically hallucinates and fills in the gap!).
