# The Human Microbiome

> **The Big Idea:** You are half-microbe. Your body hosts 38 trillion bacterial cells—primarily in the gut—that synthesize vitamins, train your immune system, and influence mental mood via the gut-brain axis.

---

## 🦠 The Second Brain

* **Cell Ratio:** Roughly 1:1 ratio of microbial cells to human cells.
* **Gut-Brain Axis:** The Vagus nerve connects your gut directly to the brain; gut microbes manufacture over 90% of your body's Serotonin!
* **Health Impact:** An impoverished microbiome (from poor diet or overused antibiotics) is linked to obesity, depression, autoimmune disorders, and allergies.
