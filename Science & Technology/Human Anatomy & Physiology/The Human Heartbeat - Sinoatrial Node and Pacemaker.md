# The Human Heartbeat - Sinoatrial Node and Pacemaker

> **The Big Idea:** How the heart beats without brain intervention: specialized self-exciting electrical tissue in the right atrium (**The SA Node**) fires rhythmic electrical waves through cardiac muscle.

---

## ⚡ Electrical Conduction
* **Sinoatrial (SA) Node:** The natural pacemaker, setting the baseline resting heart rate at 60–100 beats per minute.
* **Atrioventricular (AV) Node:** Delays the electrical pulse for a split second, allowing atria to fully empty blood into ventricles before ventricles contract with immense pressure.
* **ECG (Electrocardiogram):** P-wave (atrial contraction), QRS-complex (ventricular contraction), and T-wave (ventricular electrical reset).
