# Human Reproduction and Development

> **The Big Idea:** From the fusion of sperm and egg into a single zygote to a fully formed infant in 40 weeks—the biological stages of human embryonic development.

---

## 👶 The 3 Trimesters

* **First Trimester (Weeks 1-12):** Major organs, heart, limbs, and brain take shape; embryo becomes a fetus.
* **Second Trimester (Weeks 13-26):** Rapid growth, fetal movement felt, fingernails and eyelashes form.
* **Third Trimester (Weeks 27-40):** Lung maturation, massive brain development, and fat deposits prepare the baby for birth.
