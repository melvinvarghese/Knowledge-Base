# The Muscular System - Contraction and Biomechanics

> **The Big Idea:** The body contains over 600 muscles making up 40% of body weight. Muscles only pull; they never push—operating in antagonistic pairs to produce movement.

---

## 💪 The 3 Muscle Types

* **Skeletal Muscle:** Voluntary, striated muscles anchored to bones by tendons (Biceps, Quads).
* **Smooth Muscle:** Involuntary muscles lining internal organs (stomach churns, blood vessel walls).
* **Cardiac Muscle:** Specialized striated heart muscle that contracts automatically without fatigue.
* **Antagonistic Pairs:** To bend your arm, your Bicep contracts while your Tricep relaxes; to straighten, your Tricep contracts while your Bicep relaxes.
