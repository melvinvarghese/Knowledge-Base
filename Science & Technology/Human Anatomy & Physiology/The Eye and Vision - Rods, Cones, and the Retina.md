# The Eye and Vision - Rods, Cones, and the Retina

> **The Big Idea:** How light rays pass through the cornea and adjustable lens to project an inverted image onto the light-sensitive neural retina.

## 1. What It Is
How light rays pass through the cornea and adjustable lens to project an inverted image onto the light-sensitive neural retina.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Rods provide sensitive monochromatic night vision; cones concentrated in the fovea deliver high-acuity trichromatic color vision.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Cornea -> Pupil / Iris Aperture -> Crystalline Lens -> Retina Rods & Cones -> Optic Nerve
```
