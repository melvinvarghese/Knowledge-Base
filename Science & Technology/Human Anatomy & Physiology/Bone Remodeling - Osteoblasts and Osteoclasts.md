# Bone Remodeling - Osteoblasts and Osteoclasts

> **The Big Idea:** The continuous lifelong process where mature bone tissue is resorbed and new bone tissue is synthesized to repair microfractures.

---

## 1. What It Is
The continuous lifelong process where mature bone tissue is resorbed and new bone tissue is synthesized to repair microfractures.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Osteoclasts secrete acid and enzymes to dissolve old bone matrix; osteoblasts lay down new collagen and calcium hydroxyapatite crystals.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Microfracture Detected -> Osteoclast Acid Resorption -> Osteoblast Hydroxyapatite Mineralization
```
