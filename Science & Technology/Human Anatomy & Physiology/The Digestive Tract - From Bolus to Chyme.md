# The Digestive Tract - From Bolus to Chyme

> **The Big Idea:** The continuous 9-meter muscular gastrointestinal tube running from the mouth through the esophagus, stomach, intestines, and colon.

## 1. What It Is
The continuous 9-meter muscular gastrointestinal tube running from the mouth through the esophagus, stomach, intestines, and colon.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Uses mechanical chewing and rhythmic peristaltic contractions combined with hydrochloric acid and digestive enzymes to break down food.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Mouth (Chewing & Amylase) -> Stomach (Acid & Pepsin) -> Small Intestine (Enzymatic Breakdown)
```
