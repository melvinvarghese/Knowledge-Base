# The Respiratory Membrane and Alveolar Gas Exchange

> **The Big Idea:** The microscopic interface inside the lungs where oxygen enters the bloodstream and carbon dioxide is expelled into exhaled air.

---

## 1. What It Is
The microscopic interface inside the lungs where oxygen enters the bloodstream and carbon dioxide is expelled into exhaled air.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Roughly 300 million grape-like alveoli provide 70 square meters of surface area separated from capillary blood by an ultrathin membrane.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Inhaled Alveolar Oxygen -> Diffuses Across 0.5-Micron Membrane -> Binds Capillary Hemoglobin
```
