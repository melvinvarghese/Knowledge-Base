# The Musculoskeletal System - Bones, Tendons, and Levers

> **The Big Idea:** The framework of 206 bones, cartilages, ligaments, and skeletal muscles that provides structural support, organ protection, and movement.

---

## 1. What It Is
The framework of 206 bones, cartilages, ligaments, and skeletal muscles that provides structural support, organ protection, and movement.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Bones store 99% of body calcium, house blood-producing marrow, and operate as mechanical levers powered by contracting muscle sarcomeres.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Bone Frame (Support) + Tendon Cable + Skeletal Muscle Sarcomere Contraction = Motion
```
