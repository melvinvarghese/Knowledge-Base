# The Spleen - Blood Filter and Red Pulp

> **The Big Idea:** A fist-sized purple organ in the upper left abdomen that acts as the blood's quality-control filter, recycling worn-out red blood cells and staging immune lymphocyte defenses.

---

## 🩸 Red Pulp vs. White Pulp
* **Red Pulp:** Old, rigid red blood cells (120 days old) squeeze through narrow endothelial slits; damaged cells that burst are devoured by resident macrophages, and their iron is salvaged for new blood.
* **White Pulp:** Lymphoid tissue monitoring blood for traveling pathogens, activating antibody production during bloodstream infections.
