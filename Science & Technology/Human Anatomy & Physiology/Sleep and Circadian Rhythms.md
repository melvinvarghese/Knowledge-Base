# Sleep and Circadian Rhythms

> **The Big Idea:** Your body is governed by an internal 24-hour biological clock (**Circadian Rhythm**). Sleep is not inactive downtime; it is an active period of brain cleaning and memory consolidation.

---

## 🌙 The Sleep Architecture

* **The SCN (Suprachiasmatic Nucleus):** The master clock in the brain that uses blue morning sunlight to suppress melatonin and nighttime darkness to trigger it.
* **Deep Sleep (NREM):** Physical tissue repair, growth hormone release, and the **Glymphatic System** flushing toxic metabolic waste from the brain.
* **REM Sleep (Dreaming):** Emotional processing, creative problem solving, and long-term memory synthesis.
