# The Kidneys and Nephrons - Blood Filtration and Homeostasis

> **The Big Idea:** The paired bean-shaped organs that filter roughly 180 liters of blood plasma daily to excrete metabolic wastes as concentrated urine.

---

## 1. What It Is
The paired bean-shaped organs that filter roughly 180 liters of blood plasma daily to excrete metabolic wastes as concentrated urine.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Over a million microscopic nephrons use glomerular pressure filtration followed by selective tubular reabsorption of water, glucose, and electrolytes.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Glomerulus (High-Pressure Filtration) -> Proximal Tubule -> Loop of Henle -> Collecting Duct
```
