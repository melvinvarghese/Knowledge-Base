# The Cranial Nerves - The 12 Pathways

> **The Big Idea:** 12 paired nerves emerging directly from the brainstem and brain rather than through the spinal cord, controlling vision, smell, facial expressions, taste, and heart rate.

---

## 🧠 The Famous Twelve
* **CN I (Olfactory):** Smell.
* **CN II (Optic):** Vision.
* **CN V (Trigeminal):** Facial sensation and chewing jaw muscles.
* **CN VII (Facial):** Facial expressions and taste on the front of the tongue (affected in Bell's Palsy).
* **CN X (The Vagus Nerve):** The wandering wanderer; carries parasympathetic signals to the heart, lungs, and digestive tract.
