# The Liver - The Body's Chemical Processing Plant

> **The Big Idea:** The body’s largest internal organ, performing over 500 vital functions: filtering toxins, manufacturing bile, regulating blood sugar, and producing clotting factors.

---

## 🧪 A Master Biochemist

* **Detoxification:** Breaks down alcohol, medications, and metabolic poisons.
* **Glucose Regulator:** Stores excess sugar as **Glycogen** and releases it when blood glucose drops.
* **Bile Production:** Produces greenish bile to emulsify and break down fats in digestion.
* **Regeneration:** The only internal organ capable of fully regenerating itself from as little as 25% remaining tissue!
