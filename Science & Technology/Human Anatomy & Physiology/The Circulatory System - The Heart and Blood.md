# The Circulatory System - The Heart and Blood

> **The Big Idea:** Your cardiovascular network is an unbroken 100,000-kilometer biological highway. A muscular four-chambered pump (the heart) beats 100,000 times a day to deliver oxygen, nutrients, and immune defenses to every single cell in your body.

---

## ❤️ The Anatomy of the 4-Chambered Heart

```
                             SUPERIOR VENA CAVA
                                     │
           [ RIGHT ATRIUM ] ◄────────┘ (Oxygen-poor blood from body)
                  │
                  ▼ (Tricuspid Valve)
           [ RIGHT VENTRICLE ] ──► [ PULMONARY ARTERY TO LUNGS ] (Picks up oxygen)
                                                │
                                                ▼
           [ LEFT VENTRICLE ]  ◄── [ LEFT ATRIUM ] (Oxygen-rich blood from lungs)
                  │
                  ▼ (Aortic Valve)
              [ AORTA ] ───────► (Oxygen-rich blood pumped to ENTIRE BODY)
```

1. **Right Side (The Pulmonary Circuit):** Collects blue, deoxygenated blood from the body and pumps it to the lungs to drop off $CO_2$ and pick up fresh $O_2$.
2. **Left Side (The Systemic Circuit):** Receives bright red oxygenated blood from the lungs and pumps it through the massive **Aorta** under high pressure to your brain, muscles, and organs.

## 🩸 The 4 Components of Blood

If you spin a tube of blood in a centrifuge, it separates into four layers:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. PLASMA (55%): Yellow liquid; 92% water; carries     │
│    proteins, hormones, glucose, and dissolved salts.   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. RED BLOOD CELLS (44%): Packed with Hemoglobin;      │
│    iron-based disc that binds and carries oxygen.      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. WHITE BLOOD CELLS (<1%): Immune defense security    │
│    patrol that hunts and destroys pathogens.           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. PLATELETS (<1%): Cell fragments that create clots   │
│    and scabs to stop you from bleeding out when cut.   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🛣️ Arteries vs. Veins vs. Capillaries

* **Arteries (Away):** Thick, muscular, high-pressure vessels carrying oxygen-rich blood *away* from the heart (carotid, femoral, aorta).
* **Veins (Back):** Low-pressure vessels with one-way valves preventing backflow, returning blood *to* the heart.
* **Capillaries (Exchange):** Microscopic vessels just **one red blood cell wide**, where oxygen and nutrients diffuse into surrounding tissues.
