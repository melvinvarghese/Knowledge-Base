# Hemoglobin and Oxygen Transport

> **The Big Idea:** The iron-containing tetrameric protein packed inside red blood cells responsible for carrying oxygen from the lungs to peripheral tissues.

---

## 1. What It Is
The iron-containing tetrameric protein packed inside red blood cells responsible for carrying oxygen from the lungs to peripheral tissues.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Exhibits cooperative binding: when one oxygen molecule binds an iron heme group, structural changes make the remaining three sites bind oxygen faster.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Deoxyhemoglobin (Tense State) + O2 -> Conformational Shift -> Oxyhemoglobin (Relaxed State)
```
