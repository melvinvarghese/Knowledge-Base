# Cerebrospinal Fluid Dynamics - Choroid Plexus and Arachnoid Villi

## Overview
Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) is a clear, acellular fluid bathing the brain and spinal cord, providing physical mechanical buoyancy, spatial shock protection, and chemical metabolic waste clearance.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
Choroid Plexus Secretion: Secreted at ~500 mL/day by modified ependymal epithelial cells of the choroid plexuses in brain ventricles via active $Na^+/K^+$ ATPases and aquaporin-1 water channels.

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Circulation & Glymphatic Drainage: Flows from lateral ventricles through the foramen of Monro $\rightarrow$ third ventricle $\rightarrow$ cerebral aqueduct $\rightarrow$ fourth ventricle $\rightarrow$ subarachnoid space, reabsorbed into dural venous sinuses via arachnoid granulations and the nocturnal **glymphatic system**.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
Hydrocephalus diagnostics and VP shunt surgical placement, lumbar puncture diagnostic biomarker sampling, Alzheimer's amyloid clearance.

---

## Key Terms
- **Choroid Plexus**: Vascular network of specialized ependymal cells in ventricles secreting cerebrospinal fluid.
- **Arachnoid Granulations**: One-way micro-valve protrusions of arachnoid membrane draining CSF into venous sinuses.
- **Glymphatic System**: Glia-mediated convective perivascular waste clearance pathway active during slow-wave sleep.
