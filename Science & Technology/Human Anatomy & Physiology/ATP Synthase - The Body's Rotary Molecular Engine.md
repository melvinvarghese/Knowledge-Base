## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Driven by a proton gradient created by the electron transport chain, protons flow through the $F_0$ membrane rotor, causing the central stalk to rotate at over 9,000 RPM. This rotation mechanically deforms the catalytic $F_1$ headpiece, smashing ADP and inorganic phosphate together into ATP.
