# The Cardiac Cycle - Systole, Diastole, and Heart Valves

> **The Big Idea:** The rhythmic sequence of muscle contraction (systole) and relaxation (diastole) that pumps roughly 5 liters of blood per minute.

---

## 1. What It Is
The rhythmic sequence of muscle contraction (systole) and relaxation (diastole) that pumps roughly 5 liters of blood per minute.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** The sinoatrial node sets the pace, coordinating atria filling followed by ventricular contraction governed by the AV, aortic, and pulmonary valves.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
SA Node Impulse -> Atrial Systole -> AV Node Delay -> Ventricular Systole (Pumping Wave)
```
