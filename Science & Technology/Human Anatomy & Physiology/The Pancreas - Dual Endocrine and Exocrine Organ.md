# The Pancreas - Dual Endocrine and Exocrine Organ

> **The Big Idea:** A dual-function organ tucked behind the stomach: its exocrine cells secrete powerful digestive enzymes into the intestine, while its endocrine Islets release insulin and glucagon.

---

## ⚖️ Dual Architecture
* **Exocrine Function (98%):** Secretes 1.5 liters of digestive juice daily containing enzymes (Amylase for carbs, Lipase for fats, Trypsin for proteins) and alkaline bicarbonate to neutralize stomach acid.
* **Endocrine Function (2%):** Islets of Langerhans:
  * **Beta Cells:** Produce **Insulin** (instructs muscle and liver cells to absorb glucose from blood).
  * **Alpha Cells:** Produce **Glucagon** (commands liver to break down glycogen and release sugar into blood).
