# The Respiratory System - Lungs and Gas Exchange

> **The Big Idea:** Your respiratory system exchanges vital gases across an astonishing surface area the size of a tennis court, bringing in oxygen and exhaling carbon dioxide waste.

---

## 🫁 The Branching Airway

```
[ TRACHEA (Windpipe) ] ──► [ BRONCHI ] ──► [ BRONCHIOLES ] ──► [ ALVEOLI (Air Sacs) ]
```

* **Alveoli:** 600 million microscopic air bubbles wrapped in capillaries. Oxygen diffuses across their ultra-thin membranes into blood while $CO_2$ diffuses out.
* **The Diaphragm:** A dome-shaped muscle beneath the lungs. When it flattens downward, it creates negative vacuum pressure, pulling air in.
