## 1. What It Is

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Elastic arteries buffer high cardiac pressure; microscopic single-cell capillaries exchange gases; veins use one-way valves to prevent backflow.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
High-Pressure Arteries -> Resistive Arterioles -> Capillary Exchange Bed -> One-Way Valved Veins
```
