# The Human Brain and Nervous System

> **The Big Idea:** The human brain is the most complex structure in the known universe. Weighing just 1.4 kg (3 pounds), its **86 billion neurons** communicate through trillions of synaptic electrical connections, consuming 20% of the body's total energy.

---

## 🧠 The 4 Lobes of the Cerebral Cortex

```
                     TOP-DOWN VIEW OF THE BRAIN
                                 ▲ FRONT
                                 │
                 ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                 │        FRONTAL LOBE           │
                 │ Executive planning, decisions,│
                 │ personality, voluntary motor. │
                 ├───────────────┬───────────────┤
                 │ PARIETAL LOBE │ TEMPORAL LOBE │
                 │ Touch, space, │ Hearing,      │
                 │ body position.│ memory, speech│
                 ├───────────────┴───────────────┤
                 │       OCCIPITAL LOBE          │
                 │ Vision & visual processing.   │
                 └───────────────────────────────┘
                                 ▼ BACK
```

1. **Frontal Lobe:** The CEO. Impulse control, logical reasoning, abstract planning, and motor output.
2. **Parietal Lobe:** The Sensory Integrator. Processes physical touch, pain, temperature, and spatial navigation.
3. **Temporal Lobe:** Audio & Memory. Interprets language (Wernicke's Area) and stores long-term memories via the **Hippocampus**.
4. **Occipital Lobe:** The Visual Monitor. Decodes raw optical signals from your eyes into recognizable shapes and colors.

## ⚡ How Neurons Communicate: The Synapse

Information travels across your nervous system through specialized cells called **Neurons**:

```
[ DENDRITES ] ──► [ CELL BODY ] ──► [ AXON (Myelin sheath) ] ──► [ SYNAPTIC TERMINAL ]
(Receives signal) (Generates impulse) (Electrical highway)      (Releases Neurotransmitters)
```

* **Action Potential:** An electrical pulse travels down the axon at speeds up to **400 km/h (250 mph)**.
* **The Synapse:** Neurons don't physically touch. When the electrical signal hits the end, it releases chemical messengers (**Neurotransmitters**) like Dopamine, Serotonin, and Adrenaline across the gap to activate the next neuron.

---

## 🛡️ Central vs. Peripheral Nervous System

* **Central Nervous System (CNS):** The Brain and Spinal Cord (the command center).
* **Peripheral Nervous System (PNS):** Nerve cables branching into every limb and organ:
  * **Sympathetic:** "Fight or Flight" (dilates pupils, accelerates heart rate).
  * **Parasympathetic:** "Rest and Digest" (slows heart rate, restores energy).
