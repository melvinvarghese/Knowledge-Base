# The Glymphatic System - The Brain's Nighttime Wash Cycle

> **The Big Idea:** A specialized waste clearance network in the central nervous system that flushes neurotoxic waste products during slow-wave sleep.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
A specialized waste clearance network in the central nervous system that flushes neurotoxic waste products during slow-wave sleep.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Because the brain lacks conventional lymphatic vessels, glial cells (astrocytes) express aquaporin-4 water channels along their endfeet. During deep sleep, interstitial space expands by $60\%$, allowing pressurized pulses of cerebrospinal fluid to wash across brain tissue, clearing beta-amyloid and tau proteins.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Human Anatomy & Physiology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Deep N3 Sleep -> Astrocytes Shrink Interstitial Space -> Cerebrospinal Fluid Wave -> Toxic Protein Flush
```
