# Sympathetic vs Parasympathetic - Fight or Flight

> **The Big Idea:** The two opposing divisions of the autonomic nervous system that balance energetic expenditure and restorative recuperation.

---

## 1. What It Is
The two opposing divisions of the autonomic nervous system that balance energetic expenditure and restorative recuperation.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Sympathetic activation releases adrenaline, dilates pupils, and speeds heart rate; parasympathetic (via vagus nerve) slows heart and promotes digestion.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Fight-or-Flight (Adrenaline, High Heart Rate) <--> Rest-and-Digest (Vagus Nerve, Digestion)
```
