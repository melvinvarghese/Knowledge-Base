# The Small Intestine - Villi and Nutrient Absorption

> **The Big Idea:** The primary site of chemical nutrient digestion and absorption, measuring roughly 6 meters long in adult humans.

## 1. What It Is
The primary site of chemical nutrient digestion and absorption, measuring roughly 6 meters long in adult humans.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Its surface area is magnified 600-fold to roughly the size of a tennis court by circular folds, finger-like villi, and microscopic microvilli.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Duodenum (Pancreatic Enzyme Mixing) -> Jejunum (Villi Absorption) -> Ileum (B12 & Bile Salts)
```
