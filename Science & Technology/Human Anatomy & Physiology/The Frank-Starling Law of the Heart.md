# The Frank-Starling Law of the Heart

## Overview
The Frank-Starling Law of the Heart states that the stroke volume of the left ventricle increases in direct proportion to the volume of blood filling the heart (end-diastolic volume) prior to contraction.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
Sarcomere Length-Tension Mechanics: Increasing venous return stretches myocardial sarcomeres toward their optimal functional length (~2.2 µm), increasing troponin C calcium sensitivity and optimizing actin-myosin cross-bridge overlap.

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Intrinsically Balanced Output: Ensures that left ventricular stroke volume continuously and precisely balances right ventricular cardiac output, preventing pulmonary congestion without requiring immediate neural input.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
Congestive heart failure management, intraoperative fluid resuscitation titration, hemodynamic shock monitoring.

---

## Key Terms
- **Stroke Volume (SV)**: Volume of blood ejected by the left ventricle into the aorta in one contraction.
- **End-Diastolic Volume (EDV)**: Total volume of blood remaining in the ventricle at the conclusion of filling (diastole).
- **Preload**: Initial stretching of the cardiac myocytes prior to contraction, approximated by EDV.
