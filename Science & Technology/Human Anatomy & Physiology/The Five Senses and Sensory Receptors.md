# The Five Senses and Sensory Receptors

> **The Big Idea:** How the human brain samples external reality: specialized sensory receptors translate light, vibration, chemical shapes, and temperature into neural code.

---

## 👃 Receptor Specialization

* **Photoreceptors:** Rods and cones in the eyes detecting light waves.
* **Mechanoreceptors:** Skin receptors detecting pressure, vibration, and stretch.
* **Chemoreceptors:** Taste buds on the tongue (Sweet, Salty, Sour, Bitter, Umami) and olfactory receptors in the nose detecting airborne molecules.
* **Thermoreceptors & Nociceptors:** Sensors detecting hot/cold and physical tissue damage (pain).
