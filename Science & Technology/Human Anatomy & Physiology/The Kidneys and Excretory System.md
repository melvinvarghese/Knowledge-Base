# The Kidneys and Excretory System

> **The Big Idea:** Two bean-shaped filtration marvels that process 200 liters of blood every day, balancing fluids, blood pressure, and filtering urea waste into urine.

---

## 🌊 The Nephron Filters

* **Nephrons:** Each kidney contains 1 million microscopic filtering units called Nephrons.
* **Glomerulus:** High-pressure sieve that strips water, salts, and urea out of blood.
* **Reabsorption:** The kidney selectively reclaims 99% of filtered water and vital minerals, passing just 1 to 2 liters of waste out as urine.
