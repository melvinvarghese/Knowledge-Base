# Bone Remodeling - Osteoblasts vs Osteoclasts

> **The Big Idea:** Your skeleton is completely replaced every 10 years through a dynamic tug-of-war between bone-destroying cells (**Osteoclasts**) and bone-building cells (**Osteoblasts**).

---

## 🦴 Constant Renewal
* **Osteoclasts (Demolition Crew):** Secrete acid and enzymes to dissolve old, micro-cracked bone matrix and release calcium into blood.
* **Osteoblasts (Construction Crew):** Lay down fresh collagen scaffolds and coat them in calcium phosphate minerals (**Hydroxyapatite**).
* **Wolff's Law:** Bone strengthens in response to mechanical stress. Heavy lifting and running signal osteoblasts to thicken bone density.
