# The Stomach - Acid Chemistry and Pepsin

> **The Big Idea:** A muscular J-shaped acid churning chamber that dissolves food into chyme using concentrated Hydrochloric Acid ($pH \approx 1.5$) and the protein-digesting enzyme **Pepsin**.

---

## 🧪 Why Doesn't the Stomach Digest Itself?
* **Gastric Mucus Barrier:** Goblet cells secrete a thick alkaline bicarbonate mucus blanket that lines the stomach wall, preventing stomach acid from burning holes through its own muscular tissue.
* **Peptic Ulcers:** Usually caused not by stress, but by the corkscrew bacterium **Helicobacter pylori** burrowing through this protective mucus shield.
