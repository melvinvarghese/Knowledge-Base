# Cellular Respiration - How Cells Produce ATP

> **The Big Idea:** The metabolic pathway by which human cells break down glucose and oxygen into carbon dioxide, water, and usable energy in the form of ATP.

---

## 1. What It Is
The metabolic pathway by which human cells break down glucose and oxygen into carbon dioxide, water, and usable energy in the form of ATP.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Includes cytoplasmic glycolysis, the mitochondrial Krebs cycle, and the inner membrane electron transport chain pumping protons for ATP synthase.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Glucose -> Glycolysis (2 ATP) -> Krebs Cycle (2 ATP) -> Electron Transport Chain (32 ATP)
```
