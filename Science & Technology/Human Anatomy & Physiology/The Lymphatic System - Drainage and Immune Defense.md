# The Lymphatic System - Drainage and Immune Defense

> **The Big Idea:** The secondary fluid network that reabsorbs excess interstitial fluid leaked from capillaries and filters it through bean-shaped lymph nodes.

## 1. What It Is
The secondary fluid network that reabsorbs excess interstitial fluid leaked from capillaries and filters it through bean-shaped lymph nodes.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Transports dietary fats absorbed by intestinal lacteals and hosts armies of B and T lymphocytes ready to intercept circulating antigens.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Interstitial Capillary Leakage -> Lymphatic Vessel Uptake -> Lymph Node Filtration -> Venous Return
```
