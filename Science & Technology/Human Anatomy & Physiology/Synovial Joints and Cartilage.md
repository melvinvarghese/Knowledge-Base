# Synovial Joints and Cartilage

> **The Big Idea:** Freely movable biological joints engineered with ultra-slippery articular cartilage and pressurized **Synovial Fluid**, achieving a friction coefficient 5x smoother than ice on ice.

---

## 🦵 Natural Low-Friction Engineering
* **Articular Cartilage:** Glassy, smooth hyaline cartilage coating bone ends; has no nerves or direct blood vessels, relying on joint motion to squeeze in nutrients.
* **Ligaments vs. Tendons:** Ligaments connect **Bone to Bone** (stabilizing joints); Tendons connect **Muscle to Bone** (transmitting pulling force).
* **Arthritis:** Osteoarthritis occurs when protective cartilage wears away over decades, causing painful bone-on-bone grinding.
