# The Ear - Hearing and Balance

> **The Big Idea:** The ear does double duty: translating air vibrations into sound perceptions, and using fluid-filled semicircular canals to tell your brain which way is up.

---

## 👂 Anatomy of Hearing

* **Outer & Middle Ear:** Eardrum vibrates three tiny bones (Hammer, Anvil, Stirrup—the smallest bones in the human body).
* **Cochlea:** Snail-shaped fluid organ lined with microscopic hair cells that vibrate to specific frequencies.
* **Vestibular System:** Semicircular canals filled with fluid detect head tilt and angular acceleration, maintaining physical balance.
