# Skin - The Integumentary Shield

> **The Big Idea:** Your largest organ, covering roughly 2 square meters. A waterproof, self-healing shield that regulates temperature, senses touch, and prevents dehydration.

---

## 🛡️ The 3 Skin Layers

* **Epidermis:** Waterproof outer layer of dead keratin cells constantly flaking off and renewing every 28 days.
* **Dermis:** Tough structural middle layer packed with collagen, nerve endings, sweat glands, hair follicles, and blood vessels.
* **Hypodermis (Subcutaneous):** Insulating fat layer protecting against cold and impacts.
