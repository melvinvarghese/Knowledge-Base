# Synovial Joints and Cartilage Mechanics

> **The Big Idea:** The articulated junctions between bones lubricated by viscous synovial fluid and capped with smooth, wear-resistant hyaline cartilage.

---

## 1. What It Is
The articulated junctions between bones lubricated by viscous synovial fluid and capped with smooth, wear-resistant hyaline cartilage.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Includes ball-and-socket joints (hip, shoulder), hinge joints (knee, elbow), and pivot joints that enable multi-axis fluid articulation.

- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Bone End -> Hyaline Articular Cartilage -> Synovial Fluid Cavity -> Fibrous Joint Capsule
```
