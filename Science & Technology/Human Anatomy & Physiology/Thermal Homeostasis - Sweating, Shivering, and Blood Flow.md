# Thermal Homeostasis - Sweating, Shivering, and Blood Flow

> **The Big Idea:** How the human body maintains a strict $37^{\circ}\text{C} (98.6^{\circ}\text{F})$ core temperature: using hypothalamic thermoregulation, evaporative sweating, and vasoconstriction.

---

## 🌡️ The Internal Thermostat
* **When Overheating:** Blood vessels near the skin dilate (**Vasodilation**) to radiate heat outward, and 2.5 million sweat glands release water for **Evaporative Cooling**.
* **When Freezing:** Blood vessels constrict (**Vasoconstriction**) to keep warm blood centered in core organs; skeletal muscles involuntarily twitch rapidly (**Shivering**) to generate emergency friction heat.
