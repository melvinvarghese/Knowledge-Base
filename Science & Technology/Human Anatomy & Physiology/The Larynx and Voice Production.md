# The Larynx and Voice Production

> **The Big Idea:** The 'voice box' in the neck containing twin vocal cords that vibrate like reed instruments as lung air passes through, shaped into words by the tongue, teeth, and lips.

---

## 🗣️ Human Vocal Mechanics
* **Vocal Cords:** Folds of mucous membrane stretched across the larynx. Tightening them produces high-pitched notes; relaxing them produces deep bass tones.
* **The Epiglottis:** A flexible cartilage leaf that automatically flips down like a trapdoor over the larynx every time you swallow, routing food into the esophagus and keeping lungs clear.
