# The Vestibular System - Balance and Equilibrium

> **The Big Idea:** The sensory apparatus of the inner ear consisting of three fluid-filled semicircular canals and two otolith organs (utricle and saccule).

---

## 1. What It Is
The sensory apparatus of the inner ear consisting of three fluid-filled semicircular canals and two otolith organs (utricle and saccule).

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Semicircular canals detect angular rotational head movements; calcium carbonate otolith stones sense linear acceleration and gravitational pull.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Rotational Canals (Angular Acceleration) + Otolith Organs (Gravity & Linear Tilt) = Balance
```
