# Sensory Receptors - How the Body Senses the World

> **The Big Idea:** Specialized neural structures that transduce external physical and chemical stimuli into electrochemical nerve impulses.

---

## 1. What It Is
Specialized neural structures that transduce external physical and chemical stimuli into electrochemical nerve impulses.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Mechanoreceptors detect touch and pressure; thermoreceptors sense temperature; nociceptors signal tissue damage and pain.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of human anatomy & physiology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Physical Stimulus (Pressure/Heat) -> Membrane Ion Channel Depolarization -> Sensory Nerve Action Potential
```
