# The Endocrine System and Hormones

> **The Big Idea:** The body’s wireless chemical communication network. Endocrine glands release hormones into the bloodstream to regulate growth, metabolism, stress, and mood.

---

## 📬 Key Endocrine Glands

* **Pituitary Gland:** The 'Master Gland' in the brain controlling thyroid, adrenals, and growth.
* **Thyroid:** Regulates metabolic rate (how fast your cells burn energy).
* **Adrenal Glands:** Releases Adrenaline and Cortisol for emergency stress responses.
* **Pancreas:** Releases **Insulin** to lower blood sugar and **Glucagon** to raise it.
