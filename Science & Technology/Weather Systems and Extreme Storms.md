# Weather Systems and Extreme Storms

> **The Big Idea:** Weather is Earth's giant air-conditioning engine. The Sun heats the equator more intensely than the poles, creating global pressure belts, atmospheric jet streams, and violent storm vortices like hurricanes and tornadoes that distribute heat across the planet.

---

## 🌬️ High Pressure vs. Low Pressure

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│        HIGH-PRESSURE SYSTEM (H)      │       │        LOW-PRESSURE SYSTEM (L)       │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤       ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Cool, dense air sinks downward.      │  vs.  │ Warm, moist air rises upward.        │
│ Compresses and dries out clouds.     │       │ Expands, cools, and condenses.       │
│ Result: Sunny, clear, dry skies.     │       │ Result: Clouds, wind, rain & storms. │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🌀 Hurricanes (Tropical Cyclones / Typhoons)

Hurricanes are nature's most energetic engines, fueled by warm ocean water (at least 26.5°C):

```
                        ANATOMY OF A HURRICANE
                                  │
          [ THE EYE: Eerie calm, clear skies, low pressure (30-50 km wide) ]
                                  │
          [ EYEWALL: Ring of towering thunderstorms with maximum destructive winds ]
                                  │
          [ SPIRAL RAINBANDS: Squalls sweeping out for hundreds of kilometers ]
```

* **The Coriolis Effect:** Earth’s rotation spins storm systems counterclockwise in the Northern Hemisphere and clockwise in the Southern Hemisphere.
* **The Deadliest Threat: Storm Surge:** High winds and low atmospheric pressure push a colossal dome of ocean water inland, causing catastrophic coastal flooding.

---

## 🌪️ Tornadoes: The Most Violent Winds on Earth

While a hurricane is hundreds of miles wide, a tornado is compact and concentrated:
* **Birth:** Cold, dry polar air collides with warm, moist tropical air, creating a rotating supercell thunderstorm (**Mesocyclone**).
* **Wind Speeds:** Can exceed **480 km/h (300 mph)**, ripping asphalt off roads and leveling brick buildings in seconds.

---

## 🛠️ Key Takeaway

* Air always flows from **High Pressure to Low Pressure**. The greater the pressure difference between two regions, the faster the air rushes across the landscape, creating fierce winds.
