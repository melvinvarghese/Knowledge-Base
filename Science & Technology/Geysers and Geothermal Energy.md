# Geysers and Geothermal Energy

> **The Big Idea:** Geysers are rare natural geothermal springs that periodically erupt with boiling water and steam due to underground magma chambers and tight plumbing systems.

---

## ♨️ How a Geyser Erupts

1. Cold groundwater seeps deep underground near shallow magma chambers.
2. Pressure from the weight of the water column prevents boiling at 100°C, superheating the bottom water.
3. Once bubbles form and push some water out the top vent, pressure drops instantly—flashing the remaining superheated water into explosive steam (e.g., Old Faithful).
