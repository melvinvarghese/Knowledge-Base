# Fungi - The Decomposers and Mycelial Networks

> **The Big Idea:** Fungi are neither plants nor animals. Connected by underground fungal webs (**mycelium**), they are Earth's primary recyclers, breaking down dead wood and feeding ecosystems.

---

## 🍄 The Wood Wide Web

* **External Digestion:** Fungi release digestive enzymes outside their bodies to dissolve organic matter, then absorb the nutrients.
* **Mycorrhizal Networks:** Underground fungal threads connect forest tree roots, trading soil minerals for plant sugars and passing warning signals between trees.
* **The Fruit of the Fungus:** A mushroom is merely the temporary reproductive fruiting body; the true living organism is miles of underground mycelial threads.
