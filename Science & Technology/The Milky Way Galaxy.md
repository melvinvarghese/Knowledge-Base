# The Milky Way Galaxy

> **The Big Idea:** Our cosmic home: a barred spiral galaxy containing 100 to 400 billion stars, stretching 100,000 light-years across, with a supermassive black hole at its core.

---

## 🌌 Our Galactic Neighborhood

* **Structure:** A central bar-shaped bulge surrounded by four major spiral arms (we live on the Orion Spur).
* **Sagittarius A*:** The supermassive black hole at the center, packing the mass of 4.3 million Suns.
* **Cosmic Year:** Our Solar System orbits the galactic center at 828,000 km/h, taking roughly 230 million years to complete one orbit.
