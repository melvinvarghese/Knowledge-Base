# Alkaline Earth Metals and Water Hardness

## Overview
Alkaline Earth Metals (Group 2) are reactive divalent metals ($s^2$ valence), prominent in geological minerals, biological skeletons, and municipal water hardness.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Water Hardness Chemistry: Caused by dissolved calcium ($Ca^{2+}$) and magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$) ions. Temporary hardness (bicarbonates) precipitates boiler scale ($CaCO_3$) when heated:

$$Ca(HCO_3)_2(aq) \xrightarrow{\Delta} CaCO_3(s) + CO_2(g) + H_2O(l)$$

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Biological Mineralization: Calcium phosphate (hydroxyapatite) forms vertebrate skeletal bone and teeth enamel; magnesium is the essential central coordinating ion in chlorophyll.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Municipal water softening via ion-exchange zeolite resins, Portland cement manufacturing, and lightweight magnesium structural alloys.

---

## Key Terms
- **Alkaline Earth Metal**: Group 2 divalent metal forming basic alkaline oxides.
- **Water Hardness**: Concentration of multivalent metallic cations (chiefly $Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+}$) in water.
- **Scale**: Precipitated mineral crust ($CaCO_3$) coating pipes and heating elements.
