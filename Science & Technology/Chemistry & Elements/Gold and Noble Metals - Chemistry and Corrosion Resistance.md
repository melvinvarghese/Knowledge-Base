# Gold and Noble Metals - Chemistry and Corrosion Resistance

> **The Big Idea:** Element #79: a lustrous, heavy metal that does not tarnish, rust, or corrode. Its atomic electrons travel so close to the speed of light that relativistic effects give gold its yellow sheen.

---

## 👑 The Uncorruptible Metal
* **Chemical Inactivity:** Gold does not react with oxygen, moisture, or strong acids; only a fresh mixture of nitric and hydrochloric acid (**Aqua Regia**) can dissolve it.
* **Extreme Malleability:** A single ounce of gold can be hammered into a translucent sheet 9 square meters wide, or drawn into an 80-kilometer microscopic wire!
* **Cosmic Origin:** Almost all gold on Earth was created during catastrophic neutron star mergers billions of years ago.
