# Thermodynamics - Enthalpy, Entropy, and Gibbs Free Energy

> **The Big Idea:** The physical laws governing energy transformations and spontaneous directions of chemical and physical processes.

---

## 1. What It Is
The physical laws governing energy transformations and spontaneous directions of chemical and physical processes.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** A reaction is thermodynamically spontaneous when its change in Gibbs free energy ($\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$) is negative.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Enthalpy Delta H (Heat Content) - Temperature * Entropy Delta S = Gibbs Free Energy
```
