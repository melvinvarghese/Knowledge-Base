- **Core Mechanism:** Gives water unusually high boiling points, surface tension, heat capacity, and causes solid ice to expand and float on liquid water.

- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Polar O-H Dipoles -> Intermolecular Hydrogen Bonds -> Ice Lattice Expands (Floats)
```
