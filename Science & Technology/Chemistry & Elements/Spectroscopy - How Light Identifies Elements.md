# Spectroscopy - How Light Identifies Elements

> **The Big Idea:** Analytical techniques that identify chemical compositions by measuring how matter absorbs, emits, or scatters electromagnetic radiation.

---

## 1. What It Is
Analytical techniques that identify chemical compositions by measuring how matter absorbs, emits, or scatters electromagnetic radiation.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Each element produces a unique spectral barcode of discrete absorption and emission lines corresponding to quantum electron shell transitions.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
White Light Source -> Atomic Gas Absorption -> Prism Spectrometer -> Distinct Emission Lines
```
