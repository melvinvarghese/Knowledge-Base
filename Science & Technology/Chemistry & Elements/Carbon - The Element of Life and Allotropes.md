# Carbon - The Element of Life and Allotropes

> **The Big Idea:** Element #6: the chameleon of chemistry. With four valence electrons, carbon can form strong, stable bonds with itself and other elements, constructing complex organic molecules and diamonds.

---

## 💎 The Diverse Allotropes of Carbon
* **Diamond:** Each carbon atom is bound tetrahedrally to 4 others in a rigid 3D crystal lattice—the hardest natural mineral on Earth.
* **Graphite:** Carbon arranged in flat, slippery hexagonal sheets that slide off onto paper (pencil lead).
* **Graphene:** A single-atom-thick sheet of carbon 200 times stronger than steel and an extraordinary conductor of electricity!
