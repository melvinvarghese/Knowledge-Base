# Alkali Metals - Group 1 Reactivity and Flame Colors

> **The Big Idea:** Soft, highly reactive silvery metals (lithium, sodium, potassium) that possess a single valence electron readily donated to form $+1$ cations.

---

## 1. What It Is
Soft, highly reactive silvery metals (lithium, sodium, potassium) that possess a single valence electron readily donated to form $+1$ cations.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** React violently with water to produce flammable hydrogen gas and alkaline metal hydroxides, emitting characteristic spectral flame colors.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Single Outer Valence Electron -> Readily Ionized -> Exothermic Reaction with Water
```
