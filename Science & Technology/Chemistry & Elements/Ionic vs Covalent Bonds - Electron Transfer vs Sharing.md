# Ionic vs Covalent Bonds - Electron Transfer vs Sharing

> **The Big Idea:** The fundamental chemical bonding mechanisms that hold atoms together into molecules and extended crystalline lattices.

## 1. What It Is
The fundamental chemical bonding mechanisms that hold atoms together into molecules and extended crystalline lattices.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Ionic bonds form between metals and nonmetals via complete electron transfer; covalent bonds form between nonmetals sharing electron pairs.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Electron Transfer (Ionic Lattice) vs Shared Electron Pairs (Covalent Molecular Orbitals)
```
