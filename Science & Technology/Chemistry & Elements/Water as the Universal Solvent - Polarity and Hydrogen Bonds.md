# Water as the Universal Solvent - Polarity and Hydrogen Bonds

> **The Big Idea:** Water ($H_2O$) is chemically unique: a polar molecule whose bent shape and electrical polarity allow it to dissolve more substances than any other liquid on Earth.

---

## 💧 The Anomalies of Water
* **Polarity:** Oxygen pulls electrons strongly, giving the oxygen side a partial negative charge ($\delta^-$) and hydrogen sides a positive charge ($\delta^+$).
* **Hydrogen Bonds:** Weak electrical attractions between neighboring water molecules give water surface tension, high boiling point, and capillary suction in trees.
* **Ice Floats:** Unlike almost every other liquid, water **expands when it freezes**, making ice less dense than liquid water! (If ice sank, lakes and oceans would freeze solid from the bottom up).
