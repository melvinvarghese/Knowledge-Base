# Electrochemistry - Batteries, Fuel Cells, and Electrolysis

> **The Big Idea:** The study of chemical reactions that produce electrical current or are driven by applied electrical voltages via oxidation-reduction (redox) reactions.

---

## 1. What It Is
The study of chemical reactions that produce electrical current or are driven by applied electrical voltages via oxidation-reduction (redox) reactions.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** In lithium-ion batteries, lithium ions deintercalate from the cathode and flow through electrolyte to intercalate into the anode during charging.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Anode (Oxidation / Electron Loss) -> External Circuit Wire -> Cathode (Reduction / Electron Gain)
```
