# Hydrogen - The Most Abundant Cosmic Element

> **The Big Idea:** Element #1: the simplest, lightest, and most abundant element in the universe, making up 75% of all matter. The fuel of stars and one-half of water molecules.

---

## 💧 The First Atom
* **Structure:** Just 1 proton and 1 electron; has no neutrons in its common form (Protium).
* **Clean Energy Carrier:** Burns cleanly with oxygen, releasing enormous heat and producing **pure water ($H_2O$) as its only exhaust** ($2H_2 + O_2 \rightarrow 2H_2O$).
* **Cosmic Origin:** Created in the first 3 minutes of the Big Bang; every other element on the periodic table was forged from hydrogen fusing inside stars!
