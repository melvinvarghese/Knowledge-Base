# States of Matter - Solid, Liquid, Gas, and Plasma

> **The Big Idea:** The four fundamental physical states matter adopts depending upon thermal kinetic energy and ambient pressure conditions.

---

## 1. What It Is
The four fundamental physical states matter adopts depending upon thermal kinetic energy and ambient pressure conditions.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Plasma consists of ionized gas where thermal kinetic energy strips electrons from atomic nuclei, making up over 99% of the visible cosmos.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Solid (Rigid) -> Liquid (Fluid) -> Gas (Expanding) -> Plasma (Ionized Electron Soup)
```
