# Metallic Bonding - The Electron Sea Model

> **The Big Idea:** The electrostatic attraction between positively charged metal cations and a delocalized 'sea' of freely moving conduction electrons.

---

## 1. What It Is
The electrostatic attraction between positively charged metal cations and a delocalized 'sea' of freely moving conduction electrons.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Explains why metals exhibit high thermal and electrical conductivity, metallic luster, malleability, and ductility.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Fixed Positive Metal Cations + Delocalized Freely Flowing Valence Electrons
```
