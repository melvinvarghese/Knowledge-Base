# Nuclear Fission - Splitting the Heavy Atom

> **The Big Idea:** The splitting of a massive fissile nucleus (like uranium-235 or plutonium-239) into lighter fragments upon capturing a thermal neutron.

---

## 1. What It Is
The splitting of a massive fissile nucleus (like uranium-235 or plutonium-239) into lighter fragments upon capturing a thermal neutron.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Releases massive thermal kinetic energy and extra neutrons, sustaining controlled nuclear chain reactions in commercial nuclear power reactors.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
U-235 + Thermal Neutron -> Unstable U-236 -> Fission Fragments + 2-3 Fast Neutrons + 200 MeV
```
