# Corrosion and Rusting - The Degradation of Metals

> **The Big Idea:** The electrochemical oxidation of refined metals into thermodynamically stable oxides upon exposure to atmospheric oxygen and water.

---

## 1. What It Is
The electrochemical oxidation of refined metals into thermodynamically stable oxides upon exposure to atmospheric oxygen and water.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Iron rusts via galvanic micro-cells forming iron(III) oxide hydroxide, prevented by cathodic protection or galvanization with sacrificial zinc.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Iron Anode (Fe -> Fe2+) + Oxygen Cathode (O2 + H2O) -> Hydrated Iron(III) Oxide Rust
```
