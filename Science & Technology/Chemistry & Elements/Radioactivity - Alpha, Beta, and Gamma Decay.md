# Radioactivity - Alpha, Beta, and Gamma Decay

> **The Big Idea:** The spontaneous breakdown of unstable atomic nuclei accompanied by the emission of ionizing particles or high-energy electromagnetic radiation.

---

## 1. What It Is
The spontaneous breakdown of unstable atomic nuclei accompanied by the emission of ionizing particles or high-energy electromagnetic radiation.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Alpha decay emits a helium-4 nucleus; beta decay converts a neutron to a proton with an electron; gamma decay emits high-frequency photons.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
