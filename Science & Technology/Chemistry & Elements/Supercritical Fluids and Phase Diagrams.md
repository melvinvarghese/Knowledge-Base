# Supercritical Fluids and Phase Diagrams

> **The Big Idea:** States of matter existing at temperatures and pressures beyond a substance's thermodynamic critical point where distinct liquid and gas phases vanish.

---

## 1. What It Is
States of matter existing at temperatures and pressures beyond a substance's thermodynamic critical point where distinct liquid and gas phases vanish.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Supercritical $CO_2$ effuses through solids like a gas yet dissolves organic compounds like a liquid, used for industrial decaffeination.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Triple Point (Solid/Liquid/Gas Coexist) -> Critical Point -> Supercritical Fluid
```
