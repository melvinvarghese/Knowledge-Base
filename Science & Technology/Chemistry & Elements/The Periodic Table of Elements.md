# The Periodic Table of Elements

> **The Big Idea:** Dmitri Mendeleev’s masterwork. The 118 known chemical elements organized by atomic number and recurring chemical properties into rows (periods) and columns (groups).

---

## 🧪 The Master Map of Matter

* **Periods (Rows):** Indicate the number of electron energy shells.
* **Groups (Columns):** Elements in the same group have the same number of outer valence electrons, giving them identical chemical personalities.
  * **Group 1 (Alkali Metals):** Highly reactive, explode in water (Sodium, Potassium).
  * **Group 17 (Halogens):** Toxic nonmetals that form salts (Chlorine, Iodine).
  * **Group 18 (Noble Gases):** Full outer electron shells; completely inert and unreactive (Helium, Neon, Argon).
