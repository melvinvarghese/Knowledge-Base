# Salts and Electrolytes - Ionic Solutions

> **The Big Idea:** Compounds formed by ionic bonds between metals and nonmetals. When dissolved in water, salts dissociate into free-floating ions (**Electrolytes**) that conduct electrical nerve impulses.

---

## ⚡ Conducting Life
* **Dissociation:** Solid table salt ($NaCl$) crystals dissolve into free $Na^+$ cations and $Cl^-$ anions surrounded by water molecules.
* **Nerve Firing:** The brain, heart, and skeletal muscles rely entirely on Sodium, Potassium, and Calcium electrolyte gradients across cell membranes to transmit thoughts and muscle contractions.
