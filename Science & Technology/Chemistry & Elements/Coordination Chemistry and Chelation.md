# Coordination Chemistry and Chelation

> **The Big Idea:** Molecules consisting of a central metal ion surrounded by nonmetal ions or molecules termed ligands that donate lone electron pairs.

---

## 1. What It Is
Molecules consisting of a central metal ion surrounded by nonmetal ions or molecules termed ligands that donate lone electron pairs.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Chelating agents wrap multiple claw-like bonds around heavy toxic metal ions (lead, mercury) so they can be safely flushed from the body.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Central Metal Cation <== Coordinate Covalent Bonds <== Surrounding Electron Ligands
```
