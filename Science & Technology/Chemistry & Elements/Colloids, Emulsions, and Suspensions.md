# Colloids, Emulsions, and Suspensions

> **The Big Idea:** Heterogeneous mixtures where microscopic insoluble particles of one substance are dispersed evenly throughout another continuous medium.

---

## 1. What It Is
Heterogeneous mixtures where microscopic insoluble particles of one substance are dispersed evenly throughout another continuous medium.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Milk is an emulsion of butterfat globules stabilized in water by casein; mayonnaise uses egg yolk lecithin as an amphiphilic emulsifier.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Solution (Dissolved Molecules) vs Colloid (Dispersed Nanoparticles) vs Suspension (Settling Particles)
```
