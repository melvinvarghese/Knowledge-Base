# Acids, Bases, and the pH Scale

> **The Big Idea:** The chemical measure of hydrogen ion concentration. Acids donate protons ($H^+$), bases accept protons ($OH^-$), and the logarithmic pH scale runs from 0 to 14.

---

## 🧪 The pH Scale

```
[ STRONG ACID ] ◄────────────── [ NEUTRAL ] ──────────────► [ STRONG BASE ]
pH 0 to 3                       pH 7.0                      pH 11 to 14
(Battery acid, Lemon juice)     (Pure Water, Blood 7.4)     (Bleach, Drain Cleaner)
```

* **Logarithmic:** Each step on the pH scale represents a **10-fold change**. A pH of 5 is ten times more acidic than a pH of 6, and 100 times more acidic than 7!
* **Neutralization:** Acid + Base $\rightarrow$ Water + Salt ($HCl + NaOH \rightarrow H_2O + NaCl$).
