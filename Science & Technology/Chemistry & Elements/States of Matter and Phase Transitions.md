# States of Matter and Phase Transitions

> **The Big Idea:** Matter changes its physical state based on temperature and pressure: Melting, Freezing, Vaporization, Condensation, and direct phase leaps like Sublimation.

---

## 🧊 The Transitions

* **Sublimation:** Solid skips liquid and turns directly into gas (Dry Ice / Frozen $CO_2$).
* **Deposition:** Gas skips liquid and freezes directly into solid (Frost on a winter windshield).
* **Triple Point:** The exact temperature and pressure where solid, liquid, and gas can coexist in thermodynamic equilibrium.
