# Nuclear Fusion - Powering the Stars on Earth

> **The Big Idea:** The thermonuclear merging of light atomic nuclei (deuterium and tritium) at extreme temperatures and pressures to form heavier helium nuclei.

---

## 1. What It Is
The thermonuclear merging of light atomic nuclei (deuterium and tritium) at extreme temperatures and pressures to form heavier helium nuclei.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Overcomes repulsive positive electrostatic Coulomb barriers to release four times more energy per gram of fuel than nuclear fission.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Deuterium + Tritium -> (100 Million K Plasma) -> Helium-4 + Free Neutron + 17.6 MeV
```
