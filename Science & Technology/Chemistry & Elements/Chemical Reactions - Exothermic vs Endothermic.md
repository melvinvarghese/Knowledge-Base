# Chemical Reactions - Exothermic vs Endothermic

> **The Big Idea:** The breaking of chemical bonds in reactant molecules followed by the formation of new chemical bonds in products.

---

## 1. What It Is
The breaking of chemical bonds in reactant molecules followed by the formation of new chemical bonds in products.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Exothermic reactions release thermal energy to surroundings (combustion); endothermic reactions absorb heat (photosynthesis).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Reactants -> Activation Energy Barrier -> Bond Cleavage -> Product Bond Formation
```
