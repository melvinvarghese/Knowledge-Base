# Oxygen - The Element of Combustion and Respiration

> **The Big Idea:** Element #8: the third most abundant element in the universe and the fuel of cellular respiration. A reactive gas that drives fire, rusts iron, and forms 21% of Earth's atmosphere.

---

## 🔥 The Fire Breath
* **High Electronegativity:** Oxygen hungrily strips electrons from other atoms, releasing thermal energy (**Oxidation / Combustion**).
* **The Ozone Form ($O_3$):** A pale blue toxic gas in the lower atmosphere, but a lifesaving UV shield in the stratosphere.
* **Liquid Oxygen:** Pale blue and magnetic; attracted between the poles of a strong horseshoe magnet!
