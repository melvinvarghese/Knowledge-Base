# Chromatography - Paper, Thin-Layer, and Gas Chromatography

## Overview
Chromatography is an analytical separation technique that resolves complex chemical mixtures based on differential partitioning between a stationary phase and a mobile phase.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Partition Coefficients and Retention: Components separate because affinity differences govern migration velocity ($R_f = \text{distance traveled by solute} / \text{distance traveled by solvent}$).

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Stationary vs Mobile Phase Mechanics: Thin-layer chromatography (TLC) uses polar silica stationary plates with organic solvent mobile phases; Gas Chromatography (GC) vaporizes analytes carried by helium gas through capillary columns.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Forensic toxicology screening, pharmaceutical purity verification, and petrochemical impurity monitoring.

---

## Key Terms
- **Chromatography**: Physical method of separation distributing components between stationary and mobile phases.
- **Retention Factor ($R_f$)**: Ratio of solute migration distance relative to the mobile phase solvent front.
- **Chromatogram**: Detector output chart showing resolved peaks over elution time.
