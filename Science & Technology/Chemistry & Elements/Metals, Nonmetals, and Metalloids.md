# Metals, Nonmetals, and Metalloids

> **The Big Idea:** Elements divide into three broad classes based on electrical conductivity, malleability, and how readily they share or accept electrons.

---

## 🧱 The 3 Elemental Classes

* **Metals (75% of elements):** Shiny, malleable, ductile, excellent conductors of electricity and heat (Gold, Iron, Copper).
* **Nonmetals:** Brittle, dull, insulators that gain electrons in reactions (Oxygen, Carbon, Sulfur).
* **Metalloids (Semimetals):** Possess intermediate properties; semiconductors that power all modern microchips and computers (Silicon, Germanium).
