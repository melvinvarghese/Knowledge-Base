# Chemical Kinetics - Rate Laws and Activation Energy

## Overview
Chemical Kinetics investigates the speeds of chemical reactions and the molecular mechanisms by which reactants transform into products.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Rate Laws and Reaction Order: Differential rate law $\text{Rate} = k [A]^m [B]^n$. Integrated rate laws dictate linear concentration vs time plots: 0th order ($[A]$ vs $t$), 1st order ($\ln[A]$ vs $t$), 2nd order ($1/[A]$ vs $t$).

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Collision Theory: Reaction occurs only if colliding molecules possess sufficient kinetic energy exceeding activation energy ($E_a$) and correct geometric orientation.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Pharmaceutical drug shelf-life stability testing, nuclear waste half-life tracking, and combustion engine ignition timing.

---

## Key Terms
- **Rate Constant ($k$)**: Proportionality coefficient in a rate law equation dependent on temperature.
- **Activation Energy ($E_a$)**: Minimum energy barrier reactants must overcome to initiate a chemical reaction.
- **Reaction Mechanism**: Step-by-step sequence of elementary molecular reactions yielding overall change.
