# Chemical Reactions and Catalysts

> **The Big Idea:** Chemical reactions rearrange atoms into new molecular configurations by breaking and forming chemical bonds without ever creating or destroying matter.

---

## 💥 Conservation of Mass

* **Conservation of Mass:** The total mass of reactants must equal the total mass of products in any closed chemical reaction.
* **Exothermic vs. Endothermic:** Exothermic reactions release heat (combustion, fire); Endothermic reactions absorb heat (chemical cold packs, photosynthesis).
* **Catalysts & Enzymes:** Substances that dramatically speed up chemical reactions by lowering the activation energy barrier without being consumed.
