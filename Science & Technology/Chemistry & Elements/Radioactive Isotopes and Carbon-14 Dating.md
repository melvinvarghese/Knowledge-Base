# Radioactive Isotopes and Carbon-14 Dating

> **The Big Idea:** How nuclear decay measures deep time: unstable Carbon-14 decays into Nitrogen-14 with a precise half-life of 5,730 years, allowing archaeologists to date ancient organic artifacts.

---

## ⏳ The Radioactive Clock
* **The Half-Life Principle:** The time required for exactly 50% of radioactive parent atoms to decay into stable daughter atoms.
* **Carbon-14 Dating:** Cosmic rays turn nitrogen in the air into Carbon-14. Living trees and humans breathe it in. When an organism dies, Carbon-14 intake stops, and existing atoms decay steadily, acting as an atomic stopwatch accurate up to 50,000 years!
