# Organic Chemistry - The Carbon Skeleton

> **The Big Idea:** The branch of chemistry focused on carbon-containing compounds, enabled by carbon's unique ability to form four stable covalent bonds (catenation).

---

## 1. What It Is
The branch of chemistry focused on carbon-containing compounds, enabled by carbon's unique ability to form four stable covalent bonds (catenation).

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Carbon forms infinite chains, branched rings, double bonds, and triple bonds that construct all biological macromolecules.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of chemistry & elements, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Tetravalent Carbon -> C-C Catenation Chains -> Functional Groups (Alcohols, Ketones, Amines)
```
