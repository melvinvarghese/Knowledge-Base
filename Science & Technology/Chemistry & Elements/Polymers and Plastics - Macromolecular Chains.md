# Polymers and Plastics - Macromolecular Chains

> **The Big Idea:** Giant synthetic and natural macromolecules formed by linking thousands of small repeating chemical units (**monomers**) into long, durable molecular chains.

---

## 🧵 Long-Chain Chemistry
* **Natural Polymers:** DNA, cellulose in wood, proteins in silk, and natural rubber.
* **Synthetic Plastics (Polyethylene, PVC, Nylon):** Lightweight, waterproof, electrical insulators, and resistant to chemical decay.
* **The Microplastic Problem:** Because synthetic polymer bonds do not rot naturally, plastics break down into microscopic particles that contaminate oceans and food chains.
