# Oxidation-Reduction (Redox) Reactions

> **The Big Idea:** The fundamental chemical dance of electron transfer: **Oxidation** is the loss of electrons; **Reduction** is the gain of electrons (**OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain**).

---

## 🔋 Driving Batteries and Fire
* **Coupled Reactions:** Electrons cannot float alone; every time one chemical is oxidized (loses electrons), another must be reduced (gains them).
* **Examples:**
  * **Rusting:** Iron loses electrons to oxygen ($4Fe + 3O_2 \rightarrow 2Fe_2O_3$).
  * **Lithium-ion Batteries:** Lithium atoms give up electrons at the anode, driving electrical current through your phone before being collected at the cathode.
