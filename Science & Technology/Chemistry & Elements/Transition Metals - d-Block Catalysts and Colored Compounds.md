# Transition Metals - d-Block Catalysts and Colored Compounds

> **The Big Idea:** Metals characterized by the progressive filling of inner d-orbitals, capable of adopting multiple stable oxidation states.

---

## 1. What It Is
Metals characterized by the progressive filling of inner d-orbitals, capable of adopting multiple stable oxidation states.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Form brightly colored coordination complexes and serve as the premier industrial catalysts (iron in Haber process, platinum in catalytic converters).
