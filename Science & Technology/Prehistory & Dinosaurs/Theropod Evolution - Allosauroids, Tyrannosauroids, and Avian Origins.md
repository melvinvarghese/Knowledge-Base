# Theropod Evolution - Allosauroids, Tyrannosauroids, and Avian Origins

## Overview
Theropoda is a diverse clade of bipedal saurischian dinosaurs that encompassed the Mesozoic apex terrestrial carnivores and gave rise to modern birds.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Evolutionary Trajectory & Apex Radiations: Evolving in the Triassic as agile carnivores (*Coelophysis*), theropods diversified in the Jurassic into massive allosauroids (*Allosaurus*) possessing slicing ziphodont teeth. In the Cretaceous, tyrannosauroids developed immense skulls, reduced forelimbs, stereoscopic vision, and bone-crushing bite forces exceeding 35,000 Newtons (*Tyrannosaurus rex*).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Maniraptoran Transition to Aves: Coelurosaurian theropods evolved hollow pneumatized bones, three-toed feet, furculae (wishbones), brooding parental behavior, and filamentous proto-feathers, progressively transitioning through dromaeosaurids (*Velociraptor*) and microraptorines to crown-group birds.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Skeletal cladistic transition documentation, feather melanosome structural color reconstruction.

## Key Terminology
- **Theropod**: Clade of bipedal saurischian dinosaurs including all predatory dinosaurs and modern birds.
- **Ziphodont**: Tooth morphology characterized by laterally compressed, recurved crowns with serrated edges for cutting meat.
- **Furcula**: Wishbone formed by the fusion of the two clavicles, an essential avian flight adaptation present in theropods.
