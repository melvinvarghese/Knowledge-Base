# Pterosaurs - Reptiles of the Mesozoic Skies

> **The Big Idea:** The first vertebrates to achieve powered flight. Flying reptiles (not dinosaurs) spanning from sparrow-sized flyers to *Quetzalcoatlus*, a monster with a 11-meter wingspan.

---

## 🦇 Wings of Skin
* **Wing Anatomy:** A wing membrane of skin and muscle supported by a ridiculously elongated **fourth finger**.
* **Quetzalcoatlus:** As tall as a giraffe on the ground; launched into the air using a powerful four-limbed pole-vault vault maneuver.
* **Fuzzy Insulation:** Covered in hair-like pycnofibers, proving pterosaurs were active, warm-blooded flyers.
