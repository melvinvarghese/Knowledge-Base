> **The Big Idea:** Bipedal herbivores known for solid bone cranial domes up to 25 cm thick surrounded by decorative bony knobs and spikes.

## 1. What It Is
Bipedal herbivores known for solid bone cranial domes up to 25 cm thick surrounded by decorative bony knobs and spikes.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Domes were reinforced with dense perpendicular bone fibers, used in head-butting or flank-butting dominance contests.
