# Crocodylomorph Diversity - Deinosuchus, Simosuchus, and Notosuchians

## Overview
Crocodylomorpha is the ancient reptilian archosaur lineage that includes modern crocodilians alongside extinct forms that encompassed terrestrial runners, pug-nosed herbivores, and colossal giant ambushers.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Notosuchians & Herbivorous Crocs: In the Cretaceous, Southern Hemisphere notosuchians abandoned aquatic habitats for terrestrial life: *Simosuchus* was a stubby, pug-nosed herbivore with leaf-shaped teeth; *Baurusuchus* was an active, long-legged terrestrial cursorial predator armed with ziphodont slicing teeth that competed with theropods.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Colossal Apex Semi-Aquatic Crocs (*Deinosuchus* and *Sarcosuchus*): Giant crocodyliforms reached terrifying dimensions: Late Cretaceous *Deinosuchus* grew up to 10–12 meters long with bone-crushing jaws generating bite forces exceeding 100,000 Newtons, preying on juvenile hadrosaurs and tyrannosaurs at river estuaries.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Long-term evolutionary endurance surviving the K-Pg extinction event, archosaurian anatomical diversification.

## Key Terminology
- **Crocodylomorpha**: Diverse clade of archosaurian reptiles that includes modern crocodilians and their extinct relatives.
- **Deinosuchus**: Extinct colossal crocodylian of the Late Cretaceous that preyed on large dinosaurs along coastal waterways.
- **Simosuchus**: Bizarre, short-snouted terrestrial herbivorous crocodyliform from the Late Cretaceous of Madagascar.
