# Theropod Evolution - The Path to Birds

> **The Big Idea:** The carnivorous bipedal dinosaur clade that progressively developed hollow bones, wishbones (furculae), feathers, and reduced digits.

---

## 1. What It Is
The carnivorous bipedal dinosaur clade that progressively developed hollow bones, wishbones (furculae), feathers, and reduced digits.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Birds are avian theropods that survived the Chicxulub impact, making living hummingbirds and eagles direct living dinosaurs.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Basal Theropod -> Coelurosaurian Feathers -> Maniraptoran Wings -> Modern Birds
```
