# Mass Extinctions - The Big Five in Earth's History

> **The Big Idea:** Over 99% of all species that ever lived are extinct. Five times in Earth's history, planetary catastrophes wiped out over 70% of life, resetting the evolutionary clock.

---

## 💀 The Big 5 Extinctions

1. **Ordovician-Silurian (443 Mya):** Intense ice age and plunging sea levels (85% extinct).
2. **Late Devonian (375 Mya):** Ocean deoxygenation wiping out marine ecosystems (75% extinct).
3. **The Permian-Triassic 'Great Dying' (252 Mya):** Massive Siberian volcanic basalt floods release $CO_2$ and acidify oceans (**96% of all marine species wiped out**).
4. **Triassic-Jurassic (201 Mya):** Volcanic greenhouse warming clear way for dinosaurs.
5. **Cretaceous-Paleogene (66 Mya):** Chicxulub asteroid impact wipes out non-avian dinosaurs.
