# Triassic Archosaurs and the Origin of Dinosauria

## Overview
Following the Great Dying, Archosauria radiated during the Triassic period, splitting into pseudosuchians (crocodilian ancestors) and avemetatarsalians (pterosaurs and dinosaurs), setting the stage for dinosaur dominance.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Anatomical Innovations: Evolution of an upright, parasagittal limb posture (legs tucked directly beneath the torso rather than sprawling laterally) enabled sustained bipedal running and decoupled breathing from locomotion (Carrier's constraint).

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
The Earliest Dinosaurs: Small, bipedal carnivorous agile runners like *Eoraptor* and *Herrerasaurus* discovered in Argentina's Ischigualasto Formation (~231 Ma), bearing an open (perforated) acetabulum hip socket.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Adaptive radiation into vacant post-extinction ecological niches, evolutionary origin of avian physiology.

---

## Key Terms
- **Archosaur**: Clade of diapsid reptiles encompassing modern birds, crocodilians, and extinct dinosaurs.
- **Parasagittal Posture**: Erect limb posture where legs move parallel to the body's midline plane beneath the trunk.
- **Perforated Acetabulum**: Hole in the center of the hip socket defining the diagnostic skeletal trait of all dinosaurs.
