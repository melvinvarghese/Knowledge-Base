# Origin of Mammals - The Morganucodontids and Cynodont Transition

## Overview
True mammals (Mammaliaformes) emerged during the Late Triassic (c. 210 Ma) from advanced cynodont synapsids, coexisting with dinosaurs for over 140 million years as small nocturnal insectivores.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Morganucodon & The Dentary-Squamosal Jaw Joint: *Morganucodon* represents the crown mammaliaform transition. Unlike reptiles (where the lower jaw consists of multiple bones), *Morganucodon* evolved a lower jaw composed of a single enlarged **dentary bone** articulating with the squamosal bone of the skull. The displaced articular and quadrate jaw bones migrated into the middle ear to become the **malleus** and **incus**.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Nocturnal Bottleneck & Diphyodont Dentition: To evade diurnal predatory dinosaurs, early mammals adapted to nocturnal lifestyles: evolving large olfactory and auditory brain lobes, endothermy, insulating fur, and a single juvenile-to-adult tooth replacement cycle (**diphyodonty**) with specialized cusped molars.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Evolutionary origin of lactation, sensory neocortex expansion, ancestral lineage to all placental and marsupial mammals.

## Key Terminology
- **Dentary Bone**: Single anterior bone of the lower jaw that bears the teeth, comprising the entire lower jaw in mammals.
- **Diphyodont**: Pattern of having only two successive sets of teeth (deciduous milk teeth and permanent adult teeth).
- **Nocturnal Bottleneck**: Evolutionary hypothesis suggesting that mammals were forced into a nocturnal niche during the Mesozoic, shaping mammalian sensory adaptations.
