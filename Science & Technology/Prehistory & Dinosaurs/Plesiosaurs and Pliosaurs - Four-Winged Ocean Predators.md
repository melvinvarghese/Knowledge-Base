# Plesiosaurs and Pliosaurs - Four-Winged Ocean Predators

> **The Big Idea:** Marine reptiles that swam through Mesozoic oceans by underwater flight, flapping four massive hydrofoil flippers like sea turtles.

---

## 1. What It Is
Marine reptiles that swam through Mesozoic oceans by underwater flight, flapping four massive hydrofoil flippers like sea turtles.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Plesiosaurs had extremely long flexible necks for snagging fish, while short-necked pliosaurs like Kronosaurus were apex macropredators.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
