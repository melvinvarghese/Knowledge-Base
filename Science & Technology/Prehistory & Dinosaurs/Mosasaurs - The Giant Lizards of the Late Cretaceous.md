# Mosasaurs - The Giant Lizards of the Late Cretaceous

> **The Big Idea:** Fierce marine squamates closely related to modern monitor lizards and snakes that replaced pliosaurs as oceanic apex predators.

---

## 1. What It Is
Fierce marine squamates closely related to modern monitor lizards and snakes that replaced pliosaurs as oceanic apex predators.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Tylosaurus and Mosasaurus possessed double-hinged jaws and pterygoid teeth in the roof of the mouth to swallow giant ammonites and sharks whole.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**

Monitor Lizard Ancestor -> Paddle Limbs -> Double-Hinged Jaws -> Apex Sea Ruler
