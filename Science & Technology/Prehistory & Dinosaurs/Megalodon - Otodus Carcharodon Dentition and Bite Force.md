# Megalodon - Otodus Carcharodon Dentition and Bite Force

## Overview
*Otodus megalodon* (Miocene-Pliocene, ~23–3.6 Ma) was a colossal lamniform megatooth shark reaching lengths of **15 to 20 meters (50–65 feet)**, equipped with massive triangular serrated teeth engineered to crush whales.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Dental Anatomy and Bite Force: Massive fossilized teeth exceed 18 cm (7 inches) in diagonal height; 3D finite element modeling calculates a bite force exceeding **108,000 to 182,000 Newtons**—the highest known bite force in animal history.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Ecology and Marine Whale Predation: Fossilized cetacean bones frequently display deep healed and unhealed Megalodon bite gouges and severed vertebral columns, confirming specialized predation on marine whales.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Apex marine predator metabolic constraints, Pliocene cooling and ocean current shift extinction drivers.

---

## Key Terms
- **Megalodon**: Extinct species of giant megatooth shark that lived from the early Miocene to Pliocene.
- **Lamniform**: Order of mackerel sharks including the modern great white, mako, and extinct Megalodon.
- **Dental Serrations**: Saw-like enamel edges along tooth margins designed for cutting through fibrous meat and bone.
