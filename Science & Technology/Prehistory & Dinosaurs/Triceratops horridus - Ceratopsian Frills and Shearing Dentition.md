# Triceratops horridus - Ceratopsian Frills and Shearing Dentition

## Overview
*Triceratops horridus* was a massive ceratopsian dinosaur (9 meters, 12 tons) featuring three prominent facial horns, an expansive solid bone neck frill, and high-efficiency shearing dental batteries.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Cranial Horns & Frill Mechanics: Dual 1-meter brow horns and nasal horn served for intraspecific jousting and defense against *T. rex*; solid frill lacked fenestrae, protecting the vulnerable neck and anchoring massive jaw muscles.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Self-Sharpening Dental Batteries: Jaws packed hundreds of teeth into continuous batteries that moved with a vertical slicing shear motion, wearing enamel against dentin to maintain self-sharpening blades capable of slicing tough fibrous palms.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Tooth micro-wear mechanical analysis, social combat behavior in prehistoric megafauna.

---

## Key Terms
- **Ceratopsian**: Herbivorous horned ornithischian dinosaur characterized by beak, facial horns, and neck frill.
- **Dental Battery**: Complex arrangement of interlocking replacement teeth functioning as a continuous grinding/shearing surface.
- **Intraspecific Combat**: Physical competition occurring between members of the same biological species for mates or territory.
