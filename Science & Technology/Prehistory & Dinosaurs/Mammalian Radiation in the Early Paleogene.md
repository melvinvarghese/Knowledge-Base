# Mammalian Radiation in the Early Paleogene

## Overview
Following the extinction of non-avian dinosaurs at the K-Pg boundary, surviving small, nocturnal, burrowing mammals underwent explosive **adaptive radiation**, filling vacant ecological megafaunal niches.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Morphological Diversification Pulse: Mammals expanded within a few million years from tiny insectivores into diverse orders: placentals, marsupials, ungulates, carnivores, cetaceans, and primates.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Body Size Escalation: Mammalian body mass escalated by three orders of magnitude, evolving into massive herbivores (*Pantodonts*) and apex predators within the warm greenhouse climates of the Paleocene and Eocene.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Understanding ecosystem recovery dynamics following global mass extinctions, early primate evolutionary origins.

---

## Key Terms
- **Adaptive Radiation**: Rapid evolutionary diversification of a single ancestral lineage into diverse ecological roles.
- **Placental Mammal**: Mammal whose offspring complete early development inside the uterus supported by a placenta.
- **Ecological Niche**: Functional role and position of an organism within its surrounding ecological community.
