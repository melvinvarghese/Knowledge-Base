# Mosasaur and Plesiosaur - Mesozoic Sea Monsters

> **The Big Idea:** While dinosaurs ruled the land, terrifying marine reptiles dominated the oceans: serpentine Mosasaurs (ancient monitor lizards) and long-necked Plesiosaurs.

---

## 🌊 Cretaceous Ocean Predators
* **Mosasaurs:** True marine lizards related to Komodo dragons and snakes. Grew up to 17 meters long with double-hinged jaws and flipper limbs.
* **Plesiosaurs:** Flapped four wing-like flippers to 'fly' underwater like sea turtles; had necks with up to 76 vertebrae to dart their heads into schools of fish.
* **Breathing Air:** Because they were reptiles, they had lungs and had to surface for air, giving birth to live young at sea rather than laying eggs on land.
