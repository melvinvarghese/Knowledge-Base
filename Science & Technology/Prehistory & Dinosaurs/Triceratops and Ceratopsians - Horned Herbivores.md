# Triceratops and Ceratopsians - Horned Herbivores

> **The Big Idea:** A 9-meter, 10-ton rhinoceros-like herbivore armed with three face horns and a massive bony neck frill, built to defend against T-Rex and duel rival mates for dominance.

---

## 🛡️ The Bony Frill
* **Horns & Frill:** A 1-meter brow horn over each eye and a short snout horn. The neck frill protected vulnerable neck arteries from predator bites.
* **Shearing Beak:** Possessed a parrot-like beak and dental batteries of hundreds of teeth arranged in vertical magazines that self-sharpened to slice tough cycads and palms.
