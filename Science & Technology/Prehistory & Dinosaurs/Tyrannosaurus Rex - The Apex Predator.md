# Tyrannosaurus Rex - The Apex Predator

> **The Big Idea:** The iconic Late Cretaceous carnivore: 12 meters long, 8 tons in weight, with serrated bone-crushing teeth and the most powerful bite force of any terrestrial animal in history.

---

## 🦖 The King of Tyrant Lizards
* **Bite Force:** Estimated at **35,000 to 57,000 Newtons**—enough to shatter and swallow Triceratops hip bones whole.
* **Sensory Prowess:** Massive olfactory bulbs provided an extraordinary sense of smell, and forward-facing binocular vision rivaled modern birds of prey.
* **Tiny Arms:** Despite looking comical, T-Rex arms were heavily muscled and could lift over 200 kg each, likely used to hold struggling prey or assist in standing.
