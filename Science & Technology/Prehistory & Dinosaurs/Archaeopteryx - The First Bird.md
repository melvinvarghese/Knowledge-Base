# Archaeopteryx - The First Bird

> **The Big Idea:** Discovered in 1861 in German limestone, *Archaeopteryx* is the ultimate 'missing link' transitional fossil connecting non-avian dinosaurs directly to modern birds.

---

## 🪶 The Dinosaur-Bird Bridge
* **Dinosaur Traits:** Sharp teeth in jaws, three clawed fingers on each wing, and a long bony reptilian tail.
* **Bird Traits:** True flight feathers, aerodynamic wing architecture, and a wishbone (furcula).
* **Significance:** Discovered just two years after Charles Darwin published *On the Origin of Species*, providing undeniable proof of evolutionary transition.
