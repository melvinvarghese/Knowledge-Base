> **The Big Idea:** The largest known flying animal in Earth's history, an azhdarchid pterosaur that stood 5 meters tall on four limbs with a 10-meter wingspan.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The largest known flying animal in Earth's history, an azhdarchid pterosaur that stood 5 meters tall on four limbs with a 10-meter wingspan.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Unlike birds that launch with their hindlegs, Quetzalcoatlus vaulted into the air using a powerful quadrupedal launch powered by its colossal pectoral and forelimb flight muscles. Once airborne, lightweight hollow trabecular bones and expansive air sacs enabled soaring over thousands of miles.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Prehistory & Dinosaurs**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
