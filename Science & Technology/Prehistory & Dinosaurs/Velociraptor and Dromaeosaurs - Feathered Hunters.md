# Velociraptor and Dromaeosaurs - Feathered Hunters

> **The Big Idea:** Agile, feathered, bipedal predators of the Cretaceous. Popular culture made them human-sized and scaly, but real Velociraptors were turkey-sized and covered in bird-like plumage.

---

## 🪶 Real vs. Hollywood Velociraptor
* **Actual Size:** Velociraptor stood roughly 0.5 meters tall at the hip and weighed only 15 kg (Jurassic Park's 'Velociraptor' was actually based on the larger cousin *Deinonychus*).
* **The Sickle Claw:** The iconic curved claw on the second toe was carried off the ground while walking and used to pin down struggling prey like modern eagles.
* **Quill Knobs:** Fossils discovered in Mongolia show direct quill knobs on the arm bones, proving true aerodynamic feather wings.
