# Ice Age Megafauna - Woolly Mammoths and Saber-Toothed Cats

> **The Big Idea:** During the Pleistocene epoch (2.5 million to 11,700 years ago), giant mammals adapted to extreme cold dominated the steppes: Mammoths, Smilodon cats, and Giant Ground Sloths.

---

## 🦣 Giants of the Cold
* **Woolly Mammoth:** Dual-layer shaggy coat, 4-meter curved tusks to shovel snow away from grass, and small ears to prevent frostbite.
* **Smilodon (Saber-Tooth):** Possessed 28-cm canine teeth; used heavy muscular forelimbs to pin prey before delivering a precision throat bite.
* **The Quaternary Extinction:** Around 10,000 years ago, warming climates combined with the spread of skilled human hunters drove 70% of large mammal species to extinction.
