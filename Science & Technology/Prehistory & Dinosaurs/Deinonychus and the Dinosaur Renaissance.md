## 1. What It Is
The 1969 discovery of Deinonychus by John Ostrom that overturned the view of dinosaurs as sluggish cold-blooded lizards to active, warm-blooded hunters.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Its stiffened balancing tail, sickle-shaped switchblade claw on the second toe, and high metabolic demands sparked the modern bird-dinosaur link.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Sluggish Cold-Blooded Paradigm -> Deinonychus Agility -> Warm-Blooded Bird Connection
```
