# Sauropods - The Long-Necked Giants

> **The Big Idea:** The largest land animals in history. Titanosaurs and Brachiosaurs grew up to 40 meters long and weighed 70+ tons, engineered with pneumatic hollow bones and bird-like lungs.

---

## 🦕 Living Skyscrapers
* **Gigantism Architecture:** Small lightweight skulls on 12-meter necks allowed sauropods to vacuum vast amounts of tree foliage without moving their heavy bodies.
* **Hollow Bones:** Skeletons were filled with internal air sacs (like modern birds), dramatically reducing weight without sacrificing structural strength.
* **Gizzard Stones (Gastroliths):** Swallowed large rocks that ground tough pine needles inside muscular gizzards since they could not chew with their peg-like teeth.
