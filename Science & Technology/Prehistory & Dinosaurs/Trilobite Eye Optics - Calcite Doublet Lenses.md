# Trilobite Eye Optics - Calcite Doublet Lenses

## Overview
Trilobites (Arthropoda) possessed Earth's first sophisticated image-forming visual systems, featuring rigid compound eyes made of optical-grade single-crystal **calcite** lenses with zero spherical aberration.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Huygens-Descartes Calcite Doublet Lenses: Phacopid trilobites evolved doublet lenses combining pure calcite crystals ($n=1.66$) with underlying organic magnesium-rich layers, correcting spherical aberration identical to optical designs patented by Christian Huygens centuries later.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Holochroal vs Schizochroal Eyes: Holochroal eyes packed up to 15,000 tiny hexagonal calcite lenses under a single corneal membrane; schizochroal eyes featured large, individually isolated lenses separated by sclerotized interlens capsules.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Biomimetic aberration-free optical camera lenses, evolution of predatory visual arms races in Paleozoic seas.

---

## Key Terms
- **Trilobite**: Extinct marine arthropod characterized by a three-lobed, hard exoskeleton.
- **Schizochroal Eye**: Compound eye type where large individual calcite lenses are separated by thick cuticle walls.
- **Birefringence Correction**: Alignment of calcite optical c-axes parallel to visual lines of sight to prevent double vision.
