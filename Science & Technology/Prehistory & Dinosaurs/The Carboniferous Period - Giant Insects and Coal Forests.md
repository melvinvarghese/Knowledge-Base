# The Carboniferous Period - Giant Insects and Coal Forests

> **The Big Idea:** 350 million years ago, Earth was covered in vast swamp forests of giant club mosses. Atmospheric oxygen spiked to 35%, allowing dragonflies the size of hawks to dominate the skies.

---

## 🪓 Where Coal Came From
* **Why Insects Were Huge:** Insects breathe via passive tracheal tubes. High atmospheric oxygen (35% vs. 21% today) allowed giant growth: *Meganeura* dragonflies had 70-cm wingspans, and *Arthropleura* was a 2.5-meter millipede.
* **The Origin of Coal:** When giant swamp trees died, bacteria and fungi had not yet evolved the enzymes to digest **Lignin** (wood bark). Piles of unrotted trees were compressed under pressure into the vast coal beds humanity burns today!
