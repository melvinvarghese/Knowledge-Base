# Dimetrodon and the Synapsids - Mammal-like Reptiles

> **The Big Idea:** Apex terrestrial predators of the Permian Period possessing a prominent dorsal sail and differentiated teeth, belonging to the mammal stem lineage.

## 1. What It Is
Apex terrestrial predators of the Permian Period possessing a prominent dorsal sail and differentiated teeth, belonging to the mammal stem lineage.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Possessed a single temporal fenestra opening behind the eye socket, making them synapsids more closely related to mammals than dinosaurs.
