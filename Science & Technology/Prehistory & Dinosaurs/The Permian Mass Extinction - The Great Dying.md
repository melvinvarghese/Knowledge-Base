> **The Big Idea:** The most catastrophic extinction event in Earth's history 252 million years ago, wiping out roughly 95% of marine and 70% of terrestrial species.

## 1. What It Is

```
Siberian Traps Volcanism -> CO2 & Methane Spike -> Global Hyperthermia -> Ocean Anoxia
```
