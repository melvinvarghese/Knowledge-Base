# The Cambrian Explosion - Rise of Complex Life

> **The Big Idea:** 541 million years ago, a sudden 20-million-year burst of evolutionary radiation produced virtually all modern animal body plans (phyla), hard shells, eyes, and active predators.

---

## 💥 Biology's Big Bang
* **Pre-Cambrian Simplicity:** Life had spent billions of years as single-celled microbes or soft squishy mats (Ediacaran biota).
* **The Arms Race:** The evolution of the first complex eyes (Trilobites) ignited an evolutionary arms race between predators (*Anomalocaris*) and prey, driving the evolution of protective mineralized shells and claws.
* **Burgess Shale:** A famous Canadian fossil deposit preserving soft tissues of bizarre Cambrian creatures in astonishing detail.
