# Human Evolution - From Australopithecus to Homo Sapiens

> **The Big Idea:** The 7-million-year hominin evolutionary lineage marked by habitual bipedalism, tool manufacture, encephalization, and complex symbolic culture.

---

## 1. What It Is
The 7-million-year hominin evolutionary lineage marked by habitual bipedalism, tool manufacture, encephalization, and complex symbolic culture.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** From Lucy (Australopithecus afarensis) walking upright, to Homo erectus mastering fire, to Neanderthals and Homo sapiens interbreeding.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Ardipithecus (Bipedal) -> Australopithecus -> Homo habilis (Tools) -> Homo erectus -> Homo sapiens
```
