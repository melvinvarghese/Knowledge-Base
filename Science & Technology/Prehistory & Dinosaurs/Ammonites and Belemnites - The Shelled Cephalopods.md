## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Ammonites had complex, undulating suture lines reinforcing their chambered shells against deep water crushing pressures.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**

Phragmocone Chambers -> Siphuncle Buoyancy Regulation -> Complex Suture Shells
