# Carboniferous Hyper-Oxygenation and Giant Arthropods

> **The Big Idea:** A geologic era 300 million years ago when atmospheric oxygen spiked to 35%, fueling the growth of giant insects and massive land invertebrates.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
A geologic era 300 million years ago when atmospheric oxygen spiked to 35%, fueling the growth of giant insects and massive land invertebrates.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Because arthropods lack lungs and rely on passive oxygen diffusion through branching tracheal tubes, oxygen concentrations set a hard biological limit on maximum body size. High oxygen enabled Meganeura dragonflies with 75-cm wingspans and Arthropleura millipedes stretching over 2 meters long.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Prehistory & Dinosaurs**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Abundant Coal Swamp Forests -> 35% Atmospheric O2 -> Enhanced Tracheal Diffusion -> Giant Arthropods
```
