# Trilobites - Rulers of the Paleozoic Seas

> **The Big Idea:** Extinct marine arthropods with hard calcite exoskeletons that flourished for nearly 300 million years throughout the Paleozoic Era.

---

## 1. What It Is
Extinct marine arthropods with hard calcite exoskeletons that flourished for nearly 300 million years throughout the Paleozoic Era.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Possessed the first sophisticated compound eyes made of optical-grade calcite crystals, evolving thousands of diverse genera.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of prehistory & dinosaurs, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Cephalon (Head) | Thorax (Segmented Body) | Pygidium (Tail Plate)
```
