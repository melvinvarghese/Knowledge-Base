## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Dunkleosteus had no true teeth; instead, its armored jaws featured interlocking gnathal plates of razor-sharp bone that self-honed as they sheared past each other. Using a four-bar linkage jaw mechanism, it snapped its mouth open in 20 milliseconds to suction prey, then sheared with 5,000 Newtons of crushing force.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Prehistory & Dinosaurs**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
