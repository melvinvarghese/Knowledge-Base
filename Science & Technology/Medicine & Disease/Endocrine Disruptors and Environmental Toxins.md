# Endocrine Disruptors and Environmental Toxins

> **The Big Idea:** Synthetic environmental chemicals (BPA, phthalates, PFAS, pesticides) that mimic or block natural hormones, interfering with reproduction and development.

---

## 1. What It Is
Synthetic environmental chemicals (BPA, phthalates, PFAS, pesticides) that mimic or block natural hormones, interfering with reproduction and development.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** They bind estrogen or thyroid hormone receptors at parts-per-billion concentrations, driving developmental abnormalities and metabolic disorders.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Synthetic Chemical -> Mimics Endogenous Hormone -> Binds Hormone Receptor -> Disrupts Development
```
