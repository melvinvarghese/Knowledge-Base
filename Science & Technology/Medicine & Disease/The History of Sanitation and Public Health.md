# The History of Sanitation and Public Health

> **The Big Idea:** The single greatest saver of human lives in history: not modern hospital surgery, but the Victorian sanitary revolution—clean chlorinated drinking water, sewage systems, and handwashing.

---

## 🚰 The Sanitation Miracle
* **John Snow and the Broad Street Pump (1854):** Mapped cholera deaths in London, proving cholera was waterborne and dismantling the ancient 'miasma' (bad air) theory of disease.
* **Life Expectancy Doubled:** Human life expectancy hovered around 35–40 years for millennia. Clean water, sewage pipes, and food pasteurization doubled global life expectancy to 70+ years in less than a century!
