# Stroke - Ischemic Blockage vs Hemorrhagic Bleed

> **The Big Idea:** A medical emergency where interrupted blood supply to a portion of the brain causes rapid neural oxygen starvation and cell death.

---

## 1. What It Is
A medical emergency where interrupted blood supply to a portion of the brain causes rapid neural oxygen starvation and cell death.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Ischemic strokes (87%) are caused by arterial blood clots treated with clot-busting tPA; hemorrhagic strokes result from ruptured brain aneurysms.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Ischemic (Clot Blocks Blood Flow) vs Hemorrhagic (Vessel Wall Ruptures Bleeding into Brain)
```
