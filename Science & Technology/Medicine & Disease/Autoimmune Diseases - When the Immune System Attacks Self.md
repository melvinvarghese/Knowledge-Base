# Autoimmune Diseases - When the Immune System Attacks Self

> **The Big Idea:** Pathological conditions where the adaptive immune system loses self-tolerance, producing autoantibodies that destroy the body's own tissues.

---

## 1. What It Is
Pathological conditions where the adaptive immune system loses self-tolerance, producing autoantibodies that destroy the body's own tissues.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Examples include Type 1 diabetes (destroying pancreatic beta cells), rheumatoid arthritis (attacking joints), and multiple sclerosis (stripping myelin).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Breakdown of Self-Tolerance -> Auto-Reactive T-Cells & B-Cells -> Tissue Destruction
```
