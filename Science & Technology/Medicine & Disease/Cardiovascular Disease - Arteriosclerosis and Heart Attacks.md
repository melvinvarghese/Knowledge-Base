# Cardiovascular Disease - Arteriosclerosis and Heart Attacks

> **The Big Idea:** The world's leading cause of death. Decades of chronic inflammation and cholesterol accumulate as hardened plaques (**Atherosclerosis**) inside coronary arteries until blood flow is blocked.

---

## 💔 Anatomy of a Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction)
1. Excess LDL cholesterol and inflammatory white blood cells accumulate in the coronary artery wall, forming a fragile fatty plaque.
2. The plaque cap ruptures; platelets immediately rush to form a blood clot over the crack.
3. The clot completely blocks blood flow; downstream heart muscle cells starve of oxygen and begin dying within minutes.
