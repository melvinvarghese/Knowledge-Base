# Anesthesia - The History and Chemistry of Painless Surgery

> **The Big Idea:** The medical miracle that enabled modern surgery: using gases (like sevoflurane) and intravenous drugs (propofol) to induce reversible unconsciousness, analgesia, and muscle relaxation.

---

## 😴 The Triad of General Anesthesia
* **Hypnosis:** Reversible loss of consciousness (patient is asleep and forms zero memories).
* **Analgesia:** Blockade of pain reflex signals reaching the brain.
* **Muscle Relaxation:** Paralyzes skeletal muscles so surgeons can operate without involuntary reflex spasms.
* **GABA Enhancement:** Most general anesthetics bind to GABA-A receptors in the brain, flooding neurons with chloride ions to shut down conscious cortical firing.
