# Alzheimer's Disease - Amyloid Plaques and Tau Tangles

> **The Big Idea:** The most common progressive neurodegenerative dementia, marked by irreversible loss of synaptic connections, memory, and cognitive faculties.

## 1. What It Is
The most common progressive neurodegenerative dementia, marked by irreversible loss of synaptic connections, memory, and cognitive faculties.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Characterized by extracellular accumulation of beta-amyloid plaques and intracellular hyperphosphorylated tau neurofibrillary tangles.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Amyloid-Beta Misfolding -> Plaque Accumulation -> Tau Neurofibrillary Tangles -> Cortical Brain Atrophy
```
