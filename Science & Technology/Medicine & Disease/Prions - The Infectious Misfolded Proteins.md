# Prions - The Infectious Misfolded Proteins

> **The Big Idea:** Acellular infectious agents composed entirely of abnormally folded proteins that transmit lethal neurodegenerative diseases without nucleic acids.

---

## 1. What It Is
Acellular infectious agents composed entirely of abnormally folded proteins that transmit lethal neurodegenerative diseases without nucleic acids.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** PrPSc prions induce normal cellular PrPC proteins to misfold into insoluble beta-sheet aggregates, causing spongy brain degeneration (Kuru, Creutzfeldt-Jakob).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Normal PrPC Protein + Infectious PrPSc Prion -> Chain-Reaction Misfolding -> Spongiform Encephalopathy
```
