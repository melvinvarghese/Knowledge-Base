# Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) - Emphysema and Chronic Bronchitis

## Overview
COPD is a progressive, preventable lung condition characterized by persistent, non-reversible airflow limitation caused by toxic particle inhalation (chiefly cigarette smoke), encompassing **Emphysema** and **Chronic Bronchitis**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
Protease-Antiprotease Imbalance in Emphysema: Cigarette smoke recruits alveolar macrophages and neutrophils, releasing neutrophil elastase that destroys elastin fibers in alveolar septa, collapsing small airways during expiration and forming dynamic hyperinflation (air trapping).

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Chronic Bronchitis Pathology: Chronic airway irritation causes submucosal mucous gland hypertrophy (Reid index $> 0.5$) and ciliary dysfunction, producing chronic productive cough for $\ge 3$ consecutive months across 2 years.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
Pulmonary function spirometry ($FEV_1/FVC < 0.70$), long-acting muscarinic antagonists (LAMA: tiotropium), supplemental home oxygen therapy improving mortality.

---

## Key Terms
- **Emphysema**: Permanent abnormal enlargement of airspaces distal to terminal bronchioles with wall destruction.
- **Neutrophil Elastase**: Proteolytic serine protease enzyme degrading structural alveolar elastin fibers.
- **Air Trapping**: Inability to fully exhale inspired air resulting in pathological pulmonary hyperinflation.
