# Prions and Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies

> **The Big Idea:** Lethal infectious pathogens composed entirely of misfolded protein molecules that propagate without DNA or RNA genomes.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
Lethal infectious pathogens composed entirely of misfolded protein molecules that propagate without DNA or RNA genomes.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** When the normal cellular prion protein ($PrP^C$) encounters an abnormal, misfolded scrapie isoform ($PrP^{Sc}$), the misfolded protein acts as a template, forcing the healthy protein to misfold into insoluble beta-sheet amyloid fibrils that resist autoclaving, enzymes, and digestion.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Medicine & Disease**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Normal PrPC (Alpha-Helix) + PrPSc Template -> Misfolding into Beta-Sheets -> Sponge-Like Brain Cavities
```
