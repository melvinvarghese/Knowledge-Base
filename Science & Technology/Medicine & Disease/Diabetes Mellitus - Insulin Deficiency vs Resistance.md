# Diabetes Mellitus - Insulin Deficiency vs Resistance

> **The Big Idea:** A chronic metabolic disease characterized by elevated blood glucose levels leading to severe vascular and neurological complications over time.

---

## 1. What It Is
A chronic metabolic disease characterized by elevated blood glucose levels leading to severe vascular and neurological complications over time.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Type 1 is an autoimmune destruction of insulin-producing pancreatic beta cells; Type 2 is cellular resistance to insulin driven by diet and genetics.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Type 1: Zero Insulin Produced | Type 2: Receptors Resistant to Insulin -> High Blood Glucose
```
