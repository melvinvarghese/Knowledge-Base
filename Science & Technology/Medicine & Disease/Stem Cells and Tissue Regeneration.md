# Stem Cells and Tissue Regeneration

> **The Big Idea:** The body’s master cells. Undifferentiated cells that have the unique ability to divide indefinitely and transform into any specialized cell type in the body.

---

## 🧬 Cell Potency

* **Embryonic Stem Cells (Pluripotent):** Can transform into all 200+ specialized cell types in the human body (heart, nerve, muscle).
* **Adult Stem Cells (Multipotent):** Found in bone marrow and skin to repair local tissue.
* **iPSCs (Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells):** Adult skin cells reprogrammed in the lab back into stem cells, revolutionizing regenerative medicine.
