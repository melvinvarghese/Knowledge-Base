# Genetic Disorders - Sickle Cell and Cystic Fibrosis

> **The Big Idea:** Diseases caused by inherited mutations in specific DNA genes: Sickle Cell (hemoglobin distortion) and Cystic Fibrosis (faulty mucus salt channels).

---

## 🧬 Single-Letter Catastrophes
* **Sickle Cell Disease:** A single typo in the DNA sequence coding for hemoglobin causes red blood cells to deform into stiff sickle shapes, clogging capillaries.
  * *Evolutionary Advantage:* Carrying a single copy of the sickle cell gene provides near-total immunity to deadly **Malaria**!
* **Cystic Fibrosis:** A mutation in the CFTR gene impairs chloride ion transport, causing thick, sticky mucus to clog the lungs and pancreas.
