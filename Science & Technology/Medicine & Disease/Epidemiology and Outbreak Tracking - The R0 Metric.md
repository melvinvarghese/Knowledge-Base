## 1. What It Is
The scientific study of how infectious diseases spread across populations, utilizing contact tracing, statistical models, and transmission metrics.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** The basic reproduction number ($R_0$) measures how many secondary infections an infected individual generates in a completely susceptible population.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
R0 > 1 (Epidemic Expands) | R0 = 1 (Endemic Equilibrium) | R0 < 1 (Outbreak Extinguishes)
```
