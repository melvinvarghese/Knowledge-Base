# Antibiotics and Penicillin - The Mold Revolution

> **The Big Idea:** Alexander Fleming’s accidental 1928 discovery of *Penicillium* mold destroying bacterial cultures launched the antibiotic age, transforming once-fatal infections into minor inconveniences.

---

## 💊 Mechanism of Action
* **Targeting Bacterial Cell Walls:** Penicillin disables the bacterial enzyme that cross-links peptidoglycan cell walls. Without strong walls, bacteria absorb water and burst like overfilled balloons.
* **Harmless to Humans:** Human cells have cell membranes, but no cell walls, meaning penicillin destroys bacteria while leaving human cells completely untouched!
