# Viruses vs Bacteria vs Parasites - The Pathogen Spectrum

> **The Big Idea:** The biological distinction between acellular infectious genetic capsules (viruses), living single-celled prokaryotes (bacteria), and eukaryotic parasites.

---

## 1. What It Is
The biological distinction between acellular infectious genetic capsules (viruses), living single-celled prokaryotes (bacteria), and eukaryotic parasites.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Bacteria reproduce independently and respond to antibiotics; viruses hijack host cell machinery and require antiviral agents or vaccination.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Acellular Hijacker (Virus) | Autonomous Cell (Bacterium) | Complex Eukaryote (Parasite)
```
