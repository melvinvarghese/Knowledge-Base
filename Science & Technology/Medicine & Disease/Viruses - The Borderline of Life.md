# Viruses - The Borderline of Life

> **The Big Idea:** Viruses are not technically alive. They are microscopic genetic packages (DNA or RNA wrapped in a protein coat) that hijack host cell machinery to reproduce.

---

## 🦠 The Hijackers

* **Are they alive?** No: they cannot produce energy, cannot grow, and cannot reproduce without a living host cell.
* **The Lytic Cycle:** A virus injects its genetic code into a cell, commands the cell to manufacture thousands of virus copies, and bursts the cell open to infect neighbors.
* **Antibiotics Do NOT Kill Viruses:** Antibiotics attack bacterial cell walls; only antivirals and vaccines can fight viral infections.
