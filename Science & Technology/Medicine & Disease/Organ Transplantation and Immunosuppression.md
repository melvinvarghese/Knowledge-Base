# Organ Transplantation and Immunosuppression

> **The Big Idea:** The surgical replacement of failing diseased organs (kidney, heart, liver) with healthy donor organs from deceased or living donors.

---

## 1. What It Is
The surgical replacement of failing diseased organs (kidney, heart, liver) with healthy donor organs from deceased or living donors.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Requires matching human leukocyte antigens (HLA) and lifelong immunosuppressive drugs (cyclosporine, tacrolimus) to prevent host immune rejection.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Donor Organ HLA Matching -> Surgical Graft -> T-Cell Immunosuppression -> Organ Preservation
```
