# Vaccines and Herd Immunity

> **The Big Idea:** How community vaccination protects entire populations: when enough people are immunized against a contagious disease, chains of transmission break.

---

## 🛡️ Community Protection

* **Herd Immunity Threshold:** Usually 80% to 95% of a population must be immune to prevent outbreaks.
* **Protecting the Vulnerable:** Shields newborns, cancer patients, and immunocompromised people who cannot safely receive vaccines.
* **Eradication:** Wiped out **Smallpox** globally in 1980 and pushed Polio to the brink of extinction.
