# Epidemiology and Contact Tracing

> **The Big Idea:** The medical detective science investigating the patterns, causes, and control of disease outbreaks across populations using statistical modeling and transmission tracing.

---

## 🔍 Epidemiological Metrics
* **$R_0$ (Basic Reproduction Number):** The average number of secondary people infected by one sick individual in an unimmune population (e.g., Measles has an astonishing $R_0$ of 12 to 18).
* **Contact Tracing:** Identifying and isolating exposed contacts before they develop symptoms, breaking the exponential growth chain of an epidemic.
