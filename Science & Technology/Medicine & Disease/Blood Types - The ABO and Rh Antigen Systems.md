# Blood Types - The ABO and Rh Antigen Systems

> **The Big Idea:** The classification of human blood based on the presence or absence of specific inherited carbohydrate antigens on red blood cell membranes.

---

## 1. What It Is
The classification of human blood based on the presence or absence of specific inherited carbohydrate antigens on red blood cell membranes.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Mismatching blood types during transfusions causes recipient antibodies to bind foreign antigens, triggering fatal hemolytic agglutination.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
