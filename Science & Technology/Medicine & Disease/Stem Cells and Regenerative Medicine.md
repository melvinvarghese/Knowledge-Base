# Stem Cells and Regenerative Medicine

> **The Big Idea:** Undifferentiated biological cells capable of self-renewal and differentiating into specialized tissue cell types.

---

## 1. What It Is
Undifferentiated biological cells capable of self-renewal and differentiating into specialized tissue cell types.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Embryonic stem cells are pluripotent (can form any body cell); induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) can be reprogrammed from adult skin cells.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Pluripotent Stem Cell -> Directed Signaling Cues -> Specialized Tissue Replacement
```
