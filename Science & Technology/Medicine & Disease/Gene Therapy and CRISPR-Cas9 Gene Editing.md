# Gene Therapy and CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing

> **The Big Idea:** Molecular technologies that correct defective genetic mutations inside living human patient cells to cure inherited diseases.

## 1. What It Is
Molecular technologies that correct defective genetic mutations inside living human patient cells to cure inherited diseases.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** CRISPR-Cas9 uses a guide RNA sequence to locate precise genomic DNA coordinates, where the Cas9 nuclease makes targeted cut-and-paste edits.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Guide RNA Locates Mutation -> Cas9 Protein Cleaves DNA -> Cellular Repair Template Corrects Gene
```
