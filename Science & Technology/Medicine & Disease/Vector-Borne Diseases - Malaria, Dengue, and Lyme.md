# Vector-Borne Diseases - Malaria, Dengue, and Lyme

> **The Big Idea:** Human illnesses caused by parasites, viruses, and bacteria transmitted through the bites of hematophagous arthropod vectors like mosquitoes and ticks.

---

## 1. What It Is
Human illnesses caused by parasites, viruses, and bacteria transmitted through the bites of hematophagous arthropod vectors like mosquitoes and ticks.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Malaria is caused by Plasmodium parasites injected by Anopheles mosquitoes, infecting liver hepatocytes and multiplying inside red blood cells.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Infected Mosquito Vector Bite -> Sporozoites Enter Blood -> Liver Replication -> Erythrocyte Lysis
```
