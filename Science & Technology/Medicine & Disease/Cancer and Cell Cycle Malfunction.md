# Cancer and Cell Cycle Malfunction

> **The Big Idea:** A disease of unchecked cell division. When genetic mutations disable the cell's internal brakes (**Tumor Suppressor Genes**) and activate growth throttles (**Oncogenes**), cells multiply uncontrollably.

---

## 🦀 The Hallmarks of Cancer
* **Evading Apoptosis:** Normal cells commit programmed suicide when damaged; cancer cells ignore suicide signals.
* **Angiogenesis:** Tumors send chemical signals tricking the body into growing new blood vessels directly into the tumor mass to feed it glucose and oxygen.
* **Metastasis:** Cancer cells break loose from the primary tumor, travel through the bloodstream, and colonize distant organs.
