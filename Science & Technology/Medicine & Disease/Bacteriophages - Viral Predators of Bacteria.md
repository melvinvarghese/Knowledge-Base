# Bacteriophages - Viral Predators of Bacteria

> **The Big Idea:** Specialized viruses with crystalline icosahedral heads and spider-like landing legs that hunt, infect, and lyse bacterial cells.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
Specialized viruses with crystalline icosahedral heads and spider-like landing legs that hunt, infect, and lyse bacterial cells.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Bacteriophages land on specific bacterial surface receptors, inject their genetic payload through a contractile tail sheath, and force the host bacterium to synthesize hundreds of viral copies before producing endolysin enzymes that burst the cell open from within.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Medicine & Disease**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Phage Tail Fiber Adsorption -> DNA Injection -> Bacterial Synthesis Hijacked -> Endolysin Cell Lysis
```
