# Diabetes Mellitus - Type 1 vs Type 2

> **The Big Idea:** A metabolic disorder of blood glucose regulation. Type 1 is an autoimmune failure to produce insulin; Type 2 is cellular resistance to insulin driven by genetics and lifestyle.

---

## 💉 The Two Types

* **Type 1 Diabetes (Autoimmune):** Pancreas cannot produce insulin. Patients require daily external insulin injections to survive.
* **Type 2 Diabetes (Insulin Resistance):** The pancreas produces insulin, but exhausted cellular receptors stop responding to it ('key no longer turns the lock'). Glucose builds to toxic levels in the bloodstream, damaging eyes, kidneys, and nerves.
