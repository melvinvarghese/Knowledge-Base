# Pharmacology - How Drugs Work in the Body

> **The Big Idea:** The scientific study of drug actions, encompassing pharmacokinetics (what the body does to the drug) and pharmacodynamics (what the drug does to the body).

---

## 1. What It Is
The scientific study of drug actions, encompassing pharmacokinetics (what the body does to the drug) and pharmacodynamics (what the drug does to the body).

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Molecules act as agonists that activate specific cell receptors or antagonists that block endogenous neurotransmitters and hormones.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Absorption into Blood -> Distribution to Tissues -> Receptor Binding -> Liver Metabolism -> Renal Clearance
```
