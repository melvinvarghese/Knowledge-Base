## 1. What It Is
A collection of diseases characterized by unregulated cell proliferation, evasion of programmed apoptosis, and invasion of distant tissues.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Mutations in proto-oncogenes (accelerators) combined with disabled tumor suppressor genes like p53 (brakes) drive malignant progression.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of medicine & disease, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Normal Cell DNA Damage -> p53 Repair Fails -> Unchecked Division -> Angiogenesis & Metastasis
```
