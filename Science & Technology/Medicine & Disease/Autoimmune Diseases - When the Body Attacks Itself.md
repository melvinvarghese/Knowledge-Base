# Autoimmune Diseases - When the Body Attacks Itself

> **The Big Idea:** When the immune system loses **Self-Tolerance**: confusing the body’s own healthy cells for foreign pathogens and deploying antibodies to destroy joint cartilage, nerves, or organs.

---

## 🛡️ Friendly Fire
* **Type 1 Diabetes:** Immune T-cells mistakenly identify insulin-producing beta cells in the pancreas as foreign invaders and wipe them out.
* **Multiple Sclerosis (MS):** The immune system attacks the fatty **Myelin Sheath** insulating nerves in the brain and spine, disrupting signal conduction.
* **Rheumatoid Arthritis:** Antibodies attack synovial joint linings, causing chronic inflammation, pain, and bone deformity.
