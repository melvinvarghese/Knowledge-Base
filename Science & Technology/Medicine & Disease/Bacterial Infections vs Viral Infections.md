# Bacterial Infections vs Viral Infections

> **The Big Idea:** The vital medical distinction between single-celled living bacteria (strep throat, tuberculosis) and non-living viral invaders (influenza, COVID-19, common colds).

---

## 🔬 Critical Differences

| Feature | Bacterial Infection | Viral Infection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature** | Living single-celled organism | Strands of DNA/RNA in protein coat |
| **Treatment** | Antibiotics work effectively | Antibiotics do NOT work; Antivirals |
| **Reproduction** | Divides on its own via fission | Must hijack living host cells |
| **Examples** | Strep throat, UTI, Cholera | Flu, Measles, HIV, Common Cold |
