# Abstract Algebra - Group Theory and Symmetry Operations

## Overview
Group Theory is the mathematical study of symmetry, formalizing algebraic structures consisting of a set equipped with an operation satisfying four fundamental group axioms.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
The Four Group Axioms: A group $(G, *)$ satisfies: 1. Closure ($a * b \in G$); 2. Associativity ($(a * b) * c = a * (b * c)$); 3. Identity (unique $e \in G$ such that $a * e = a$); 4. Inverse (unique $a^{-1}$ such that $a * a^{-1} = e$).

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
Lagrange's Theorem & Classification of Finite Simple Groups: The order of any subgroup $H$ divides the order of finite group $G$ ($|G| = [G:H] \cdot |H|$); the Enormous Theorem classified all finite simple building-block groups, culminating in the Monster Group.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Noether's Theorem in theoretical physics (symmetries yielding conservation laws), Rubik's cube permutation mathematics, crystallography.

---

## Key Terms
- **Group**: Set paired with an associative binary operation possessing an identity element and inverses for all elements.
- **Abelian Group**: Group whose binary operation satisfies commutativity: $a * b = b * a$.
- **Lagrange's Theorem**: Theorem stating the order of a subgroup divides the order of a finite group.
