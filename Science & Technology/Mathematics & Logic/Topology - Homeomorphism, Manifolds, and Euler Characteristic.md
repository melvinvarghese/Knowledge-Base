# Topology - Homeomorphism, Manifolds, and Euler Characteristic

## Overview
Topology ('rubber-sheet geometry') studies properties of geometric objects that remain invariant under continuous deformations (stretching, twisting, crumpling) without cutting or gluing.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Homeomorphism & The Coffee Cup Equivalence: Two spaces are homeomorphic ($X \cong Y$) if a continuous invertible bijection with continuous inverse exists between them; a doughnut (torus) is topologically identical to a coffee mug because both possess exactly one through-hole ($g = 1$).

---

## 2. Structural Analysis & Mathematical Modeling
The Euler Characteristic ($\chi$): Topological invariant for polyhedra: $\chi = V - E + F = 2 - 2g$. For any sphere, $\chi = 2$; for a torus, $\chi = 0$.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Scientific Impact
Topological data analysis (TDA), topological insulators in solid-state quantum physics, DNA knotting enzymes.

---

## Key Terms
- **Homeomorphism**: A continuous bijective mapping between topological spaces with a continuous inverse.
- **Manifold**: Topological space that locally resembles Euclidean space near each point.
- **Genus ($g$)**: Topological invariant counting the number of handles or through-holes in a surface.
