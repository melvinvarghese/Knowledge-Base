# Functional Analysis - Hilbert and Banach Spaces

## Overview
Functional Analysis studies infinite-dimensional vector spaces equipped with topological limits, norms, and linear operators.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Banach vs Hilbert Spaces: A Banach space is a complete normed vector space $(V, \|\cdot\|)$ where every Cauchy sequence converges. A Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ is a Banach space equipped with an inner product $\langle u, v \rangle$ defining norm $\|u\| = \sqrt{\langle u, u \rangle}$, preserving geometric concepts of orthogonality and projections.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Riesz Representation & Spectral Theorems: The Riesz Representation Theorem establishes an isometric isomorphism between a Hilbert space and its continuous dual space $\mathcal{H}^*$. Linear bounded operators act on infinite-dimensional functions, enabling the spectral analysis of differential equations.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Mathematical foundation of quantum wave mechanics ($\mathcal{L}^2$ Hilbert spaces), finite element methods in structural engineering.

## Key Terminology
- **Hilbert Space**: Complete vector space equipped with an inner product that defines distances and angles.
- **Banach Space**: Complete normed vector space where every Cauchy sequence converges to an element within the space.
- **Dual Space**: Vector space of all continuous linear functionals mapping a normed space into its underlying scalar field.
