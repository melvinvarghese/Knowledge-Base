# Mountains and Orogeny

> **The Big Idea:** Orogeny is the geological process of mountain building. Mountains form when tectonic plates collide, crumpling the crust into folded ridges or volcanic arcs.

---

## 🏔️ The 3 Mountain Types

* **Fold Mountains (Himalayas, Alps):** Continental plates collide head-on, buckling rock layers into giant accordion folds. The Himalayas are still rising 5 mm every year!
* **Fault-Block Mountains (Sierra Nevada):** Crustal tension cracks rock along faults; large blocks drop down while others tilt upward.
* **Volcanic Mountains (Cascades, Andes):** Built layer by layer from erupted lava and ash.
