# Soil - The Living Skin of the Earth

> **The Big Idea:** Soil is not dead dirt; it is a complex, living ecosystem of weathered rock minerals, organic humus, air, water, and billions of microorganisms essential for human food.

---

## 🌱 The Soil Horizons

* **O Horizon:** Organic leaf litter and decaying matter on the surface.
* **A Horizon (Topsoil):** Dark, nutrient-dense humus where roots grow and earthworms feed.
* **B Horizon (Subsoil):** Clay and mineral deposits washed down from above.
* **C Horizon:** Weathered parent rock chunks above solid bedrock.
* A single teaspoon of healthy soil contains more microorganisms than there are people on Earth!
