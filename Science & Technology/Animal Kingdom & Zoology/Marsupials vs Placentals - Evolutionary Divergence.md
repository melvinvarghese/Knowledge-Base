# Marsupials vs Placentals - Evolutionary Divergence

> **The Big Idea:** When the ancient supercontinent Pangaea split, isolated Australia became a playground for pouch-bearing marsupials, while placental mammals dominated the rest of the world.

---

## 🦘 Convergent Evolution
* **Convergent Evolution:** Independent species evolved virtually identical body plans to fill matching ecological niches:
  * Placental Wolf $\leftrightarrow$ Marsupial Thylacine (Tasmanian Tiger)
  * Placental Flying Squirrel $\leftrightarrow$ Marsupial Sugar Glider
  * Placental Anteater $\leftrightarrow$ Marsupial Numbat
* **Reproductive Strategy:** Marsupials invest metabolic energy in external pouch lactation; placentals invest energy in long internal uterine gestation.
