# Parental Investment and Reproductive Strategies (r/K Selection)

> **The Big Idea:** The ecological trade-off between producing many cheap offspring with minimal parental care (r-selected) versus few well-nourished young (K-selected).

---

## 1. What It Is
The ecological trade-off between producing many cheap offspring with minimal parental care (r-selected) versus few well-nourished young (K-selected).

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Sea turtles lay hundreds of eggs and abandon them (r-selection); elephants invest two years of gestation and decades of herd care (K-selection).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
r-Strategy (High Offspring, Zero Care) <--> K-Strategy (Few Offspring, High Investment)
```
