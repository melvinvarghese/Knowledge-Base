# Fish - Anatomy of Aquatic Vertebrates

> **The Big Idea:** The earliest vertebrates on Earth. Fish mastered underwater life through gills for gas exchange, streamlined hydrodynamics, lateral line sensors, and swim bladders.

---

## 🐟 Adaptations for Water

* **Gills:** Feathery filaments packed with capillaries; use counter-current exchange to extract dissolved oxygen from water.
* **Swim Bladder:** Internal gas-filled sac that allows fish to maintain neutral buoyancy at any depth without sinking or floating.
* **The Lateral Line:** A sensory line along the fish's flank that detects micro-vibrations and pressure waves in the water.
