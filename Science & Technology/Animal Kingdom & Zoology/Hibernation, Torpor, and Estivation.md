# Hibernation, Torpor, and Estivation

> **The Big Idea:** States of drastically depressed metabolic rate, heart rate, and body temperature used by animals to survive extreme environmental resource scarcity.

---

## 1. What It Is
States of drastically depressed metabolic rate, heart rate, and body temperature used by animals to survive extreme environmental resource scarcity.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Arctic ground squirrels can lower core body temperatures below freezing ($-2.9^\circ\text{C}$) during deep winter torpor without ice crystallization.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Abundant Resources -> Metabolic Switch -> Depressed Core Temp & Heart Rate -> Periodic Arousal
```
