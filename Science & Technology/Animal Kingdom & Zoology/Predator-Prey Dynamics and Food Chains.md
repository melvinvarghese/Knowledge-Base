# Predator-Prey Dynamics and Food Chains

> **The Big Idea:** Energy flows through ecosystems in trophic levels. As energy moves up from producers to apex predators, roughly 90% is lost at each step as metabolic heat.

---

## 🦁 The 10% Trophic Rule

```
[ APEX PREDATORS ] (1 Calorie)     ◄── Lions, Eagles, Orcas
        ▲
[ SECONDARY CONSUMERS ] (10 Cal)   ◄── Frogs, Small Fish
        ▲
[ PRIMARY CONSUMERS ] (100 Cal)    ◄── Herbivores (Zebra, Rabbits)
        ▲
[ PRIMARY PRODUCERS ] (1,000 Cal)  ◄── Plants, Phytoplankton
```

* Because 90% of energy is burned for warmth and movement at each level, an ecosystem can only support a very small number of apex predators.
