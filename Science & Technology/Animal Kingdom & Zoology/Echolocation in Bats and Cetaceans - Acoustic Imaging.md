# Echolocation in Bats and Cetaceans - Acoustic Imaging

> **The Big Idea:** The biological sonar system used by microbats, toothed whales, and dolphins to navigate and hunt in total darkness.

## 1. What It Is
The biological sonar system used by microbats, toothed whales, and dolphins to navigate and hunt in total darkness.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Dolphins produce ultrasonic clicks through nasal phonic lips, focus the beam through an oily melon, and receive echoes through fatty jawbones.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Phonic Lips Click -> Fatty Melon Focuses Beam -> Prey Echo -> Fatty Lower Jaw to Inner Ear
```
