# Mimicry - Batesian, Mullerian, and Aggressive Tactics

> **The Big Idea:** Evolutionary adaptations where one organism evolves resemblance to another organism or object to gain protective or predatory advantage.

---

## 1. What It Is
Evolutionary adaptations where one organism evolves resemblance to another organism or object to gain protective or predatory advantage.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Batesian mimicry: harmless hoverfly mimics stinging wasp. Mullerian mimicry: two toxic butterflies share identical warning patterns.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Batesian (Harmless Imposter) | Mullerian (Toxic Mutual Warning) | Aggressive (Predator Trap)
```
