# Echolocation - How Bats and Dolphins See with Sound

> **The Big Idea:** Biological sonar: emitting high-frequency ultrasonic clicks and analyzing returning sound echoes to construct high-resolution 3D mental maps in total darkness.

---

## 🦇 Biological Sonar
* **Microbats:** Emit ultrasonic cries up to 150 kHz through nose leaves or mouths; specialize ear flaps to calculate prey distance, speed, and wing flutter.
* **Dolphins:** Focus acoustic clicks through a lipid-filled forehead organ called the **Melon**, receiving echoes through acoustic fat channels in their lower jawbone.
