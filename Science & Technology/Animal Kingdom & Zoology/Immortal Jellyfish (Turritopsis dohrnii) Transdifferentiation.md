# Immortal Jellyfish (Turritopsis dohrnii) Transdifferentiation

## Overview
The Immortal Jellyfish (*Turritopsis dohrnii*) is biologically immortal, capable of reverting from a sexually mature adult medusa back into a juvenile colonial polyp through **cellular transdifferentiation** under environmental stress.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Transdifferentiation Process: When physically damaged or starved, medusa umbrella and tentacle cells lose specialized identities, undergoing genetic reprogramming directly into embryonic stem cells that form a new polyp colony.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Circumventing Senescence: This ontogenetic reversal can theoretically repeat indefinitely, evading natural biological death from aging, resetting telomeres and cellular senescence clocks.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Stem cell reprogramming research (induced pluripotent stem cells, iPSCs), cancer dedifferentiation therapeutics.

---

## Key Terms
- **Transdifferentiation**: Biological process where one mature differentiated non-stem cell transforms into another mature cell type.
- **Medusa**: Free-swimming, umbrella-shaped sexual adult life stage of hydrozoans and jellyfish.
- **Polyp**: Sessile, asexual colonial growth phase anchored to marine substrates.
