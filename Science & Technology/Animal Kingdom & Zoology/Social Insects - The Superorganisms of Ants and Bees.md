# Social Insects - The Superorganisms of Ants and Bees

> **The Big Idea:** Eusocial colonies where sterile workers sacrifice personal reproduction to serve a single reproductive queen, operating as an integrated collective intelligence.

---

## 🐜 Eusocial Mastery
* **The Superorganism:** The queen acts as the reproductive ovary; workers act as foraging hands and defending white blood cells; pheromones act as the nervous system.
* **Ant Architecture:** Leafcutter ants cultivate subterranean fungus gardens using minced leaves; army ants construct living bridges from their own linked bodies.
* **The Waggle Dance:** Honeybees perform an acoustic figure-8 dance to communicate the exact angle and distance of nectar flowers relative to the Sun.
