# Extreme Animal Venom vs Poison - Chemical Weapons

> **The Big Idea:** Venoms are actively injected toxins (via fangs, stingers, spines) containing complex cocktails of neurotoxins, hemotoxins, and cytotoxins.

---

## 1. What It Is
Venoms are actively injected toxins (via fangs, stingers, spines) containing complex cocktails of neurotoxins, hemotoxins, and cytotoxins.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Cone snails, box jellyfish, and inland taipans produce toxins that disable ion channels in milliseconds to paralyze fast prey.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Neurotoxins (Block Nerve Signals) | Hemotoxins (Destroy Red Blood Cells & Clotting)
```
