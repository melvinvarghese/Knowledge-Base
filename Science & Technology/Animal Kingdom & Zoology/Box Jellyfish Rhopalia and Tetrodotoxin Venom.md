# Box Jellyfish Rhopalia and Tetrodotoxin Venom

## Overview
Box Jellyfish (Cubozoa: *Chironex fleckeri*) possess advanced sensory rhopalia with image-forming lens eyes and fire venomous nematocysts that cause fatal cardiac arrest within minutes.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Complex Rhopalia Visual System: Four sensory clubs (rhopalia) carry 24 eyes, including upper and lower lens eyes with corneas, crystalline lenses, and retinas capable of navigation and obstacle avoidance.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Nematocyst Explosive Discharge: Pressurized stinging capsules fire barbed hollow tubules at 5,000,000 g acceleration in 700 nanoseconds, injecting cardiotoxic porins that punch pores in red blood cells, causing massive hyperkalemia and cardiac failure.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Pore-forming cardiotoxin molecular mapping, antivenom immunoglobulin therapies, and high-speed mechanical puncture dynamics.

---

## Key Terms
- **Nematocyst**: Subcellular explosive stinging organelle containing pressurized venom and an eversible barbed tubule.
- **Rhopalium**: Sensory structure in cubozoans housing gravity-sensing statocysts and complex eyes.
- **Hyperkalemia**: Abnormally elevated blood potassium levels resulting from rapid erythrocyte lysis.
