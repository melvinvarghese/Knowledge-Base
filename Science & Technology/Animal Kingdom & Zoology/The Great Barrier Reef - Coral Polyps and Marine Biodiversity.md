# The Great Barrier Reef - Coral Polyps and Marine Biodiversity

> **The Big Idea:** The largest living structure on Earth, stretching 2,300 km along Australia. An underwater rainforest built by trillions of tiny soft coral polyps secreting calcium carbonate.

---

## 🐠 Underwater Cities
* **Symbiotic Algae:** Coral polyps host photosynthetic algae (**Zooxanthellae**); algae provide 90% of coral nutrients through sunlight; corals provide shelter.
* **Coral Bleaching:** When ocean water warms by just 1–2°C, stressed corals expel their algae, turning bone-white and starving to death unless waters cool rapidly.
* **Biodiversity Hub:** Occupies less than 0.1% of ocean area, but provides nursery shelter for 25% of all marine species.
