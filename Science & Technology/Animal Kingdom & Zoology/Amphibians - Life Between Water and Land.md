# Amphibians - Life Between Water and Land

> **The Big Idea:** The evolutionary bridge from water to land. Frogs, toads, and salamanders lead a double life (*Amphi-Bios*), breathing through permeable skin and laying jelly eggs in water.

---

## 🐸 Cutaneous Respiration

* **Skin Breathing:** Amphibians can absorb oxygen directly through their moist skin, making them extraordinarily sensitive to pollution and environmental toxins.
* **Metamorphosis:** Aquatic gilled tadpoles metamorphose into air-breathing, four-legged adults.
* **Ecological Canary in the Coal Mine:** Worldwide amphibian declines signal ecosystem breakdowns and fungal outbreaks (Chytrid fungus).
