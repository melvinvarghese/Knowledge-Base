# Electric Eel Bioelectrogenesis and Electrocytes

## Overview
Electric Eels (*Electrophorus electricus*) are neotropical knifefish capable of discharging electrical shocks exceeding 860 volts to stun prey, defend against terrestrial predators, and navigate turbid waters.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Electrocyte Stacking & Serial Summation: Main, Hunter's, and Sachs organs contain thousands of electrocytes—stacked modified muscle cells. Synchronous acetylcholine release opens ion channels, summing trans-membrane potentials into a biological battery (860 V, 1 A).

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Low vs High Voltage Functions: Low-voltage pulses (~10 V, 25 Hz) emitted from Sachs organ act as navigational electrolocation; high-voltage volleys (hundreds of volts, 400 Hz) involuntarily seize prey motor neurons.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Bio-inspired flexible hydrogel electrical batteries, soft implantable power sources for medical pacemakers.

---

## Key Terms
- **Electrocyte**: Specialized biological cell generating electric charge across its membrane.
- **Bioelectrogenesis**: Generation of electricity by living organisms via coordinated ion fluxes.
- **Electrolocation**: Biological sensory perception of objects using self-generated electric fields.
