# Archerfish Ballistic Water Jet Hydrodynamics

## Overview
Archerfish (*Toxotes*) hunt terrestrial insects perched on overhanging branches by shooting high-velocity water jets from their mouths, actively modulating hydrodynamic slug shape to account for optical Snell's Law refraction.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Snell's Law Optical Compensation: The fish calculates true target position beneath the water surface by compensating for refraction at the air-water interface without moving its eyes above water.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Hydrodynamic Velocity Profiling: By adjusting buccal cavity volume during jet ejection, the fish accelerates the tail of the water jet faster than the head. The trailing water catches up just prior to target impact, creating a massive kinetic drop focused on the insect.

## Key Terms
- **Snell's Law**: Formula describing the refraction angle of light transitioning across refractive media boundaries.
- **Buccal Cavity**: Anatomical mouth cavity utilized to generate hydrodynamic pressure gradients.
- **Water Jet Slug**: A concentrated packet of fluid focused hydrodynamically to deliver maximum kinetic impact.
