# Insects - The Most Successful Animals on Earth

> **The Big Idea:** With over 1 million identified species, insects represent more than half of all known living organisms. Their success stems from small size, exoskeletons, wings, and rapid reproduction.

---

## 🐜 The 3-Part Body Plan

* **Head:** Compound eyes, antennae, and specialized mouthparts.
* **Thorax:** Center of locomotion holding **6 jointed legs** and usually 2 or 4 wings.
* **Abdomen:** Contains digestive, excretory, and reproductive organs.
* **Exoskeleton:** Tough waterproof suit made of **Chitin** protecting against dehydration.
