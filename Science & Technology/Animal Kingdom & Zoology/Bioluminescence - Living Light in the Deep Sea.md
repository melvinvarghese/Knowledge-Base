# Bioluminescence - Living Light in the Deep Sea

> **The Big Idea:** The biochemical production of cold light by living organisms through the enzymatic oxidation of **Luciferin**. Over 75% of deep-sea creatures generate their own light.

---

## ✨ Glowing in the Dark
* **Chemical Reaction:**  
  $$\text{Luciferin} + O_2 + \text{Luciferase enzyme} \longrightarrow \text{Oxyluciferin} + \text{Light}$$
* **Uses:** Counter-illumination camouflage (matching faint sunlight filtering from above), luring prey (anglerfish glowing lantern), and startling predators (flashing squid ink).
