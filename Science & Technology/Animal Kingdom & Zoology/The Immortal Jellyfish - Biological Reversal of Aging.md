# The Immortal Jellyfish - Biological Reversal of Aging

> **The Big Idea:** Turritopsis dohrnii, a tiny hydrozoan capable of completely resetting its developmental clock from mature medusa back to juvenile polyp.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
Turritopsis dohrnii, a tiny hydrozoan capable of completely resetting its developmental clock from mature medusa back to juvenile polyp.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** When subjected to physical stress, starvation, or old age, the jellyfish dissolves its umbrella and tentacles. Specialized somatic cells undergo transdifferentiation—reprogramming their gene expression from differentiated muscle or nerve cells back into pluripotency—sprouting a new genetically identical polyp colony.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Animal Kingdom & Zoology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Adult Medusa Under Stress -> Cellular Transdifferentiation -> Stolon Matrix -> Juvenile Polyp Colony
```
