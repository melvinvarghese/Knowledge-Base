# The Axolotl - Flawless Organ and Tissue Regeneration

> **The Big Idea:** A neotenic Mexican salamander that can regrow perfectly functional amputated limbs, hearts, spinal cords, and brain tissue without scars.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
A neotenic Mexican salamander that can regrow perfectly functional amputated limbs, hearts, spinal cords, and brain tissue without scars.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Upon injury, wound epidermis cells migrate over the stump to form an apical epithelial cap. Underlying mature tissues dedifferentiate into progenitor cells, forming a proliferative blastema that follows developmental patterning cues to reconstruct bones, muscles, and nerves identical to the original.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Animal Kingdom & Zoology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Wound Epidermis Formation -> Blastema Cell Proliferation -> Embryonic Morphogen Patterning -> Full Limb
```
