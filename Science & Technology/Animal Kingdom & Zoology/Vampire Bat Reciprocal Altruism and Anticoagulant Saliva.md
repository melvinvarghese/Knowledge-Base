# Vampire Bat Reciprocal Altruism and Anticoagulant Saliva

## Overview
Common Vampire Bats (*Desmodus rotundus*) survive exclusively on blood (obligate hematophagy), exhibiting social reciprocal altruism through regurgitated blood-sharing and anticoagulant saliva.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Draculin Anticoagulant Saliva: Bat saliva contains draculin, a glycoprotein inhibitor that selectively disables activated coagulation factor X (FXa) and factor IX (FIXa), keeping prey bite wounds bleeding freely.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Reciprocal Altruism Dynamics: Bats starve after 60 hours without blood; successful roost mates regurgitate blood to nourish starving unrelated peers, expecting future reciprocation under strict social grooming tracking.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Medical stroke clot-busting pharmaceuticals (Desmoteplase), mathematical modeling of evolutionary cooperation and game theory.

---

## Key Terms
- **Draculin**: Potent anticoagulant peptide in vampire bat saliva inhibiting blood clotting factors.
- **Hematophagy**: Practice of consuming blood as a primary nutritional source.
- **Reciprocal Altruism**: Behavior whereby an organism provides benefits to another, expecting future returned assistance.
