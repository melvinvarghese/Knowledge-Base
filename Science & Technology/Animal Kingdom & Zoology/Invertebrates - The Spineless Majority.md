# Invertebrates - The Spineless Majority

> **The Big Idea:** Over 95% of all animal species on Earth lack a backbone. From microscopic tardigrades to colossal squids, invertebrates dominate every ecosystem on the globe.

---

## 🪱 Major Phyla

* **Porifera:** Sponges with no tissues or organs.
* **Cnidaria:** Jellyfish and corals with stinging cells (nematocysts).
* **Mollusca:** Snails, clams, octopuses (soft bodies, often hard shells).
* **Arthropoda:** Insects, crabs, spiders (jointed legs and hard exoskeletons).
* **Echinodermata:** Starfish and sea urchins with radial symmetry.
