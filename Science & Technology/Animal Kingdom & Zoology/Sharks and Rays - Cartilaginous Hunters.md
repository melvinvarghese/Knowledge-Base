# Sharks and Rays - Cartilaginous Hunters

> **The Big Idea:** Chondrichthyes (cartilaginous fish) have ruled the oceans for over 400 million years—older than trees and dinosaurs. Their skeletons are made of flexible cartilage instead of bone.

---

## 🦈 Apex Evolution

* **No True Bones:** Lightweight cartilage skeleton provides agility and speed.
* **Ampullae of Lorenzini:** Electro-receptor pores on the snout that detect the heartbeat and muscle contractions of hidden prey buried under sand.
* **Endless Teeth:** Sharks possess multiple conveyor-belt rows of teeth, shedding and replacing up to 30,000 teeth in a lifetime.
