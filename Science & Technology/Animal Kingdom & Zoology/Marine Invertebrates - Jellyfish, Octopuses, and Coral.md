# Marine Invertebrates - Jellyfish, Octopuses, and Coral

> **The Big Idea:** The oceans harbor astonishing invertebrate diversity: jellyfish with no brain or heart, corals that build underwater cities, and octopuses with decentralized distributed intelligence.

---

## 🐙 Ocean Geniuses and Wonders

* **Octopuses:** Two-thirds of an octopus's neurons are distributed in its eight arms. They can solve puzzles, open jars, and camouflage color and texture in milliseconds.
* **Jellyfish:** 95% water; drift with currents and fire venomous microscopic harpoons (nematocysts) faster than a speeding bullet.
* **Corals:** Tiny polyps that build limestone skeletons; partner with photosynthetic algae (Zooxanthellae) to build coral reef ecosystems.
