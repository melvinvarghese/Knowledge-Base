# Venomous Snakes - Viper and Elapid Delivery Systems

> **The Big Idea:** Evolutionary chemical weaponry: highly modified saliva injected through hypodermic fangs to immobilize prey and initiate internal digestion before ingestion.

---

## 🐍 The 2 Delivery Systems
* **Vipers (Rattlesnakes, Adders):** Long, hollow, folding fangs that swing out like switchblades; primarily deliver **Hemotoxic venom** that destroys tissue, melts blood vessels, and causes internal hemorrhaging.
* **Elapids (Cobras, Mambas, Coral Snakes):** Short, fixed hollow fangs; deliver fast-acting **Neurotoxic venom** that blocks acetylcholine nerve receptors, causing suffocation and paralysis in minutes.
