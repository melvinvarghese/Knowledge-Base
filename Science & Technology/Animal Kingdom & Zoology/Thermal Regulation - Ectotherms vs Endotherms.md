# Thermal Regulation - Ectotherms vs Endotherms

> **The Big Idea:** How animals manage core body temperatures through metabolic heat generation (endothermy) or environmental absorption (ectothermy).

---

## 1. What It Is
How animals manage core body temperatures through metabolic heat generation (endothermy) or environmental absorption (ectothermy).

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Endotherms (mammals, birds) burn metabolic fuel for constant optimal temperature; ectotherms (reptiles, fish) conserve energy through behavioral basking.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Basking Ectotherm (Low Caloric Need) vs Internal Combustion Endotherm (High Caloric Need)
```
