# Reptiles - Cold-Blooded Masters

> **The Big Idea:** Reptiles conquered dry land through two revolutionary evolutionary inventions: waterproof scaly skin to prevent dehydration, and shelled amniotic eggs that don't need water.

---

## 🦎 Conquering the Dry Land

* **Ectothermic ("Cold-Blooded"):** Rely on external heat sources (basking in the sun) to regulate body temperature, requiring 90% less food than warm-blooded mammals.
* **The Amniotic Egg:** A private pond inside a shell, allowing embryo development far away from lakes or rivers.
* **Major Groups:** Snakes and lizards (Squamata), turtles and tortoises (Testudines), and crocodilians.
