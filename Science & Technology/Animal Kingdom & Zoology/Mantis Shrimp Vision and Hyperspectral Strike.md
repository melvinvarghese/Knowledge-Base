# Mantis Shrimp Vision and Hyperspectral Strike

## Overview
Mantis Shrimps (Stomatopoda) possess the most complex visual system in the animal kingdom with 16 photoreceptor types and dactyl clubs executing hypervelocity strikes that induce acoustic cavitation.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Hyperspectral and Polarized Vision: Compound apposition eyes with a 6-row midband, perceiving ultraviolet, visible, and both linear and circular polarization states independently with trinocular depth perception.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Dactyl Strike Biomechanics: Saddle spring-latch mechanism accelerates dactyl clubs at 10,000 g to speeds of 23 m/s, striking prey with 1,500 N force and forming collapsing cavitation bubbles emitting 5,000 K flashes (sonoluminescence).

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Biomimetic impact-resistant composite body armor, non-invasive early-stage cancer polarization imaging sensors.

---

## Key Terms
- **Stomatopod**: Marine crustacean belonging to the order Stomatopoda equipped with raptorial appendages.
- **Cavitation**: Formation and violent implosion of vapor cavities in a liquid due to rapid pressure drops.
- **Circular Polarization**: Electromagnetic wave state where the electric field vector rotates in a circle.
