# Coral Reef Architecture and Symbiosis

> **The Big Idea:** Massive limestone structures built over millennia by colonies of tiny soft-bodied coral polyps secreting calcium carbonate skeletons.

## 1. What It Is
Massive limestone structures built over millennia by colonies of tiny soft-bodied coral polyps secreting calcium carbonate skeletons.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Corals depend upon photosynthetic endosymbiotic dinoflagellates (zooxanthellae) for up to 90% of their metabolic energy.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Coral Polyp CaCO3 Skeleton <--> Zooxanthellae Algal Photosynthesis & Sugar Exchange
```
