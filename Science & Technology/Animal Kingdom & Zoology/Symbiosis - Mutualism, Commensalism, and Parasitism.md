# Symbiosis - Mutualism, Commensalism, and Parasitism

> **The Big Idea:** Symbiosis describes close, persistent biological interactions between different species: win-win partnerships, one-sided benefits, and harmful exploitation.

---

## 🤝 The 3 Types of Relationships

* **Mutualism (+ / +):** Both species benefit (Clownfish protected by Sea Anemone tentacles while cleaning it; Bees pollinating flowers for nectar).
* **Commensalism (+ / 0):** One benefits, the other is unharmed (Barnacles hitching a ride on a whale's skin).
* **Parasitism (+ / -):** One benefits by harming the host (Tapeworms, ticks, malaria parasites).
