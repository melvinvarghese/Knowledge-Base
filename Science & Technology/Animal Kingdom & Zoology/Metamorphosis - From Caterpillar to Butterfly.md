# Metamorphosis - From Caterpillar to Butterfly

> **The Big Idea:** Complete metamorphosis is one of biology's most radical transformations: an insect completely dissolves its larval body into cellular soup inside a chrysalis to rebuild an adult.

---

## 🦋 Complete vs. Incomplete Metamorphosis

* **Complete (Holometabolous - 85% of insects):**  
  $$\text{Egg} \longrightarrow \text{Larva (Caterpillar/Maggot)} \longrightarrow \text{Pupa (Chrysalis)} \longrightarrow \text{Adult}$$
  Inside the chrysalis, larval tissues break down into cellular broth, and **Imaginal Discs** assemble wings, eyes, and long legs.
* **Incomplete (Hemimetabolous):**  
  $$\text{Egg} \longrightarrow \text{Nymph (Miniature adult)} \longrightarrow \text{Adult (Grasshoppers, Roaches)}$$
