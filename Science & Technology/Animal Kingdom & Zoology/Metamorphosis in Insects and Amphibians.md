# Metamorphosis in Insects and Amphibians

> **The Big Idea:** The dramatic biological transformation from juvenile larva (caterpillar, tadpole) to adult imago (butterfly, frog) via programmed cell remodeling.

---

## 1. What It Is
The dramatic biological transformation from juvenile larva (caterpillar, tadpole) to adult imago (butterfly, frog) via programmed cell remodeling.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** In holometabolous insects, juvenile tissues dissolve into imaginal discs inside the pupa, orchestrated by juvenile hormone and ecdysone.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Egg -> Larva (Feeding Machine) -> Pupa / Chrysalis (Tissue Dissolution) -> Adult Imago
```
