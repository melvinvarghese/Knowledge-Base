# Spiders and Arachnids

> **The Big Idea:** Arachnids are 8-legged predatory arthropods. Spiders produce silk stronger than steel by weight to engineer webs, egg sacs, and draglines.

---

## 🕷️ Spiders vs. Insects

* **Body Plan:** 2 segments (Cephalothorax and Abdomen), **8 legs**, zero wings, and zero antennae.
* **Spider Silk:** Protein fiber spun through spinneret glands. Incredibly tensile, elastic, and lightweight.
* **Venom:** Almost all spiders use hollow fangs (chelicerae) to inject neurotoxic or cytotoxic venom that liquefies prey organs for external drinking.
