# Animal Migration - Monarch Butterflies and Arctic Terns

> **The Big Idea:** Epic seasonal journeys across thousands of kilometers driven by food scarcity, temperature, and breeding. Guided by magnetic compasses, celestial navigation, and scent.

---

## 🧭 Global Travelers
* **Arctic Tern:** The champion migrant: flies from the Arctic to Antarctica and back every year (over 70,000 km annually), seeing more daylight than any creature on Earth.
* **Monarch Butterfly:** A multi-generational relay: four successive generations take turns migrating 4,500 km from Canada to Mexican mountain fir forests without having ever been there before!
* **Magnetoreception:** Birds utilize quantum cryptochrome proteins in their retinas to literally 'see' Earth's magnetic field lines.
