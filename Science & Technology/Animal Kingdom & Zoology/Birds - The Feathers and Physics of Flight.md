# Birds - The Feathers and Physics of Flight

> **The Big Idea:** Direct descendants of theropod dinosaurs, birds conquered the air with hollow honeycomb bones, powerful four-chambered hearts, high-efficiency air sacs, and aerodynamic feathers.

---

## 🦅 The Mechanics of Flight

* **Aerofoil Wings:** Curved upper wing surface forces air to move faster over the top, creating low pressure and generating **Lift** (Bernoulli's principle).
* **Hollow Bones:** Pneumatic bones reinforced with internal struts make skeletons feather-light yet incredibly strong.
* **Continuous Air Flow:** Birds have specialized air sacs ensuring lungs receive fresh oxygen during both inhalation and exhalation!
