# Naked Mole-Rat Longevity and Cancer Resistance

## Overview
The Naked Mole-Rat (*Heterocephalus glaber*) is a subterranean, eusocial rodent with an extraordinary lifespan exceeding 30 years (10x expected for rodent body size), exhibiting near-complete resistance to cancer.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
High Molecular Mass Hyaluronan (HMM-HA): Secretes uniquely long-chain hyaluronan chains (~6 MDa) that accumulate in extracellular matrix, signaling early contact inhibition via the CD44 receptor and arresting tumor cell proliferation.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Protein Translation Fidelity and Hypoxia Tolerance: Possesses ultra-accurate ribosomal translation mechanisms, high proteasome activity resisting oxidative protein damage, and the capacity to survive 18 minutes of complete anoxia by metabolizing fructose.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Human cancer prevention therapies, anti-aging cellular senescence interventions, and stroke ischemia protection.

---

## Key Terms
- **Hyaluronan (HA)**: High-molecular-weight glycosaminoglycan abundant in extracellular matrices.
- **Contact Inhibition**: Regulatory mechanism halting cell division when cells contact neighboring boundaries.
- **Eusociality**: Complex social structure characterized by cooperative brood care, overlapping generations, and reproductive caste division.
