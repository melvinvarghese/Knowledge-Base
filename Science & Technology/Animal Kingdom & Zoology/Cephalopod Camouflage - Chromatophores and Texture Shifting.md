# Cephalopod Camouflage - Chromatophores and Texture Shifting

> **The Big Idea:** The ability of cuttlefish and octopuses to change skin color, polarization, and 3D surface texture in less than 200 milliseconds.

---

## 1. What It Is
The ability of cuttlefish and octopuses to change skin color, polarization, and 3D surface texture in less than 200 milliseconds.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Driven by muscular expansion of pigment sacs (chromatophores), reflective iridophores, and leucophores controlled directly by motor neurons.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of animal kingdom & zoology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
