# Cephalopods - Octopuses, Squids, and Cuttlefish

> **The Big Idea:** The intellectual marvels of the ocean. Mollusks that abandoned heavy shells for jet propulsion, three hearts, blue blood, and decentralized problem-solving intelligence.

---

## 🐙 Distributed Cognition
* **Chromatophores:** Elastic skin pigment sacs that expand and contract in milliseconds, allowing cephalopods to mimic rocks, coral, or flashing warning displays.
* **3 Hearts & Blue Blood:** Two branchial hearts pump blood to the gills; one systemic heart pumps to the body. Blood uses copper-based **Hemocyanin**, turning it blue.
* **Giant Squid:** Possesses the largest eyes in the animal kingdom (size of dinner plates) to spot glowing sperm whales in the deep abyss.
