# Mammals - Warm Blood, Fur, and Milk

> **The Big Idea:** Mammals are warm-blooded vertebrates defined by hair or fur, specialized teeth, and mammary glands that produce milk to nurse their young.

---

## 🐾 The 3 Mammalian Groups

* **Monotremes (Egg-laying):** Platypus and echidna (lay eggs but nurse hatched young with milk).
* **Marsupials (Pouches):** Kangaroos and koalas (give birth to tiny embryos that climb into a pouch to finish development).
* **Placental Mammals (95% of mammals):** Whales, humans, dogs, mice (nourish fetus inside the uterus via a placenta).
