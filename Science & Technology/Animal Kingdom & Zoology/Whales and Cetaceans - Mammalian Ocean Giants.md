# Whales and Cetaceans - Mammalian Ocean Giants

> **The Big Idea:** Terrestrial mammals that returned to the sea 50 million years ago. The Blue Whale is the largest animal ever known to have lived, weighing up to 200 tons.

---

## 🐋 Baleen vs. Toothed Whales
* **Baleen Whales (Mysticetes):** Blue, Humpback, Fin whales; filter millions of gallons of water through keratin baleen plates to trap swarms of krill.
* **Toothed Whales (Odontocetes):** Sperm whales, Orcas, Dolphins; active hunters that dive over 2,000 meters down into freezing trenches to battle giant squid.
* **Vestigial Pelvis:** Inside whale blubber lie tiny, detached pelvic hip bones—ghostly reminders of their ancient four-legged walking ancestors (*Pakicetus*).
