## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
An snapping shrimp with an asymmetrical claw that generates a high-velocity water jet, creating a 210-decibel acoustic shockwave.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Snapping the claw shut at over $100\text{ km/h}$ shoots a high-speed water jet. The sheer velocity creates a transient low-pressure cavitation bubble that collapses with a sound louder than a jet engine, stunning small fish and crabs instantly.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Animal Kingdom & Zoology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
