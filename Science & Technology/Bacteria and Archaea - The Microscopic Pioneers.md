# Bacteria and Archaea - The Microscopic Pioneers

> **The Big Idea:** The earliest and most numerous organisms on the planet. Simple single-celled prokaryotes that drive global chemical cycles, digestion, and biotechnology.

---

## 🦠 Tiny Masters of the Planet

* **Prokaryotic Design:** No membrane-bound nucleus or organelles; loose circular DNA plasmids.
* **Reproduction:** Rapid asexual binary fission (under ideal conditions, bacteria can double every 20 minutes).
* **Beneficial Roles:** Digesting food in guts, fermenting yogurt/cheese, decomposing waste, and producing lifesaving antibiotics.
