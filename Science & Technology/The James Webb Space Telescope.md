# The James Webb Space Telescope

> **The Big Idea:** NASA's premier infrared observatory stationed 1.5 million kilometers from Earth at Lagrange Point 2. Its 6.5-meter gold-coated beryllium mirror peers back in time to the first stars.

---

## 🛰️ Engineering Marvel

* **Infrared Vision:** Unlike Hubble (visible light), JWST sees infrared light, allowing it to penetrate cosmic dust clouds and spot ancient light stretched by cosmic expansion.
* **The Sunshield:** A 5-layer tennis-court-sized sunshield keeping instruments cooled below -233°C.
* **Mission Goals:** Spot the first galaxies formed after the Big Bang, study exoplanet atmospheres for bio-signatures, and observe star formation.
