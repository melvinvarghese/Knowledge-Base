# The Skeletal System - Bones and Joints

> **The Big Idea:** The human skeleton is an internal architectural framework of 206 bones that protects vital organs, anchors muscles, manufactures red blood cells, and stores calcium.

---

## 🦴 Living Bone Architecture

* **Living Tissue:** Bones are not dry sticks; they are rich in blood vessels, nerves, and collagen fibers.
* **Bone Marrow:** The spongy core of long bones that produces millions of new red and white blood cells every second.
* **Joint Types:** Hinge joints (knees, elbows), Ball-and-Socket joints (shoulders, hips with 360° rotation), and Fixed sutures (skull bones).
