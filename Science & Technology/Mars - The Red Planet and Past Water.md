# Mars - The Red Planet and Past Water

> **The Big Idea:** The fourth planet from the Sun. Once warm, wet, and active, Mars lost its protective magnetic field to solar wind, leaving behind a frozen, oxidized red desert with signs of ancient rivers.

---

## 🔴 The Red Planet's Anatomy

* **Why is it Red?** Its soil is rich in iron oxide (rust).
* **Giant Geological Wonders:**
  * **Olympus Mons:** The largest volcano in the Solar System (22 km high, 3x Everest).
  * **Valles Marineris:** A canyon system 4,000 km long and 7 km deep (dwarfing the Grand Canyon).
* **The Search for Water and Life:** Rovers (Curiosity, Perseverance) have proven ancient lakebeds, dried river deltas, and subsurface water ice.
