# Coasts and Wave Dynamics

> **The Big Idea:** Coastlines are the ever-changing battleground between ocean hydraulic force and continental rock, shaped by longshore drift, sea cliffs, arches, and barrier islands.

---

## 🏖️ Coastal Landforms

* **Headland Erosion:** Waves carve sea caves into headlands $\rightarrow$ caves break through to form **Arches** $\rightarrow$ arches collapse into isolated **Sea Stacks**.
* **Longshore Drift:** Waves hitting beaches at an angle push sand zigzagging down the coast, constructing spits and barrier beaches.
