# Flowering Plants and Pollination

> **The Big Idea:** Angiosperms (flowering plants) revolutionized the planet 130 million years ago by partnering with insects, birds, and wind to achieve cross-pollination and seed dispersal.

---

## 🌺 Anatomy of a Flower

* **Male Organs (Stamen):** Anther produces pollen grains; filament holds it up.
* **Female Organs (Pistil/Carpel):** Sticky Stigma catches pollen, Style carries it down to the Ovary where seeds develop.
* **Mutualistic Co-Evolution:** Flowers evolved bright colors, nectar rewards, and sweet aromas to entice bees, bats, and butterflies to carry pollen from flower to flower.
