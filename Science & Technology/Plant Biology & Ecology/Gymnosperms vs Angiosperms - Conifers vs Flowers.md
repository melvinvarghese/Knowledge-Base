# Gymnosperms vs Angiosperms - Conifers vs Flowers

> **The Big Idea:** The two great divisions of seed plants: primitive Gymnosperms (naked seeds in pinecones) and evolutionary dominant Angiosperms (seeds enclosed in flowering fruits).

---

## 🌲 Conifers vs. Flowers
* **Gymnosperms (Pine, Fir, Spruce, Ginkgo):** Evolved 300 million years ago; depend on wind to blow pollen between cones; needle leaves resist freezing and moisture loss.
* **Angiosperms (Flowering Plants):** Evolved 130 million years ago; wrap seeds inside delicious fruits to trick animals into transporting and fertilizing them.
