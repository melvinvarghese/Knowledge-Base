# Peat Bogs and Carbon Sequestration

> **The Big Idea:** Waterlogged wetland ecosystems where incomplete decomposition of dead sphagnum moss builds up meters-thick organic peat layers over millennia.

## 1. What It Is
Waterlogged wetland ecosystems where incomplete decomposition of dead sphagnum moss builds up meters-thick organic peat layers over millennia.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Peatlands cover only 3% of Earth's land surface yet store twice as much carbon as all the world's forests combined.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Waterlogged Acidic Bog -> Anaerobic Inhibition of Bacteria -> Unbroken Peat Carbon Sinks
```
