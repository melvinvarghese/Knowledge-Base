# C3, C4, and CAM Photosynthesis - Surviving Arid Climates

> **The Big Idea:** Three distinct metabolic photosynthetic pathways evolved to fix carbon under varying temperatures, moisture levels, and sunlight intensities.

---

## 1. What It Is
Three distinct metabolic photosynthetic pathways evolved to fix carbon under varying temperatures, moisture levels, and sunlight intensities.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** C4 plants (corn, sugarcane) spatially separate carbon fixation to avoid wasteful photorespiration; CAM plants (cacti) open stomata only at night.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
C3 (Standard Temperate) | C4 (Spatial Separation) | CAM (Temporal Night Fixation)
```
