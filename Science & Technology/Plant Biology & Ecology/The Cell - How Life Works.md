# The Cell - How Life Works

> **The Big Idea:** The cell is the fundamental unit of all living things. Every human body is a microscopic metropolis of **37 trillion cells**, each operating like a miniature automated factory with specialized machinery, power plants, and instruction manuals.

---

## 🔬 The 2 Types of Cells

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│          PROKARYOTIC CELLS           │       │          EUKARYOTIC CELLS            │
│         (Bacteria & Archaea)         │       │      (Animals, Plants, Fungi)        │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤       ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Simple, tiny (1-5 µm).               │  vs.  │ Complex, large (10-100 µm).          │
│ No nucleus; loose circular DNA.      │       │ DNA protected inside a Nucleus.      │
│ No membrane-bound organelles.        │       │ Packed with specialized organelles.  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 🏭 Inside an Animal Cell: The City Analogy

| Organelle | City / Factory Role | Biological Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Nucleus** | City Hall & Blueprint Vault | Houses **DNA**, the master code that guides protein production. |
| **Mitochondria** | Power Plants | Converts glucose and oxygen into **ATP** (cellular energy currency). |
| **Ribosomes** | Assembly Line Workers | Reads RNA copies of DNA and builds physical proteins. |
| **Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)** | Highway & Manufacturing | Modifies and transports newly synthesized proteins and lipids. |
| **Golgi Apparatus** | Post Office / FedEx | Packages, sorts, and ships proteins inside or outside the cell. |
| **Lysosomes** | Waste Disposal / Recycling | Digestive enzymes break down cell debris and invaders. |
| **Cell Membrane** | Security Gate & Border Wall | Lipid bilayer regulating what enters and exits the cell. |

## 🌱 The 3 Extra Features of Plant Cells

Plant cells have three unique parts animal cells lack:
1. **Chloroplasts:** Solar power stations containing green chlorophyll to perform **Photosynthesis** (converting sunlight, $CO_2$, and water into sugar and oxygen).
2. **Cell Wall:** Rigid cellulose outer frame that gives trees and stems strength to stand upright without bones.
3. **Large Central Vacuole:** Giant water storage balloon that creates internal turgor pressure; when dry, plants wilt.

---

## 🧬 DNA & Cell Division (Mitosis)

* **DNA:** The double helix made of four chemical letters (A, T, C, G) that contains the code for every protein in your body.
* **Mitosis:** How one cell duplicates its DNA and splits into two identical twin cells for growth and tissue repair (healing a papercut).
