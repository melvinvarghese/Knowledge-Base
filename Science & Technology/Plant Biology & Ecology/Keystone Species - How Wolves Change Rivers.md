# Keystone Species - How Wolves Change Rivers

> **The Big Idea:** A species that exerts a disproportionately massive influence on the balance of its entire ecosystem relative to its abundance. Removing it causes the ecosystem to unravel.

---

## 🐺 Trophic Cascades in Yellowstone
* **The Reintroduction of Wolves (1995):** When wolves were reintroduced to Yellowstone National Park, they hunted overpopulated deer and elk.
* **The River Transformation:** Elk stopped loitering in open river valleys; willow and aspen saplings grew into forests; beavers returned to build dams; stabilizing plant roots stopped riverbank erosion and literally stabilized the flow of rivers!
