## 1. What It Is
Mutualistic underground fungal hyphae networks that envelop plant roots, vastly extending their water and phosphorus absorption capacity.

- **Core Mechanism:** Plants trade up to 20% of their photosynthetic sugars in exchange for soil minerals and biochemical alarm signals transmitted across entire forest canopies.

Tree Photosynthetic Sugars <==> Mycorrhizal Hyphae Mineral Nutrients & Water
