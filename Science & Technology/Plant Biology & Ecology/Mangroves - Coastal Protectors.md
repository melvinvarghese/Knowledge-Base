# Mangroves - Coastal Protectors

> **The Big Idea:** Tough, salt-tolerant trees that thrive in intertidal marine mudflats. Their tangled aerial root systems act as vital marine nurseries and natural storm-surge sea walls.

---

## 🦀 Salt Survival Mechanics
* **Salt Filtration:** Mangrove roots filter out 90% of ocean salt; excess salt is pushed into 'sacrificial leaves' that turn yellow and fall off.
* **Pneumatophores (Snorkel Roots):** Pencil-like roots poke vertically above suffocating intertidal mud at low tide to breathe atmospheric oxygen.
* **Erosion Defense:** Dense mangrove forests absorb up to 66% of wave energy, protecting coastal villages from tsunami and hurricane impacts.
