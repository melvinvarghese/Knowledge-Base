# Seed Dispersal Mechanisms - Wind, Water, and Animals

> **The Big Idea:** Plants cannot walk, so they evolved brilliant mechanical and biological mechanisms to scatter their offspring far away from parent shade and competition.

---

## 🌰 Strategies for Travel
* **Wind (Anemochory):** Parachutes (Dandelions), helicopter wings (Maple samaras).
* **Water (Hydrochory):** Floating, fibrous waterproof husks (Coconuts drift across entire oceans).
* **Animals (Zoochory):** Edible sweet fleshy fruits eaten by birds; or Velcro-like hooks that hitchhike on animal fur (Burdock seeds, which inspired George de Mestral to invent Velcro!).
* **Ballistic Explosions:** Squirting cucumbers and touch-me-nots build hydraulic turgor pressure to shoot seeds meters away.
