## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Venus flytraps utilize sensitive trigger hairs that fire electrical action potentials, closing traps within a tenth of a second when double-stimulated.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Two Hair Triggers Fired -> Bioelectric Action Potential -> Leaf Cell Expansion -> Trap Snaps
```
