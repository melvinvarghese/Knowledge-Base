# Ecological Succession - Primary vs Secondary

> **The Big Idea:** The predictable process of gradual ecological repair and community turnover following an ecosystem disturbance, culminating in a stable climax community.

---

## 🌲 How Ecosystems Rebuild
* **Primary Succession:** Begins on barren sterile rock with zero soil (after a volcanic lava eruption or retreating glacier). Pioneer species (lichens, mosses) acidify and break down rock into the first thin layer of dirt.
* **Secondary Succession:** Follows a disturbance where soil remains intact (after a forest fire or abandoned farmland). Fast-growing weeds and grasses sprout first, followed by shrubs, pine trees, and finally mature hardwood oaks.
