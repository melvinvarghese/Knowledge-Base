# The Cohesion-Tension Theory of Water Transport

> **The Big Idea:** How giant redwoods pull hundreds of gallons of water over 100 meters high without a mechanical mechanical pump.

## 1. What It Is
How giant redwoods pull hundreds of gallons of water over 100 meters high without a mechanical mechanical pump.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Water molecules form a continuous cohesive chain bonded by hydrogen bonds, pulled upward by negative suction tension created as leaf stomata transpire.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Leaf Stomata Evaporation -> Negative Meniscus Tension -> Cohesive Hydrogen Chain -> Root Uptake
```
