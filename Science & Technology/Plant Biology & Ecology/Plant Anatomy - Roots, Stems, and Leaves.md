# Plant Anatomy - Roots, Stems, and Leaves

> **The Big Idea:** Plants are precision hydraulic systems designed to harvest sunlight and soil minerals through specialized tissues: Xylem pipelines, Phloem food delivery, and Stomata pores.

---

## 🌿 The Internal Plumbing

* **Roots:** Anchor the plant, store starch, and absorb water and minerals through fine root hairs.
* **Xylem:** Tubes of dead cells that pull water and minerals UP from roots to leaves using capillary suction.
* **Phloem:** Living vascular highway that transports sugary sap DOWN from photosynthetic leaves to the rest of the plant.
* **Stomata:** Microscopic pores on leaf undersides that open to take in $CO_2$ and close to prevent water loss.
