# Deciduous vs Evergreen Forests

> **The Big Idea:** Two contrasting botanical survival strategies for harsh winters: dropping thin, high-surface leaves to save water, or protecting waxy needle foliage all year round.

---

## 🍂 Why Leaves Change Color in Autumn
* **Chlorophyll Breakdown:** In autumn, declining daylight signals deciduous trees to stop producing green chlorophyll and absorb its nutrients back into trunk storage.
* **Underlying Pigments:** With green gone, underlying **Carotenoids** (yellow/orange) and newly produced **Anthocyanins** (crimson red) are finally unmasked!
