# The Six Kingdoms of Life

> **The Big Idea:** Biologists classify all organisms into six broad kingdoms based on cell structure, how they obtain nutrition, and their evolutionary history.

---

## 👑 The 6 Kingdoms

1. **Archaebacteria:** Ancient, single-celled extremophiles without nuclei.
2. **Eubacteria:** True bacteria (E. coli, Cyanobacteria).
3. **Protista:** Diverse single-celled or simple multicellular eukaryotes (Amoebas, Algae).
4. **Fungi:** Decomposers with chitin cell walls that absorb nutrients externally (Mushrooms, Molds, Yeasts).
5. **Plantae:** Multicellular autotrophs performing photosynthesis with cellulose cell walls.
6. **Animalia:** Multicellular, mobile heterotrophs that ingest food and lack cell walls.
