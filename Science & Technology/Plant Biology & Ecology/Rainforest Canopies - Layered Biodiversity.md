# Rainforest Canopies - Layered Biodiversity

> **The Big Idea:** Tropical rainforests cover only 6% of Earth's land, but harbor more than 50% of all terrestrial species, organized into four vertical ecological vertical stories.

---

## 🌴 The 4 Forest Stories
* **1. Emergent Layer (50m+):** Giant hardwood trees poking above the canopy, braving intense sun and strong winds (harpy eagles, bats).
* **2. The Canopy (30m):** Dense ceiling of overlapping leaves; holds 90% of all rainforest animal life (monkeys, toucans, tree frogs).
* **3. Understory (20m):** Dark, humid middle zone; broad-leaf shrubs that compete for filtered flecks of sunlight.
* **4. Forest Floor (0m):** Pitch dark (only 2% of sunlight reaches here); armies of termites, fungi, and ants decomposing dead leaf litter within hours.
