# Floral Pigments - Anthocyanins, Carotenoids, and Pollinator Attraction

## Overview
Flowering plants synthesize structural and biochemical floral pigments—primarily anthocyanins, carotenoids, and betalains—to signal and attract specific insect and avian pollinator mutualists.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Anthocyanin pH-Dependent Chromophores: Anthocyanins are water-soluble flavonoid pigments in vacuoles; chemical structural shifts between flavylium cations (red at acidic pH) and quinonoidal bases (blue at alkaline pH) tune flower hues.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Ultraviolet Nectar Guides & Carotenoids: Lipid-soluble carotenoids impart bright yellow and orange colors; many flowers display UV-absorbing flavonoids visible exclusively to bee compound eyes as targeted runway bullseyes.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Natural food colorant extraction, breeding ornamental floriculture varieties, and studying coevolutionary sensory biology.

---

## Key Terms
- **Anthocyanin**: Water-soluble flavonoid plant pigment responsible for red, purple, and blue floral coloration.
- **Carotenoid**: Lipid-soluble tetraterpenoid pigment imparting yellow, orange, and red coloration.
- **Nectar Guide**: Visual floral markings, often UV-reflective, directing pollinators toward reproductive organs.
