# Parasitic Plants - Mistletoe and Rafflesia

> **The Big Idea:** Plants that abandoned honest photosynthesis. Using specialized root suckers (**Haustoria**), they tap into the plumbing of host trees to steal water, minerals, and sugars.

---

## 🌺 Thieves of the Plant World
* **Rafflesia arnoldii (The Monster Flower):** Produces the largest individual flower in the world (up to 1 meter wide). It has zero leaves, stems, or true roots, living entirely inside jungle vines until blooming with the stench of rotting flesh (**Corpse Flower**) to attract carrion flies.
* **Mistletoe:** Hemiparasite growing in tree branches; sticky seeds spread through bird droppings, penetrating tree bark to suck xylem moisture.
