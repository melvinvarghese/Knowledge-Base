# Photosynthesis - How Plants Power Life

> **The Big Idea:** Photosynthesis is the chemical miracle that powers nearly all biological life on Earth. Plants, algae, and cyanobacteria harvest sunlight energy to turn atmospheric carbon dioxide and water into glucose sugar, releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

---

## 🌿 The Chemical Formula of Life

$$\mathbf{6CO_2} \text{ (Carbon Dioxide)} + \mathbf{6H_2O} \text{ (Water)} + \text{Sunlight} \longrightarrow \mathbf{C_6H_{12}O_6} \text{ (Glucose)} + \mathbf{6O_2} \text{ (Oxygen)}$$

```
[ SUNLIGHT ENERGY ] ──► [ CHLOROPLASTS (Chlorophyll pigment) ]
                                    │
               ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
               ▼                                         ▼
   PULLS IN:   $CO_2$ from air via stomata pores         $H_2O$ from soil via roots
                                    │
                                    ▼
   PRODUCES:   GLUCOSE (Stored food energy)   +          OXYGEN (Released into air)
```

---

## 🔬 Inside the Chloroplast: The 2 Stages

Photosynthesis takes place inside microscopic green organelles called **Chloroplasts**:

1. **The Light Reactions (In the Thylakoids):**
   * Green **Chlorophyll** pigments absorb blue and red light waves (reflecting green light, which is why plants look green).
   * Sunlight splits water molecules ($H_2O$), releasing oxygen gas ($O_2$) and charging energy carrier molecules (ATP and NADPH).
2. **The Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent Reactions):**
   * Enzymes capture carbon dioxide from the air and use the charged ATP energy to construct high-energy **Glucose** sugar molecules.

---

## 🌍 Why Earth Would Collapse Without Photosynthesis

* **The Base of Every Food Web:** Herbivores eat plants for glucose energy; carnivores eat herbivores. Virtually every calorie you consume originally came from sunlight captured by a plant.
* **Atmospheric Oxygen:** Before cyanobacteria evolved photosynthesis 2.4 billion years ago (**The Great Oxidation Event**), Earth's air had zero free oxygen. Photosynthesis created the air we breathe and the ozone layer that shields us.
* **Carbon Sink:** Forests and ocean phytoplankton absorb billions of tons of greenhouse carbon dioxide annually, regulating the global climate.
