# Gibberellins, Cytokinins, and Plant Growth Regulation

## Overview
Gibberellins and Cytokinins are complementary plant hormones that regulate seed germination, stem elongation, cellular division, and delaying leaf senescence.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Morphological Principles
Gibberellin Signaling & Seed Germination: Water imbibition triggers gibberellin synthesis in embryos, inducing the aleurone layer to produce $\alpha$-amylase, mobilizing stored endosperm starch into glucose to fuel germination.

---

## 2. Physiological Dynamics & Evolutionary Analysis
Cytokinin Cell Division & Senescence Retardation: Produced in root tips, cytokinins stimulate cell division (cytokinesis) in shoot meristems and counteract auxin to promote lateral bud outgrowth, preserving green chlorophyll in aging leaves.

---

## 3. Ecological Significance & Scientific Applications
Green Revolution semi-dwarf wheat varieties (reduced gibberellin sensitivity preventing lodging), commercial seedless grape berry enlargement.

---

## Key Terms
- **Gibberellin**: Phytohormone promoting seed germination, stem elongation, and flowering.
- **Cytokinin**: Plant hormone stimulating cytokinesis and promoting shoot organogenesis.
- **Aleurone Layer**: Protein-rich outer cell layer of cereal seed endosperms responsive to gibberellins.
