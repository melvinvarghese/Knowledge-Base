# Phototropism, Gravitropism, and Thigmotropism

> **The Big Idea:** Plant directional growth responses to environmental stimuli mediated by asymmetric hormone redistribution across stems and roots.

---

## 1. What It Is
Plant directional growth responses to environmental stimuli mediated by asymmetric hormone redistribution across stems and roots.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Stems grow toward light (positive phototropism) and away from gravity (negative gravitropism); vine tendrils coil around trellises upon touch.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Stimulus Detected -> Auxin Transported to Far Side -> Cell Wall Acidification -> Directed Bending
```
