## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Bees favor blue/yellow ultraviolet-marked flowers; hummingbirds seek tubular unscented red blossoms; nocturnal moths target white perfumed blooms.

```
Flower Visual / Scent Attractant -> Pollen Dusts Animal -> Inter-Plant Cross Pollination
```
