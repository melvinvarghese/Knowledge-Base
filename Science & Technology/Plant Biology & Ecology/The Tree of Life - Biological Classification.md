# The Tree of Life - Biological Classification

> **The Big Idea:** Earth is home to an estimated 8.7 million species. **Taxonomy** is the scientific system pioneered by Carl Linnaeus that catalogs, names, and connects every living creature into a shared, branching evolutionary family tree.

---

## 🌳 The 8 Levels of Taxonomic Hierarchy

You can memorize the hierarchy using the famous mnemonic:  
**"Dear King Philip Came Over For Good Soup"**

```
  D ── DOMAIN     (Broadest: Archaea, Bacteria, Eukarya)
  K ── KINGDOM    (Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protista)
  P ── PHYLUM     (Chordata - Animals with backbones)
  C ── CLASS      (Mammalia - Warm-blooded, fur, milk)
  O ── ORDER      (Primates - Large brains, grasping hands)
  F ── FAMILY     (Hominidae - Great apes)
  G ── GENUS      (Homo - Toolmakers)
  S ── SPECIES    (Sapiens - Wise human)
```

---

## 🧬 The 3 Domains of Life

Every organism on Earth belongs to one of three primary branches:
1. **Archaea:** Ancient single-celled organisms that thrive in extreme environments (boiling volcanic springs, deep-sea hydrothermal vents, salt flats).
2. **Bacteria:** Ubiquitous microscopic single-celled organisms found in soil, water, and human guts.
3. **Eukarya:** All complex life whose cells contain a nucleus and organelles (Animals, Plants, Fungi, and Protists).

---

## 🐾 Binomial Nomenclature: The Universal Naming System

Common names cause mass confusion (e.g., a "mountain lion," "cougar," "puma," and "panther" are all the exact same animal).

Linnaeus introduced **two-word Latin names** used universally by scientists across the globe:
* **Genus** (Capitalized) + **Species** (Lowercase, italicized):
  * Human: *Homo sapiens*
  * Lion: *Panthera leo*
  * Tiger: *Panthera tigris*
  * Wolf: *Canis lupus*
  * Domestic Dog: *Canis lupus familiaris*

---

## 🛠️ Key Takeaway

* The Tree of Life proves that **all living things share a single common ancestor** that lived roughly 3.8 billion years ago (LUCA: Last Universal Common Ancestor). We share roughly 99% of our DNA with chimpanzees, 85% with mice, and even 50% with bananas!
