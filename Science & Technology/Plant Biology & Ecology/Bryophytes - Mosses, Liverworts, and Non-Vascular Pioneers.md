# Bryophytes - Mosses, Liverworts, and Non-Vascular Pioneers

> **The Big Idea:** Primitive non-vascular land plants that lack true roots, xylem, and phloem, absorbing water directly across damp cell walls.

---

## 1. What It Is
Primitive non-vascular land plants that lack true roots, xylem, and phloem, absorbing water directly across damp cell walls.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Dominated by the gametophyte generation, requiring film of water for flagellated sperm to swim to female archegonia for fertilization.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Spores Germinate -> Protonema -> Leafy Gametophyte Carpet -> Water-Assisted Fertilization
```
