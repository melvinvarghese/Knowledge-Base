# Mangrove Ecosystems - Coastal Tidal Guardians

> **The Big Idea:** Salt-tolerant halophytic trees and shrubs that thrive in coastal intertidal zones across tropical and subtropical coastlines.

---

## 1. What It Is
Salt-tolerant halophytic trees and shrubs that thrive in coastal intertidal zones across tropical and subtropical coastlines.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Possess stilt prop roots and vertical breathing roots (pneumatophores) that filter salt, stabilize coastlines, and protect marine nurseries.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Stilt Prop Roots (Intertidal Anchor) + Pneumatophores (Oxygen Air Spikes) + Salt Gland Secretion
```
