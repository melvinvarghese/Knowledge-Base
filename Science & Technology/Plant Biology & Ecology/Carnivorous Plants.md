# Carnivorous Plants

> **The Big Idea:** Plants that evolved to trap and digest insects and small animals to obtain vital nitrogen and phosphorus in acidic, nutrient-poor bogs.

---

## 🪴 Ingenious Traps

* **Snap Traps (Venus Flytrap):** Sensitive trigger hairs snap shut in 1/10th of a second when brushed twice.
* **Pitfall Traps (Pitcher Plants):** Slippery rim and digestive acid pool drown falling insects.
* **Flypaper Traps (Sundews):** Sticky glistening mucilage droplets trap bugs like glue.
