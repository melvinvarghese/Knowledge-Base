# Algae and Phytoplankton - The Ocean's Lungs

> **The Big Idea:** Microscopic photosynthetic drifters in the upper sunlit layers of the ocean. Marine phytoplankton produce **over 50% of the oxygen in Earth's atmosphere**.

---

## 🌊 The Real Producers of Oxygen
* **Diatoms:** Microscopic single-celled algae encased in jewel-like glass shells made of silica ($SiO_2$).
* **The Biological Pump:** When phytoplankton die, their carbon-rich bodies sink to the deep ocean floor, locking millions of tons of atmospheric carbon into sea sediments for millions of years.
* **Harmful Algal Blooms (Red Tides):** Agricultural fertilizer runoff causes explosive algae blooms that consume all dissolved oxygen and release neurotoxins, suffocating fish.
