# Circadian Rhythms and Photoperiodism in Plants

> **The Big Idea:** How plants measure day and night lengths using light-sensitive phytochrome pigments to time seasonal events like flowering and dormancy.

---

## 1. What It Is
How plants measure day and night lengths using light-sensitive phytochrome pigments to time seasonal events like flowering and dormancy.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Short-day (long-night) plants require uninterrupted darkness past a critical threshold to trigger florigen hormone synthesis in leaves.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Phytochrome Red (Pr) <--> Phytochrome Far-Red (Pfr) Light Ratio -> Flowering Triggered
```
