# Phytoplankton - The Oceanic Base of the Biosphere

> **The Big Idea:** Microscopic photosynthetic organisms (diatoms, dinoflagellates, coccolithophores) drifting in the sunlit photic zone of oceans and lakes.

---

## 1. What It Is
Microscopic photosynthetic organisms (diatoms, dinoflagellates, coccolithophores) drifting in the sunlit photic zone of oceans and lakes.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Produce over 50% of Earth's atmospheric oxygen and drive the oceanic biological carbon pump by sequestering carbon to the ocean floor.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of plant biology & ecology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Solar Photic Zone Photosynthesis -> Half of Global Oxygen Produced -> Base of All Marine Food Chains
```
