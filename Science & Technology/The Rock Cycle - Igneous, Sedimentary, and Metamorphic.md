# The Rock Cycle - Igneous, Sedimentary, and Metamorphic

> **The Big Idea:** Earth's crust is in a continuous state of recycling. Over millions of years, heat, pressure, melting, and weathering transform rocks between three fundamental classes.

---

## 🪨 The 3 Rock Families

```
[ MAGMA ] ──(Cooling)──► [ IGNEOUS ROCK ] (Basalt, Granite)
                               │ (Weathering & Erosion)
                               ▼
[ SEDIMENTARY ROCK ] ◄──(Compaction)── [ SEDIMENTS ] (Sandstone, Limestone)
        │ (Heat & Pressure)
        ▼
[ METAMORPHIC ROCK ] (Marble, Slate) ──(Melting)──► [ MAGMA ]
```

* **Igneous:** Cooled from molten magma or lava (Granite, Basalt, Obsidian).
* **Sedimentary:** Built from compressed layers of sand, mud, and ancient shells (Sandstone, Shale, Limestone). Contains all fossils!
* **Metamorphic:** Cooked by intense underground heat and pressure without melting (Limestone becomes Marble; Shale becomes Slate).
