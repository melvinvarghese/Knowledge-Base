# Radioactivity and Nuclear Fission

> **The Big Idea:** Unstable heavy atomic nuclei decay naturally by releasing ionizing radiation. When struck by a neutron, Uranium-235 splits in two (**Nuclear Fission**), releasing staggering energy.

---

## ☢️ Radiation & Fission

* **The 3 Radiation Types:**
  * **Alpha ($\alpha$):** Helium nuclei; blocked by a single sheet of paper.
  * **Beta ($\beta$):** Fast electrons; blocked by thin aluminum foil.
  * **Gamma ($\gamma$):** Energetic photons; requires thick lead or concrete to stop.
* **Fission Chain Reaction:** Splitting one Uranium nucleus releases 2 or 3 free neutrons that trigger neighboring nuclei to split, unleashing exponential energy in nuclear reactors or atomic bombs.
