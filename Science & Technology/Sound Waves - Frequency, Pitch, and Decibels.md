# Sound Waves - Frequency, Pitch, and Decibels

> **The Big Idea:** Sound is a mechanical longitudinal wave of compressed air molecules. Pitch depends on frequency (Hz), while loudness is measured on the logarithmic decibel (dB) scale.

---

## 🔊 Sound Mechanics

* **No Sound in Space:** Sound requires a physical medium (air, water, steel) to vibrate molecules. In the vacuum of space, nobody can hear you scream!
* **Speed of Sound:** Approximately **343 m/s (1,235 km/h)** in dry air; travels 4x faster in water and 15x faster in steel.
* **The Doppler Effect:** Sound waves compress as a source approaches (higher pitch) and stretch as it moves away (lower pitch).
