# Optical Fiber - Communicating with Light

> **The Big Idea:** Ultra-pure strands of flexible glass as thin as human hair that transmit digital data pulses over thousands of kilometers using **Total Internal Reflection** at the speed of light.

---

## 💡 Undersea Light Cables
* **Total Internal Reflection:** Light pulses traveling inside the core hit the glass cladding at shallow angles, reflecting 100% of light back inside without leaking.
* **Undersea Highway:** Over 99% of all intercontinental internet traffic travels through undersea fiber-optic cables resting on ocean floors, carrying terabits of data per second!
