# Renewable Energy - Solar, Wind, and Hydroelectric

> **The Big Idea:** Energy derived from natural resources that replenish faster than they are consumed. Clean alternatives to fossil fuels that produce zero direct greenhouse emissions.

---

## ⚡ Clean Power Technologies

* **Solar Photovoltaics:** Silicon solar cells knock electrons loose from sunlight photons, generating direct electrical current.
* **Wind Turbines:** Kinetic energy of wind turns aerodynamic blades, spinning a generator to produce AC electricity.
* **Hydroelectric Dams:** Gravitational potential energy of dammed water rushes through penstocks to spin massive water turbines.
