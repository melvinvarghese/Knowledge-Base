# Scanning Tunneling Microscopes - Seeing Individual Atoms

> **The Big Idea:** An instrument that visualizes surfaces at the single-atom atomic scale using the quantum mechanical phenomenon of electron tunneling.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
An instrument that visualizes surfaces at the single-atom atomic scale using the quantum mechanical phenomenon of electron tunneling.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** An ultrasharp conductive metal needle with a single atom at its tip is brought within fractions of a nanometer of a sample surface. Applying a bias voltage allows electrons to quantum tunnel across the vacuum gap; piezoelectric positioners maintain constant tunneling current, tracing atomic landscapes.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Inventions & Computing**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Atomic Tip Needle -> Sub-Nanometer Gap -> Quantum Electron Tunneling Current -> Piezoelectric Atom Map
```
