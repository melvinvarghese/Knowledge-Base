# Microprocessors - The Transistor Revolution

> **The Big Idea:** The integration of a computer's central processing unit (CPU) onto a single silicon semiconductor microchip, miniaturizing room-sized mainframes into pockets and wristwatches.

---

## 💻 The Brain on a Chip
* **Intel 4004 (1971):** The world's first single-chip commercial microprocessor, holding 2,300 transistors on a 10-micrometer scale.
* **Modern Processors (Apple M3, Nvidia H100):** Pack over **50 to 90 billion transistors** onto a silicon square the size of a postage stamp, with gate widths measuring just 3 nanometers (the width of a few dozen atoms)!
