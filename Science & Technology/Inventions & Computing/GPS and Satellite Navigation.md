# GPS and Satellite Navigation

> **The Big Idea:** The Global Positioning System: a constellation of 31 satellites orbiting at 20,200 km altitude, broadcasting atomic clock time signals allowing receivers on Earth to triangulate their position within centimeters.

---

## 🛰️ Trilateration & Relativity
* **Trilateration:** A GPS receiver calculates distance from 4 satellites by measuring signal arrival delay; the intersection of the 4 spheres pinpoint latitude, longitude, and elevation.
* **Einstein's Relativity in Your Pocket:** Because satellites move fast (Special Relativity: clocks tick slower) and sit in weaker gravity (General Relativity: clocks tick faster), satellite clocks run **38 microseconds fast per day**. Without Einstein's mathematical corrections, GPS would drift by 10 km every day!
