# The Internet and ARPANET - Packet Switching Networks

> **The Big Idea:** The decentralized global network of computers developed from ARPANET (1969) that routes digital data packets without central points of failure.

---

## 1. What It Is
The decentralized global network of computers developed from ARPANET (1969) that routes digital data packets without central points of failure.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Paul Baran and Donald Davies conceived packet switching, breaking messages into small numbered chunks that navigate multiple paths.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of inventions & computing, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Data Message -> Broken into Numbered IP Packets -> Dynamic Network Routers -> Reassembled
```
