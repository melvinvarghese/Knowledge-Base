# Flight - The Wright Brothers and Aerodynamics

> **The Big Idea:** On December 17, 1903, Orville and Wilbur Wright achieved the first controlled, sustained, powered heavier-than-air human flight at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina.

---

## ✈️ 3-Axis Control System
* **The Real Breakthrough:** Earlier aviation pioneers focused on power; the Wright brothers realized the real secret was **3-Axis Flight Control**:
  * **Roll:** Warping wings (modern Ailerons).
  * **Pitch:** Front elevator flap.
  * **Yaw:** Movable rear rudder.
* In less than 66 years, humanity progressed from the Wright flyer's 12-second hop to landing astronauts on the surface of the Moon!
