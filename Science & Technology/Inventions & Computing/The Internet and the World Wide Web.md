# The Internet and the World Wide Web

> **The Big Idea:** The global network of networks: ARPANET's packet-switching protocols (TCP/IP) merged with Tim Berners-Lee's 1989 World Wide Web protocols (HTTP, HTML) to construct humanity's shared digital mind.

---

## 🌐 Packet Switching & The Web
* **The Internet vs. The Web:**
  * **The Internet:** The physical network hardware (cables, routers, servers).
  * **The Web:** The software layer of hyperlinks, websites, and browsers running on top of the internet.
* **Packet Switching:** Files are sliced into small numbered packets sent through different network routes and reassembled instantly at the destination.
