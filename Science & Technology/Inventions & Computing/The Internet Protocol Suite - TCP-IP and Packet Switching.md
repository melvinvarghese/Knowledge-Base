# The Internet Protocol Suite - TCP-IP and Packet Switching

## Overview
The Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP) is the conceptual communications architecture developed by Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn, enabling heterogeneous computer networks to interconnect into the global Internet.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Packet Switching vs Circuit Switching: Messages are disassembled into small independent packets containing headers and payloads, dynamically routed across distributed nodes and reassembled at the destination.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
The 4-Layer Architectural Model: 1. Application Layer (HTTP, DNS, SSH); 2. Transport Layer (TCP connection-oriented reliability, UDP low-latency datagrams); 3. Internet Layer (IP logical addressing and routing); 4. Link Layer (Ethernet, Wi-Fi).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Global Internet backbone routing, World Wide Web data transmission, voice over IP (VoIP), and streaming video distribution.

---

## Key Terms
- **Packet Switching**: Transmission method grouping data into packets routed independently over shared communication channels.
- **Transmission Control Protocol (TCP)**: Transport protocol providing reliable, ordered, error-checked packet delivery.
- **Internet Protocol (IP)**: Principal communications protocol governing addressing and packet routing across network boundaries.
