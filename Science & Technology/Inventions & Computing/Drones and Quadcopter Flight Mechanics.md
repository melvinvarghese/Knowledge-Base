# Drones and Quadcopter Flight Mechanics

> **The Big Idea:** Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) stabilized in 3D flight through rapid microcontroller adjustment of four opposing electric motor speeds.

---

## 1. What It Is
Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) stabilized in 3D flight through rapid microcontroller adjustment of four opposing electric motor speeds.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Two clockwise and two counter-clockwise propellers balance net gyroscopic torque; varying motor speeds controls pitch, roll, yaw, and altitude.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of inventions & computing, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Opposing Motor Pairs (Cancel Torque) + Gyroscope IMU Feedback -> Millisecond Speed Adjustments
```
