# The Internal Combustion Engine - The Four-Stroke Cycle

> **The Big Idea:** The mechanical powerhouse of modern transportation: burning vaporized gasoline or diesel inside closed cylinders across four rhythmic strokes: **Intake, Compression, Power, Exhaust**.

---

## 🚗 The 4 Strokes: "Suck, Squeeze, Bang, Blow"
1. **Intake:** Piston moves down, sucking fuel-air mist into cylinder.
2. **Compression:** Piston moves up, squeezing mixture into tight space.
3. **Power:** Spark plug fires; exploding gas violently drives piston down.
4. **Exhaust:** Piston rises, sweeping burned exhaust gases out the tailpipe.
