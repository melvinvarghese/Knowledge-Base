# Tokamak Fusion Reactors and Toroidal Magnetic Traps

> **The Big Idea:** Magnetic confinement fusion devices that heat and trap hydrogen isotopes in a doughnut-shaped vacuum vessel to replicate the power of the Sun.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
Magnetic confinement fusion devices that heat and trap hydrogen isotopes in a doughnut-shaped vacuum vessel to replicate the power of the Sun.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** To prevent 150-million-degree plasma from melting the reactor walls, superconducting magnetic coils generate both toroidal and poloidal magnetic fields. The resulting helical magnetic cage keeps charged deuterium and tritium ions circulating in a closed loop long enough for thermonuclear fusion to occur.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Inventions & Computing**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Toroidal Coils + Central Solenoid -> Helical Magnetic Bottle -> 150 Million °C Confined Plasma
```
