# The Steam Engine - Powering the Industrial Revolution

> **The Big Idea:** James Watt's 1776 condenser innovations transformed the steam engine from an inefficient mine pump into a universal mechanical motor, unshackling manufacturing from muscle and watermills.

---

## 🚂 Mechanical Muscle
* **The Thermodynamics:** Burning coal boils water into high-pressure steam, driving pistons that turn circular flywheels and locomotive wheels.
* **Social Transformation:** Enabled deep coal mining, mechanized textile factories, steam locomotive railways, and steamship transatlantic crossings, founding the modern industrial world.
