# The Printing Press - Gutenberg's Information Revolution

> **The Big Idea:** Invented around 1440 by Johannes Gutenberg, movable metal type democratized knowledge, broke monopolies on information, and catalyzed the Renaissance, Reformation, and Scientific Revolution.

---

## 📚 The Medieval Internet
* **Before Gutenberg:** Monks hand-copied books on vellum skins taking months per copy; only royalty and churches could afford libraries.
* **Movable Type:** Durable lead alloy letters arranged in lines and pressed with oil-based ink onto paper could print hundreds of book pages per day.
* **Impact:** Exploding literacy rates, standardized vernacular languages, and rapid dissemination of scientific discoveries across Europe.
