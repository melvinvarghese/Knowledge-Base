## 1. What It Is

- **Core Mechanism:** Vastly more compact and energy-dense than steam engines, making individual passenger cars, commercial trucking, and aviation possible.

```
Intake (Fuel/Air) -> Compression -> Power (Spark Ignition) -> Exhaust (Expels Gasses)
```
