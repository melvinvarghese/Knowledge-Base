# Artificial Intelligence and Computing

> **The Big Idea:** From Alan Turing’s computational theory to modern Deep Learning neural networks. How human beings engineered machines that can process language, recognize patterns, and reason.

---

## 🤖 The Architecture of AI

* **Binary Computation:** All software fundamentally reduces to binary logic gates (AND, OR, NOT) operating on 1s and 0s.
* **Machine Learning:** Instead of writing explicit hand-coded rules, algorithms learn patterns and statistical weights directly from vast datasets.
* **Neural Networks:** Layered mathematical algorithms inspired by biological brain neurons that excel at vision, speech synthesis, and natural language understanding.
