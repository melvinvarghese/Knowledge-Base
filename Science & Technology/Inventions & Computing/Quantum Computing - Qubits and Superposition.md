# Quantum Computing - Qubits and Superposition

> **The Big Idea:** Computing based on quantum mechanics: replacing classical binary bits (0 or 1) with **Qubits** that can exist in superpositions of both 0 and 1 simultaneously, solving complex calculations in seconds that would take supercomputers millennia.

---

## ⚛️ Exponential Quantum Power
* **Superposition & Entanglement:** While $N$ classical bits can represent one of $2^N$ states at a time, $N$ entangled qubits represent all $2^N$ possibilities simultaneously.
* **Target Applications:** Simulating molecular drug designs, optimizing global logistics networks, and breaking legacy RSA encryption codes.
