# Semiconductors and the Microchip Revolution

> **The Big Idea:** Modern digital civilization is built on **Silicon**. Semiconductors can switch between conducting and insulating electricity, enabling billions of microscopic on/off transistors on a microchip.

---

## 💻 The Digital Switch

* **Doping Silicon:** Adding trace impurities (boron or phosphorus) creates P-type (positive holes) or N-type (free electrons) semiconductors.
* **The Transistor:** An electronic switch. Apply a small voltage to the gate, and current flows (a digital '1'); remove it, and current stops (a digital '0').
* **Moore's Law:** The observation that the number of transistors packed onto a microchip doubles roughly every two years.
