## 1. What It Is
Rechargeable electrochemical batteries that revolutionized portable consumer electronics and electric vehicular transportation.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** High energy density, absence of memory effect, and slow self-discharge are achieved by shuttling lithium ions between graphite and cobalt oxide layers.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of inventions & computing, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Discharge: Li+ Ions Flow from Anode to Cathode | Charge: External Voltage Forces Li+ Back
```
