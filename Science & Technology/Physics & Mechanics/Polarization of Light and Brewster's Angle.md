# Polarization of Light and Brewster's Angle

## Overview
Polarization specifies the geometrical orientation of the oscillating electric field vector in a transverse electromagnetic wave.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Brewster's Law & Reflection Polarization: $\tan\theta_B = n_2 / n_1$. When unpolarized light strikes a dielectric interface at Brewster's angle, reflected light is 100% s-polarized.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Linear, Circular, and Elliptical Polarization: Superposition of orthogonal electric field components ($E_x, E_y$) with controlled phase differences ($0, \pi/2$).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Polarized sunglasses blocking reflective road/water glare, liquid crystal display (LCD) pixel gates, and 3D cinema glasses.

---

## Key Terms
- **Brewster's Angle**: Angle of incidence where reflected light is completely linearly polarized.
- **Birefringence**: Optical property where a crystal exhibits two distinct indices of refraction.
- **Malus's Law**: Transmitted intensity law through a polarizer: $I = I_0 \cos^2\theta$.
