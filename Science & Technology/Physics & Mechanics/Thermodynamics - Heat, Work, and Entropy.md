# Thermodynamics - Heat, Work, and Entropy

> **The Big Idea:** The universal laws governing heat and energy. They state that energy can never be created or destroyed, and that the disorder of the universe (**Entropy**) always increases.

---

## 🔥 The Core Laws

1. **First Law (Conservation of Energy):** Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it only changes form (kinetic to thermal, chemical to electrical).
2. **Second Law (Entropy):** In any spontaneous process, the total entropy (disorder) of an isolated system always increases. Heat flows spontaneously from hot to cold, never the reverse.
3. **Third Law (Absolute Zero):** As temperature approaches Absolute Zero (-273.15°C or 0 Kelvin), molecular motion stops completely.
