# Einstein's Theory of Special Relativity

> **The Big Idea:** Albert Einstein’s 1905 breakthrough: the speed of light is the universal cosmic speed limit ($c = 300,000 \text{ km/s}$). As you travel closer to the speed of light, time slows down and mass increases.

---

## 🚀 Relativistic Effects

1. **Time Dilation:** Moving clocks tick slower relative to stationary observers. At 99.9% light speed, one year for an astronaut equals decades for people on Earth!
2. **Length Contraction:** Objects traveling near light speed physically shorten in the direction of motion.
3. **$E = mc^2$:** Mass and energy are equivalent. A tiny pinch of matter contains an enormous amount of stored energy.
