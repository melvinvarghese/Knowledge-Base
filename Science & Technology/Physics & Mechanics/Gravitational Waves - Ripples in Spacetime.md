# Gravitational Waves - Ripples in Spacetime

> **The Big Idea:** Ripples in the fabric of spacetime predicted by Albert Einstein in 1916 and directly detected a century later in 2015 by LIGO when two colliding black holes merged.

---

## 🌊 Cosmic Vibrations
* **The Detection Miracle:** LIGO’s laser interferometers detected a spacetime squeeze smaller than **1/10,000th the width of a proton** across a 4-kilometer laser vacuum arm!
* **A New Sense for Astronomy:** For thousands of years, humans only 'saw' the universe through light photons; gravitational wave detectors allow us to 'hear' colliding black holes and neutron star mergers across the cosmos.
