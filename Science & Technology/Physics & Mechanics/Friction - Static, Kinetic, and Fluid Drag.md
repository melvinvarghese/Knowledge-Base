# Friction - Static, Kinetic, and Fluid Drag

> **The Big Idea:** The resistive force opposing relative motion between two surfaces in contact. Caused by microscopic surface roughness interlocking like tiny mountain ranges.

---

## 🛑 The 3 Friction Regimes
* **Static Friction:** The force you must overcome to get a stationary object moving (always higher than kinetic friction).
* **Kinetic (Sliding) Friction:** The resistance encountered while an object is already sliding.
* **Fluid Drag:** Resistance experienced traveling through air or water (increases with the square of speed, $F_{drag} \propto v^2$).
