# Bernoulli's Principle and Aerodynamic Lift

## Overview
Bernoulli's Principle establishes that an increase in fluid velocity occurs simultaneously with a decrease in static pressure or potential energy, forming the foundation of aerodynamic lift generation.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Energy Conservation in Streamline Flow: $P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho g h = \text{constant}$. High speed airflow corresponds to reduced static normal force on adjacent surfaces.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Airfoil Upper Camber Acceleration & Downwash: Accelerated flow over the convex surface creates a strong suction pressure field, while downward momentum deflection generates an upward Newtonian reaction force ($F = dp/dt$).

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Aviation wing design, motorsport downforce underbodies, carburetors, and industrial aspirators.

---

## Key Terms
- **Airfoil**: A streamlined body designed to maximize lift-to-drag ratio.
- **Angle of Attack**: Acute angle between wing chord and relative oncoming wind.
- **Downwash**: Downward velocity vector imparted to fluid behind a lifting wing.
