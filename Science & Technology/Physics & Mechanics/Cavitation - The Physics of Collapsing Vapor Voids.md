# Cavitation - The Physics of Collapsing Vapor Voids

> **The Big Idea:** The rapid formation and catastrophic collapse of vapor bubbles in a liquid subjected to rapid changes in local dynamic pressure.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The rapid formation and catastrophic collapse of vapor bubbles in a liquid subjected to rapid changes in local dynamic pressure.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** When fluid velocity causes local static pressure to drop below water's vapor pressure (Bernoulli effect), microscopic liquid cavities boil into vapor bubbles. As the fluid enters higher-pressure zones, the bubbles implode symmetrically, focusing microjets of liquid at thousands of meters per second that erode ship propellers and turbine blades.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Physics & Mechanics**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
High Fluid Velocity -> Pressure Drops Below Vapor Point -> Void Forms -> Symmetrical Shockwave Implosion
```
