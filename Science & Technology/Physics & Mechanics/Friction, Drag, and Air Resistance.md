# Friction, Drag, and Air Resistance

> **The Big Idea:** Resistive forces that oppose relative sliding motion between contacting surfaces or through surrounding fluid media.

---

## 1. What It Is
Resistive forces that oppose relative sliding motion between contacting surfaces or through surrounding fluid media.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Friction arises from microscopic surface asperities interlocking; aerodynamic drag scales with velocity squared ($v^2$) at high speeds.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Static Friction (Resists Initial Slip) > Kinetic Friction (Sliding Drag)
```
