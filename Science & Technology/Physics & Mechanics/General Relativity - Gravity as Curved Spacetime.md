# General Relativity - Gravity as Curved Spacetime

> **The Big Idea:** Einstein's 1915 geometric theory of gravitation showing that massive objects do not exert an invisible tug, but warp the fabric of 4D spacetime.

---

## 1. What It Is
Einstein's 1915 geometric theory of gravitation showing that massive objects do not exert an invisible tug, but warp the fabric of 4D spacetime.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Planets follow straight inertial paths (geodesics) through curved spacetime created by the Sun's colossal mass density.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Mass Tells Spacetime How to Curve <==> Curved Spacetime Tells Mass How to Move
```
