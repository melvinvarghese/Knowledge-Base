# Centripetal Force - Circular Motion and Orbits

> **The Big Idea:** An inward-directed force required to keep an object moving in a curved path. Without centripetal force, inertia would carry the object in a straight tangent line.

---

## 🎡 Turning Corners and Orbits
* **Formula:**  
  $$F_c = \frac{m \cdot v^2}{r}$$
* **Orbits are Falling Around the Earth:** Isaac Newton's cannonball thought experiment: if you shoot a cannonball fast enough ($7.9 \text{ km/s}$), the curve of the falling cannonball matches the curvature of the Earth, keeping it in perpetual free-fall (orbit)!
