# The Electromagnetic Spectrum

> **The Big Idea:** Light is not just what our eyes see. Visible light is a tiny sliver of the vast **Electromagnetic (EM) Spectrum**—waves of coupled electric and magnetic fields traveling at the speed of light ($300,000 \text{ km/s}$), from kilometer-long radio waves to microscopic, energetic gamma rays.

## 🔍 The 7 Bands Explained in Plain English

1. **Radio Waves (Wavelengths: football fields):** Longest waves. Pass effortlessly through walls and air; used for FM radio, air-traffic control, and satellite communication.
2. **Microwaves (Wavelengths: centimeters):** Perfectly tuned to vibrate water molecules in food to create friction heat (microwave ovens); also powers 5G and WiFi.
3. **Infrared (Heat Radiation):** Emitted by warm objects. Thermal cameras and night-vision goggles detect infrared photons given off by human bodies.
4. **Visible Light (The Human Rainbow):** The tiny window between 380 nm (violet) and 700 nm (red) that human retinal photoreceptors can detect.
5. **Ultraviolet (UV):** Higher energy than visible light. Triggers vitamin D synthesis in skin, but excess UV breaks chemical bonds, causing sunburns and skin cancer.
6. **X-Rays:** Extremely high frequency. Penetrate soft skin and muscle, but are absorbed by dense bone and metal, creating medical diagnostic shadows.
7. **Gamma Rays:** The most energetic photons in the cosmos. Emitted by dying stars, supernovae, and nuclear decay; can destroy cancer cells in radiotherapy.

---

## ⚡ The Wave-Particle Duality

Light has a double personality:
* Travels through space like a continuous spreading **Wave** (can diffract, refract, and interfere).
* Interacts with matter and atoms like discrete packets of energy called **Photons** (the photoelectric effect that powers solar panels).

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway

* The sky is blue because Earth's atmosphere scatters short blue wavelengths of visible light far more than long red wavelengths (**Rayleigh Scattering**). At sunset, sunlight passes through much thicker atmosphere, scattering away the blues and leaving warm oranges and reds.
