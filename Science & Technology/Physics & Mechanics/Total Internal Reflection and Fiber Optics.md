# Total Internal Reflection and Fiber Optics

## Overview
Total Internal Reflection occurs when a light wave traveling in a higher-index medium hits a lower-index boundary at an angle steeper than the critical angle, reflecting 100% of light.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Snell's Law and Critical Angle: $n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2$. When $\theta_1 \ge \theta_c = \arcsin(n_2 / n_1)$, transmission drops to zero and complete reflection occurs.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Fiber Core and Cladding Architecture: High-index silica core ($n_1 \approx 1.48$) encased in lower-index cladding ($n_2 \approx 1.46$) confines light via continuous internal reflections.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Transoceanic internet fiber-optic cables, medical surgical endoscopes, and optical prism binoculars.

---

## Key Terms
- **Critical Angle**: Minimum incidence angle where light is completely reflected internally.
- **Index of Refraction ($n$)**: Ratio of speed of light in vacuum to speed in medium.
- **Evanescent Wave**: Near-field electromagnetic field decaying exponentially beyond the boundary.
