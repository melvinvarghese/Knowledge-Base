# Optics - Reflection, Refraction, and Lenses

> **The Big Idea:** Optics is the branch of physics studying light behavior: how mirrors reflect rays, how lenses bend light (**Refraction**), and how optical glass powers microscopes and telescopes.

---

## 🔍 Reflection vs. Refraction

* **Law of Reflection:** The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection (flat mirrors).
* **Refraction (Snell's Law):** Light changes speed when entering a denser medium (like glass or water), causing the light beam to bend. This is why a straw looks broken in a glass of water!
* **Convex vs. Concave Lenses:** Convex lenses converge light to a focal point (magnifying glasses, eyeglasses for farsightedness); Concave lenses diverge light.
