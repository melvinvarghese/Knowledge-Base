# The Higgs Boson - The Origin of Mass

> **The Big Idea:** The particle associated with the universal **Higgs Field** discovered in 2012 at CERN's Large Hadron Collider, explaining why fundamental subatomic particles have mass.

---

##  molasses of the Universe
* **The Molasses Analogy:** Picture the Higgs field as an invisible cosmic pool of thick molasses filling all space.
* Photons glide through without interacting, remaining massless and moving at light speed.
* Quarks and electrons drag through the molasses, experiencing drag that manifests as **Inertial Mass**. Without the Higgs field, electrons would fly at light speed and atoms could never form!
