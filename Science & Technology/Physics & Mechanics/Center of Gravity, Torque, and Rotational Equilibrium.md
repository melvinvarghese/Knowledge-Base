## 1. What It Is

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** An object remains in stable equilibrium as long as its center of gravity falls directly within its foundational base of support.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Pivot Point -> Moment Arm r * Perpendicular Force F = Rotational Torque
```
