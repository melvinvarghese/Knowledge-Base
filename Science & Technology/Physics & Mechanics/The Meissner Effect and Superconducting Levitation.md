# The Meissner Effect and Superconducting Levitation

> **The Big Idea:** The complete expulsion of magnetic flux lines from the interior of a material as it transitions into a superconducting state.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The complete expulsion of magnetic flux lines from the interior of a material as it transitions into a superconducting state.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Unlike an ideal normal conductor, a superconductor cooled below its critical temperature ($T_c$) actively induces surface screening currents that cancel all interior magnetic fields ($B = 0$). In type-II superconductors, magnetic flux lines become pinned in nanoscale vortex defects, locking objects in stable mid-air levitation.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Physics & Mechanics**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Material Cooled Below Tc -> Screening Supercurrents Induced -> Magnetic Flux Expelled -> Quantum Levitation
```
