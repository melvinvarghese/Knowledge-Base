# Superconductivity - Zero Electrical Resistance

> **The Big Idea:** A quantum state where certain materials cooled near Absolute Zero lose all electrical resistance, conducting electricity with 100% efficiency and expelling magnetic fields (**Meissner Effect**).

---

## 🧲 Quantum Levitation
* **Zero Heat Loss:** Ordinary copper power cables lose 10% of electricity as heat; superconducting cables can carry massive current forever with zero loss.
* **Magnetic Levitation:** Superconductors completely repel magnetic fields, allowing magnets to levitate locked in mid-air (used in high-speed 600 km/h Maglev bullet trains and medical MRI scanners).
