# Optics - Reflection, Refraction, and Snell's Law

> **The Big Idea:** The branch of physics studying the behavior, properties, and manipulation of light passing through different optical media.

---

## 1. What It Is
The branch of physics studying the behavior, properties, and manipulation of light passing through different optical media.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Light slows down when entering denser transparent media, bending toward the normal axis according to Snell's law of refraction ($n_1 \sin \theta_1 = n_2 \sin \theta_2$).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Incident Light Ray -> Media Boundary -> Reflected Ray + Refracted Bending Ray
```
