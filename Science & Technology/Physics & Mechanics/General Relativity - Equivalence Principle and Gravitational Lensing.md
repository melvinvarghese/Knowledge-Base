# General Relativity - Equivalence Principle and Gravitational Lensing

## Overview
General Relativity reformulates gravity not as a conventional Newtonian force, but as the curvature of four-dimensional spacetime induced by mass-energy distributions.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Einstein Field Equations: $G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$. Matter dictates spacetime curvature, while curved spacetime dictates matter motion along geodesics.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
The Equivalence Principle & Gravitational Lensing: Gravitational and inertial mass are indistinguishable locally. Photons grazing massive objects bend by deflection angle $\theta = 4GM/(c^2 b)$.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Gravitational microlensing exoplanet detection, cosmological dark matter cluster mapping, and relativistic clock correction in GPS.

---

## Key Terms
- **Equivalence Principle**: Local equivalence between gravitational acceleration and an accelerating reference frame.
- **Geodesic**: Shortest or extremal path connecting two events across curved spacetime.
- **Einstein Ring**: Deformation of distant light into a ring around a massive gravitational lens.
