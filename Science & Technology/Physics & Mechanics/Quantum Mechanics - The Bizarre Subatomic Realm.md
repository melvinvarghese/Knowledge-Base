# Quantum Mechanics - The Bizarre Subatomic Realm

> **The Big Idea:** At the atomic scale, the classical rules of reality break down. Particles exist in clouds of probability, pass through solid walls, and communicate across distance instantaneously.

---

## 🌌 The Quantum Paradoxes

* **Wave-Particle Duality:** Electrons behave like waves until they are observed, when they collapse into localized particles.
* **Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle:** You cannot simultaneously know both the precise position and momentum of a subatomic particle.
* **Quantum Superposition:** A quantum system exists in all possible states at once until measured (Schrödinger's Cat).
* **Quantum Entanglement:** Two particles linked so intimately that measuring one instantly dictates the state of the other across light-years ('Spooky action at a distance').
