# Thermal Radiation and Blackbody Physics

> **The Big Idea:** The electromagnetic radiation emitted from the surface of an object due to its internal thermodynamic temperature.

---

## 1. What It Is
The electromagnetic radiation emitted from the surface of an object due to its internal thermodynamic temperature.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Max Planck solved the classical ultraviolet catastrophe by postulating that light is emitted in discrete quantized packets ($E = hf$).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Wien's Displacement Law (Peak Wavelength) + Stefan-Boltzmann Law (Total Power ~ T^4)
```
