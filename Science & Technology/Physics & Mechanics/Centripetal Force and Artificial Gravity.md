# Centripetal Force and Artificial Gravity

## Overview
Centripetal Force is the inward net force required to compel an object to traverse a curved path. In rotating space habitats, the reactive outward centrifugal sensation provides artificial gravity.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Rotational Acceleration Formula: $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 r$. Normal contact force exerted by rotating floor simulates gravitational floor pressure $N = m\omega^2 r$.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Physiological Rotation Constraints: To prevent vestibular motion sickness caused by cross-coupled Coriolis forces during head turns, rotation rates must generally remain below 2–4 RPM.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Rotating wheel space habitat designs (O'Neill cylinders), high-speed industrial laboratory centrifuges, and centrifuge pilot G-force training.

---

## Key Terms
- **Centripetal Acceleration**: Inward acceleration vector directed toward the center of curvature.
- **Centrifugal Force**: Apparent fictitious inertial force directed radially outward in a rotating reference frame.
- **O'Neill Cylinder**: Proposed rotating space settlement utilizing artificial centrifugal gravity.
