# Resonance and Forced Oscillations

## Overview
Resonance occurs when an external periodic force drives an oscillatory system at its natural frequency, generating maximum amplitude response and energy transfer.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Damped Driven Harmonic Equation: $m \ddot{x} + c \dot{x} + kx = F_0 \cos(\omega t)$. Amplitude peaks sharply when excitation frequency matches $\omega_0 = \sqrt{k/m}$.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Quality Factor ($Q$): $Q = \omega_0 m / c$ measures peak sharpness and stored vs dissipated energy. Phase lag crosses 90° at exact resonance.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), LC radio frequency receivers, and skyscraper tuned mass dampers.

---

## Key Terms
- **Resonance**: Amplified vibrational response when driven at resonant frequency.
- **Quality Factor ($Q$)**: Parameter measuring sharpness of a resonance curve.
- **Tuned Mass Damper**: Inertial counterweight designed to cancel structural sway.
