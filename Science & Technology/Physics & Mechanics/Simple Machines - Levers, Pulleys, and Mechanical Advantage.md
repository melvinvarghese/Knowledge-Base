# Simple Machines - Levers, Pulleys, and Mechanical Advantage

> **The Big Idea:** The six classical simple machines that multiply human force: Lever, Wheel and Axle, Pulley, Inclined Plane, Wedge, and Screw.

---

## 🏗️ The Mechanical Trade-Off
* **The Golden Law:** Machines can multiply force, but they **cannot multiply energy**. Work = Force × Distance ($W = F \times d$).
* If a crowbar or pulley lets you lift a 500 kg boulder with only 50 kg of pushing effort (10x force advantage), you must push your end of the lever **10 times farther**!
