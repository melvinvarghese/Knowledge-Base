# The Doppler Effect - Sound and Light Shifts

> **The Big Idea:** The change in observed wave frequency caused by relative motion between the wave source and an observer.

---

## 1. What It Is
The change in observed wave frequency caused by relative motion between the wave source and an observer.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Approaching sources compress waves into higher frequencies (blueshift in light, higher pitch in sound); receding sources stretch waves (redshift).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Approaching Source (Compressed Waves / High Pitch) | Receding Source (Stretched / Redshift)
```
