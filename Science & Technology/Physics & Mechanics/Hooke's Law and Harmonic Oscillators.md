# Hooke's Law and Harmonic Oscillators

## Overview
Hooke's Law states that the restoring force exerted by an elastic material is proportional to displacement, providing the governing equation for simple harmonic motion.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Linear Restoring Force & Natural Frequency: $F = -kx \implies m \frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + kx = 0$. Natural angular frequency is $\omega_0 = \sqrt{k/m}$.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Phase Space Conservation: Continuous conservative exchange between potential energy ($U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$) and kinetic energy ($K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$) forms closed phase space ellipses.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Mechanical chronometer hairsprings, atomic bond vibrational spectroscopy, and vehicle suspension coil springs.

---

## Key Terms
- **Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)**: Periodic oscillation where restoring force is proportional to displacement.
- **Spring Constant ($k$)**: Measure of elastic stiffness in a spring.
- **Isochronism**: Property where oscillation period is independent of amplitude.
