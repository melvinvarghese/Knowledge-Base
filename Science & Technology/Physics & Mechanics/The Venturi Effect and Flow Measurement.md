# The Venturi Effect and Flow Measurement

## Overview
The Venturi Effect describes the drop in fluid static pressure that accompanies fluid acceleration through a constricted choke section of a conduit.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Continuity and Pressure Differential: $Q = A_1 A_2 \sqrt{\frac{2(P_1 - P_2)}{\rho(A_1^2 - A_2^2)}}$. Fluid speed peaks in the narrow throat, causing a measurable drop in pressure.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Venturi Tube Profile: Smooth converging inlet cone accelerates fluid with minimal losses; gradual 5°–7° diverging diffuser recovers static head downstream.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Industrial pipeline flowmeters, chemical jet pump eductors, and clinical Venturi oxygen masks.

---

## Key Terms
- **Choke**: The minimum cross-sectional constriction in a fluid conduit.
- **Entrainment**: Capturing and drawing in ambient fluid by a high-velocity jet.
- **Diffuser**: Diverging duct section designed to decelerate fluid and recover pressure.
