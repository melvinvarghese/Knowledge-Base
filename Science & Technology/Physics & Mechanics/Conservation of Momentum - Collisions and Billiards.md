# Conservation of Momentum - Collisions and Billiards

> **The Big Idea:** A fundamental physical conservation law: in any closed system, total momentum (Mass × Velocity, $p = mv$) remains strictly constant before and after a collision.

---

## 🎱 Elastic vs. Inelastic Collisions
* **Elastic Collisions:** Both momentum and kinetic energy are conserved; objects bounce off each other with no heat generated (Billiards balls, atom collisions).
* **Inelastic Collisions:** Momentum is conserved, but kinetic energy converts into heat and deformation (a car crash where metal crumples to absorb impact energy).
