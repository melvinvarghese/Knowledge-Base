# Particle Accelerators - Cyclotrons and Synchrotrons

## Overview
Particle Accelerators utilize electric fields to accelerate charged particles to relativistic speeds and magnetic fields to steer and focus them into high-energy particle beams.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Lorentz Force & Relativistic Mechanics: Particles accelerated across RF resonant cavities with voltage $V$. Relativistic mass increase requires magnetic field ramp-up in synchrotrons ($B = p / (q r)$).

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Cyclotron vs Synchrotron Architecture: Cyclotrons use fixed magnetic fields and expanding spiral orbits; synchrotrons ramp magnetic field in fixed-radius rings to reach TeV energy regimes.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Large Hadron Collider (LHC) particle discovery (Higgs boson), synchrotron radiation light sources for structural biology, and proton cancer therapy.

---

## Key Terms
- **Synchrotron**: Circular accelerator where magnetic steering ramps synchronously with increasing beam energy.
- **RF Cavity**: Electromagnetic resonator supplying high-frequency oscillating electric fields for acceleration.
- **Synchrotron Light**: Brilliant electromagnetic radiation emitted when charged particles accelerate radially.
