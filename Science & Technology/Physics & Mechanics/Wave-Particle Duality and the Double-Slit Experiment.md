# Wave-Particle Duality and the Double-Slit Experiment

> **The Big Idea:** The quantum mechanical cornerstone proving that matter and light exhibit behaviors of both continuous waves and discrete localized particles.

---

## 1. What It Is
The quantum mechanical cornerstone proving that matter and light exhibit behaviors of both continuous waves and discrete localized particles.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Firing single electrons through two narrow slits produces an interference pattern on the detector screen until a detector monitors which slit they pass through.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Single Particle Fired -> Passes Through Both Slits as Probability Wave -> Interference Screen
```
