# Energy - Kinetic, Potential, and Conservation

> **The Big Idea:** Energy is the quantitative property transferred to an object to perform work. It exists in kinetic motion or stored potential forms, perfectly conserved in every process.

---

## ⚡ The Rollercoaster Analogy

* **Gravitational Potential Energy ($PE = mgh$):** Stored energy due to an object's position (sitting at the top peak of a roller coaster track).
* **Kinetic Energy ($KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$):** Energy of motion (plunging down the track at 100 km/h).
* Notice that velocity is squared: driving twice as fast means your car carries **four times as much kinetic energy**, requiring 4x the braking distance!
