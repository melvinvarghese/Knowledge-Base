# Einstein's Theory of General Relativity

> **The Big Idea:** Gravity is not an invisible pulling force. Massive objects (like planets and stars) curve the 4-dimensional fabric of **Spacetime**; objects simply follow the curves.

---

## 🎳 The Trampoline Analogy

* Picture a heavy bowling ball placed on a trampoline: it creates a deep bowl depression.
* Roll a marble across the trampoline, and it circles around the bowling ball.
* That is an orbit: the Earth isn't being 'pulled' by an invisible rope; it is simply rolling along the curved geometry of spacetime created by the Sun's mass!
* **Gravitational Lensing:** Massive galaxies bend light passing near them, acting as natural cosmic magnifying glasses.
