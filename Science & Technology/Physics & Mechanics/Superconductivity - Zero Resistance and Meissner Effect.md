# Superconductivity - Zero Resistance and Meissner Effect

> **The Big Idea:** A physical state occurring in certain materials cooled below a critical temperature ($T_c$) characterized by exactly zero electrical resistance.

---

## 1. What It Is
A physical state occurring in certain materials cooled below a critical temperature ($T_c$) characterized by exactly zero electrical resistance.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Electrons form paired Cooper pairs that slide through crystalline lattices without scattering, expelling external magnetic fields (Meissner levitation).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Cooling Below Tc -> Cooper Pair Formation -> Zero Electrical Resistance + Magnetic Levitation
```
