# Quantum Teleportation and Entanglement Networks

> **The Big Idea:** The protocol where the exact quantum state of a particle is transmitted instantaneously to a distant particle using quantum entanglement and classical communication.

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The protocol where the exact quantum state of a particle is transmitted instantaneously to a distant particle using quantum entanglement and classical communication.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** By performing a Bell-state joint measurement on a message qubit and one half of an entangled EPR particle pair, the original state is destroyed locally and reconstructed at the receiving end once two classical bits of measurement data are transmitted.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Physics & Mechanics**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Entangled Qubit Pair (Alice & Bob) -> Alice Bell Measurement -> Classical 2-Bit Send -> Bob Unitary Gate Reconstruction
```
