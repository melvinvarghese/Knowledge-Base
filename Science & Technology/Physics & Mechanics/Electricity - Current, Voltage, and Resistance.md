# Electricity - Current, Voltage, and Resistance

> **The Big Idea:** Electricity is the flow of electric charge (electrons). Governed by **Ohm’s Law** ($V = I \times R$), circuits balance electrical pressure, flow rate, and resistance.

---

## ⚡ Ohm's Law: $V = I \cdot R$

* **Voltage ($V$ in Volts):** The electrical pressure pushing electrons through a wire (like water pressure in a hose).
* **Current ($I$ in Amperes):** The rate of flow of electrons passing a point per second.
* **Resistance ($R$ in Ohms):** How strongly a material resists electron flow (copper has low resistance; rubber has high resistance).
* **AC vs. DC:** Direct Current flows in one direction (batteries); Alternating Current switches direction 50-60 times a second (household wall outlets).
