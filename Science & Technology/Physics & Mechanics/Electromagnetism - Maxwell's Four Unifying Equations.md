# Electromagnetism - Maxwell's Four Unifying Equations

> **The Big Idea:** The four partial differential equations formulated by James Clerk Maxwell that mathematically unified electricity, magnetism, and light.

---

## 1. What It Is
The four partial differential equations formulated by James Clerk Maxwell that mathematically unified electricity, magnetism, and light.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Gauss's laws for electricity and magnetism, Faraday's law of induction, and Ampere's law with Maxwell's addition show changing magnetic fields induce electric fields.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Electric Charge -> Divergence E | No Magnetic Monopoles | Changing B Induces E | Changing E Induces B
```
