# Special Relativity - Time Dilation and Length Contraction

> **The Big Idea:** Albert Einstein's 1905 framework establishing that the laws of physics and speed of light ($c$) are identical for all inertial observers.

---

## 1. What It Is
Albert Einstein's 1905 framework establishing that the laws of physics and speed of light ($c$) are identical for all inertial observers.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** As an object approaches light speed relative to an observer, time slows down (time dilation) and physical length contracts along the motion axis.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Relative Speed Approaches c -> Time Ticks Slower -> Spatial Length Contracts
```
