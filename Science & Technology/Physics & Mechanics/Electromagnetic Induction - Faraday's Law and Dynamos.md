# Electromagnetic Induction - Faraday's Law and Dynamos

> **The Big Idea:** The generation of an electromotive force (voltage) across an electrical conductor when placed inside a changing magnetic field.

---

## 1. What It Is
The generation of an electromotive force (voltage) across an electrical conductor when placed inside a changing magnetic field.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Forms the mechanical working basis for every electrical generator, turbine, transformer, and induction motor powering global civilization.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Rotating Wire Coil in Magnetic Field -> Changing Magnetic Flux -> Induced Electrical Current
```
