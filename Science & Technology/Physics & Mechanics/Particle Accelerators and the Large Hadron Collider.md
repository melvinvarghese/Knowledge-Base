# Particle Accelerators and the Large Hadron Collider

> **The Big Idea:** Massive scientific machines that use superconducting radiofrequency cavities and dipole magnets to accelerate subatomic particles near light speed.

## 1. What It Is
Massive scientific machines that use superconducting radiofrequency cavities and dipole magnets to accelerate subatomic particles near light speed.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Collisions at 13.6 TeV recreate cosmic conditions a trillionth of a second after the Big Bang, confirming fundamental particles like the Higgs boson.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Proton Injection -> Superconducting Dipole Steer -> Near Light Speed Collision -> Higgs Decay
```
