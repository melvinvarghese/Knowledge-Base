# Electromagnetism - Motors and Generators

> **The Big Idea:** Electricity and magnetism are two sides of the same coin. An electric current creates a magnetic field, and a moving magnetic field generates electricity (Faraday's Law).

---

## 🔄 Motors vs. Generators

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│           ELECTRIC MOTOR             │       │          ELECTRIC GENERATOR          │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤       ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Converts ELECTRICAL energy into      │  vs.  │ Converts MECHANICAL kinetic motion   │
│ MECHANICAL motion (fans, EVs, drills)│       │ into ELECTRICITY (dams, wind turbines│
└──────────────────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Electromagnets:** Wrapping copper wire around an iron nail and running current turns it into a powerful magnet that can be turned on and off with a switch (used in junkyard cranes, speakers, and MRI machines).
