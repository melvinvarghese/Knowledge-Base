## 1. What It Is
The universal physical principles governing temperature, thermal heat transfer, work, and the inevitable arrow of time.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** First Law: Conservation of energy. Second Law: Total entropy of an isolated system always increases. Third Law: Absolute zero cannot be reached.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Energy Conserved (Law 1) -> Entropy Increases (Law 2) -> Absolute Zero Inaccessible (Law 3)
```
