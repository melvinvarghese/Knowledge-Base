# Antimatter and Positron Annihilation

## Overview
Antimatter consists of subatomic antiparticles possessing identical mass but opposite electric charge, magnetic moment, and quantum numbers relative to standard matter particles.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Dirac Equation & Mass-Energy Annihilation: Dirac's relativistic wave equation predicted negative-energy states. Matter-antimatter annihilation converts 100% of rest mass to photons: $e^- + e^+ \rightarrow 2\gamma$ (511 keV each).

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
CP Violation & Baryon Asymmetry: CPT symmetry is exact, but slight CP violation in weak interactions is key to explaining why the observable universe is matter-dominated.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Positron Emission Tomography (PET) cancer scans, antiproton deceleration physics at CERN, and aerospace antimatter propulsion concepts.

---

## Key Terms
- **Positron**: The positively charged antimatter counterpart of the electron.
- **Annihilation**: Reaction where a particle and its antiparticle destroy each other, releasing pure energy.
- **Baryogenesis**: Theoretical physical process that generated the universe's matter-antimatter asymmetry.
