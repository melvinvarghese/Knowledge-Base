# Pascal's Law and Hydraulic Systems

## Overview
Pascal's Law states that pressure applied to an enclosed, incompressible fluid is transmitted undiminished in all directions, enabling mechanical force multiplication.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Hydraulic Force Scaling: $P = F_1 / A_1 = F_2 / A_2 \implies F_2 = F_1 (A_2 / A_1)$. Conservation of work ($W = F_1 d_1 = F_2 d_2$) means displacement scales inversely.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Closed Circuit Fluid Dynamics: High-pressure positive displacement pumps drive non-compressible hydraulic oils through directional control valves into dual-action actuator rams.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Automotive hydraulic disc brakes, multi-ton construction excavators, and industrial forging presses.

---

## Key Terms
- **Hydrostatic Pressure**: Compressive normal stress in a fluid at rest.
- **Incompressibility**: Property of a fluid whose volume remains constant under pressure.
- **Mechanical Advantage**: Force amplification ratio in mechanical systems.
