# The Speed of Light - The Universal Limit

> **The Big Idea:** The fundamental speed limit of the universe: exactly **299,792,458 meters per second** in a vacuum ($c$). No object carrying mass or information can ever exceed this speed.

---

## ⚡ Why Nothing Can Go Faster
* **Infinite Mass Barrier:** As an object accelerates closer to light speed, its relativistic mass increases toward infinity, requiring infinite energy to push it faster.
* **Speed of Causality:** Light speed is not just about light; it is the speed at which cause-and-effect information travels across the universe. If the Sun vanished right now, Earth would continue orbiting for 8 minutes and 20 seconds before feeling the gravitational change.
