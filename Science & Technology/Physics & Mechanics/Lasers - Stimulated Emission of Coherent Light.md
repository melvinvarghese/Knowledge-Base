# Lasers - Stimulated Emission of Coherent Light

> **The Big Idea:** Devices that emit monochromatic, highly directional, coherent light through the quantum optical process of stimulated emission of radiation.

---

## 1. What It Is
Devices that emit monochromatic, highly directional, coherent light through the quantum optical process of stimulated emission of radiation.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Excited atoms in a resonant optical cavity are stimulated by passing photons of identical frequency to release duplicate in-phase photons.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Pump Energy Source -> Population Inversion -> Stimulated Photon Emission -> Coherent Laser Beam
```
