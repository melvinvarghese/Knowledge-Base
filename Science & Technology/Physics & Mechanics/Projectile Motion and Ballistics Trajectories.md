# Projectile Motion and Ballistics Trajectories

> **The Big Idea:** The curved parabolic path followed by an unpowered object launched into a gravitational field with initial velocity.

---

## 1. What It Is
The curved parabolic path followed by an unpowered object launched into a gravitational field with initial velocity.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Horizontal motion proceeds at constant velocity (neglecting air drag), while vertical motion undergoes continuous downward gravitational acceleration ($g$).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Constant Horizontal Velocity Vx + Downward Gravitational Acceleration Vy = Parabolic Arc
```
