# Nuclear Fusion - Recreating the Sun on Earth

> **The Big Idea:** The Holy Grail of clean energy: smashing hydrogen isotopes together at 100 million degrees to create helium, producing zero carbon emissions and zero long-lived radioactive waste.

---

## ☀️ Fusion vs. Fission

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│           NUCLEAR FISSION            │       │            NUCLEAR FUSION            │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤       ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Splits heavy atoms (Uranium).        │  vs.  │ Fuses light atoms (Deuterium/Tritium)│
│ Risk of meltdown; radioactive waste. │       │ Inherently safe; fuel from seawater; │
│ Current commercial nuclear plants.   │       │ zero long-lived radioactive waste.   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Tokamaks & Magnetic Confinement:** Giant donut-shaped magnetic cages (like ITER in France) using magnetic fields to suspend superheated plasma hotter than the core of the Sun.
