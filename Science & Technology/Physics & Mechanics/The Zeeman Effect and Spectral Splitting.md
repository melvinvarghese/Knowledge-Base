# The Zeeman Effect and Spectral Splitting

## Overview
The Zeeman Effect is the splitting of atomic spectral emission or absorption lines into multiple discrete components in the presence of an external static magnetic field.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Hamiltonian Interaction: $\Delta E = -\boldsymbol{\mu} \cdot \mathbf{B} = g_J \mu_B m_j B$. Energy levels shift based on the projection of total angular momentum quantum number $m_j$.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Normal vs Anomalous Zeeman Effect: Normal triplet splitting occurs in singlet spin-zero states ($S=0$); anomalous complex splitting arises when non-zero electron spin ($S \ne 0$) couples via LS coupling.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Mapping solar and stellar magnetic fields in astrophysics, atomic laser cooling magneto-optical traps, and NMR chemical shift calibration.

---

## Key Terms
- **Zeeman Splitting**: Separation of degenerate atomic energy levels by an external magnetic field.
- **Landé g-factor**: Multiplicative factor determining magnetic moment for paired spin and orbital angular momentum.
- **Magneto-Optical Trap (MOT)**: Device using Zeeman tuning to slow and trap neutral atoms.
