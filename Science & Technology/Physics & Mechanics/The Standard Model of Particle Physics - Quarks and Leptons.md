# The Standard Model of Particle Physics - Quarks and Leptons

> **The Big Idea:** The periodic table of fundamental subatomic particles: 12 matter fermions (Quarks and Leptons) and 5 force-carrying gauge bosons that compose all known physical reality.

---

## ⚛️ The Fundamental Lego Bricks
* **Quarks:** Up and Down quarks bind together in trios via the Strong Nuclear Force to construct protons (2 Up + 1 Down) and neutrons (1 Up + 2 Down).
* **Leptons:** Lightweight particles including Electrons and ghost-like **Neutrinos** (trillions pass through your body every second without touching an atom).
* **Force Bosons:** Photons (Electromagnetism), Gluons (Strong Force), W/Z Bosons (Weak Force), and the Higgs Boson.
