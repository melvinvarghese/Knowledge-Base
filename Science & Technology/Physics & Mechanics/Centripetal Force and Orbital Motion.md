# Centripetal Force and Orbital Motion

> **The Big Idea:** The inward center-directed force required to keep an object moving along a curved circular path ($F_c = mv^2 / r$).

---

## 1. What It Is
The inward center-directed force required to keep an object moving along a curved circular path ($F_c = mv^2 / r$).

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** In celestial orbits, gravity acts as the centripetal force continuously pulling a satellite toward Earth while its forward speed keeps it missing the planet.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Forward Inertial Velocity Tangent <== Inward Centripetal Gravity Pull ==> Stable Circular Orbit
```
