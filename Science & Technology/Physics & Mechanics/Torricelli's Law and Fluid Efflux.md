# Torricelli's Law and Fluid Efflux

## Overview
Torricelli's Law determines that fluid exiting a tank orifice under gravity travels at the same speed as an object falling freely from the fluid's surface height.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Principles
Efflux Velocity Equation: $v = \sqrt{2gh}$. Derived directly by applying Bernoulli's equation between the open reservoir surface and the discharge orifice.

---

## 2. Structural Mechanics & Scientific Analysis
Vena Contracta and Flow Discharge: Fluid inertia causes jet contraction downstream of the orifice ($Q = C_d A \sqrt{2gh}$), where discharge coefficient $C_d \approx 0.62$.

---

## 3. Real-World Applications & Technological Impact
Hydroelectric dam spillway gates, chemical reservoir emergency dump ports, and the Torricellian mercury barometer.

---

## Key Terms
- **Efflux**: The escape or outflow of fluid through a narrow aperture.
- **Vena Contracta**: Minimum cross-sectional diameter of a discharged fluid jet.
- **Fluid Head**: Vertical height of a static liquid column representing potential pressure.
