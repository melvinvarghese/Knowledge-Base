# Fluid Dynamics and Bernoulli's Principle

> **The Big Idea:** The physical laws governing fluid motion, stating that an increase in fluid velocity occurs simultaneously with a decrease in pressure.

---

## 1. What It Is
The physical laws governing fluid motion, stating that an increase in fluid velocity occurs simultaneously with a decrease in pressure.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Governs airplane wing lift, venturi carburetors, pitot tube airspeed sensors, and the curveball trajectory of spinning sports balls (Magnus effect).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
High Fluid Velocity -> Low Static Pressure | Low Fluid Velocity -> High Static Pressure
```
