# Magnetism and Magnetic Fields

> **The Big Idea:** Magnetism is a fundamental physical force produced by the alignment of electron spins in materials and by moving electric charges.

---

## 🧲 Dipoles and Domains

* **Always Dipoles:** Every magnet has a North and South pole. If you cut a bar magnet in half, you don't get separate poles; you get two smaller two-pole magnets.
* **Opposites Attract:** Like poles repel ($N-N$ or $S-S$); opposite poles attract ($N-S$).
* **Magnetic Domains:** In non-magnetic iron, atomic magnetic fields point in random directions; in a permanent magnet, external forces align all domains in parallel.
