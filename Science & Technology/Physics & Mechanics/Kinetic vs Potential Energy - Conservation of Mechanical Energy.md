# Kinetic vs Potential Energy - Conservation of Mechanical Energy

> **The Big Idea:** The fundamental physical principle that energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed between kinetic and potential states.

---

## 1. What It Is
The fundamental physical principle that energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed between kinetic and potential states.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** A swinging pendulum continuously trades gravitational potential energy at its highest arcs for peak kinetic energy at the bottom.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of physics & mechanics, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Potential Energy (Stored Gravitational / Elastic) <==> Kinetic Energy (Motion 1/2 mv^2)
```
