# Bernoulli's Principle - Fluid Dynamics and Airplane Wings

> **The Big Idea:** A core rule of fluid dynamics: as the speed of a moving fluid (or gas) increases, its internal pressure decreases. Applied to generate aerodynamic lift on aircraft wings.

---

## ✈️ How Wings Generate Lift
* **The Aerofoil Shape:** Air flowing over the curved top of an airplane wing moves faster than air underneath.
* **Pressure Difference:** Fast air over the top creates lower pressure; slower air underneath creates higher pressure, pushing the wing upward into the sky (**Lift**).
