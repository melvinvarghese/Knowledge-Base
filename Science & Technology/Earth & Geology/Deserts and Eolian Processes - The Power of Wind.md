## 1. What It Is
Arid environments receiving less than 250 mm of precipitation annually, shaped predominantly by wind erosion and sand deposition.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Forms barchan dunes, yardangs, and desert pavement through the continuous winnowing of fine silt and clay (deflation).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Wind Deflation -> Sand Saltation -> Dune Migration -> Desert Pavement
```
