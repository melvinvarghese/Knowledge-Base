# Fluvial Geomorphology - Meandering Rivers and Oxbow Lakes

## Overview
Fluvial Geomorphology investigates how flowing water sculpts landscapes, transports sediment, and drives the continuous dynamic evolution of **meandering river channels** and **oxbow lakes**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
Helicoidal Flow Dynamics: Centrifugal force at a channel bend drives water outward; bottom friction creates a helical cross-channel current eroding the outer bank (**cut bank**) and depositing sediment on the inner bank (**point bar**).

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Oxbow Lake Evolution: Continuous erosion tightens the river loop until high floodwaters breach the narrow neck (chute cutoff), establishing a new straight channel and abandoning the crescentic loop as an **oxbow lake**.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
River flood risk mapping, wetland ecological restoration, civil engineering bridge scour protection.

---

## Key Terms
- **Meander**: Winding curve or loop in the course of a river or stream.
- **Cut Bank**: Steep outer bank of a meandering stream subject to active hydrodynamic erosion.
- **Oxbow Lake**: U-shaped lake formed when a wide meander of a river is cut off, creating a free-standing body of water.
