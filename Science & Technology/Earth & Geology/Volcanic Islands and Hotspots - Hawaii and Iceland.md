# Volcanic Islands and Hotspots - Hawaii and Iceland

> **The Big Idea:** Stationary plumes of superheated mantle material (hotspots) that melt through moving tectonic plates like a blowtorch, creating chains of volcanic islands like Hawaii.

---

## 🌋 The Hotspot Trail
* **The Hawaiian Chain:** The mantle hotspot stays stationary while the Pacific Plate slides northwest at 7 cm/year. As an island drifts off the hotspot, its volcanoes go extinct and a new island forms above the plume.
* **Loihi:** A submarine volcano currently growing underwater southeast of Hawaii; in 100,000 years, it will breach the ocean surface as Earth's newest island.
