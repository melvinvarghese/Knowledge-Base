# The Deep Sea - Trenches and Hydrothermal Vents

> **The Big Idea:** The abyss is Earth's final frontier: pitch-black, freezing, and under bone-crushing pressure, yet thriving with alien-like life fueled by volcanic hydrothermal chemistry.

---

## 🐙 Life Without Sunlight

* **The Mariana Trench:** The deepest trench on Earth (Challenger Deep plunges nearly 11,000 meters down).
* **Hydrothermal Vents (Black Smokers):** Volcanic chimneys spewing mineral-rich water heated to 400°C.
* **Chemosynthesis:** Instead of sunlight, deep-sea bacteria consume toxic hydrogen sulfide to make food, supporting giant tube worms and ghost crabs.
