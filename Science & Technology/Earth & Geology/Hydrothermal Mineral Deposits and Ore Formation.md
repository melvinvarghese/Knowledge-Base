# Hydrothermal Mineral Deposits and Ore Formation

> **The Big Idea:** Concentrations of economic metals like copper, gold, and silver formed when superheated groundwater dissolves and precipitates metals along faults.

## 1. What It Is
Concentrations of economic metals like copper, gold, and silver formed when superheated groundwater dissolves and precipitates metals along faults.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Porphyry deposits and vein networks supply the vast majority of raw conductive metals required for modern global industry.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Magmatic Heat Source -> Metal Leaching by Brines -> Cooling in Fractures -> Ore Veins
```
