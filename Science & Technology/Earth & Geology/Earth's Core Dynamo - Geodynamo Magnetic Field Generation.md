# Earth's Core Dynamo - Geodynamo Magnetic Field Generation

## Overview
The Geodynamo is the self-sustaining magnetohydrodynamic physical process occurring in Earth's liquid iron-nickel outer core that generates the planetary geomagnetic dipole shield protecting our atmosphere.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
Magnetohydrodynamic Induction Equations: Convective churning of low-viscosity, molten liquid iron driven by compositional buoyancy (crystallization of solid inner core releasing light sulfur/silicon) and Coriolis deflection generates helical electrical currents.

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Geomagnetic Field Reversals: Numerical dynamo simulations and paleomagnetic volcanic basalt records reveal that Earth's magnetic poles periodically flip polarity (normal to reversed) over non-periodic timescales averaging 250,000 years.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
Planetary atmospheric retention (protecting atmosphere from solar wind stripping), animal migratory magnetoreception, directional compass navigation.

---

## Key Terms
- **Geodynamo**: Mechanism by which fluid motion in Earth's outer core generates and sustains the planetary magnetic field.
- **Coriolis Deflection**: Inertial rotation effect organizing liquid iron convective plumes into aligned helical columns.
- **Magnetic Reversal**: Geological phenomenon where magnetic north and magnetic south completely invert positions.
