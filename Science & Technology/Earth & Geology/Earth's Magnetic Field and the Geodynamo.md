# Earth's Magnetic Field and the Geodynamo

> **The Big Idea:** Generated by swirling molten iron in the outer core, Earth's magnetosphere extends tens of thousands of kilometers into space, shielding our atmosphere from solar wind.

---

## 🧲 The Shield of Life

* **The Geodynamo:** Convection currents of molten iron and nickel combined with Earth's rotation (Coriolis effect) create an electric generator.
* **Magnetic Reversals:** Earth's magnetic north and south poles flip periodically (on average every 200,000 to 300,000 years).
* **Without it:** Solar wind would strip away our atmosphere and oceans, turning Earth into a barren desert like Mars.
