# Oceans - Currents and the Global Conveyor Belt

> **The Big Idea:** The oceans cover 71% of Earth's surface. Global thermohaline circulation acts as a colossal planetary conveyor belt, distributing heat and regulating world climates.

---

## 🌊 Thermohaline Circulation

* **The Engine:** Cold, salty water near the poles is dense and sinks to the ocean abyss. Warm surface water rushes in to replace it.
* **The Gulf Stream:** Transports equatorial heat to Western Europe, keeping London and Paris far warmer than Canadian cities at identical latitudes.
* **Ocean Acidification:** Oceans absorb 30% of human carbon emissions, lowering pH and threatening coral reefs and shellfish.
