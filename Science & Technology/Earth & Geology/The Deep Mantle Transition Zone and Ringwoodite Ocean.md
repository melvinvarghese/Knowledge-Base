# The Deep Mantle Transition Zone and Ringwoodite Ocean

> **The Big Idea:** The discovery of hydrated ringwoodite mineral inclusions in deep mantle diamonds confirming that Earth's transition zone holds oceans worth of water.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The discovery of hydrated ringwoodite mineral inclusions in deep mantle diamonds confirming that Earth's transition zone holds oceans worth of water.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Between 410 and 660 km depth, olivine transforms under high pressure into the high-density mineral ringwoodite. In 2014, analysis of diamond inclusions revealed that ringwoodite holds up to $1.5\%$ water by weight as trapped hydroxide ($OH^-$) ions, suggesting the transition zone contains as much water as all surface oceans combined.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Earth & Geology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Subducting Oceanic Slab -> High-Pressure Olivine Transition -> Hydroxide Trapped in Ringwoodite Lattice
```
