# The Carbon Cycle

> **The Big Idea:** Carbon is the chemical backbone of all organic life. Earth's carbon cycle shuttles carbon atoms continuously between the atmosphere, biosphere, oceans, and geosphere.

---

## 🔄 The Fast and Slow Carbon Loops

* **Fast Cycle:** Plants absorb $CO_2$ via photosynthesis; animals breathe out $CO_2$ via respiration; decomposition releases carbon back into the soil and air.
* **Slow Cycle:** Dead organisms trapped under sediment are compressed into limestone and fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas) over hundreds of millions of years.
* **Human Disruption:** Burning millions of years of fossil fuel in two centuries has overwhelmed natural sinks, driving climate change.
