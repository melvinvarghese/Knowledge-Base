# Geysers and Hydrothermal Vents

> **The Big Idea:** Intermittent hot springs where groundwater heated by shallow magma chambers erupts under steam pressure through narrow rock vents.

## 1. What It Is
Intermittent hot springs where groundwater heated by shallow magma chambers erupts under steam pressure through narrow rock vents.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Yellowstone National Park holds more than half of the world's geysers, fueled by a continental supervolcano hotspot.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Deep Groundwater Heating -> Steam Bubble Trapping -> Pressure Overcome -> Eruption
```
