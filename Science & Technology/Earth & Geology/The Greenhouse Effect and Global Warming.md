# The Greenhouse Effect and Global Warming

> **The Big Idea:** Earth's natural atmospheric blanket keeps our planet livable, but human emissions of greenhouse gases are trapping excess heat and altering the global climate.

---

## 🌡️ The Physics of the Greenhouse Effect

1. High-energy visible sunlight passes easily through the atmosphere and warms Earth's surface.
2. The warm ground radiates energy back toward space as **low-energy infrared heat**.
3. Greenhouse gases ($CO_2$, Methane, Water Vapor, Nitrous Oxide) absorb and re-emit this infrared heat in all directions, warming the lower atmosphere.
* **Consequences:** Rising sea levels, melting glaciers, intensified droughts, and extreme weather events.
