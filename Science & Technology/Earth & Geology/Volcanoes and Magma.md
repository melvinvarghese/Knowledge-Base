# Volcanoes and Magma

> **The Big Idea:** A volcano is a rupture in Earth’s crust that allows superheated molten rock (**magma**), toxic gases, and volcanic ash to escape from deep underground. Volcanoes are destructive, but they also created Earth's original atmosphere, oceans, and rich agricultural soils.

## 🌋 The 3 Main Types of Volcanoes

```
1. SHIELD VOLCANOES:      Broad, gentle slopes (Mauna Loa, Hawaii).
                          Runny basalt lava flows for miles like syrup; non-explosive.

2. COMPOSITE (STRATO):    Steep, towering peaks (Mt. Fuji, Mt. St. Helens).
                          Thick, sticky magma traps gases until building catastrophic pressure.

3. CINDER CONES:          Small, steep cone hills built from blobs of congealed lava
                          and pyroclastic cinders ejected from a single vent.
```

## 💥 The Real Killers in an Eruption

Most people fear slow-moving glowing lava, but lava flows can usually be outwalked. The real deadly hazards are:
1. **Pyroclastic Flows:** Superheated clouds of gas, ash, and pumice racing down mountain slopes at **over 400 km/h** at temperatures exceeding 800°C. Nothing survives in their path (as in Pompeii, 79 CE).
2. **Lahars (Volcanic Mudflows):** When hot ash instantly melts summit snow and glaciers, creating boiling concrete-like rivers that bury entire valleys.
3. **Volcanic Ash Clouds:** Microscopic shards of jagged volcanic glass that choke airplane jet engines and collapse building roofs under immense weight.

## 🛠️ Mind-Bending Fact

Volcanoes aren't just on Earth:
* **Olympus Mons on Mars** is a shield volcano three times taller than Mount Everest (22 km high).
* Jupiter’s moon **Io** is the most volcanically active body in the Solar System, shooting sulfur plumes 300 km into space.
