# Soil Horizon Stratification - O, A, E, B, C Profiles and Pedogenesis

## Overview
Pedogenesis is the long-term process of soil development through the physical and chemical weathering of mineral rock and biological decomposition, organizing soil into distinct horizontal **master horizons**.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
The Soil Profile Master Horizons: 1. **O Horizon** (organic leaf litter layer); 2. **A Horizon** (humus-rich, dark topsoil); 3. **E Horizon** (eluviated, leached mineral layer); 4. **B Horizon** (subsoil zone of illuviation and clay accumulation); 5. **C Horizon** (partially weathered parent bedrock regolith).

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Hans Jenny's ClORPT Equation: Soil formation is a mathematical function of five state factors: $S = f(\text{Climate}, \text{Organisms}, \text{Relief}, \text{Parent material}, \text{Time})$.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
Agricultural soil conservation practices, erosion prevention, forensic soil profile analysis.

---

## Key Terms
- **Pedogenesis**: The process of soil formation and development governed by chemical, physical, and biological actions.
- **Eluviation**: Washing out and downward transport of dissolved or suspended minerals from an upper soil horizon.
- **Humus**: Dark, stable organic component of soil formed by the microbial decomposition of plant and animal material.
