# Soil Horizons and Pedogenesis - The Skin of the Earth

> **The Big Idea:** The dynamic stratified layer of weathered mineral particles, organic humus, air, and moisture that supports all terrestrial plant life.

---

## 1. What It Is
The dynamic stratified layer of weathered mineral particles, organic humus, air, and moisture that supports all terrestrial plant life.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Soil profiles are divided into distinct layers: O (organic humus), A (topsoil), B (subsoil mineral accumulation), and C (weathered parent rock).
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
O-Horizon (Humus) | A-Horizon (Topsoil) | B-Horizon (Subsoil) | C-Horizon (Parent Bedrock)
```
