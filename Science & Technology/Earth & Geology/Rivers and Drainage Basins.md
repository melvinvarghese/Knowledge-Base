# Rivers and Drainage Basins

> **The Big Idea:** Rivers are the planetary veins that carry freshwater runoff from mountain peaks to the ocean, carving valleys and depositing fertile floodplains along their course.

---

## 🌊 The 3 River Stages

* **Upper Course:** Steep mountain torrents; high erosion carving V-shaped valleys and waterfalls.
* **Middle Course:** River widens and slows, forming looping bends (**Meanders**).
* **Lower Course & Delta:** Flat, slow-moving river deposits fertile silt and sand as it fans out into the sea (e.g., Nile Delta, Mississippi Delta).
