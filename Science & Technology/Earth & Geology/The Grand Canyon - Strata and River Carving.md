# The Grand Canyon - Strata and River Carving

> **The Big Idea:** A 277-mile chasm carved by the Colorado River exposing nearly two billion years of Earth's geological history through stacked sedimentary and metamorphic rock strata.

---

## 🏔️ A Window into Deep Time
* **The Great Unconformity:** A missing gap in the rock record where 500-million-year-old rock sits directly atop 1.7-billion-year-old rock, representing 1.2 billion years of missing erosion time!
* **Uplift + Erosion:** The Colorado Plateau was uplifted thousands of feet into the air by tectonic forces, giving the Colorado River the steep gravitational slope required to carve 1.6 km deep.
