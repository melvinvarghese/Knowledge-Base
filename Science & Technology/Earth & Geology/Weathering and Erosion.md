# Weathering and Erosion

> **The Big Idea:** The two relentless surface sculptors of Earth. Weathering breaks solid rock down into pieces where it sits; Erosion carries the broken pieces away.

---

## 🪨 Breaking and Moving

* **Mechanical Weathering:** Physical breakdown (frost wedging where freezing water expands in cracks and splits granite).
* **Chemical Weathering:** Chemical alteration (acid rain dissolving limestone; oxygen rusting iron minerals).
* **Erosion Agents:** Running water, blowing wind, grinding glaciers, and gravity (landslides).
