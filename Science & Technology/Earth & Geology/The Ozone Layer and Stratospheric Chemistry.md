# The Ozone Layer and Stratospheric Chemistry

> **The Big Idea:** A delicate shield of triatomic oxygen ($O_3$) in the stratosphere that absorbs over 98% of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet (UV-B) radiation.

---

## 🛡️ Ozone Dynamics

* **Formation:** Solar UV light splits normal $O_2$ molecules; free oxygen atoms instantly bind to $O_2$ to form ozone ($O_3$).
* **The CFC Crisis:** Chlorofluorocarbons (used in old aerosol spray cans and fridges) broke down ozone catalytic chains.
* **The Montreal Protocol (1987):** The most successful environmental treaty in history, phasing out CFCs and allowing the ozone layer to recover.
