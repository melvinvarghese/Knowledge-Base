# Fjord Formations - Glacial Carvings

> **The Big Idea:** Long, deep, narrow sea inlets with towering cliffs carved below sea level by giant valley glaciers during Ice Ages, and later flooded by rising ocean waters.

---

## 🏔️ Glacial Gouging
* **Over-Deepening:** Glaciers carved bedrock deeper than sea level (some Norwegian fjords are over 1,300 meters deep).
* **Threshold (Sill):** At the mouth of the fjord, melting glaciers dropped their rocky debris (moraines), creating a shallow underwater lip where the fjord meets the open ocean.
