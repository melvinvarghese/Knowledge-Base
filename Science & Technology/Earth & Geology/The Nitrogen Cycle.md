# The Nitrogen Cycle

> **The Big Idea:** Nitrogen makes up 78% of our air, but plants and animals cannot breathe it directly. Specialized bacteria must 'fix' nitrogen into usable soil fertilizers.

---

## 🌾 Chemical Transformation

1. **Nitrogen Fixation:** Soil bacteria (and lightning) convert atmospheric $N_2$ into ammonium ($NH_4^+$).
2. **Nitrification:** Other bacteria convert ammonium into nitrates ($NO_3^-$), which plant roots absorb to build proteins and DNA.
3. **Denitrification:** Decomposers and anaerobic bacteria return nitrogen back to the atmosphere as $N_2$ gas.
