# Ocean Currents and the Thermohaline Conveyor

> **The Big Idea:** Global ocean circulation driven by temperature and salinity density gradients that redistributes solar heat around the globe.

---

## 1. What It Is
Global ocean circulation driven by temperature and salinity density gradients that redistributes solar heat around the globe.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Cold, saline water sinks in the North Atlantic, driving a 1,000-year conveyor belt that moderates global climate patterns.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Warm Surface Water Flows North -> Freezing & Evaporation Increases Salinity -> Sinking Deep Current
```
