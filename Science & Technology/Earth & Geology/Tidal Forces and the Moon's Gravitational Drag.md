# Tidal Forces and the Moon's Gravitational Drag

> **The Big Idea:** The differential gravitational attraction exerted by the Moon across Earth's diameter that creates ocean tidal bulges.

---

## 1. What It Is
The differential gravitational attraction exerted by the Moon across Earth's diameter that creates ocean tidal bulges.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Tidal friction gradually slows Earth's rotation (adding milliseconds per century) while pushing the Moon 3.8 cm farther away each year.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Moon Pull -> Ocean Tidal Bulge -> Earth Spins Ahead -> Gravitational Drag Slows Earth
```
