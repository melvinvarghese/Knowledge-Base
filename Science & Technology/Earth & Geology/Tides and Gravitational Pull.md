# Tides and Gravitational Pull

> **The Big Idea:** The periodic rise and fall of ocean waters caused by the gravitational tug-of-war between the Moon, the Sun, and the rotating Earth.

---

## 🌊 Spring Tides vs. Neap Tides

* **Tidal Bulge:** The Moon pulls ocean water toward it, creating a high tide bulge on the near side. Inertia creates a matching bulge on the far side.
* **Spring Tides (Extreme):** When the Sun, Earth, and Moon align (Full Moon and New Moon), their gravities combine to create maximum high tides.
* **Neap Tides (Gentle):** When the Sun and Moon are at 90° right angles (Quarter Moons), their pulls partially cancel out, creating minimal tidal swings.
