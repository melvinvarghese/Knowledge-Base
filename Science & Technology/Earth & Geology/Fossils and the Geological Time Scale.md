# Fossils and the Geological Time Scale

> **The Big Idea:** Fossils are the preserved remains, impressions, or traces of ancient life buried in sedimentary rock. They act as a chronological tape recorder for Earth's 4.5-billion-year history.

---

## 🦴 How Fossils Form (Permineralization)

1. An organism dies and is quickly buried under sediment before scavengers or bacteria destroy it.
2. Mineral-rich groundwater seeps through bones and shells, replacing organic molecules with stone crystals atom by atom.
3. Millions of years later, erosion exposes the petrified rock layer.
* **Index Fossils:** Common, widespread fossils (like Trilobites or Ammonites) used to date rock layers accurately.
