# Desertification - Dryland Degradation

> **The Big Idea:** The process by which fertile, semi-arid drylands become desert through climate change, deforestation, and overgrazing, destroying the biological productivity of topsoil.

---

## 🏜️ Expanding Dust
* **The Sahel Crisis:** The transition zone south of the Sahara desert is rapidly degrading due to prolonged droughts and agricultural pressure.
* **The Great Green Wall:** An ambitious African-led initiative planting an 8,000-kilometer wall of drought-resistant trees across the entire width of Africa to stop desert expansion.
