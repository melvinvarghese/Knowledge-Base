# Magnetic Field Reversals - Geomagnetic Polarity Transitions

> **The Big Idea:** Periodic flips of Earth's magnetic north and south poles recorded in magnetised volcanic basalt on either side of mid-ocean ridges.

---

## 1. What It Is
Periodic flips of Earth's magnetic north and south poles recorded in magnetised volcanic basalt on either side of mid-ocean ridges.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** The last major polarity reversal was the Brunhes-Matuyama event roughly 780,000 years ago, taking thousands of years to complete.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Normal Polarity Crust | Reversal Stripe | Reversed Polarity Crust (Paleomagnetic Tape)
```
