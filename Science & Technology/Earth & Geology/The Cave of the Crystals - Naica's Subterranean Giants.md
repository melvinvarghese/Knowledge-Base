# The Cave of the Crystals - Naica's Subterranean Giants

> **The Big Idea:** A underground limestone cavern in Chihuahua, Mexico, housing the largest natural selenite gypsum crystals ever discovered, up to 12 meters long.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
A underground limestone cavern in Chihuahua, Mexico, housing the largest natural selenite gypsum crystals ever discovered, up to 12 meters long.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Located 300 meters beneath Naica Mountain above a magma chamber, the cave was flooded with mineral-rich groundwater for roughly 500,000 years. Sustained at a steady $58^\circ\text{C}$ in $100\%$ humidity, gypsum precipitated at an ultralow crystallization rate, growing single crystal pillars weighing up to 55 tons.
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Earth & Geology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Magma Chamber Heat -> Saturated Anhydrite Water -> Stable 58°C Mineral Bath -> 12-Meter Gypsum Crystals
```
