# Earthquakes and Tsunamis

> **The Big Idea:** When tectonic plates grind past each other, immense friction locks them in place. Stress builds over decades until the rock violently snaps, releasing waves of energy through the crust as an **Earthquake**—and when occurring under oceans, displacing massive columns of water into a **Tsunami**.

---

## ⚡ The Anatomy of an Earthquake

```
                           [ EPICENTER ] (Point on surface directly above)
                                 │
              Shockwaves ◄───────┼───────► Shockwaves (Seismic Waves)
                                 │
                            [ FOCUS / HYPOCENTER ]
                            (Exact underground fracture point)
```

### The 3 Seismic Waves:
1. **P-Waves (Primary / Compressional):** Fastest waves (6 km/s); push and pull rock like an accordion; can travel through solid rock and liquid core.
2. **S-Waves (Secondary / Shear):** Slower (3.5 km/s); shake rock side-to-side; **cannot travel through liquids** (this is how scientists proved Earth's outer core is liquid!).
3. **Surface Waves (Rayleigh & Love):** Slowest, but roll across Earth's surface like ocean waves; cause almost all building collapses and destruction.

---

## 🌊 How an Undersea Earthquake Becomes a Tsunami

A tsunami is **not** a regular wind wave. It is a giant shockwave moving through the entire depth of the ocean:

```
[ TECTONIC PLATE SNAPS UPWARD ]
              │
              ▼
[ COLUMN OF OCEAN WATER THRUST UPWARD ]
              │
              ▼
[ TSUNAMI TRAVELS ACROSS DEEP OCEAN (Jet speed: 800 km/h; height only 1 meter) ]
              │
              ▼
[ COASTAL SHALLOW WATER: Wave slows down to 40 km/h and SHOALS UPWARD (10 to 30+ meters tall) ]
```

* **The Warning Sign:** Before a tsunami strikes, the sea often recedes hundreds of meters away from the shore, exposing reefs and stranded fish. **This is the signal to run to high ground immediately.**

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway: The "Drop, Cover, and Hold On" Rule

* **Myth:** Standing in a doorway protects you (doorways in modern buildings are not reinforced).
* **Reality:** Drop to hands and knees, take cover under a sturdy desk or table, and hold on until shaking stops.
