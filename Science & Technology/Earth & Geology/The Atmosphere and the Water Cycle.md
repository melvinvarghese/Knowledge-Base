# The Atmosphere and the Water Cycle

> **The Big Idea:** Life on Earth is shielded by a multi-layered atmospheric blanket and powered by an unbroken, solar-driven water recycling system that cleans and redistributes fresh water across the globe.

---

## 🌬️ The 5 Atmospheric Layers

```
  SPACE ──────────────────────────────────────────
  600+ km     │ EXOSPHERE (Fades into space; satellites orbit here)
  85-600 km   │ THERMOSPHERE (Auroras; International Space Station)
  50-85 km    │ MESOSPHERE (Meteors burn up here from friction)
  12-50 km    │ STRATOSPHERE (Ozone Layer blocks deadly UV rays)
  0-12 km     │ TROPOSPHERE (Where we live, breathe, and weather happens)
  EARTH ──────────────────────────────────────────
```

* **Troposphere:** 78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, 1% Argon & trace gases. Holds 80% of atmospheric mass and all cloud/weather phenomena.
* **The Ozone Layer ($O_3$):** Located in the Stratosphere. Acts as Earth's natural sunscreen, absorbing harmful ultraviolet-B radiation.
* **The Greenhouse Effect:** Naturally traps heat so Earth's average temperature stays a comfortable 15°C instead of a frozen -18°C.

---

## 💧 The Endless Water Cycle (Hydrological Cycle)

Every drop of water on Earth today is millions of years old. The water in your morning coffee was once drunk by dinosaurs.

```
                         [ CLOUDS IN SKY ]
                            ▲        │
               Evaporation  │        │ Precipitation
                            │        ▼ (Rain, Snow)
              [ OCEANS & LAKES ] ◄── [ RUNOFF & GROUNDWATER ]
                     ▲
                     │ Transpiration (Plants breathing out moisture)
                [ FORESTS ]
```

1. **Evaporation:** Solar heat warms ocean water, turning liquid into invisible water vapor that rises into the atmosphere.
2. **Transpiration:** Plants pull groundwater through their roots and release water vapor through leaf pores (stomata).
3. **Condensation:** Rising vapor cools and condenses around microscopic dust particles to form clouds.
4. **Precipitation:** As droplets collide and grow heavy, gravity pulls them down as rain, sleet, hail, or snow.
5. **Collection & Runoff:** Water filters through soil into rivers, aquifers, and flows back into the oceans, resetting the cycle.

---

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaway

* Oceans hold **97% of Earth's water**, and 2% is locked in polar ice caps. Only **1%** is liquid fresh water available for all humanity, agriculture, and land animals.
