# Caves and Karst Landscapes

> **The Big Idea:** Caves are subterranean cathedrals carved by acidic water slowly dissolving soluble bedrock like limestone over hundreds of thousands of years.

---

## 🦇 Speleothems: Stalactites vs. Stalagmites

* Rainwater absorbs $CO_2$ from air and soil, forming mild carbonic acid ($H_2CO_3$).
* The acidic water dissolves calcium carbonate in limestone, carving caverns and sinkholes.
* **Stalactites:** Hang **tight** from the ceiling as mineral water drips.
* **Stalagmites:** Grow **mighty** from the ground up as drips hit the floor.
