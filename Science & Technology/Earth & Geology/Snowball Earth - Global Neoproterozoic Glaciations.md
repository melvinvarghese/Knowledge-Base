# Snowball Earth - Global Neoproterozoic Glaciations

> **The Big Idea:** Geological hypotheses that Earth froze over completely from poles to equator roughly 700 to 650 million years ago.

---

## 1. What It Is
Geological hypotheses that Earth froze over completely from poles to equator roughly 700 to 650 million years ago.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Runaway ice-albedo feedback cooled the planet until volcanic carbon dioxide accumulated sufficiently to trigger a super-greenhouse melt.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Ice Advance -> Higher Albedo -> Global Freeze -> Volcanic CO2 Buildup -> Rapid Thaw
```
