# Mass Wasting - Landslides, Mudflows, and Rockfalls

> **The Big Idea:** The downslope movement of rock, regolith, and soil under the direct influence of gravity when shear stress exceeds shear strength.

---

## 1. What It Is
The downslope movement of rock, regolith, and soil under the direct influence of gravity when shear stress exceeds shear strength.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Triggered by water saturation, seismic shaking, over-steepening, and loss of stabilizing root systems from deforestation.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Slope Saturation -> Pore Water Pressure Rises -> Shear Strength Fails -> Rapid Downslope Slide
```
