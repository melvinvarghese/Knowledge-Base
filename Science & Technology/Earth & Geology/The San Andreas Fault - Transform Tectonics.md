# The San Andreas Fault - Transform Tectonics

> **The Big Idea:** An 800-mile transform fault slicing through California where the Pacific Plate and North American Plate grind horizontally past each other at 5 cm per year, driving major earthquakes.

---

## 💥 Strike-Slip Fault Mechanics
* **Locked and Creeping Segments:** Some sections creep smoothly, but locked sections accumulate immense elastic strain for centuries until violently rupturing (e.g., 1906 San Francisco earthquake).
* **Future Shaking:** Los Angeles and San Francisco are moving toward each other; in about 20 million years, they will be adjacent coastal neighbors.
