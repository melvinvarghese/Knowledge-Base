# Waterfalls - Plunge Pools and Retreat

> **The Big Idea:** Dramatic geological features formed where a river flows over a layer of hard, resistant rock resting on softer rock, carving plunge pools and slowly retreating upstream over centuries.

---

## 💧 Hydraulic Sculpting
* **Undercutting:** Water erodes the soft shale underneath the hard caprock. Eventually, the overhang collapses into the plunge pool.
* **Niagara Falls Retreat:** Niagara Falls has migrated 11 kilometers upstream over the past 12,000 years as its limestone caprock repeatedly collapsed.
