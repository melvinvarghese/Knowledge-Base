# Volcanic Ash and Volcanic Winter

> **The Big Idea:** Finely pulverized rock, mineral, and glass shards ejected into the stratosphere, reflecting incoming solar radiation.

## 1. What It Is
Finely pulverized rock, mineral, and glass shards ejected into the stratosphere, reflecting incoming solar radiation.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Sulfur dioxide aerosols oxidize into sulfuric acid droplets, cooling global temperatures for years (e.g. Mount Tambora 1815 'Year Without a Summer').
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
SO2 Gas Injection -> Stratospheric Sulfate Aerosols -> Sunlight Reflection -> Global Cooling
```
