# The Carbon Cycle - Geologic and Biological Fluxes

> **The Big Idea:** The biogeochemical cycle by which carbon is exchanged among the biosphere, pedosphere, geosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere.

---

## 1. What It Is
The biogeochemical cycle by which carbon is exchanged among the biosphere, pedosphere, geosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Volcanic outgassing and carbonate precipitation in marine organisms form limestone, acting as Earth's long-term thermostat.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Atmospheric CO2 <-> Photosynthesis / Respiration <-> Ocean Carbonate <-> Limestone Burial
```
