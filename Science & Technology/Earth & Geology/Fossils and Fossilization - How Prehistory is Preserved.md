# Fossils and Fossilization - How Prehistory is Preserved

> **The Big Idea:** The preservation of ancient biological remains or traces in rock through permineralization, cast and mold formation, carbonization, or amber entombment.

---

## 1. What It Is
The preservation of ancient biological remains or traces in rock through permineralization, cast and mold formation, carbonization, or amber entombment.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Rapid burial in anaerobic, fine-grained sediment is essential to prevent scavengers and aerobic bacteria from destroying organic remains.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Organism Dies -> Rapid Sediment Burial -> Mineralized Groundwater Permeation -> Rock Petrifaction
```
