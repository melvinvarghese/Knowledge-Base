# Glaciers and Ice Ages

> **The Big Idea:** Glaciers are massive rivers of compressed ice that flow slowly under their own weight. They are nature's bulldozers, carving iconic U-shaped valleys and fjords.

---

## 🧊 Ice Shaping Landscapes

* **Valley Glaciers vs. Ice Sheets:** Alpine glaciers carve mountain valleys, while continental ice sheets (Antarctica, Greenland) bury entire continents in ice kilometers thick.
* **U-Shaped Valleys:** Unlike V-shaped river valleys, glaciers scrape flat bottoms and vertical walls.
* **Milankovitch Cycles:** Periodic variations in Earth's orbital eccentricity, tilt, and wobble that trigger Ice Ages every 100,000 years.
