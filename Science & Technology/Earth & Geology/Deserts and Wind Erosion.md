# Deserts and Wind Erosion

> **The Big Idea:** Covering one-third of Earth's land, deserts are defined by aridity (less than 25 cm of rainfall per year), where wind (Aeolian processes) shapes the terrain.

---

## 🏜️ Desert Realities

* **Hot vs. Cold Deserts:** Deserts are defined by lack of water, not heat. The Sahara is the largest hot desert, but **Antarctica is the largest desert on Earth**!
* **Dunes:** Wind blows sand grains up gentle slopes until they collapse down steep slip-faces (Barchan, Star, and Transverse dunes).
* **Desert Varnish:** Dark manganese and iron oxide coating baked onto desert stones over millennia.
