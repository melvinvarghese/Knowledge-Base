# Permafrost and Tundra Landscapes

> **The Big Idea:** Ground that remains completely frozen ($0^{\circ}\text{C}$ or colder) for at least two consecutive years. Permafrost underlies 15% of the Northern Hemisphere and stores vast amounts of ancient organic carbon.

---

## ❄️ The Melting Carbon Bomb
* **Active Layer:** The top few inches of soil that thaws during summer, allowing moss and lichens to grow.
* **Methane Release:** When permafrost thaws, prehistoric plants and mammoths decompose, releasing immense volumes of methane ($CH_4$), triggering a runaway warming feedback loop.
* **Infrastructure Collapse:** Thawing permafrost causes roads, pipelines, and buildings to buckle and tilt across the Arctic.
