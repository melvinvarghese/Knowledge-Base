# Caves and Karst Landscapes - Underground Water Carving

> **The Big Idea:** Topographies formed from the dissolution of soluble rocks such as limestone, dolomite, and gypsum by mildly acidic groundwater.

---

## 1. What It Is
Topographies formed from the dissolution of soluble rocks such as limestone, dolomite, and gypsum by mildly acidic groundwater.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Carbonic acid dissolves calcite, creating subterranean caverns, sinkholes, disappearing streams, stalactites, and stalagmites.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
CO2 + Rainwater -> Carbonic Acid -> Calcite Dissolution -> Cavern System
```
