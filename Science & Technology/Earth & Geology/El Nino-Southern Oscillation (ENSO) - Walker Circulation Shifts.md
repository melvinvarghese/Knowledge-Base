# El Nino-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) - Walker Circulation Shifts

## Overview
The El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) is an irregular, multi-year coupled ocean-atmosphere climate cycle across the tropical Pacific Ocean that alters global weather patterns and jet streams.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations & Governing Mechanisms
The Walker Circulation & Thermocline Dynamics: Under normal conditions, trade winds push warm water westward, upwelling cold, nutrient-rich water along Peru; during **El Niño**, trade winds weaken, allowing the warm Pacific pool to surge eastward, flattening the thermocline and shutting down Peruvian upwelling.

---

## 2. Structural & Pathological Analysis
Global Teleconnections: El Niño alters high-altitude atmospheric Rossby waves, bringing torrential rains and flooding to South America while triggering extreme drought in Australia and Indonesia and disrupting Atlantic hurricanes.

---

## 3. Clinical Significance & Real-World Applications
Global agricultural commodity yield forecasting, flood and drought municipal preparedness planning.

---

## Key Terms
- **El Niño**: Warm phase of ENSO characterized by anomalous sea surface warming in the central and eastern tropical Pacific.
- **La Niña**: Cool phase of ENSO characterized by abnormally cold sea surface temperatures in the eastern tropical Pacific.
- **Teleconnection**: Long-distance atmospheric links between weather patterns in widely separated global regions.
