# The Great Oxygenation Event - Cyanobacteria Transform the Air

> **The Big Idea:** The biological turning point 2.4 billion years ago when photosynthetic cyanobacteria filled the oceans and atmosphere with free oxygen.

---

## 1. What It Is
The biological turning point 2.4 billion years ago when photosynthetic cyanobacteria filled the oceans and atmosphere with free oxygen.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Reacted with dissolved iron to create massive banded iron formations (BIFs) and wiped out obligate anaerobic life in the first great extinction.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Cyanobacteria Photosynthesis -> Ocean Iron Rusts Out (BIFs) -> Free Atmospheric O2
```
