# Sinkholes and Karst Collapse

> **The Big Idea:** Geological depressions formed when acidic groundwater dissolves subterranean limestone or gypsum bedrock, hollowing out caverns until the surface soil suddenly collapses.

---

## 🕳️ Karst Mechanics
* **Cover-Collapse Sinkholes:** The most catastrophic type; structural cavern ceilings thin until roofs drop without warning, swallowing roads, houses, and trees.
* **Cenotes of the Yucatán:** Natural sinkholes in Mexico that exposed the groundwater table, revered by ancient Mayans as sacred gateways to the underworld.
