# Lake Baikal - Ancient Rift Lake of Deep Freshwater

> **The Big Idea:** The world's oldest, deepest, and most voluminous freshwater lake, holding roughly 20% of the world's unfrozen surface fresh water.

---

## 1. What It Is & Why It Fascinates
The world's oldest, deepest, and most voluminous freshwater lake, holding roughly 20% of the world's unfrozen surface fresh water.

## 2. Core Mechanics & Scientific Principles
- **Underlying Principle:** Formed along an active continental rift zone in Siberia 25 million years ago, Lake Baikal reaches a maximum depth of 1,642 meters. Deep water oxygenation is sustained by unique cold thermohaline convection currents, supporting over 2,500 endemic species including the world's only freshwater seal (nerpa).
- **Subject Integration:** Within **Earth & Geology**, this phenomenon illustrates the outer boundaries of how natural laws and physical systems operate under extreme conditions.
- **Process & Architecture:**
```
Continental Crustal Rifting -> 25-Million-Year Deep Grabens -> Deep Water Convection Circulation
```
