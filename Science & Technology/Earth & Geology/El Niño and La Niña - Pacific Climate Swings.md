# El Niño and La Niña - Pacific Climate Swings

> **The Big Idea:** The El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO): periodic warming and cooling of equatorial Pacific Ocean surface waters that shifts atmospheric jet streams and disrupts weather patterns worldwide.

---

## 🌊 The Pacific Seesaw
* **El Niño (Warm Phase):** Trade winds weaken, allowing warm western Pacific water to slosh east toward South America. Causes severe droughts in Australia and heavy rains/floods in California.
* **La Niña (Cold Phase):** Trade winds blow unusually strong, pushing cold deep water to the surface in the eastern Pacific. Intensifies Atlantic hurricane seasons and triggers southern droughts.
