# Mid-Atlantic Ridge - Underwater Mountain Range

> **The Big Idea:** The longest mountain chain on Earth, winding for 16,000 km along the floor of the Atlantic Ocean where tectonic plates pull apart and create brand-new oceanic crust.

---

## 🌊 Seafloor Spreading
* **Divergent Boundary:** The Eurasian and North American plates drift apart at 2.5 cm per year. Magma wells up from the mantle to fill the gap, solidifying into fresh basalt rock.
* **Iceland:** The only place where the Mid-Atlantic Ridge rises above sea level, allowing geologists to physically walk between two tectonic continents on dry land.
