# Dating the Earth - Radiometric Decay and Isotope Clocks

> **The Big Idea:** The determination of absolute rock ages by measuring the ratio of unstable parent radioactive isotopes to stable daughter isotopes.

---

## 1. What It Is
The determination of absolute rock ages by measuring the ratio of unstable parent radioactive isotopes to stable daughter isotopes.

## 2. How It Works & Key Insights
- **Core Mechanism:** Uranium-lead and potassium-argon decay have fixed half-lives unaffected by temperature or pressure, dating Earth to 4.54 billion years.
- **Scientific Foundation:** In the study of earth & geology, this concept represents a fundamental pillar of physical reality and natural order.
- **Detailed Mechanics:**
```
Parent Isotope (100%) -> Half-Life 1 (50:50) -> Half-Life 2 (25:75) -> Stable Daughter
```
