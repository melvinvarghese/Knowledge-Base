# Blood Types and the ABO System

> **The Big Idea:** Human blood is categorized into A, B, AB, and O based on inherited carbohydrate marker antigens sitting on the surface of red blood cells.

---

## 🩸 Compatibility Matrix

* **Type A:** Has A antigens (antibodies against B).
* **Type B:** Has B antigens (antibodies against A).
* **Type AB:** Has BOTH A and B antigens (**Universal Recipient**; has no antibodies).
* **Type O:** Has NEITHER antigen (**Universal Donor**; immune systems will not attack it).
* **Rh Factor (+/-):** Determines presence or absence of the Rh protein.
