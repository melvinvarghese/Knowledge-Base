# The Digestive System - From Food to Fuel

> **The Big Idea:** A 9-meter continuous gastrointestinal tube that mechanically crushes, chemically digests, and absorbs nutrients from food while expelling waste.

---

## 🍽️ The Journey of Food

1. **Mouth & Stomach:** Chewing and hydrochloric acid ($pH \approx 1.5$) break food down into a slurry called **Chyme**.
2. **Small Intestine:** The workhorse of digestion. Microscopic finger-like folds (**Villi**) absorb 90% of all nutrients into the bloodstream.
3. **Large Intestine (Colon):** Reabsorbs water and compacts leftover indigestible fiber into solid waste.
