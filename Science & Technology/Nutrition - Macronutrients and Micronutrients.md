# Nutrition - Macronutrients and Micronutrients

> **The Big Idea:** The biochemical fuels that keep human metabolism running: energy-yielding Macronutrients (Carbs, Fats, Proteins) and catalytic Micronutrients (Vitamins, Minerals).

---

## 🥗 The Biochemical Breakdown

* **Carbohydrates (4 kcal/g):** Primary rapid fuel broken down into glucose.
* **Proteins (4 kcal/g):** Broken down into 20 amino acids to build muscles, enzymes, and hormones.
* **Fats (9 kcal/g):** Dense energy storage, cell membrane structure, and nerve insulation.
* **Vitamins & Minerals:** Trace co-factors (e.g., Vitamin C for collagen, Iron for hemoglobin, Calcium for bone strength).
