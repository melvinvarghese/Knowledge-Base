# Classical Chinese (Wenyan)

## Overview
Classical Chinese (*Wényán*) served for over two millennia as the shared written literary, philosophical, and bureaucratic medium across China, Korea, Japan, and Vietnam. Extreme terseness, flexible word classes, and omission of understood subjects enabled philosophers from Confucius to Zhu Xi to compress vast arguments into aphorisms.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Modeled on Late Zhou and Warring States prose (c. 500–200 BCE, *Analects*, *Dao De Jing*, *Zhuangzi*), Classical Chinese favored monosyllabic words where a single character often functioned flexibly as noun, verb, or adjective depending on syntactic position. Lacking tense, plural markers, and copulas in many equational sentences, it relied heavily on rhythmic parallelism and sentence-final modal particles (*yě, yī, hū*) to signal assertion, doubt, or exclamation.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Even as spoken Chinese dialects diverged dramatically, *Wényán* remained East Asia's equivalent of Latin until the early 20th century, when the 1919 May Fourth Movement championed *Báihuà* (vernacular Mandarin prose) for mass literacy.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Classical Chinese idioms (*chéngyǔ*, four-character aphorisms) remain deeply woven into modern Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese journalism and rhetoric.

## Key Terminology
- **Wényán**: Literary Classical Chinese prose style modeled on Warring States and Han-dynasty texts.
- **Báihuà**: Written vernacular Chinese adopted in the early 20th century to replace Classical Chinese.
- **Chéngyǔ**: Four-character classical Chinese idiomatic expressions compressing historical stories.
- **Sinosphere**: East Asian cultural sphere historically sharing Chinese characters, Classical Chinese literacy, and Confucian texts.
- **Kanbun**: Japanese tradition of reading and annotating Classical Chinese texts with grammatical reordering marks.
- **Particle (*xūzì*)**: 'Empty word' serving purely grammatical or modal functions in Classical Chinese.
