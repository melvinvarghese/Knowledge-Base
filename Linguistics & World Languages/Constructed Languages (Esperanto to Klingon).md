# Constructed Languages (Esperanto to Klingon)

## Overview
Constructed languages ('conlangs') are deliberately engineered linguistic systems crafted for international peace, philosophical logic, or fictional world-building. From Ludwik Zamenhof's Esperanto and J.R.R. Tolkien's Elvish to Klingon and Dothraki, conlangs test the boundaries of human grammar.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Philosophical 'a priori' languages in the 17th century (John Wilkins) tried to mirror scientific taxonomy directly in syllables. In 1887, ophthalmologist L.L. Zamenhof launched Esperanto — an 'international auxiliary language' blending Romance/Germanic vocabulary with maximally regular, exception-free agglutinative endings — aiming to foster neutral global brotherhood without erasing native tongues; today ~100,000+ active speakers and even native family speakers use it worldwide.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
In the 20th century, philologist J.R.R. Tolkien elevated 'artlangs' by inventing Quenya and Sindarin complete with simulated centuries of sound shifts and mythological poetry ('stories grew to house the languages'). Modern Hollywood and gaming sparked professional conlanging: linguist Marc Okrand engineered Klingon with rare OVS word order and guttural phonology to sound genuinely alien, while David J. Peterson crafted Dothraki and High Valyrian with naturalistic historical evolution.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Conlangs also serve as controlled laboratory tools in psycholinguistics — letting researchers test how brains learn novel artificial grammars free from prior cultural baggage.

## Key Terminology
- **Conlang (Constructed language)**: Language whose phonology, grammar, and vocabulary were intentionally designed rather than naturally evolved.
- **Auxiliary language (Auxlang)**: Constructed language (like Esperanto or Interlingua) designed to ease neutral international communication.
- **Artlang**: Artistic constructed language built for aesthetic pleasure, fiction, or world-building (e.g., Elvish, High Valyrian).
- **Engelang**: Engineered language testing logical, mathematical, or philosophical hypotheses (e.g., Lojban, Toki Pona).
- **A priori vs. A posteriori**: Vocabulary invented from scratch versus vocabulary adapted from existing natural languages.
- **Toki Pona**: Minimalist philosophical conlang created by Sonja Lang using roughly 120–137 root words.
