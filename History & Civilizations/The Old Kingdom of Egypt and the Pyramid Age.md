# The Old Kingdom of Egypt and the Pyramid Age

## Overview
The Old Kingdom of Egypt (c. 2686–2181 BCE), known as the 'Age of the Pyramids', marked the crystallization of pharaonic divine kingship and centralized monumental statecraft.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Evolution from Mastaba to True Pyramid: Imhotep designed the Step Pyramid of Djoser at Saqqara (Third Dynasty), stacking stone mastabas. Sneferu (Fourth Dynasty) perfected true smooth-sided pyramids through trial and structural engineering at Meidum, Dahshur (Bent and Red Pyramids).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Great Pyramids of Giza & State Mobilization: Under Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure, quarrying, transport, and geometric alignment of 2.3 million limestone blocks demonstrated staggering administrative coordination, fed by seasonal Nile flood agricultural labor rather than slave labor.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Monumental stone masonry engineering, bureaucratic centralization under the Vizier, theology of pharaonic cosmic order (*Ma'at*).

## Key Terminology
- **Old Kingdom**: Era of classical Egyptian history encompassing the Third to Sixth Dynasties renowned for monumental pyramid construction.
- **Imhotep**: Royal architect and vizier who engineered the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, later deified as patron of medicine and wisdom.
- **Ma'at**: Ancient Egyptian concept of universal harmony, truth, balance, and divine cosmic order.
