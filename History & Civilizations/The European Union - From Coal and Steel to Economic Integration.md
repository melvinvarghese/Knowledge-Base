# The European Union - From Coal and Steel to Economic Integration

## Overview
The European Union (EU) is a unique supranational economic and political union of European democracies designed to prevent continental war through deep institutional integration.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Origins in the Schuman Plan (1950): French Foreign Minister Robert Schuman proposed pooling French and German coal and steel production under a common High Authority (1951 European Coal and Steel Community), making war between historic rivals 'materially impossible'. The 1957 Treaty of Rome established the European Economic Community (EEC) and common market.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Maastricht Treaty & The Euro: The 1992 Maastricht Treaty formally created the European Union, establishing EU citizenship, common foreign and security policies, and the roadmap for the Euro single currency (adopted in 1999). The Schengen Agreement abolished internal border checks across member states.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Pioneering model of peaceful supranational governance, single internal market, global regulatory standards ('The Brussels Effect').

## Key Terminology
- **European Union**: Supranational political and economic union of 27 European member states.
- **Maastricht Treaty**: 1992 treaty founding the European Union and creating the framework for the single European currency (Euro).
- **Schengen Area**: Free movement zone abolishing passport and border control at internal European borders.
