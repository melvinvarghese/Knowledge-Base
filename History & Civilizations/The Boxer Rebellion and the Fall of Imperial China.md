# The Boxer Rebellion and the Fall of Imperial China

## Overview
The Boxer Rebellion (1899–1901) was an anti-foreign, anti-colonial peasant uprising in China that precipitated the collapse of the 2,000-year-old Chinese imperial dynastic system.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
The Righteous and Harmonious Fists: Peasants in northern China formed secret martial societies known to Westerners as 'Boxers', believing spiritual rituals granted them invulnerability to foreign bullets. Driven by resentment of foreign economic domination and missionary aggression, Boxers besieged the Beijing foreign legations with tacit backing from Empress Dowager Cixi.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
The Eight-Nation Alliance & The 1911 Xinhai Revolution: An Eight-Nation Alliance (UK, US, Japan, Russia, France, Germany, Italy, Austria-Hungary) crushed the uprising, sacking Beijing and imposing the humiliating Boxer Protocol of 1901. Discredited and bankrupt, the Qing dynasty collapsed a decade later during the 1911 Xinhai Revolution led by Dr. Sun Yat-sen, establishing the Republic of China.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Abolition of the imperial examination system (1905), end of the 267-year Qing Dynasty and millennia of imperial rule.

## Key Terminology
- **Boxer Protocol**: 1901 treaty imposing a colossal 450-million-tael silver indemnity on China and stationing foreign troops in Beijing.
- **Eight-Nation Alliance**: Multi-national military coalition formed to rescue besieged legations and suppress the Boxer movement in 1900.
- **Xinhai Revolution**: The 1911 revolution that overthrew China's final imperial Qing dynasty and founded the Republic of China.
