# Consciousness and Global Workspace Theory

## Overview
How does three pounds of wet biological tissue generate subjective first-person experience — the redness of red or the sting of grief? Moving consciousness from pure philosophy into empirical neuroscience, researchers compare Neural Correlates of Consciousness (NCC) under Global Workspace Theory, Integrated Information Theory, and predictive processing.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Francis Crick and Christof Koch launched modern consciousness science in the 1990s by hunting the minimal Neural Correlates of Consciousness (NCC) — using binocular rivalry (presenting two different images to each eye so perception flips while retinal input stays constant) to separate unconscious sensory processing from conscious awareness. Two leading theories emerged. Bernard Baars and Stanislas Dehaene's Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT) proposes information becomes conscious when it triggers a nonlinear 'ignition' across long-range fronto-parietal axons, broadcasting it globally to memory, language, and decision systems.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Conversely, Giulio Tononi's Integrated Information Theory (IIT) starts from phenomenology — consciousness is unified and maximally irreducible — proposing a mathematical index (Phi, Φ) measuring how much a physical system integrates information beyond its parts, locating primary sensory experience largely in a posterior cortical 'hot zone' rather than frontal executive hubs. Recent adversarial collaborations tested IIT vs. GNWT head-to-head with mixed, nuanced results.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Clinically, consciousness metrics — notably Marcello Massimini's Perturbational Complexity Index (zapping cortex with TMS and measuring EEG echo complexity) — now detect hidden 'covert consciousness' in vegetative-state patients unable to move or speak, transforming bioethics and coma care.

## Key Terminology
- **Global Neuronal Workspace**: Dehaene and Baars's model proposing consciousness arises from widespread fronto-parietal broadcast ('ignition').
- **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**: Tononi's theory equating consciousness with irreducibly integrated causal information (Phi).
- **Neural Correlates of Consciousness**: Minimal brain mechanisms sufficient for a specific conscious percept.
- **Binocular rivalry**: Experimental paradigm presenting conflicting images to each eye to track perceptual switches independently of stimuli.
- **Covert consciousness**: Preserved conscious awareness in behaviorally unresponsive coma or vegetative-state patients.
- **Hard problem of consciousness**: David Chalmers's distinction between explaining cognitive functions ('easy problems') and why subjective qualia exist at all.
