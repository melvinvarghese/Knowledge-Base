# Hippocampus and Spatial Navigation

## Overview
Tucked inside the medial temporal lobe, the hippocampus serves a remarkable dual role: binding episodic memories across time and constructing an internal GPS-like spatial map of the environment. The Nobel Prize-winning discovery of place cells and grid cells revealed how neural firing literally encodes physical and conceptual space.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Following Henry Molaison (H.M.), whose bilateral hippocampal removal prevented new episodic learning, John O'Keefe discovered 'place cells' in rat hippocampus in 1971 — individual neurons firing selectively whenever an animal enters one specific location in a room. Decades later, May-Britt and Edvard Moser discovered 'grid cells' in the neighboring entorhinal cortex firing in astonishingly regular hexagonal lattices spanning environments, alongside head-direction and border cells.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Crucially, Eleanor Maguire's MRI study of London taxi drivers mastering 'The Knowledge' (25,000 streets) showed enlarged posterior hippocampi correlating with years of navigation — proving adult structural neuroplasticity. Furthermore, Howard Eichenbaum and Timothy Behrens demonstrated that hippocampal-entorhinal maps extend beyond physical geography to navigate abstract social hierarchies, conceptual knowledge, and sequential event timelines.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Because the hippocampus is among the earliest regions ravaged in Alzheimer's disease, spatial disorientation often heralds memory decline, driving VR spatial-navigation biomarkers for early dementia detection.

## Key Terminology
- **Place cells**: Hippocampal pyramidal neurons firing selectively when an organism occupies a specific physical location.
- **Grid cells**: Entorhinal cortex neurons firing in periodic hexagonal grids providing a metric coordinate system for navigation.
- **Cognitive map**: Internal neural representation of spatial and conceptual relationships supporting flexible shortcuts.
- **Entorhinal cortex**: Primary gateway relaying sensory cortex input into and out of the hippocampus.
- **London taxi driver study**: Maguire's landmark MRI proof of use-dependent hippocampal gray-matter growth in expert navigators.
- **Theta oscillations**: 4–8 Hz hippocampal rhythm organizing sequential place-cell firing and episodic memory encoding.
