# Islam: Qur'an, Sunnah, and Pillars

## Overview
Revealed in 7th-century Arabia through the Prophet Muhammad, Islam ('submission to God') is the world's second-largest religion (~1.9 billion adherents). Anchored in radical monotheism (*Tawhid*), the recited *Qur'an*, and the Five Pillars, Islam forged a global civilization spanning Spain to Indonesia.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Muslims hold that in 610 CE, the Angel Jibril (Gabriel) began revealing God's final Arabic word to Muhammad in Mecca, continuing over 23 years across Mecca and Medina. Compiled shortly after his death under Caliph Uthman, the *Qur'an* is revered as God's literal, inimitable speech, supplemented by the *Sunnah*—Muhammad's normative example recorded in *Hadith* reports.

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Core practice revolves around the Five Pillars (*Arkan al-Islam*): *Shahada* (witnessing there is no god but Allah and Muhammad is His messenger), *Salat* (five daily ritual prayers facing Mecca), *Zakat* (obligatory charitable alms purifying wealth), *Sawm* (dawn-to-dusk fasting during Ramadan), and *Hajj* (pilgrimage to Mecca once in a lifetime if able).

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Emphasizing direct accountability to God without clerical priesthood, Islamic civilization preserved Greek science, pioneered algebra and optics, and built enduring artistic and legal institutions.

## Key Terminology
- **Tawhid**: Absolute, indivisible oneness and uniqueness of God—the foundational creed of Islam.
- **Qur'an**: Islam's sacred scripture believed to be God's literal Arabic revelation to Prophet Muhammad.
- **Sunnah and Hadith**: Prophetic example and authenticated oral/written reports of Muhammad's sayings and actions.
- **Five Pillars of Islam**: Core ritual duties: declaration of faith (Shahada), prayer (Salat), alms (Zakat), fasting (Sawm), pilgrimage (Hajj).
- **Ummah**: Global spiritual community of Muslims transcending race, tribe, and national borders.
- **Ramadan**: Ninth Islamic lunar month observed with daytime fasting, Qur'an recitation, and charity.
