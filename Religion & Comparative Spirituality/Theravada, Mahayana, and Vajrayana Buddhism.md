# Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana Buddhism

## Overview
As Buddhism spread across Asia over 2,500 years, it evolved into three major historical vehicles—Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana—sharing the Four Noble Truths while differing in ideal archetypes, scriptures, and meditative methods.

## Core Mechanics & Foundations
Theravada ('Way of the Elders'), dominant in Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar, Laos, and Cambodia, preserves the earliest Pali Canon (*Tipiṭaka*). Emphasizing monastic discipline and *vipassanā* insight meditation, its ideal is the *Arhat*—one who attains personal *Nirvāṇa* through rigorous mindfulness and wisdom. Around the 1st century BCE, *Mahāyāna* ('Great Vehicle') arose across India, China, Korea, Japan, and Vietnam, elevating the *Bodhisattva*—one who vows to attain full Buddhahood specifically to liberate *all* sentient beings through boundless compassion (*karuṇā*) and wisdom (*prajñā*).

## Modern Applications & Advanced Significance
Mahayana philosopher Nāgārjuna revolutionized metaphysics with *Śūnyatā* ('emptiness'): all things lack fixed independent essence precisely because they exist interdependently. Later in India and Tibet, *Vajrayana* ('Diamond/Thunderbolt Vehicle') integrated Tantric visualization, *mantras*, and guru transmission to transform everyday desire and emotion rapidly into awakened clarity.

## Real-World Impact & Systems
Together, these three vehicles demonstrate Buddhism's extraordinary adaptability across diverse cultures without centralized orthodoxy.

## Key Terminology
- **Theravada**: Conservative Pali-Canon school predominant in Southeast Asia, prioritizing monasticism and *Arhat* liberation.
- **Mahayana**: 'Great Vehicle' emphasizing universal Buddha-nature, emptiness (*śūnyatā*), and the compassionate *Bodhisattva* vow.
- **Bodhisattva**: Awakening being who postpones or dedicates enlightenment to liberate all suffering creatures.
- **Śūnyatā (Emptiness)**: Nāgārjuna's insight that phenomena lack fixed, independent self-existence because all things are interdependent.
- **Vajrayana**: Tantric Buddhist path using sacred mantra, mandala visualization, and deity yoga for swift awakening.
- **Pali Canon (Tipiṭaka)**: Earliest complete surviving Buddhist scripture collection preserved in Pali ('Three Baskets').
